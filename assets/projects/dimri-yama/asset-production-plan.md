## Visual Review Notes

The generated poster is directionally useful because it shows:

- four-building compound,
- sea and park context,
- premium sales-center tone,
- visible 3D/apartment-selection cue,
- enough atmosphere to invite a click.

It is not final because:

- it is not an official Dimri render,
- building massing may not match the exact approved design,
- in-image Hebrew must be reviewed before public use,
- it must not imply official availability, prices or permits.

## Asset Budgets

- Poster: target under 350 KB after WebP compression.
- Facade SVG: target under 120 KB.
- Prototype GLB: target under 8 MB.
- Interior stills: WebP, under 250 KB each.
- Do not bundle heavy media in the plugin ZIP.

- poster/intro,

- facade picker immediately beside it,

Mobile:

- intro,
