## Official / Public Sources

1. Dimri official project page:
   https://www.dimri.co.il/dimriyama-2/

   Public facts found:
   - Between the sea coast and Yarkon Park.
   - Four buildings, 9 to 39 floors.
   - Rani Ziss Architects.
   - Kelly Hoppen CBE interior design.
   - Green internal courtyard and western facade with wave-like balconies.
   - Amenities include rooftop infinity pool, indoor semi-Olympic pool, spa/sauna,
     private chef kitchen, gym, work/library spaces and family/lifestyle spaces.

2. Sde Dov project page:
   https://sdedov.co.il/project/dimri-yama/

   Public facts found:
   - Project sits in the Sde Dov district near the Tel Aviv coast.
   - Plan categories listed by building:
     - Building A: 2, 3, 4 rooms.
     - Building B: 2, 3, 5 rooms.
     - Building C: 2, 3, 4 rooms.
     - Building D: studio, 2, 3 rooms.
   - Amenities listed include infinity pool, indoor pool, spa, gym, yoga/pilates,
     library/work space, kids room and wine room.
