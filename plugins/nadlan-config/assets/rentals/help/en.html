1. Getting started

Rental management is a digital file for every apartment you rent out in Israel: the building and its apartments, the tenants and the lease, rent and cheques, repairs and documents. Everything in one place, in English or Hebrew, on a computer or a phone.

Good to know from day one: the money never passes through us. Your tenant pays straight into your bank account or your Bit, as today. The system is not connected to your bank and never charges the tenant. It records, reminds and keeps your books in order.

Creating your account

  1. Go to the rental management page on the NadLan site: nad-lan.co.il/my-rentals.
  2. Tap Start managing, free.
  3. Sign in with your account on the site. No account yet? Create one on the sign-in page.
  4. Your account opens empty, with three steps waiting for you.

The first three steps of an empty account

  1. Add a building: street, city and number of floors. Your building appears in 3D.
  2. Mark your apartments: tap the apartment on the building, then fill in the tenant, the rent and the lease dates.
  3. Send the tenant a link: on WhatsApp, with no app and no password. Through it the tenant sees payments, reports repairs and signs the lease.

Underneath there are three buttons: New property starts your first building, Import from Excel brings in many apartments at once (chapter 2), and Look at a sample file first opens a ready-made file to try things out.

Look at a sample file first

Want a feel for it before you enter anything? Tap Look at a sample file first. A landlord's file opens with three buildings and five apartments: a tenant who is late, cheques to deposit, an urgent repair, and a vacant apartment with applicants. At the top you will see Sample data: tap and change anything, nothing is sent and nothing is saved. When you are done, tap Back to my data.

The main screen: Today

This screen opens every time you sign in. Four numbers at the top: Collected this month out of what is expected, the Open balance of all your tenants, Vacant apartments and Open repairs. Below them, Needs your attention (chapter 4), and next to it This month, apartment by apartment, each apartment in its colour: green is paid, amber is not paid yet, red is late, grey is vacant.

The menu: Today, Properties, People, Money and Repairs. On a phone, More also holds Leasing, Documents, Reports, Settings and Guide. This guide lives there, inside rental management, in the language you work in. The top bar has a search for an apartment, tenant or repair, a switch between English and Hebrew, and an Add button for anything new.

First things first: your own details

Before your first tenant, open Settings and fill in:

  • The name tenants see and My WhatsApp. Your number is also needed for your quick link to manage from the phone.
  • How tenants pay you: Account details for transfers, Bit number and Payment link (if any). Whatever you write here appears to the tenant on their personal page and in every reminder.

2. Entering all your properties

One building

  1. Tap New property (on the Properties screen, or through Add).
  2. Fill in Street and number, City, Number of floors and Apartments per floor. You may add Year built and Protection, and tick Elevator, Parking and Solar water heaters on the roof.
  3. Tap Save. The building page opens, with the building drawn in 3D from your details.

The apartments: floors and positions

The building page has two parts that work together: the building in 3D, and next to it the Floors, one row per floor. A coloured apartment is yours. A dashed square is an apartment you have not marked yet: tap it to add a new apartment on the right floor and in the right position. You can also tap an apartment on the building itself.

In the apartment form, fill in Apartment name (for example Apt 7), Floor, Position on the floor, Rooms, Size (m²) and Facing. For the position, 0 is the first apartment to the right of the entrance. For a vacant apartment, add the Asking rent and Available from as well.

Each apartment on the building also carries a mark when it has an open repair, or when its lease ends within 90 days. Tapping a floor's name, in the floors list or on the corner of the building, highlights the floor and opens a card for that floor: the apartments on that floor with tenant, rent and debt, the floor's total rent and open debt, and its open repairs. Every line opens the apartment or the repair. Back to the whole building closes the card.

Further down the building page: Apartments in the building as a table, Building expenses, and the building's Documents, such as the insurance policy or the land registry extract.

The apartment card

Tapping an apartment anywhere in the system opens its card. At the top, the action buttons: WhatsApp reminder, Mark as paid, New repair and Tenant link. Below them, the tabs: Overview, Lease, Payments, Repairs, Documents, Journal and The apartment.

The journal records everything that happened: messages sent, messages from the tenant, a signature, a new document. You can add your own Journal entry: a note, a phone call, a meeting or a message you received.

Import from Excel

Already keep a spreadsheet of your apartments? No need to type it all again.

  1. In Excel or Google Sheets, select the rows together with the header row and copy them.
  2. In the app, tap Import from Excel and paste into Paste the rows here.
  3. Tap Check. A summary appears, such as "Found 2 buildings, 3 apartments and 2 tenants", with a preview of the first rows.
  4. If everything looks right, tap Import.

Column names are recognised automatically, in English or Hebrew. The header only has to be one of these, or start with one of them:

ColumnEnglish headers recognised
Address (required)address, street
City (required)city, town
Apartmentapartment, apt, unit
Floorfloor

The same columns are recognised by their usual Hebrew headers too, so a sheet kept in Hebrew imports as it is. Capital letters do not matter.

What the import does:

  • Every row is an apartment. Rows with the same address and city go into the same building. Its number of floors is the highest floor in the sheet, with four apartments per floor; you can correct this later with Edit on the building.
  • Dates are read as 1.3.2026, 01/03/26 or 2026-03-01: day first, then month. Amounts may include a comma, such as 5,200.
  • A row with a tenant also creates a contact. If it also has a rent and a lease start, an active lease is created. A lease that started before the current month is set to Follow payments from the 1st of this month, so old months never show as debt. Without a lease end, the lease runs for one year. Without a payment day, rent is due on the 1st. The lease is created unlinked, paid by bank transfer, and the tenant gets the language you are working in. You can change all of this later by editing the lease.

Import each sheet once only. Importing the same sheet again creates its buildings and apartments again. Adding apartments later? Paste only the new rows.

An existing lease: Follow payments from

Most landlords join in the middle of a lease. So that no old debts are invented, there is a special field. The Excel import does this by itself. When the lease form gets a start date before the current month, the field Follow payments from appears, already set to the 1st of this month. Rent is recorded from that date on; anything before it is not counted.

If the tenant really does owe something from before, record that debt separately as a Charge (chapter 4).

3. Tenants and leases

A new lease

  1. On a vacant apartment's card, tap New lease (or on the Leasing screen, or through Add).
  2. Choose the Apartment and the Tenant. Tenant not on the list yet? Choose New tenant (fill in below) and enter their name, phone and language.
  3. Under Dates and money: Lease start, Lease end, if there is an option also Option, notice by, Rent, Payment day of the month (1 to 28) and Payment method.
  4. Fill in the linkage and the securities (next sections), and tap Save.

From then on the system records the rent every month by itself, on the payment day, in advance for the month, as is usual in Israel. The first charge is never earlier than the lease start.

The tenant's language matters. Every message to the tenant, their personal page and the lease translation are in the language you chose for them, even when you work in Hebrew.

CPI linkage

Under Linkage, choose Linked to the Consumer Price Index, then:

  • Linkage update: Once a year, Every six months, Every quarter or Every month.
  • Linkage percent: 100 for full linkage, or less if that is what you agreed (say 50).
  • Never below the base amount: if the index falls, the rent stays at the original amount.

The base index is the one of the lease start date. At every update the system calculates the change by the known index, straight from the Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS) calculator, and adjusts the charge by itself. On the apartment card's Lease tab, Check the linkage now shows what the updated rent would be today.

Securities and the legal cap

Under Securities, fill in the Security cheque, Promissory note and Cash deposit, and tick Guarantors if there are any. While you type, a line shows the legal cap for this lease. If the security cheque and the cash deposit together go over it, the line turns into a warning, and the lease also appears on your to-do list.

What the law says: under Israel's Rental and Borrowing Law (the Fair Rental amendment), all securities together are capped at the lower of two amounts: three months' rent, or one third of the rent for the whole term of the lease. For a one-year lease at ₪6,000 a month, the cap is ₪18,000. The system will not record a cash deposit above the cap.

The lease document

On the Lease tab, tap Printable lease. A Residential Lease Agreement (unprotected tenancy) opens, built from your details: the parties, the apartment, the term, the rent, the linkage, the securities, the option and pets. A clause that does not apply to your lease is left out. The lease opens in the tenant's language; the English translation states that the Hebrew text governs.

The template follows the Rental and Borrowing Law. For a complex deal, or special terms, have a lawyer go over it.

Signing by personal link

On the Lease tab, in the Signature card, tap Send for signature. A ready WhatsApp message to the tenant opens, in their language, with a personal link valid for 14 days. The tenant:

  1. opens the link on the phone and taps Read the full lease;
  2. types their full name and signs with a finger in Sign here with your finger or mouse;
  3. ticks I have read the lease and agree to its terms and taps Sign.

The signature is kept with the exact text that was signed (with a digital fingerprint of the text), the date and time, and the link it came through. The apartment card then shows Signed, with the name and the time. A lease that was sent but is not signed yet appears on your to-do list with Resend for signature.

The tenant's personal link

On the apartment card, tap Tenant link. A WhatsApp message in the tenant's language opens, with a personal link. Your tenant installs nothing and needs no password: they open the link from WhatsApp and see one page with everything:

  • the rent, the payment day and the next payment, and how to pay you (from your settings);
  • an I've paid button to report a payment (chapter 4);
  • Report a repair with a photo, and the deadline the law gives for the repair (chapter 5);
  • the lease details, and the documents you chose to share;
  • WhatsApp to you, and Write a message, which goes into the lease's journal.

The link is valid until the end of the lease plus 60 days, the time in which the deposit is returned. No phone number for the tenant? A Your link is ready window opens so you can copy the link or share it on WhatsApp.

To share a document with the tenant (the signed lease, say, or the move-in report), upload it to the apartment's Documents tab and tap Share with the tenant. You can undo it any time with Stop sharing. Every file is stored encrypted, up to 15 MB.

People: tenants, guarantors and professionals

The People screen lists every contact by type: Tenants, Applicants, Guarantors and Professionals. Each contact has a Language, and messages to them are written in it. The ID or passport number is optional and stored encrypted. Next to any tenant who owes money, the amount is shown.

4. Collecting rent

What the system does, and what it does not. It does not move money, is not connected to your bank and never charges a tenant's card. Your tenant pays you directly, by transfer, standing order, Bit or cheques. The system records the charges, reminds, takes the tenant's "I've paid" report, and keeps the books. A payment is recorded only when you record or confirm it.

The monthly charges

Every active lease gets a rent charge each month, on its payment day, in advance for the month. A payment you record closes the oldest debt first, then the next one. So it is always clear which month is open.

Recording a payment received

  1. On the to-do list, next to the apartment, tap Mark as paid. You can also do it from the apartment card, or on the Money screen with Payment received.
  2. The amount is already filled in from what is open. Check the Date and the Payment method, and add a Reference if you like: a cheque number or a transfer reference.
  3. Tap Save. The apartment's colour updates at once.

The apartment's Payments tab also has Payment confirmation: a printable note in which you confirm to the tenant that you received the latest payment. It comes from you as the landlord and is not a tax receipt.

The tenant reports: I've paid

On their personal page the tenant taps I've paid and fills in the amount, date, method and reference. The report does not count as a payment until you confirm it. Your to-do list shows "the tenant reported a payment of... Confirm?". Check that the money arrived, then tap Confirm payment.

Post-dated cheques

Got cheques for the whole year? Record each one as a Payment received with the method Cheque, the date written on it, and the cheque number as the reference. A cheque dated in the future is kept as Pending and does not count until it is deposited.

On the Money screen, the Cheques tab lists every cheque you hold by date. Two days before a cheque's date, a reminder to deposit it appears on your to-do list. After depositing, tap Deposited.

A bounced cheque

Every line in the books has a ⋯ button, Line actions. For a cheque that came back, choose Cheque bounced. The payment no longer counts and the month is open again. You may fill in a Bank fee to charge the tenant, which is recorded as a separate charge.

A mistake? Void the line, never delete it

A line in the books is never deleted. Entered a wrong amount? Under ⋯ choose Void this line and write the Reason. The line stays, marked Cancelled with the reason, and then you record the right one. Your books stay clean for any review and for your accountant.

The to-do list

Needs your attention builds itself from your data, most important first: debt, legal deadlines, repairs. What can appear on it:

  • Rent that is late, or a month not paid yet.
  • A lease ending within 90 days, a lease that has ended, an option window closing within 60 days.
  • A cheque to deposit, and a tenant's payment report waiting for you.
  • Securities above the legal cap, and a lease sent for signature but not signed.
  • A new repair, an urgent repair, and a repair past its legal deadline.
  • A former tenant who still owes, a refund due to a former tenant, and a deposit to return.
  • A vacant apartment, and a new application to rent.
  • In December and January: a reminder to report and pay on the 10% tax route.

Next to each item is the button that solves it: reminder, mark as paid, renewal offer, deposited, confirm. Eight items are shown; Show all opens the rest.

WhatsApp reminders, in the tenant's language

Tap WhatsApp reminder, on the to-do list or the apartment card. A friendly message opens, ready, in the tenant's language: the amount, the month, and how to pay you (Bit, bank account or link, from your settings). You can edit the text. Send on WhatsApp opens your own WhatsApp with the message, and you press send. You can also Copy it.

The message leaves from your phone and is logged in the journal. The system never sends messages by itself or in your name.

Manage from WhatsApp: your quick link

On Today or in Settings, tap Send a link to my WhatsApp (your number must be in Settings first). You receive a personal link valid for 14 days. It opens your to-do list on the phone with no password: mark rent as paid, confirm a tenant's report, send a reminder, move a repair to In progress or Done, and add an expense. Reports, documents and settings stay in your full account.

Extra charges and expenses

  • A Charge is an extra amount the tenant owes under the lease, such as water, electricity, municipal tax (arnona) or a repair they caused. It adds to the tenant's balance. Our own fees are never passed on to the tenant.
  • An Expense is money you paid: municipal tax, building committee, repair, insurance, mortgage, water, electricity, gas, tax, fees. Choose the property, and if you like an apartment (or The whole building). Expenses feed the profit and loss and the annual summary.

The Money screen and reports

On the Money screen, move between months with the arrows and see Expected this month, Collected, Expenses and Net. The tabs: Transactions, Cheques, Expenses, Profit and loss for the last 12 months by property, CPI linkage and Tax.

The Reports screen has four reports:

  • Rent roll: every apartment, tenant, rent, lease dates and balance. Open to print, or Excel file.
  • The current year's summary (for example, 2026 summary): income and expenses per property, for the annual report. Its Excel file downloads every line of the books from January of last year to today.
  • Lease evidence file: every payment, repair and message of one lease, in order.
  • Export all your data: one file with everything you have.

Report totals are based on money actually collected. A deposit received is not income, and a debt written off is not income.

5. Building maintenance and repairs

What the law says: under the Rental and Borrowing Law, a defect that prevents reasonable living must be fixed within 3 days, and any other defect within 30 days. Every repair in the system gets a deadline by this rule, counted from the day it was opened.

A repair request from the tenant, with a photo

On their personal page the tenant taps Report a repair, says in one sentence what happened, and adds details and a photo from the camera. Under Can you live in the apartment as usual? they mark whether it is urgent, or leave Not sure. The window also carries an emergency note: a gas smell, sparks or a structural crack means leaving the apartment and calling emergency services. The photo is made smaller before it is sent, so it uploads quickly even on a weak signal.

The repair appears on your Repairs screen under New, and on your to-do list. The tenant sees the repair and the date by which it should be fixed.

A repair you open yourself

On the apartment card, or through Add, tap New repair and write What's wrong, in one sentence. As you type, a line appears such as "Recognised from the description: Water heater · Urgent · up to 3 days to fix". In Urgency you can leave Recognise from the description, or choose Urgent or Standard yourself.

How the recognition works: by a fixed list of words, not by guessing. Words such as "no water", "no power", "gas smell", "flood", "sparks", "exposed wire" or "break-in" mark a repair as urgent, in English or Hebrew. Other words set the type: plumbing, electrical, air conditioning, water heater, locks and more. Your own choice always wins.

The repair card and the professional

Tapping a repair opens its card. At the top, the stages: New, Visit booked, In progress, Waiting, Done. From the repair's type the system suggests which professional fits, with a link to Professionals nearby.

  1. Choose a professional: pick from your list, or add one with New professional... at the end of the list. If you fill in the Visit time, the repair moves to Visit booked.
  2. Send to the professional: a WhatsApp message to them opens, with a personal link to the repair, valid for 30 days.
  3. Update the tenant: a ready message to the tenant, in their language, with the current stage.
  4. Add a photo and Journal entry for the record.

What the professional sees

The professional opens the link with no password and sees this one repair only: the address, urgency, type and deadline. From there they can message you on WhatsApp and send an Update the status: visit booked, in progress, waiting or done, with details, a cost and a photo. The update goes into the repair and its journal.

The repairs board, and a cost that becomes an expense

The Repairs screen shows every repair by stage, each with its deadline; a repair past its deadline is marked in red. On the repair card, Cost keeps what it cost. When you move a repair with a cost to Done, the system asks whether to record the cost as an expense, so it reaches your profit and loss.

The apartment's appliances and systems

On the apartment card's The apartment tab: Apartment details, a drawing of the apartment Inside the apartment with points for its appliances, and Systems and appliances. Tap Appliance or system to add a water heater, A/C, electric panel, meter, fridge, oven, washing machine and more, with brand, model, installation month, Warranty until and Last service. Each appliance shows whether it is Under warranty or the Warranty ended. Report a fault opens a new repair named after the appliance. While that repair is open, the appliance is marked on the drawing, and tapping it opens the repair.

6. The 10-year cycle

A rented apartment goes through tenants, renewals, index updates and tax years. This chapter walks you through all of it.

The deadlines of every lease

On the Lease tab, the Deadlines card lists the dates that matter, in order:

  • The last day for the landlord's notice: 90 days before the lease ends.
  • The last day for the tenant's notice: 60 days before the end.
  • The lease end, and the end of the option window if there is one.
  • Return of the securities: 60 days after the end.
  • The next linkage update.

Under the Rental and Borrowing Law, the landlord gives notice 90 days before the end and the tenant 60 days. Securities are returned within 60 days of the apartment being returned.

Renewal

Ninety days before the end, the lease appears on your to-do list with a Renewal offer: a ready WhatsApp message to the tenant. Once you agree, tap Renew the lease on the Lease tab and fill in Lease start (already set to the day after the current end), Lease end and Rent.

What happens: the current lease closes the day before the new term. Tenants, guarantors and securities move to the new lease. A cash deposit moves with the tenant, in two visible lines: returned on the old lease, received on the new one. With CPI linkage, the new base index is the one of the new start date. Send the new lease for signature again.

End of the lease

On the Lease tab, tap End the lease and fill in:

  • Date the apartment is returned: the day you got the keys back.
  • Reason: End of the term, Early exit, By agreement or Eviction.
  • Charge the last month by days: ticked by default.

Rent stops on the day the apartment is returned. Charges for months after that date are voided, and the last month is counted by days. For example, in a lease that starts on the 1st, a tenant who leaves on the 10th of a 30-day month pays a third of that month. Untick the box and the last month stays whole. If the tenant stays on after the lease end, the extra months are charged at the same rent. Any debt left stays in the books and on the to-do list until it is closed. And from that same day, the 60-day count to return the deposit starts.

The deposit: received, used, returned

The apartment's Payments tab has a Deposit card: Held now, the Legal cap, and after the end also Return by. Three buttons:

  • Deposit received: records a cash deposit you received. A deposit is not income and does not count as rent. An amount that, with the security cheque, goes over the legal cap is not recorded.
  • Use part of the deposit: part of the deposit pays a debt; choose what it was Deducted for: rent, repair, water, electricity, gas, municipal tax, building committee or other. It is recorded as a payment of the debt.
  • Return the deposit: recorded with the date and the way you returned it.

From the day the apartment is returned, the return is on your to-do list: "return a deposit of... by...". In its last two weeks it moves up the list, and once the date has passed it reads "the legal deadline to return the deposit has passed".

Only unpaid rent, damage or bills may be deducted from the deposit. The rest goes back within 60 days of the apartment being returned.

A former tenant: debt, write-off and refund

A debt does not disappear when the lease ends. On the apartment card's Overview tab, Former tenants, open matters shows the debt, the credit or the deposit still held, with a Payments button that opens the old lease's books. The debt is also on your to-do list: "a former tenant still owes...".

How a former tenant's debt is closed:

  • Use part of the deposit, if a deposit is held.
  • The tenant pays and reports I've paid from their link (it stays alive until 60 days after the lease end), and you confirm.
  • Write off the debt, if you decide to let it go. The write-off closes the debt in the books and is not counted as income.

The other way round: if the tenant paid more than was charged, a Tenant's credit appears, and the to-do list says "a former tenant is owed a refund". Refund the tenant records the refund, up to the credit.

Eviction

When a lease ends in eviction, choose Eviction as the reason when you end the lease. The legal process itself goes through a lawyer and the court, not through the system. What the system gives you is the Lease evidence file on the Reports screen: every payment, repair and message of the lease, in order. It is a summary of what was recorded, not a legal document, but it saves your lawyer a lot of work.

A new tenant

An apartment has become vacant? On its card, or on the Leasing screen, tap Application link and send it to people who are interested. The link is valid for 30 days. The applicant sees the apartment's details, fills in name, phone, who will live there and the move-in date, reads and accepts the privacy notice, and can upload payslips, a bank statement or an ID. Every file is stored encrypted.

The application appears on your to-do list, and on the Leasing screen under Applicants by stage: New, Contacted, Viewing, Applied, Approved, Signed. On the applicant's card, in the Applicant file, move them between stages, read How to check a tenant, and when you decide, tap Lease with and their name. The applicant becomes your tenant.

List the apartment takes you to the site's form for publishing a listing. Want help finding a tenant? A licensed broker from the site's brokers list does it under a signed brokerage order.

CPI updates over the years

With a linked lease there is nothing to remember: at every update the monthly charge is already calculated by the index. And if the Central Bureau of Statistics site is down on the day a charge is recorded? The charge is recorded at the base amount and marked as waiting for the index. Your to-do list says "charges are waiting for the CBS index. The difference will be recorded automatically when it arrives". When the figure arrives, the exact difference is recorded by itself as a separate charge, with the same date.

On the Money screen, the CPI linkage tab has a calculator: amount, from date, to date, and Calculate. Below it, CPI-linked leases, each with its next update date and Check the linkage now. The wording of the lease governs.

The yearly tax report

At the end of a year:

  1. On the Reports screen, open the year's summary: income per property, expenses per property and type. Print it, or download the Excel file for your accountant. The printed summary is for the current year; for last year's report, give your accountant the Excel file, which holds every line from January of last year.
  2. On the Money screen, the Tax tab estimates the two residential routes: the exemption up to the ceiling, and the 10% route. Enter the total monthly rent from all your apartments, including any not managed here.
  3. In December and January, your to-do list reminds you that on the 10% route the report and payment are due by 30 January.

The tax figure is an estimate only. Before you report, check with the Israel Tax Authority or an accountant.

Export everything

Your data, always. At any time, on the Reports screen or in Settings, tap Download all data and get one file with everything: properties, apartments, people, leases, every line of the books since the start, repairs, the journal and the list of documents. The files themselves are downloaded from each document. For work in Excel, every report has an Excel file.

Privacy and security

  • The personal details of tenants, applicants, guarantors and professionals (name, phone, email, ID number) are stored encrypted. Signatures and files are encrypted too. The encryption key is kept apart from the data itself.
  • Only you see the information. A tenant sees only their own lease, their payments and the documents you shared with them.
  • You are responsible for your tenants' information, and NadLan keeps it for you. An applicant accepts a privacy notice before sending an application.
  • Every action taken through a link is written to the journal.
  • A contact or document you delete is kept for 30 more days, then deleted for good.
  • Lines in the books are never deleted: they are voided with a reason.
  • Delete all data, in Settings under Privacy, deletes every property, tenant, payment and document. To confirm, type the word ERASE. It is immediate and final, so download all your data first.

Plans

In Settings, under My plan, you see your plan: Free, Pro or Business. The free plan suits a small number of apartments; Pro and Business are for larger portfolios and management companies. Prices and terms will appear here once they are published.

  • A plan is paid securely through Morning: card, Bit, Google Pay or Apple Pay. The invoice is emailed to you.
  • Three days before a plan ends, you get an email with a link to renew. After the end you have a week to renew, and nothing is deleted.
  • Cancel any time. Even above the free limit, no apartment is ever deleted: your apartments stay, you just cannot add new ones.

7. Questions landlords ask

Does the rent go through you?

No. Your tenant pays you directly, by transfer, standing order, Bit or cheques. The system records, reminds and reconciles. The tenant can report "I've paid" from their link, and you confirm it.

Can my tenant use it in English while I work in Hebrew, or the other way round?

Yes. Every tenant has a language. Their personal page, the lease translation and every WhatsApp message to them are in their language, while you work in yours. In the lease, the Hebrew text governs.

Is a lease signed through the link valid?

A lease may be signed electronically. The signature is kept with the exact text signed, its date and its time. For a complex deal, have a lawyer go over the lease.

My tenant gave me 12 cheques. How do I record them?

Each cheque as a Payment received with the method Cheque and the date written on it. The cheques wait on the Cheques tab, and you are reminded to deposit each one. A cheque that comes back is marked with Cheque bounced, with the bank fee.

How is the linkage calculated, and what if the index falls?

By the known index, from the Central Bureau of Statistics calculator, from the lease start to the update date, at the linkage percent you set. If you ticked Never below the base amount, a fall in the index never lowers the rent.

How much security may I ask for?

By law, all securities together are capped at the lower of three months' rent or one third of the rent for the whole term. The lease form shows the exact cap for your lease, and warns you if you go over it.

I entered a lease that has run for a year, and an old debt appeared. What now?

Edit the lease and check Follow payments from. Charges already recorded before that date can be voided with ⋯ and Void this line, with a reason. With the New lease form and with the Excel import, the field fills itself with the 1st of the current month.

My tenant leaves in the middle of the month. How much do they pay?

In End the lease, enter the date the apartment is returned and keep Charge the last month by days ticked. The last month is counted by the days the tenant was in the apartment, and later charges are voided.

What about tax on the rent?

The Tax tab estimates the exemption and the 10% route, and the Reports screen has the yearly summary and an Excel file for your accountant. It is an estimate only; the report itself goes to the Israel Tax Authority, or through your accountant.

Who sees my data, and what happens if I stop using the system?

Only you. Personal details and files are stored encrypted. At any time you can download all your data in one file, or delete everything. If a paid plan ends, nothing is deleted.
