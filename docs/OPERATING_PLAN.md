# nad-lan.co.il Operating Plan

Last updated: 2026-05-26

## Mission
Build a Hebrew real-estate money site. This is one of the highest-priority domains because real-estate leads can monetize through mortgages, brokers, lawyers, investment projects, calculators, and paid referrals.

## Niche Hypothesis
Commercial real-estate SEO should focus on buyer/seller/investor intent, not generic news. The strongest opportunities are purchase tax, mortgage, apartment buying, investment areas, new projects, legal checks, and lead capture for professionals.

## Front Page Direction
Hebrew RTL real-estate decision hub:
- calculators and tools above the fold
- buy/sell/invest navigation
- lead forms for mortgage, lawyer, broker, appraisal
- city/project guides
- trust and checklist downloads

## Theme Decision
Use custom code-first WordPress theme synced through UPress/GitHub. Elementor is not ideal for core money pages because calculators, schema, performance, and CRM hooks need controlled code.

## CRM/Funnel
Install CRM like the Jus-Tice pattern:
- capture buyer/seller/investor leads
- classify by city, budget, property type, timeline, mortgage need, legal need
- automated qualification before partner routing
- Grow/Green Invoice can be used later for paid reports, consulting, or partner billing
