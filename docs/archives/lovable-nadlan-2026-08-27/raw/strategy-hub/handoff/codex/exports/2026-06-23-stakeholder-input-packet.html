Stakeholder Input Packet

A practical call sheet for collecting the facts needed from the relevant decision-maker, advisor, contractor contact, or technical partner before NadLan turns the showroom into a contractor-facing product. This does not pause Lovable or Codex work; it protects the implementation from wrong assumptions.

Goal of the call: decide which project is the first serious demo, what real assets exist, how leads and monetization work, and whether the implementation stays inside the current WordPress plugin path.

Asset Questions

  1. Which project should be the first contractor demo?
  2. Do we have a real GLB/3D model, or only facade/photo/plans?
  3. Do we have official facade renders?
  4. Do we have floor plans per floor and per unit?
  5. Do we have coordinates for the project and building entrance?
  6. Do we have unit-level price, floor, sqm, rooms, direction, availability, parking, storage, and delivery?
  7. Do we have Matterport, Cupix, drone, panorama, interior render, or apartment-tour URLs?
  8. Are there restrictions on showing prices, availability, view, or renderings?

Product Questions

  1. What is the minimum impressive flow for a contractor demo?
  2. Should the first demo emphasize 3D, view, map, interior, AI, or lead conversion?
  3. What is the best mobile order?
  4. Should AI be public immediately or start as concierge/lead assistant?
  5. What comparison features matter most?
  6. What should happen when assets are missing?
  7. What proof will make a developer pay?

Business Questions

  1. Who pays first: contractor, buyer, professional, or partner broker?
  2. What are the contractor packages?
  3. Referral fee, advertising fee, success fee, subscription, or hybrid?
  4. Who owns the lead?
  5. What lead fields are mandatory?
  6. What is the response SLA?
  7. WhatsApp first, form first, concierge first, or combined?
  8. Which professional verticals should be monetized first?

SEO And Market Questions

  1. Which categories must NadLan win first?
  2. Which topics are legally or commercially sensitive?
  3. Which Israeli competitors are direct benchmarks?
  4. Which commercial pages must exist even if hard to rank?
  5. Which outbound markets matter now: Cyprus, Dubai/UAE, Greece, Thailand, or others?
  6. What languages matter first?
  7. What approval process is required before AI-assisted content is published?

Technical WordPress Questions

  1. Should the showroom stay in the existing nadlan-config plugin or become a separate module?
  2. What custom post types are final: projects, units, compounds, professionals, guides, cities, neighborhoods, destination countries?
  3. Which data belongs in WordPress meta, JSON assets, or external services?
  4. Who maintains Mapbox tokens, geocoding, coordinates, and map style?
  5. Which multilingual path should be used?
  6. Is there an existing CRM, email, WhatsApp, or lead destination?
  7. Should leads be stored in WordPress, sent externally, or both?
  8. What privacy/security constraints apply to leads?
  9. What UPress caching/CDN constraints matter?
  10. What mobile performance target is acceptable for 3D?
