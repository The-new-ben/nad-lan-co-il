# Product marketing context: NadLan (nad-lan.co.il)

This is the file the `social` and `marketing-plan` skills read first (`.agents/product-marketing.md`).
Hard rules live in ../RULES.md and override anything here.

## What NadLan is
- An Israeli real-estate portal (WordPress), Hebrew first, with sections in other languages (English, French and Russian pages exist for this listing).
- It lists new projects (mostly Tel Aviv and the coast), listings from licensed brokers, tools (property value estimator,
  urban renewal map, mortgage and purchase-tax calculators), area guides and a professionals directory (brokers, lawyers,
  appraisers, mortgage advisers).
- Owner: Ben. NadLan is a portal, never a broker. Listings belong to the licensed broker named on them.
- Every page carries a wide "ייעוץ חינם" WhatsApp bar.

## The first product in this campaign
Meital Katzir's exclusive listing: a brand-new mini-penthouse in Kochav HaTzafon, north Tel Aviv. Facts: FACTS-kochav.md.
Price NIS 18,000,000. Goal: real buyers write to Meital on WhatsApp and book a private viewing.

## Audiences
1. Israeli families and upgraders in north Tel Aviv, Ramat Aviv and the Sharon who want space without a house: one level,
   big terraces, parking, a safe room.
2. English-speaking buyers: olim and residents from the US, UK, South Africa and Australia, plus diaspora buyers who want a
   Tel Aviv base. They need the Israeli room count explained and the price in shekels.
3. French-speaking buyers: French Jews and olim, a large luxury segment in Tel Aviv and Netanya.
4. Russian-speaking buyers in Israel and abroad.
5. For the NadLan page over the year: developers and brokers who might list with NadLan (they watch what reach we create).

## Positioning
- The view, the terraces and one-level family living in a new boutique building next to Yarkon Park.
- Proof over adjectives: every photo was taken in the home, the plan is labelled as a rendering, the licence is on every post.
- NadLan's voice: the language of a good Israeli developer site. Location, life, light, family, calm. Never technology.

## Voice per language
- Hebrew: warm, confident, short sentences after one longer opening line. Second person plural in calls to action ("לתיאום צפייה פרטית").
- English: plain, specific, no hype. Explain Israeli conventions (room count, safe room) briefly.
- French: elegant, precise, typographically correct (non-breaking space before : ? ! ; and before units).
- Russian: direct and factual, numbers first, no exclamation marks.

## Proof points we can use
- Real media from the home (assets/ and raw-media/).
- The licensed broker's name and licence number.
- The listing pages in four languages with the full film and the floor plan.

## What we do not have (do not pretend)
- No followers yet on the NadLan page (0 on 30.10.2026).
- No testimonials, no sales record, no press. Do not invent any.
- No paid budget. Organic only.
