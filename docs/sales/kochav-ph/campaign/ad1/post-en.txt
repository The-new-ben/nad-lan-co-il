Mini penthouse above Yarkon Park, north Tel Aviv · 74 m² sun terrace

Floor 11 of 12 in a new boutique building in Kochav HaTzafon, one of north Tel Aviv's quietest, greenest neighbourhoods. About 197 m², 5 rooms, bespoke joinery, a designer kitchen with integrated appliances, VRF climate, a safe room, 2 parking spaces and storage.

The real living room is outside: a 74 m² sun terrace, open on three sides, with the Tel Aviv skyline on one side and the sea on the other. Made for long evenings, a grill and friends.

And the neighbourhood is about to change. A new walking and cycling bridge into Yarkon Park opens in 2027 (Tel Aviv-Yafo Municipality), and the park becomes your front garden: running paths along the river, cycling, tennis, the sports centre. Homes in Kochav HaTzafon average NIS 49–51K per m²; new homes nearby reach NIS 90K and more.
