## 3. Specs that matter for an organic post

- **Feed image: 4:5 at 1440×1800 px.** Minimum 600×750 px, with 3% ratio tolerance. Meta also recommends primary text of 50–150 characters and a headline of 27 characters. This is Meta's Ads Guide, Facebook Feed image [V 29.10.2026] https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/facebook-feed . The page is an ads spec; I am assuming the same 4:5 frame is the safe size for organic feed photos.
- **Reels: 9:16 at 1440×2560 px. Keep text, logos and key visuals out of the top 14%, the bottom 35% and 6% on each side.** Length 0 seconds to 15 minutes. Meta's Ads Guide, Instagram Reels [V 29.10.2026] https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels . Meta publishes these margins for ads only, not for organic Reels [NV 29.10.2026] https://www.inro.social/tools/instagram-reels-safe-zone-checker .
- **All new Facebook videos are shared as Reels, with no length or format limits** (Meta announcement, June 2025; article dated 24.6.2025) [V 29.10.2026] https://www.theweek.in/news/sci-tech/2025/06/18/meta-says-all-new-facebook-videos-to-be-categorised-as-reels.html .
- **Text before "See more":** Meta publishes no figure. Third-party tools give about 125 characters on mobile, about 477 on desktop, or "3–4 lines" [NV 29.10.2026] https://lettercounter.org/blog/facebook-character-limit-guide/ , https://www.hootsuite.com/social-media-tools/social-media-caption-preview . Working rule: the first line, about 125 characters, must carry the whole offer.
- **X:** 280 characters. Every URL counts as 23 characters (t.co). Each emoji counts as 2 [V 29.10.2026] https://docs.x.com/fundamentals/counting-characters . A post can hold up to 4 photos or 1 video; video runs 0.5–140 seconds [NV 29.10.2026] https://help.x.com/en/using-x/posting-gifs-and-pictures .

## 4. Organic reach evidence, 2025–2026

**4.1 Meta's own data: posts with links barely get seen.**
- Q2 2026, US Feed: **98.9%** of content views were on posts with no link outside Facebook; **1.1%** had a link.
- Where views came from: friends 20.2%; **groups 14.6%**; Pages followed 14.8%; unconnected recommended posts **36.6%**; other 13.8%.
- Source: Meta Widely Viewed Content Report [V 29.10.2026] https://transparency.meta.com/data/widely-viewed-content-report/

**4.2 Reach and engagement by format: link posts are last in every dataset.**
- **Socialinsider** [V 29.10.2026] https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/facebook
  - Sample: 24,958,754 organic posts from brands with 1K–1M followers. The stated period is Jan 2025–Sep 2026, while the page itself is dated 23.3.2026. That is inconsistent on the page.
  - Average engagement rate: 0.15%.
  - 2025 by type: status 0.20%, album 0.18%, Reel 0.18%, image 0.15%, **link 0.05%**.
  - Q3 2026: Reels top and flat; album 0.10%; image 0.08%; status 0.10%; link 0.05%.
  - Average views per post, Pages with 100K–1M followers: **album 1,150, image 1,125**, status 820, Reel 620, **link 200**.
  - Brands cut link posts from 19 to 10 a month.
- **Buffer** (19.3.2026, "45M+ posts") [V 29.10.2026] https://buffer.com/resources/data-best-content-format-social-media/
  - Facebook engagement rate: images 5.2%, video 4.84%, text 4.76%, **links 4.43%** (lowest).
- **Metricool 2026 study** (release dated 10.12.2025; 1,059,949 accounts; 39,762,999 posts; 2025 data) [V 29.10.2026] https://metricool.com/press-release-2026-social-media-study/
  - Facebook reach +51%, impressions +57%, interactions +56%.
  - The release has no breakdown by format.
- Takeaway: the datasets disagree on Reels against photos, but they agree that a link post is the weakest format. A native photo album plus a Reel is the evidence-backed pair.

**4.3 Link in the post body or in the first comment**
- **Meta's December 2025 test:** Pages and professional-mode profiles without Meta Verified are limited to two links. Links in comments, affiliate links and links to Meta's own apps are exempt. TechCrunch, 17.12.2025 [V 29.10.2026] https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/17/facebook-is-testing-a-link-posting-limit-for-professional-accounts-and-pages . Other outlets say the limit is two link posts per month [NV 29.10.2026] https://pressgazette.co.uk/news/facebook-tests-charging-creators-and-businesses-to-post-more-than-two-links-per-month/ .
- **Professional Dashboard tip:** the Page insight recommends putting the link in the first comment. These are users' reports from June 2025, not an official Meta statement [V 29.10.2026] https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/facebook-advice-suggests-adding-links-in-comments-not-post-caption/750754/ .
- **One controlled pair, early 2026:** with the link in the first comment, reach was 3,410 against 3,140 (about +9%), but link clicks were 38 against 61 (about −38%). The author repeated a rougher version three times and says the pattern usually, but not always, holds [V 29.10.2026] https://www.lilachbullock.com/why-facebook-pages-post-links-in-comments/ . The evidence is weak.
- For this post, the aim is WhatsApp and DM leads rather than site clicks, so keep the link out of the Facebook post body.

**4.4 Hashtags and caption length**
- **Meta, April 2025:** reach is reduced for accounts that pair long captions with excessive hashtags, and for captions unrelated to the image or video. TechCrunch, 24.4.2025 [V 29.10.2026] https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/24/facebook-cracks-down-on-spammy-content-by-cutting-reach-and-monetization .
- **Instagram, December 2025:** at most 5 hashtags per post or Reel [NV 29.10.2026] https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-implements-new-limits-on-hashtag-use/808309/ .
- Gap: no credible 2025–2026 study of the best organic Facebook post length was found. The only number is Meta's ad guidance of 50–150 characters for primary text (section 3).

**4.5 Repetition and originality, which matters for a bot**
- **Meta, July 2025:** accounts that repeatedly reuse content get reduced distribution, and duplicate videos have the copies demoted. Social Media Today, 14.7.2025 [V 29.10.2026] https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/facebook-cracking-down-unoriginal-content-ai-replicas/753001/ .
- **Meta Spam policy:** posting or sharing at a very high frequency, whether by hand or by automation, is spam. Repetitive content is a listed signal [V 29.10.2026] https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/spam/ .

**4.6 Facebook groups: etiquette and automation**
- **Meta Graph API v19.0 changelog:** the Groups API, `publish_to_groups` and the ability for group admins to install apps were scheduled for removal on 22.4.2024 [V 29.10.2026] https://developers.facebook.com/docs/graph-api/changelog/version19.0/ . Scheduling vendors confirm that third-party apps can no longer post to groups [NV 29.10.2026] https://www.ayrshare.com/facebook-removes-groups-api-access-impact-and-implications/ , https://support.agorapulse.com/en/articles/9167708-facebook-groups-update-april-2024 .
  - **Consequence: a bot cannot post to groups through Meta's official API.** Group posts have to be made by a person, by hand.
  - Pages were not part of that deprecation list. I did not verify the current Pages publishing API.
- **Group rules:** admins can set up to 10 rules. Local groups often ban listing posts from agents [NV 29.10.2026] https://groupboss.io/blog/facebook-group-rules . Read each group's rules before posting, and give each group its own variant of the text.
- Groups produce 14.6% of US Feed views (4.1).

**4.7 X: length and format**
- **Buffer, March 2026,** median engagement rate on X: **text 3.56%**, images 3.40%, video 2.96%, **links 2.25%** [V 29.10.2026] https://buffer.com/resources/data-best-content-format-social-media/ .

In October 2025 he said link posts get lower engagement because people leave the app [V 29.10.2026] https://roboin.io/article/en/2026/04/13/x-denies-link-deboost-recommends-in-post-links/ .

## 5. Israeli rules for broker ads

**5.1 Broker ethics regulations: name, broker status and licence number in every ad**
- **Name and date in force:** the Registrar of Real Estate Brokers' letter dated 9.3.2025 announces that the regulations are coming into force and names them "תקנות המתווכים במקרקעין (אתיקה מקצועית), התשפ״ד-2024" [V 29.10.2026] https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/news/ethicsregulations/he/Broker_edthicsregulations.pdf . I extracted the text locally; the PDF is a cover letter, not the regulation text.
  - Other sources call them "(Ethics and Professional Duties) 5784-2024". That is a naming discrepancy to check.
  - In force from 9.3.2025: Barnea law firm, 13.3.2025 [V 29.10.2026] https://barlaw.co.il/practice_areas/litigation/real-estate-litigation/client_updates/new-real-estate-brokers-regulations-in-israel-require-professionalism-transparency-fairness-and-accountability/
- **Advertising rule (regulation 19):** every marketing publication must state the broker's name, that the person is a broker, and the licence number. In a newspaper, stating broker status is enough. Realta guide, updated September 2026 [V 29.10.2026] https://realta.co.il/en/guides/real-estate-agents/
- **Ynet, same rule:** in every ad for sale or rent the broker must be identified by personal name, not only the business name, and the ad must say he is a broker and give the licence number. The page showed no date [V 29.10.2026] https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/bkkpzcliyl
- **Barnea law firm adds:**
  - No ad without the owner's consent.
  - No showing a property known not to be for sale.
  - The ad must be updated when the property is no longer for sale. [V 29.10.2026] (link above)
- **On the word "תיווך":** the duty is to state broker status (מתווך/מתווכת). No source found requires the word "תיווך" itself. Gap.
- **Gap:** I did not read the official text of regulation 19 itself (gov.il page 403; the PDF is the cover letter). Check it in Nevo or Reshumot before the final post.

**5.2 Consumer Protection Law: price in shekels**
- **Section 17ד:** a business may not advertise or quote a price unless it is the total price and in Israeli currency only.
