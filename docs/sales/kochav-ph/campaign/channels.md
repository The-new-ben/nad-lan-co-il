## Rules that shape every ad
- Meta: special ad category "housing" for audiences in the US, Canada, Europe (no age/gender/lookalikes; no religion targeting since 19.1.2022). Google: housing policy in US/Canada; religion is a sensitive interest everywhere.
- US Fair Housing Act 42 USC 3604(c): no religious preference in ad wording.
- Israel broker ethics regulations (in force 3.2025): the broker's name and licence number on every ad. nad-lan is the platform, never the broker.
- Privacy: Israeli Amendment 13 notice duty; GDPR / UK GDPR when targeting or tracking EU/UK people; CASL consent (Canada); CAN-SPAM (US).
- Location stated plainly on every ad (UK ASA ruling against a JC ad that blurred a location; protests at London and Paris property fairs).
