   **Failure:** After pressing `+` or double-tapping, vertical swipes starting on the stage cannot scroll the page—even when the image reaches its pan boundary. Changing back to `pan-y` does not release an already active gesture.  
   **Minimal fix:** Keep `pan-y` for the inline viewer and reserve one-finger vertical gestures for page scrolling. Use two-finger movement or explicit controls for vertical plan panning. [Gesture rules](https://www.w3.org/TR/pointerevents3/#determining-supported-direct-manipulation-behavior).

2. **Pinch-to-one-finger panning stops responding.**  
   **Snippet:** Second `pointerdown` sets `down=null`; `end()` only does `if(P.size<2)pinch=null`.  
   **Failure:** Pinch to zoom, lift one finger, then drag the remaining finger. Neither branch handles movement because both `pinch` and `down` are null. With the current `touch-action:none`, page scrolling is also unavailable.  
   **Minimal fix:** If retaining one-finger plan panning, initialize `down` from the remaining pointer’s current coordinates, with `moved:true` to prevent an accidental tap. Otherwise explicitly end the interaction; native scrolling requires a fresh gesture.

3. **The image does not follow the fingers during a moving pinch.**  
   **Snippet:** `tx=pinch.mx-(pinch.mx-tx)*(ns/s)` and the equivalent `ty` expression.  
   **Failure:** Move both fingers together while pinching. Scaling stays anchored to the original midpoint; translation of the fingers is ignored. At constant separation, two-finger panning does nothing.  
   **Minimal fix:** Save starting `tx/ty` alongside the starting scale and midpoint. Recompute the current midpoint each move:
   `tx=mxNow-(pinch.mx-pinch.tx)*(ns/pinch.s)`; likewise for `ty`.

4. **Assistive-technology activation of rooms has no click handler.**  
   **Snippet:** Room selection is handled through `pointerup` and `.room` `keydown`, but never `click`.  
   **Failure:** VoiceOver/TalkBack activation that delivers a synthesized click without the tracked pointer sequence or keyboard event cannot activate a room. The separate chip buttons still work.  
   **Minimal fix:** Add a delegated room `click` activation path, suppressing duplicate activation after handled physical taps and drag-generated clicks. [Input-accessibility guidance](https://www.w3.org/TR/pointerevents3/#intro).

5. **Releasing an uncaptured mouse outside the stage leaves a ghost drag.**  
   **Snippet:** Capture occurs only inside `if(s>1.001&&down.moved)`; release listeners exist only on `stage`.  
   **Failure:** On a zoomed plan, press near its edge, leave before crossing the 8px threshold, and release outside. `P` and `down` remain populated. Moving the mouse back can pan the image with no button pressed.  
   **Minimal fix:** Capture accepted pointers on `pointerdown`; clean up on capture loss/window blur. Reject mouse movement with `e.buttons===0` when a drag is supposedly active.

6. **A third finger can cause a sudden zoom jump.**  
   **Snippet:** Pinch initialization requires `P.size===2`, while `end()` retains `pinch` whenever two pointers remain.  
   **Failure:** During a pinch, add a third finger and lift either original finger. The remaining pair uses the original pair’s distance and scale baseline, causing an abrupt scale change on its next movement.  
   **Minimal fix:** Track the pinch pair’s IDs and rebuild its baseline whenever that pair changes. Suspend pinch calculations while more than two pointers are active.

7. **Dragging or cancellation does not invalidate double-tap history.**  
   **Snippet:** `if(e.type!=="pointerup"||!wasTap){...return}` leaves `lastTap` unchanged.  
   **Failure:** Tap, make a short drag, then tap near the original position within 320ms. The final tap unexpectedly zooms or resets instead of selecting.  
   **Minimal fix:** Clear `lastTap` when movement crosses the threshold, another pointer joins, or a gesture is cancelled.

8. **Selected-room tags are clipped on narrow phones.**  
   **Snippet:** `.np-tag{transform:translate(-50%,-120%);white-space:nowrap}` inside the transformed canvas; stage uses `overflow:hidden`.  
   **Failure:** In rendered mode, selecting `service` places its center at `0.0683 × width`—about 23px on a 340px stage. Half the Hebrew label extends past the left edge and disappears. Zooming magnifies the clipping.  
   **Minimal fix:** Make the tag an unscaled stage overlay, convert its anchor through `tx/ty/s`, and clamp its measured bounds inside the stage.
