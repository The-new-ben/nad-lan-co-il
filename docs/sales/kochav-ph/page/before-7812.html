The residence

Floor 11, views both ways, and the scale of a house

A new mini penthouse on floor 11 of 12, in a boutique building in Kochav HaTzafon, the neighborhood between Yarkon Park and Ramat Aviv. Five rooms on about 190 sqm built, with terraces totaling 76 to 80 sqm and open views across Tel Aviv. The price is NIS 18,000,000.

The plan includes a generous master suite, a children's wing with a sea-view balcony, a large living room and an equipped kitchen with a concealed integrated refrigerator. Custom joinery runs through the home, and the air conditioning is a VRF multi-split system. Two parking spaces, a storage room, a safe room and a lift come with the apartment.

The building has a residents' gym. At the edge of the neighborhood, a pedestrian and cycle bridge of about 170 m from Levi Eshkol Street into Yarkon Park has been under construction since summer 2026 and is expected to open in 2027.

The location

Kochav HaTzafon, the park to the south and Sde Dov to the north

Kochav HaTzafon is bounded by Rokach Boulevard and Yarkon Park to the south, Namir Road to the east, Shai Agnon Street and the Sde Dov district to the north, and Ibn Gabirol to the west. It was built mostly in the 1990s and 2000s as 8 to 12-floor blocks around a central park, alongside residential towers and boutique buildings. Data published by Mako put its median deal from 2023 to October 2025 at NIS 6.1 million, the fourth highest in Israel.

Yarkon Park, about 3,750 dunams including the Sportek, borders the neighborhood along Rokach Boulevard, and Tel Aviv Port is a 220-dunam leisure complex at the park's end. At Rokach and Namir, the Seminar HaKibbutzim project plans three 28-floor residential towers with 450 apartments.

The Green Line light rail is expected to open its southern section in 2028 and the full line in 2030, with Reading station on the new bridge north of Rokach Boulevard. A new 180 m pedestrian and cycle bridge over the Yarkon opened in May 2026.

Planning

What is planned nearby

Published plans and works, and what they may mean for a buyer or tenant. Based on published reports; not a substitute for a check with the planning authority.

  • The Rokach and Levi Eshkol bridge into Yarkon Park

    A direct walking and cycling link from Levi Eshkol Street into the park from 2027, with works on Rokach Boulevard until then. The plan was approved in January 2022 with NIS 35 million allocated.

  • Seminar HaKibbutzim project, Rokach Blvd corner Namir Rd

    About 55 dunams: three 28-floor towers with 450 units and three 6-floor buildings along Maurizio Vitale St, up to 1,550 sqm of commerce and 4,600 sqm of public buildings. First phase (134 units and a new college building) about 5 years; financing of about NIS 1.7 billion. Years of works and new high-rise massing at the neighborhood's south-east corner.

  • Sde Dov district towers

    The center plan, whose objections were rejected on 3 February 2025, has 7,128 homes in towers of 20 and 45 floors; most district apartments are expected within 3 to 6 years. Towers to the north may change the skyline and the view toward the sea.

  • Green Line light rail

    Better rail access from the Reading station north of Rokach Blvd once open (southern section 2028, full line 2030); construction around Rokach and the Yarkon until then.

  • Reading power station site

    Generation to end under the 2024 decision, with part of the site going to civilian use and beach and the rest kept for storage and an undersea cable link; a conservation plan converts Reading A into a museum, visitor center and event space. Timing to be confirmed.

Before you decide

The checks worth making

The questions a buyer at this price level asks, and the documents to obtain before negotiating.

  • What is the exact address, block and parcel, and which developer built the building? Can we see the completion certificate (Form 4) and check condominium registration in the Land Registry?
  • What are the built area, terrace areas (open and covered) and exposures in the permit plans, the sale contract appendix and the arnona bill?
  • What is on floor 12 above the unit, and does anyone hold remaining building rights on the roof?
  • What are the monthly management fee (including the gym) and the annual arnona, and in which arnona zone and building code is the property?
  • Are the 2 parking spaces and the storage room registered or attached to the unit, and where are they?
  • Which Sale (Apartments) Law inspection and warranty periods are still open, and have defects been reported to the developer?
  • What can be built facing the children's wing sea-view balcony and the living room views (Sde Dov towers, Seminar HaKibbutzim)? Can the seller provide a planning check?
  • How did the seller's or buyer's appraiser weight the terraces, and is there a recent appraisal?
  • Which elementary and secondary schools serve this address under the municipal registration zones?
  • The unit plan and building permit, including covered and open terrace areas
  • The occupancy certificate (Tofes 4) and condominium registration
  • A current appraisal and how it weights the terraces
  • A planning check of what could be built facing the views

Questions and answers

Questions worth asking

What is the price per sqm?
On 190 sqm built, about NIS 94,737 per sqm. Weighting the 80 sqm of terraces at a third gives about NIS 83,077, and at a half about NIS 78,261. The neighborhood average is NIS 49,000 to 51,000 per sqm, and new projects there reach NIS 90,000 to 95,000.
How much purchase tax would a buyer pay?
Under the 2026 brackets: about NIS 1,153,886 for a single-home buyer (6.41%), about NIS 1,678,899 for an additional home or a foreign resident (9.33%), and about NIS 975,976 for an eligible new immigrant (5.42%).
How do you reach Yarkon Park on foot?
A pedestrian and cycle bridge of about 170 m from Levi Eshkol Street into the park has been under construction since summer 2026 and is expected to open in 2027.
What could change in the view?
The Sde Dov district is rising to the north, with towers of 20 to 45 floors in the center plan, and three 28-floor towers are planned at Rokach and Namir. Some open views in the neighborhood are expected to be blocked.
What is it in dollars?
At the 15 September 2026 representative rate, NIS 18,000,000 is about USD 5.9 million or EUR 5.1 million.
