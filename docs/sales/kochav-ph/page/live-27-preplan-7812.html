Mini penthouse

What makes this a true mini penthouse

In Israel a mini penthouse is a home on one of the top floors with a sun terrace far larger than an ordinary balcony. A penthouse is the home on the top floor itself, usually with a roof terrace. The law has no definition, so every home is worth checking against the same signs.

The location

Kochav HaTzafon, the park to the south and Sde Dov to the north

Kochav HaTzafon is bounded by Rokach Boulevard and Yarkon Park to the south, Namir Road to the east, Shai Agnon Street and the Sde Dov district to the north, and Ibn Gabirol to the west. It was built mostly in the 1990s and 2000s as 8 to 12-floor blocks around a central park, alongside residential towers and boutique buildings. Data published by Mako put its median deal from 2023 to October 2025 at NIS 6.1 million, the fourth highest in Israel.

Yarkon Park, about 3,750 dunams including the Sportek, borders the neighborhood along Rokach Boulevard, with tennis courts and football pitches right by the home. Tel Aviv Port is a 220-dunam leisure complex at the park's end. At Rokach and Namir, the Seminar HaKibbutzim project plans three 28-floor residential towers with 450 apartments.

The Green Line light rail is expected to open its southern section in 2028 and the full line in 2030, with Reading station on the new bridge north of Rokach Boulevard. A new 180 m pedestrian and cycle bridge over the Yarkon opened in May 2026.

Value

The bridge, the park and the neighbourhood: what it does to value

What is known about the bridge, what studies found about parks and walking links and home prices, and what is planned around. Information for a decision, not a forecast.

  • The bridge to Yarkon Park

    About 170 m long and 6 m wide: 3 m for walkers and 3 m for bikes, a bamboo deck and LED lighting in the railings, step-free with no lifts. Works began in June 2026 and opening is expected in 2027 (Maariv, Walla, Bizportal, 3.8.2026). It is part of a planned north-south axis with a second bridge over the Yarkon stream.

  • The Yarkon Park study

    A Technion study (Shefer and Zakai, 2003) found that homes within 100 m of Yarkon Park sold for more than homes 100 to 1,500 m away.

  • Parks and links abroad

    Next to well-known city parks, nearby homes gained in value: about 5% around Boston's Greenway, about 20% around New York's High Line, about 23% around Klyde Warren in Dallas and 30% to 40% around Millennium Park in Chicago (HR&A, 2014). A review of 20 studies found 3% to 5% for homes near walking and cycling trails (Crompton and Nicholls, 2019). The effect is strongest next to the link and fades with distance.

  • Sde Dov, north of the neighbourhood

    About 16,000 homes, about 365 dunams of parks and towers of up to 40 floors under the approved plan.

  • The Green Line light rail

    About 39 km and 62 stations, from Rishon LeZion to Herzliya; reports put the opening in 2028.

  • Prices in the neighbourhood today

    The median deal in Kochav HaTzafon between 2023 and October 2025 was NIS 6.1 million, the fourth highest in Israel (Mako). A new home on a high floor with large terraces sits at the top of that market.

Before you decide

The checks worth making

The questions a buyer at this price level asks, and the documents to obtain before negotiating.

  • What is the exact address, block and parcel, and which developer built the building? Can we see the completion certificate (Form 4) and check condominium registration in the Land Registry?
  • What are the built area, terrace areas (open and covered) and exposures in the permit plans, the sale contract appendix and the arnona bill?
  • What is on floor 12 above the unit, and does anyone hold remaining building rights on the roof?
  • What are the monthly management fee (including the gym) and the annual arnona, and in which arnona zone and building code is the property?
  • Are the 2 parking spaces and the storage room registered or attached to the unit, and where are they?
  • Which Sale (Apartments) Law inspection and warranty periods are still open, and have defects been reported to the developer?
  • What can be built facing the children's wing sea-view balcony and the living room views (Sde Dov towers, Seminar HaKibbutzim)? Can the seller provide a planning check?
  • How did the seller's or buyer's appraiser weight the terraces, and is there a recent appraisal?
  • Which elementary and secondary schools serve this address under the municipal registration zones?

Questions and answers

Questions worth asking

Does the home have value-gain potential?
The new bridge from Levi Eshkol to Yarkon Park (about 170 m, opening expected in 2027), the Sde Dov quarter to the north and the Green Line (opening planned for 2028) are changing the area. The Technion's Yarkon Park study found higher prices for homes within 100 m of the park, and abroad, gains of 3% to 40% were measured next to links and parks, depending on distance. This is information for a decision, not a forecast.
What is the difference between a penthouse and a mini penthouse?
A penthouse is on the top floor of the building, usually with a roof terrace. A mini penthouse is on one of the top floors below it, with a sun terrace far larger than an ordinary balcony, usually 25 sqm or more. Israeli law has no definition; the land registry, the bylaws and the permit decide.
Is this home really a mini penthouse?
Yes, by every usual sign: floor 11 of 12, a sun terrace of about 74 sqm (about three times 25 sqm) and about 80 sqm with the other balconies, 197 sqm built and five rooms.
How much purchase tax would a buyer pay?
Under the 2026 brackets: about NIS 1,153,886 for a single-home buyer (6.41%), about NIS 1,678,899 for an additional home or a foreign resident (9.33%), and about NIS 975,976 for an eligible new immigrant (5.42%).
How do you reach Yarkon Park on foot?
A pedestrian and cycle bridge of about 170 m from Levi Eshkol Street into the park has been under construction since summer 2026 and is expected to open in 2027.
What could change in the view?
The Sde Dov district is rising to the north, with towers of 20 to 45 floors in the center plan, and three 28-floor towers are planned at Rokach and Namir. Some open views in the neighborhood are expected to be blocked.
What is it in dollars?
