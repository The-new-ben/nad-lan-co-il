## Who buys it, and what each one needs to see
1. **The north Tel Aviv upgrader** (Hebrew, 45-60). Trigger: an exit, vested shares, an inheritance, children in middle
   or high school. Wants: one floor, a big outdoor room, a children's wing, a safe room, schools within walking distance,
   two parking spots. Decides on: the balcony in daylight and at night, the plan, the walk to school and the park,
   real deal prices around. Fear: overpaying for a "mini penthouse" label. Answer: the comparison table.
2. **The villa owner coming down to the city** (Hebrew, 55-70, Herzliya Pituach, Savyon, Kfar Shmaryahu). Trigger:
   the children left; a big garden to maintain. Wants: an 80 m² terrace as the garden, lock-and-leave, security,
   elevator, parking, the sea and the park close. Decides on: the terrace's size and privacy, the view, the quiet,
   the building's quality, how much less work it is than the villa. Search: פנטהאוז הרצליה, דירת גן, מגורי יוקרה.
3. **The Jewish family abroad** (France, US, UK; Russian speakers). Trigger: a second home, aliyah planning, security.
   Wants: new and ready, a safe room, near the sea, close to the families' Tel Aviv, a process done from far away.
   Decides on: a full virtual walk room by room, the price in their currency, the total cost of buying (purchase tax,
   lawyer, broker), how a foreigner buys, a video call with the broker. Search in English, French, Russian.
4. **The relocated executive renting today** (Hebrew or English). Trigger: tired of rent at the port or Sarona.
   Wants: commute, the light rail, schools, the park for running, a home office. Decides on: the rent-versus-buy math.
