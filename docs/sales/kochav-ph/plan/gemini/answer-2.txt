### 3. Comparison: 06b vs 06c

* **Which realism pass is better:** **`06b` (GPT Image) is substantially better.** It produces convincing granite stone texture, authentic wood plank variation, sharp sun and pergola shadow interplay, and balanced glass transparency. In contrast, `06c` is colder, flatter, and plagued by a severe white glass reflection artifact that obscures the interior.
* **Publishing safety:**
  * **Neither pass is legally unsafe regarding fake views or altered geometry:** Neither image hallucinates an unbuilt structure, fake luxury beachfront, or false building heights.
  * **`06c` is unsafe to publish for quality reasons:** The milky reflection on the glass looks like a rendering glitch.
  * **`06b` is safe in terms of truthfulness, but commercially flawed:** While safe from misrepresentation claims (the view is neutral atmospheric haze rather than a fake city), omitting the real sea/city panorama fails to showcase the apartment's true setting.
