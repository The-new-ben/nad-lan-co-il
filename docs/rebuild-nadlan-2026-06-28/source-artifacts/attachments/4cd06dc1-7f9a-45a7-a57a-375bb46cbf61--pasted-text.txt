Three content blocks engineered to translate cleanly:

A transactional anchor (Block A) — "דירות למכירה" phrasing, the word competitors fail to put on the Rainbow page.
A price block (B) — fully cited numbers, in ₪ AND m² (international buyers think in $/€/m², so the m² figure ranks across every language).

Why this beats writing 4 separate language pages later: keywords are intent, not characters.
