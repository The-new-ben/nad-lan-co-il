# Ashira Sde Dov apartments for sale in Tel Aviv

Ashira in Sde Dov should be presented as a buyer decision page, not as a simple contact page. The page must help a buyer understand the building, choose an apartment, review the floor, facing, view, surroundings and available documents, then send a focused enquiry with the selected apartment context.

## Why room count is not enough

In a new seafront district, the number of rooms is only the beginning. Floor, facing, balcony size, relation to the sea, proximity to transport, parks and public spaces, and the position of the apartment inside the building all affect the living experience and the long-term value. The page should guide the buyer through a clear journey: understand the building, choose an apartment, review price and surroundings, then enquire.

## Choosing an apartment from the building

The showroom lets a buyer select a home by floor, facing and area. When a buyer clicks a unit, the apartment panel updates with the selected apartment details. The enquiry sent from the page should include the apartment ID, floor, rooms, area and facing, so the sales conversation starts with context instead of a generic form.

## The Sde Dov surroundings

Sde Dov is one of the most significant new residential districts in north Tel Aviv. The project value is not only the tower. It is the connection between the sea, parks, new urban planning, transport, retail, education and leisure. A serious buyer needs to understand what is around the apartment, how access works and what may change in the district over time.

## Price, deals and comparison

Do not show a single unapproved price. The correct approach is a non-binding estimate range, an update date, and an explanation that the estimate is based on public deals or available data. When there is not enough reliable data, the section should collapse instead of inventing numbers.

## What to check before enquiry

Before enquiring, a buyer should review the apartment plan, facing, balcony size, floor density, elevator location, parking, storage, payment terms, delivery timing, technical specification, taxes and extra costs. A strong project page helps the buyer prepare for that conversation.

## FAQ

### Are the details binding?

No. The page is an information aid for buyers. Price, availability, specification and sales plans must be verified with the developer or an authorized representative.

### Why use a visual building selector?

A visual selector helps buyers understand where the apartment sits in the building and how it relates to the surroundings. It does not replace the official sales plan, but it makes the enquiry more focused.

### Can buyers compare apartments?

Yes. The page structure should support choosing, saving and comparing apartments by floor, area, facing, view and status.

## Disclaimer

The model, images, estimates and project information are illustrative and intended to support an initial decision. Every detail should be verified with the developer, municipality, title record, sales agreement, official plans and professional advisers when needed.
