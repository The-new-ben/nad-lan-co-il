Rules
- Made in the Gemini app in the owner's Chrome, one at a time, each looked at before it is kept. No paid interface.
- No picture claims to be a real photo of a real home. In the product every one sits under the tag "תיק לדוגמה" / "הדמיה", and the example portfolio is labelled as an example everywhere.
- No people, no text, no logos, no brand marks in any picture. No real project's design is imitated.
- Kept outside the public repository until the board is approved: `D:\Media\nad-lan\rentals-pictures\`. The board gets small copies (about 400 px).
