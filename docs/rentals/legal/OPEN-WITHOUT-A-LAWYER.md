# Rentals world: a public version that opens without waiting for a lawyer (HAD-795)

Asked by the coordinator on 20.11.2026: there is no lawyer and no date, so the public opening must not hang on one. For each of the seven open legal items: can the public version leave it out or say less, and which truly cannot ship without a lawyer.
The rule applied: what is not known is left out, never asserted; a legal sentence rests on the statute's own text or is not shown. This is the builder's proposal, not legal advice.

| # | Item | What the product does today (version 2, administrators) | Public version without a lawyer | Needs a lawyer only if |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The landlord's own signature | The tenant signs the lease through a link (name, drawn signature, "I read it"); the signature is kept with the exact text, date and time. The landlord's signature is not collected. | SAY LESS. Keep the tenant's signing, and call it what it is: "the tenant confirmed this text on this date". No word that the lease is "signed by both sides", "binding" or "valid in court". One line: the landlord signs his own copy himself. | we want to say the digital signature is binding, or to collect the landlord's signature in the product |
| 2 | Guarantors as people | A guarantor is a contact with a name and a phone, listed on the lease ("ערב: שם"). | SAY LESS. Keep it as a plain record of who the guarantor is. No guarantor signing through the product, no sentence on what a guarantee covers. | guarantors are to sign in the product, or the product is to word the guarantee |
| 3 | Reminders to tenants | One button opens the landlord's OWN WhatsApp with a drafted message; nothing is sent by the site and nothing is automatic. | SHIP AS IS, after one check of the draft's wording: a neutral reminder (amount, month, how to pay), no deadline threats, no legal language. | the site itself is to send messages, or send them automatically |
| 4 | The screening tip | Six sentences, each resting on a statute text read on 20.11.2026 (Credit Data Law ss. 26, 27, 38(d); Criminal Information Law s. 3(b); the Bank of Israel's restricted-accounts page). | SAY LESS. Then it ships. | we want the product to tell a landlord his securities are lawful or not |

## Summary
- Nothing of the seven has to wait for a lawyer if the public version takes the "say less" column. Two pieces of work come before opening: the code change of item 7, and a read of the reminder draft and the signing screens against items 1 and 3.
- What truly cannot ship without a lawyer is only what goes BEYOND that column: a binding-signature promise, guarantors signing in the product, messages sent by the site, advice on screening, a rule for appliances, a position on section 110, a verdict on securities.
- One thing outside the seven that the opening should decide: the lease text the product can produce (i18n/lease.json). Without a lawyer it can only be offered as a sample wording the landlord is told to have checked, never as "a lawyer-approved lease". If that is not acceptable, the lease maker stays closed in the public version and everything else opens.
