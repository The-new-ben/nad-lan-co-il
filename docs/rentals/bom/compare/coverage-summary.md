## Method and limits
- Scores come from 4 to 24 vendor pages opened per product (counts in products.csv). Cells marked 2 were re-read on vendor pages; October cells were not copied unchecked.
- Soft evidence: some 2s rest on pricing or home pages or on help-center collection titles, not article bodies (for example DoorLoop showings 02.02, Rentila revision tool on the home page). Treat as 'listed by vendor'.
- Avail help center and Buildium help center could not be fetched; TurboTenant support returned 401; Hemlane support 403. Under-counting is likely for those.
- Logged-in dashboards (WeCheck, MyHome) were not entered, so reminders, receipts and exports there are '?'.
- Parts are mapped to features with the rubric in the research session (US/UK/DE/FR equivalents allowed for CPI, utilities and tax filing; Israeli tax tracks 07.02 to 07.04 only count at 2 if the exact Israeli track exists).
