## 1. POSITIONING
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

### 1.1 The category we own
**"Choose your apartment from inside the building."**
Every Israeli portal shows listings. NadLan is the only place where a buyer rotates the actual
building, taps a specific apartment, sees its floor, direction, view and an honest price estimate,
checks the neighborhood on a live map (schools, transit, future plans), runs financing numbers,
and sends an inquiry that is attached to that exact unit. Madlan has data. Yad2 has volume.
Nobody has the selection moment. That is the story in every channel.

One-line positioning (internal): *Zillow's data honesty + Compass's editorial calm + a selection
experience none of them have.*

Public tagline direction (Hebrew, buyer-facing): "בוחרים דירה מתוך הבניין. לפני שחותמים."

### 1.2 Audiences and what we say to each

| Audience | Their pain | Our message | Proof we show |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Contractors / developers** (primary revenue) | Sales offices are expensive; renders on their own site produce anonymous phone calls; no idea which apartments generate interest | "Buyers choose a specific apartment in your building and the inquiry arrives with the unit attached. You see interest per floor and per direction." | Live Ashira page, 2-minute screen recording of the picker, sample unit-level inquiry |
| **Brokers / sales agencies** | Pre-sale marketing with static PDFs | "Send your buyer a link where they pick the unit themselves. You get back a warmer, more specific conversation." | Same demo, share-a-unit deep link |
| **Private sellers** (supply side, longer term) | Listing anonymity on big portals | "A verified, honest listing with real neighborhood data and financing tools next to it." | Listing page + calculators |
| **Professionals** (lawyers, appraisers, mortgage advisors, inspectors: the 2,723) | Client acquisition | "Buyers meet you at the exact decision moment: inside a project page, next to the apartment they want." | Sponsored-slot mock on a project page |
| **Foreign buyers** (highest-margin leads) | Cannot visit; distrust remote purchases | "Tour the building and pick the apartment from abroad, in English, with the legal and financing path explained." | EN Ashira page, /en/ hub once live |

### 1.3 What we never say
- No invented traffic, client counts, or "leading portal" claims.
- No "AI-powered" framing to contractors; the product is the showroom, not the tooling.
- The 3D mechanism never leads SEO titles or H1s (buyers search apartments, not tours); it leads
  the SALES conversation with contractors, where it is the differentiator.

## 2. CHANNELS AND CADENCE
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

### 2.0 The hero asset: screen recordings of the 3D picker
Everything downstream feeds off ONE production habit: a clean 30-60s screen recording per flagship
project (rotate building → tap unit → panel opens → price range → map → inquiry with unit chip).
Record desktop and mobile (390px). Variants: 15s teaser (social), 45s full flow (YouTube/LinkedIn),
2min narrated (sales calls). This costs nothing (the product IS the demo) and is the single most
convincing artifact we own. Refresh whenever the engine visibly improves (R3 cinematic select).

### 2.1 Organic SEO (already running, the base layer)
- Continue the existing content mission (`2026-07-01-marketing-seo-revenue-strategy.md` Phase A/B):
  bulk title/meta rewrite of the 965 project pages, Cowork guide/news articles, hub-and-spoke
  internal linking. This plan does not duplicate it; it depends on it.
- Marketing addition: every project we sign gets its SEO article as part of the paid package
  ("your project ranks #1 on its own name" is an honest, low-competition promise) and a press-style
  announcement post ("פרויקט {{X}} עלה לאוויר עם בחירת דירה מתוך הבניין").
- Cadence: SEO work continuous; 1 project-launch post per signed contractor.

### 2.2 Social networks

| Network | Audience | Format | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** (owner's personal profile first, company page second) | Developers, brokers, proptech watchers | 15-45s picker screen recordings with a builder-facing caption ("this is what a buyer does on a project page now"); build-in-public notes; market-data cards from band B4 once live | 2 posts/week |
| **Facebook** | Israeli buyers 30-55, neighborhood groups | 15-30s picker clips per project, area-hub content (Sde Dov prices, future plans), calculator tips | 3 posts/week + neighborhood group participation where rules allow |
| **Instagram** (Reels) | Buyers 25-45 | Vertical picker recordings with Hebrew captions, "apartment of the week" (a real available unit: floor, direction, view, estimate range) | 2-3 Reels/week |
| **YouTube (Shorts + long)** | Search-adjacent buyers | Shorts: the same vertical clips (YouTube SEO on project names); long: 3-5min honest project reviews narrating the showroom | 2 Shorts/week, 1 long/month |
| **TikTok** | Optional, only if Reels prove out | Repost Reels | Repost only |

Production rule: one recording session per week produces all clips for all networks. No bespoke
per-network content until a channel proves conversions.

### 2.3 Email outreach (contractors) — the revenue engine
- Kit: `contractor-outreach-email-he.md` (cold, follow-up, claim-and-upgrade variants).
- List building: the 965 catalog projects already name developers/contractors; cross-reference with
  public building-permit data and company sites for contact emails. Target: 30 qualified contacts
  per week entering the sequence.
- Sequence: Day 0 cold email → Day 4 WhatsApp short version (if number known) → Day 8 follow-up
  email → Day 15 final touch → recycle after 90 days. Stop instantly on any reply.
- Cadence: 30 new contacts/week = ~120/month. At even a 3-5% meeting rate that is 4-6 meetings a
  month, enough to feed the pipeline while the site improves.
- The "claim and upgrade" variant is the highest-leverage one: the contractor's project ALREADY has
  a page among the 965. "Your project is already listed" opens more doors than any pitch.

### 2.4 WhatsApp
- Business line 972-52-510-1555 (the live footer line) is the reply-anything channel.
- Outreach shorts (in the kit) for follow-ups and warm intros.
- After a demo call: send the 45s recording + the Ashira link in the same thread. WhatsApp is where
  Israeli deals actually progress; email is for the formal record.

### 2.5 Partnerships
- **Mortgage advisors**: they need buyer leads; we have financing tools on every project page.
  Offer: a labeled advisor slot on project pages (sponsored, per band B10 logic) + reciprocal
  referrals. Start with 2-3 advisors, one per metro area.
- **Real-estate lawyers**: same slot logic at the contract-signing decision point; lawyers are also
  the natural channel to foreign buyers.
- **Appraisers / inspectors (בדק בית)**: directory upgrades from the 2,723 verified list.
- Cadence: 2 partnership conversations per week from month 2.

### 2.6 PR angles (pitch to Globes/TheMarker/Calcalist real-estate desks, Geektime for the tech angle)
1. "The first Israeli site where you choose the apartment from inside the building" (product story,
   with the Ashira demo as the visual).
2. Urban-renewal transparency: 965 projects mapped and browsable in one catalog (public-interest
   story; honest because it is true).
3. Data reports: once the comps/market-snapshot engine is live, quarterly Hebrew market reports
   (owner wish-list #9) earn backlinks and brand mentions.
- Cadence: 1 pitch cycle per month; do not pitch traffic numbers we do not have.

### 3.1 Deal participation (DISCREET — never on any public page)
The owner's share in closed deals is structured privately, in the contractor agreement, as a
**referral / success fee**: NadLan delivered the specific buyer inquiry (unit-attached, timestamped
in the lead ledger), and the agreement provides a fixed fee or small percentage per closed unit that
originated from a NadLan inquiry.
- Framing in contracts: "עמלת הפניה בגין רכישה שמקורה בפנייה מאתר נדלן" (referral fee for a purchase
  originating from a NadLan inquiry). Suggested range: a fixed ₪5,000-25,000 per closed unit OR
  0.25%-0.75% of the unit price, whichever the contractor prefers; these are negotiation ranges,
  not market facts.
- Mechanism: the lead ledger (exists) is the source of truth; monthly statement per contractor.
- HARD RULE: this never appears on the website, in outreach emails, or in marketing materials.
  Public framing is only: the contractor pays for presence and receives inquiries. The success-fee
  layer is a private commercial term discussed in person, after trust is established, and only
  where it does not conflict with the contractor's broker agreements.

## 4. VALUE LADDER AND SUGGESTED PRICING (contractors)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

All prices are SUGGESTED OPENING RANGES for negotiation, not researched market rates. Validate
against the first 5 real conversations and adjust. The existing advertiser center
(/advertiser-center/, WooCommerce products, Green Invoice recurring billing, placement auction)
is the billing rail; map each rung to a product there.

| Rung | What they get | Suggested price (range) |
|---|---|---|
| **T0 Free — directory card** (the 965) | Basic listed page, claimable, honest data, claim button | ₪0 forever (this is the top-of-funnel asset; never paywall it) |
| **T1 Claimed page (Pro)** | Verified badge, their media + description, correct unit table, sales contact, inquiries routed to them, monthly inquiry summary | ₪400-900 / month |
| **T2 Flagship showroom (Premier)** | Full 3D showroom (concept model from the factory, or their official model when supplied), per-unit selection, price-estimate ranges, neighborhood map, financing tools, SEO article, unit-attached inquiries | Setup ₪4,000-9,000 one-time + ₪1,500-3,500 / month |
| **T3 Premier + per-unit analytics** | T2 + monthly report: views, saves, compares, inquiries per unit/floor/direction ("unit 18W got 40 saves this week" is the killer artifact; no Israeli portal offers it) | +₪500-1,500 / month on top of T2 |
| **Add-on: homepage featured slot** (band B5, `ממומן` label) | First card in the dark featured band + first in catalog sorting | ₪750-2,000 / week via the existing placement auction |
| **Add-on: white-label embed** (owner wish-list #8, later) | The showroom iframed on THEIR site, our engine + leads | ₪1,000-2,500 / month; do not lead with this before month 3 |
| **Private layer: referral fee** | See 3.1 | Contract-level only |

Professionals ladder (parallel, smaller tickets): free verified listing (the 2,723) → sponsored
category×city slot on homepage band B10 and project pages, suggested ₪150-450 / month per slot.
Volume business; automate via the advertiser center, do not hand-sell below ₪400/mo.

Pricing principles: anchor high with T2 (the demo justifies it), always leave T1 as the easy yes,
first 3 signed contractors get "founding project" pricing (50% off first 3 months) in exchange for
a testimonial and permission to use their project as a case study.

## 5. 90-DAY CALENDAR (weeks 1-13)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Assumes the dev track (master-reset phases / release roadmap R1-R6) runs in parallel; marketing
never blocks on it, but weeks 5+ get easier as the flagship page de-clutters.

**Week 1 — Foundations.**
Finalize the two Hebrew kits (this folder). Record the first Ashira screen-recording set (15/45/120s,
desktop+mobile). Build the first outreach list: 50 contractors from the 965 catalog with contact
research. Set up UTM conventions (see 6.4) and a simple pipeline sheet (contact → sent → replied →
meeting → proposal → signed).

**Week 2 — Outreach begins.**
Send first 30 cold emails (variant A) + claim-and-upgrade (variant C) to contractors whose projects
are among the 965. LinkedIn: first picker recording post. Facebook/Instagram accounts set up with
brand assets (3-bar mark, NADLAN wordmark).

**Week 3 — Rhythm.**
30 new contacts; follow-ups (variant B) to week-2 non-responders; WhatsApp shorts to any known
numbers. Social cadence starts fully (2 LinkedIn, 3 FB, 2 Reels, 2 Shorts). First partnership
conversation: one mortgage advisor.

**Week 4 — First meetings.**
Demo call script: live Ashira walkthrough (screen share), then the materials-request doc
(`contractor-materials-request-he.md`) sent within one hour of any yes. Publish first
project-launch style post about Ashira as the case study seed. Month-1 review: reply rate,
meeting rate; rewrite subject lines if reply rate < 3%.

**Weeks 5-6 — First signing push.**
Goal: 1-2 signed contractors (founding pricing). On yes: run the factory (Tier B/C materials →
concept model → live page within the promised turnaround). PR pitch #1 (product story) to one
tech/real-estate desk. Partnerships: first lawyer conversation. Social: introduce
"apartment of the week" format.

**Weeks 7-8 — Case study machine.**
First new signed project goes live: record ITS picker clips, publish launch post, add it to the
outreach emails as a second live example. Ask the founding contractor for a short quote
(testimonial). Begin professionals outreach (email to the top verified pros in 4 categories,
sponsored-slot offer) once the homepage B10 band exists; otherwise hold.

**Weeks 9-10 — Scale outreach.**
Outreach list to 30/week sustained (cumulative ~250 contacts). Second PR pitch (urban-renewal
transparency angle). Foreign-buyer content: 1 EN LinkedIn post + EN Ashira clip. Partnerships:
close the first advisor/lawyer sponsored slot (even at a symbolic price; the logo matters).

**Weeks 11-12 — Analytics as product.**
Produce the first real per-unit analytics report for the founding contractor (even semi-manual from
the lead ledger + engine events). Use an anonymized version of that report as a sales artifact in
all new outreach ("this is the monthly report you receive"). Review pricing against the first
proposals sent; adjust ranges.

**Week 13 — Quarter review.**
Targets to judge the quarter: ≥250 contractors contacted, ≥10 demo meetings, 2-4 paying projects,
1 professional/partner sponsorship, weekly social cadence unbroken, first analytics report shipped.
Decide: double down on the claim-and-upgrade motion vs cold, and whether to open the white-label
conversation with the strongest client.
