## The 5 facts we weaponize (all from the article)

1. **Pre-sales are a FINANCING mechanism, not marketing.** Lenders release construction capital
   only after pre-sales cover ~65–70% of project cost. (Israeli parallel: מכירה מוקדמת כתנאי
   ליווי בנקאי — every kablan lives this.) → Our pitch stops selling "exposure" and starts
   selling "עמידה ביעד המכירות המוקדמות שהבנק המלווה דורש".
2. **Pre-launch email list converts 3–8× vs cold launch traffic.** → We sell list-building
   infrastructure: project page + calculators + magnets capturing registrations months before
   permits. The developer's list, built on our surfaces.
3. **"Virtual showrooms are now expected... The single most impactful technology investment in
   pre-construction marketing in 2026... especially for international buyers who cannot or will
   not travel."** → Third-party validation of EXACTLY our product. Goes on /advertise/, in the
   deck, in outreach (cited honestly as an industry guide).
4. **Construction-phase retention protects pre-sales** (a buyer who exits mid-construction = full
   marketing spend, zero revenue). → We sell the retention layer: milestone updates on the
   project page, progress photography, WhatsApp nurture — 180 days of campaign = the retention
   window, which justifies the duration SKU.
5. **AI search (ChatGPT/Gemini) is a buyer research channel**: structured pages + FAQPage schema
   + third-party citation authority get cited; bare landing pages are invisible. → nad-lan IS
   the third-party citation authority for a developer. Our FAQ blocks + schema + articles citing
   the project = their AI visibility. No Israeli competitor sells this today.
