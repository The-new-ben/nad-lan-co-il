# ToS · Refund · Disclaimer — contract-audit marketplace (DRAFT)

> **⚠️ NOT LEGAL ADVICE / MUST BE VERIFIED.** This is a structured draft for the owner and his counsel. The marketplace legal structure (platform = intermediary; the lawyer is the client's lawyer and issues the חשבונית) touches **לשכת עורכי הדין** rules on practising law, fee-sharing, and solicitation. **A licensed Israeli lawyer must review and approve before publishing.** Adapted from RocketLawyer's "not a substitute for an attorney / not a referral service" structure + din.co.il disclaimer language (research §4, §5e, §7).
> Owner is himself an advocate (Ben Bettesh, bar 29020) — ideal to self-review, but a second pair of eyes on the Bar-compliance angle is recommended.

---

## 1. Legal structure (the model, in plain terms)

- **נדל"ן חכם = פלטפורמה (intermediary).** It is NOT a law firm and does NOT provide legal advice.
- **The lawyer the customer selects** performs the review, forms the lawyer–client relationship **directly with the customer**, issues the **חשבונית מס**, and bears professional responsibility for the opinion.
- **Platform fee:** the platform earns a marketing/platform fee (model: per-transaction fee OR lawyer listing fee — confirm which, to keep clear of fee-sharing concerns). Payment is collected via Grow/Meshulam; the lawyer's portion is remitted to the lawyer.
- **Money held until delivery** (escrow-style assurance) where the gateway allows; otherwise delivery-gated payout.

> **Open compliance question for counsel:** does the platform-fee mechanism risk being construed as forbidden fee-sharing with a non-lawyer entity, or as the platform soliciting on the lawyer's behalf? Two safe-harbour framings to choose between:
> - (a) Customer pays the platform a **separate, itemised "שירות תיווך/הנגשה" fee** + pays the lawyer their fee (two line items / two invoices).
> - (b) Lawyer pays the platform a **flat listing/marketing fee** (not a % of legal fees).

## 3. Refund / cancellation policy (draft — write it on the page; nobody else does)

| מצב | מדיניות |
|---|---|
| **איחור במסירה** מעבר ל-48 שעות עסקים (בסיסי: 3 ימי עסקים) | **50% החזר אוטומטי**, וחוות הדעת עדיין נמסרת. החזר מלא רק אם ההזמנה ננטשה ולא בוצעה. |
| **אי-התאמת היקף** (הוזמן "בסיסי", העסקה דורשת "מלא") | תשלום **הפרש המחיר בלבד**, ללא קנס. |
| **אי-שביעות רצון מהותית** מחוות הדעת | **סבב תיקון אחד ללא תשלום**. אם עדיין לא תקין → בירור מול הפלטפורמה (מנגנון יישוב מחלוקות). |
| **ביטול לפני תחילת העבודה** (לפני שעוה"ד החל בביקורת) | החזר מלא. |
| **לאחר מסירת חוות הדעת** | אין החזר (מוצר דיגיטלי שסופק) — בכפוף לחוק הגנת הצרכן. |

> **Note:** the SLA clock = **48 business hours**, excluding Friday afternoon, Shabbat, and holidays; starts when **payment clears AND a readable contract file is uploaded**. (Owner-locked earlier this session.)
> **Counsel to verify** against חוק הגנת הצרכן (digital-services cancellation rules) — the "no refund after delivery" line for digital products needs to be squared with statutory cooling-off provisions.
