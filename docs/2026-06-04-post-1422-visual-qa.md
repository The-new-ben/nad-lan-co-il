## Verdict

The original concept-art direction is working on project cards: no stock faces, no deceptive people, and fallback project thumbnails now feel architectural instead of abandoned. The site is still not yet "elite premium" end to end. The homepage is the biggest brand gap, and the catalog layer has a few micro-failures that make the implementation feel unfinished.

## Findings

| Severity | URL / viewport | Selector / area | Observation | Suggested fix direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Positive Checks

- `/projects/` and `/professionals/` do not show horizontal overflow at 390px.
- Project fallback cards now render original SVG concept art and no stock-photo faces.
- Professional cards correctly stay icon-only, avoiding deceptive people imagery.
- Rainbow mobile has a strong real/project image lead and the generated gallery direction works.
- Search forms stack correctly on mobile.

## Not Covered

Authenticated internal flows were not tested in this pass: `/studio/`, `/advertiser-center/`, signup, claim, payment, and listing editing. The owner still wants the internal journey to feel premium end to end, but this QA run stayed on the corrected public URL list and made no code changes.
