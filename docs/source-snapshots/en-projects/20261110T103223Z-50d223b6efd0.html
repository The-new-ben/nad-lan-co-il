New projects in Tel Aviv: where they build and what it costs

Tel Aviv-Yafo started more new homes than any other Israeli city: 7,611 housing starts in the 12 months to June 2026, up 19.9%. New construction is spread across five very different parts of the city, from seafront towers in the new Sde Dov district to urban renewal in Yad Eliyahu.

From abroad, step by step

  1. An Israeli real estate lawyerYour lawyer checks the developer, the permit, the contract and the guarantee, and registers your rights. In Israel there is no notary in the deal: the lawyer does that work.
  2. Power of attorneyIf you cannot be in Israel for the signing, you sign a power of attorney at an Israeli consulate or before a local notary with an apostille, so your lawyer can sign for you.
  3. An Israeli bank accountPayments to the developer go through the payment vouchers of the project's accompanying bank, so in practice you need an Israeli account; banks ask for the source of the funds.
  4. Moving the moneyTransfer in stages, by the payment schedule. The currency rate on each transfer day changes your real price, so some buyers fix part of the amount in advance with their bank.
  5. The Sale Law guaranteeEvery payment above 7% of the price must be secured, usually by a bank guarantee issued within 14 business days of each voucher payment.

For contracts signed from 7.7.2022 (Amendment 9 to the Sale Law), up to half of each payment may be linked to the construction inputs index, the first payment of 20% of the price is never linked, and linkage stops at the contractual delivery date even if handover is late. Frequently asked questions about new projects in Israel

Can foreigners buy a new apartment in Israel?

Yes. A foreign resident can buy a new apartment from a developer in Israel. The main differences from an Israeli buyer are a purchase tax of 8% up to 6,055,070 ILS and 10% above it (a temporary order, extended to 10.2.2027), a bank mortgage of up to 50% of the value, and the need to move the money into an Israeli account for the voucher payments.

How much purchase tax do I pay on a new apartment in Israel?

It depends on who you are. An Israeli resident buying an only home pays 0% up to 1,978,745 ILS, 3.5% up to 2,347,040, 5% up to 6,055,070 and 8% and 10% above that. A foreign resident or an Israeli buying an additional home pays 8% up to 6,055,070 ILS and 10% above it. On a new apartment the tax is calculated on the price including VAT.

What purchase tax benefit do new immigrants (olim) get?

An oleh buying a single home pays 0% up to 1,978,745 ILS and 0.5% up to 6,055,070 ILS, then 8% and 10% above that. The relief applies from one year before aliyah to seven years after it and not above 20,183,565 ILS. For a 3,000,000 ILS apartment that is about 5,100 ILS instead of 45,538 ILS for an Israeli resident. Olim who arrived before 15.8.2024 may choose the older regulation 12 track, which only pays off above about 12.5 million ILS.

Can a foreign resident get a mortgage in Israel?

Yes. Israeli banks lend non-residents up to 50% of the property value under Bank of Israel directive 329. You will need proof of income and of the source of the funds, an Israeli bank account and an appraisal. Average rates on new mortgages in August 2026 were about 4.62% fixed unlinked and 3.29% fixed CPI-linked.

Can a foreigner own land in Israel?

Much of Israel's land is Israel Lands, held on long leases. The Israel Lands Law limits transfers to a "foreigner", but defines a foreigner as someone who is not an Israeli citizen or resident and not entitled to make aliyah under the Law of Return, so Jewish buyers from abroad are not foreigners for this rule. Others can be approved for one home on residential land.

Where are the new projects in Tel Aviv?

Mostly in five areas: the Sde Dov district and north Tel Aviv along the shore, luxury towers in the city centre around Ibn Gabirol and Sarona, urban renewal in east Tel Aviv (Yad Eliyahu, Nahalat Yitzhak), the southern neighbourhoods, and Jaffa. The Tel Aviv section on this page shows each area with deal prices and the developments selling there.

How much does a new apartment cost in Tel Aviv?

The official median deal for a 4-room apartment in Tel Aviv-Yafo in January to March 2026 was 5,229,800 ILS, about 1.69 million dollars, for new and resale homes together. New developments start lower in the south and east of the city and far higher in the north and on the seafront; the table on this page lists the starting prices developers publish.

Is there pre-sale in Tel Aviv?

Yes. Developers regularly open pre-sale rounds before the building permit or at the start of sales, at a price below the full launch price. At pre-sale you pay for an apartment that is further from the keys, so check the permit stage, the timeline and the security on every payment.

What does buying off-plan mean in Israel?

Buying off-plan means signing for an apartment in a building that is not yet built, and paying as it goes up. In August 2026, 61% of the apartments developers sold on the free market had a delivery date 24 months or more away. Your money is protected by the Sale Law guarantee on every payment above 7% of the price.

How long does it take from signing to getting the keys?

It depends on the stage at signing. A residential building takes on average 31.8 months to build from the start of works, according to the Central Bureau of Statistics. Signing before the permit adds the licensing time. If the developer is more than a month late, the Sale Law entitles you to compensation.

What is a Sale Law guarantee?

A developer may not take more than 7% of the price without security, usually a bank guarantee under the Sale (Apartments) Law. You pay only through the accompanying bank's voucher booklet and each payment triggers a guarantee within 14 business days. The guarantee excludes VAT, which a Finance Ministry fund refunds if the guarantee is called.

What if the developer gives no bank guarantee?

The developer may not take more than 7% of the price without one of the securities set by the Sale (Apartments) Law. When the money is secured only by a mortgage or a warning note, the regulations limit payments to building stages: 31% when the lowest floor is cast, 25% when the frame with the mamad is done, then 5%, 6%, 10%, 13%, 5% and the last 5% at occupancy and delivery.

How does index linkage work on a new apartment?

For contracts from 7.7.2022, up to half of each payment may be linked to the construction inputs index, the first 20% of the price is never linked, and linkage stops at the contractual delivery date. At most 40% of the price is linked. The index for September 2026 stood at 104.0, up 3.6% over 12 months.

What compensation do I get if delivery is late?

For contracts from 7.7.2022, after the first month of delay you get, without proving damage, 1 times the rent of a similar apartment for months 2 to 4, 1.25 times for months 5 to 10 and 1.5 times from month 11. For example, a 12-month delay on an apartment that would rent for 10,000 ILS gives 135,000 ILS.

What are 20/80 and 10/90 deals?

Developer deals where you pay 20% or 10% at signing and the rest at handover, sometimes with a developer-paid loan. They ease cash flow but are limited by the Bank of Israel since April 2025, to 31.12.2026. In August 2026 about 30% of developer sales in five tax regions included such a financing benefit.

Do I need an Israeli lawyer to buy from a developer?

In practice, yes. In Israel the buyer's lawyer checks the developer, the permit, the contract and the guarantee, handles the purchase tax filing and registers your rights; there is no notary in the deal. The developer may charge you for his own lawyer up to 5,915 ILS or 0.5% of the price plus VAT, except above 4,642,750 ILS.

Is VAT included in the price of a new apartment?

Yes. Developer prices for residential apartments in Israel include VAT, which has been 18% since 1.1.2025. Purchase tax on a new apartment is calculated on the price including VAT.

What is a mamad and does every new apartment have one?

A mamad is the reinforced safe room inside the apartment, for the residents of that apartment only. New residential buildings have been built with protected spaces since the early 1990s, and the Civil Defense regulations set a mamad at no less than 9 square meters net, 2.5 to 2.8 meters high. In a new apartment it is usually one of the bedrooms, so check in the specification whether the bedroom count includes it.

How are rooms and square meters counted in Israel?

Israeli listings count rooms, not bedrooms: the living room is a room, so a 4-room apartment usually has a living room and three bedrooms, often one of them the mamad. Half rooms such as 3.5 are common. The area in the developer's specification is measured to the outer faces of the exterior walls, so an Israeli 100 square meters (about 1,076 sq ft) includes the walls; balconies, parking and storage are listed separately.

Which cities are popular with English-speaking buyers?

Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, Ra'anana, Netanya, Herzliya, Beit Shemesh and Modi'in are cities with large English-speaking communities and many new projects. The city links on this page open the projects in each city, and the prices table compares deal prices across Israel's main cities.

How many new apartments are unsold in Israel?

According to the Central Bureau of Statistics, about 85,680 new apartments were unsold at the end of August 2026, 26.7 months of supply. About 26,330 were in the Tel Aviv district and about 9,820 in Tel Aviv-Yafo itself.

Can I buy in Israel without travelling there?

Yes. You can sign a power of attorney at an Israeli consulate or before a notary with an apostille, so your Israeli lawyer signs for you. Payments go by voucher from an Israeli account, and the guarantee is issued in your name.
