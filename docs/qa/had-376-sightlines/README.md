# HAD-376: the sight lines on Rainbow, DUO, Dimri Yama and Ashira (30.9.2026, release 1.72.372)

## What was wrong

`places.json` gives every place a `sight` label per floor band: `street` (the place itself is seen), `roof` (only its
building's top) or nothing (hidden). The labels decide which named places the view from the floor and the 360 windows show,
and at what height the 360 label sits.

`scripts/project-stage/build_places.py` compared the places with the city's buildings (`city.json` "b") in two different frames:

- **The buildings** are drawn in each stage's frame (stage-geometry.md 0.2): north-up metres with 110,574 m a degree of
  latitude, then turned with the lot's grid, 10° (Rainbow, DUO) or 11° (Dimri Yama, Ashira).
- **The places** went in north up, with 111,320 m a degree of latitude, and were not turned.
- **The landmarks** (quarter.json) were compared as their north-up x/z. The builders write those unturned on purpose, and
  the stage turns them itself.
- **The tower** was placed north up too.

At 500 m the turn alone moves a place by about 90 m, so a place was compared with the wrong buildings.

Kikar Hamedina was not affected. Its research layer is north up, with 111,320 m a degree, the same as the places.

## The fix (one code path, no per-project branch)

- **One frame for every sight line.** `build_places.py` now puts the places, the landmarks and the tower into the frame that
  the city layer declares in `city.json` "origin":
  - `lat`, `lng` and `grid_deg`;
  - `m_lat`, which defaults to 110,574.
- **A layer without a declaration is refused.**
- **The declarations:**
  - Rainbow's `city.json` had none, so it gained `"origin":{"lat":32.103168,"lng":34.784441,"grid_deg":10}`. These are
    `build_city_rainbow.py`'s own ORIGIN and GRID, and that builder now writes the key. Only those 57 bytes changed.
  - Kikar's research layer declares `"m_lat":111320.0`, and `build_area_kikar.py` writes it. Kikar's `places.json` stays
    byte for byte the same (md5 1e1ddd71…).
- **A sight-only rebuild.** `build_places.py <project> --resight` recomputes only the `sight` fields on the registry that is
  already built. It fetches nothing and keeps the date. It refuses a file that does not read back to its own bytes.

## The evidence

1. **Reproduction.** Before the fix, `--resight` rebuilt the four live files byte for byte, with the same md5s as live and as
   the last releases wrote them (361, 365). So the old labels come from exactly this code.
2. **The frame, measured** (`scripts/project-stage/sight_frame_check.py`, run on the files before the fix):
   - The landmarks' stated bearings match their x/z only when the x/z are read north up (errors of 0.0°). Read as turned,
     the errors are 9-11°.
   - Places inside a building footprint, old frame → declared frame:

     | Project | Old frame | Declared frame |
     |---|---|---|
     | Rainbow | 50 | 242 |
     | DUO | 115 | 284 |
     | Dimri | 6 | 64 |
     | Ashira | 22 | 80 |

     Within 750 m, DUO goes from 115 to 282. The P2 agent's "269/600" was measured in the same way.
   - The tower in the declared frame lands exactly on the builders' own points:
     - DUO: (16.7, 1.6), `TOWERS_MID`;
     - Dimri: (-30.9, 18.2), stage-geometry tower A.
3. **By hand, against the city itself** (`sight_handcheck.py`, results in `handcheck.json`):
   - For 4 named places per project, the Tel Aviv-Yafo buildings layer (GIS 513) was queried at the place's own lat/lng, so
     no frame of ours was involved.
   - **15 of 16:** the declared frame finds the same building (floors, year and height all equal), and the old frame found
     none or a different one.
   - **The 16th** (Rainbow, "ויצו כוכב הצפון") stands in a building that the city layer lists with no height, no floors and
     no year. The builder leaves such buildings out by rule, so no frame can find it.
4. **Only the sight lines changed.** `sight_diff.py` checked every other field of every place and landmark: identical.
5. **Every change, listed:** `changes-<project>.csv` holds every place and landmark whose label changed on any band.

   | Project | Places changed on some band | Named places | Landmarks | Band flips hidden ↔ shown |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | Rainbow | 529 of 860 | 481 | 2 of 5 | 443 now shown, 428 now hidden |
   | DUO | 798 of 1,391 | 713 | 1 of 3 | 700 now shown, 483 now hidden |
   | Dimri Yama | 324 of 835 | 282 | 2 of 5 | 244 now shown, 227 now hidden |
   | Ashira | 360 of 791 | 324 | 2 of 5 | 301 now shown, 208 now hidden |

Design was not needed (PROJECT-PAGE-CHECKLIST G1 covers visual and page changes).
