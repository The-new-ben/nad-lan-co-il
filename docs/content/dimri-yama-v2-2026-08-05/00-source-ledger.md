# DIMRI YAMA Sde Dov - frozen fact and source ledger

Frozen for drafting: 2026-08-05

Scope: the four content-only products in English, French, Russian and Arabic. This ledger controls every material fact in all four drafts. Cultural adaptation may change emphasis and explanation, but it may not change a number, date, status or evidence label.

## Identity and location

- Approved project name: `DIMRI YAMA`, also marketed by the developer as `YAMA TLV`.
- City and district: Tel Aviv-Yafo, Sde Dov district, Eshkol section.
- Planning location: lot 107 under plan TML/3001.
- The exact final postal address has not been verified in an official project-specific municipal source. `Ibn Gvirol 220` appears in commercial listings and must not be presented as a confirmed final address.
- The site is in the coastal Sde Dov district. This does not establish a sea view, an unobstructed view or a permanent view for any particular apartment.

## Scale and uses

- The company reports 458 apartments planned for sale.
- The wider project includes four buildings, a 70-room hotel component and approximately 1,500 square metres of commercial space.
- The 2025 annual report describes a 39-storey tower, a 16-storey tower and two lower buildings of approximately 8 to 9 storeys, above five shared basement levels.
- The developer's English marketing graphic labels the buildings 38, 15, 8 and 8 floors.
- The 2023 municipal design decision counts 40, 16, 9 and 9 levels. For the main tower it identifies a ground level, 38 residential levels and a technical level. These three source-specific counts are a real public conflict, likely reflecting different counting conventions. Every draft must disclose the difference and tell buyers to verify the signed building and apartment documents. Do not silently normalize the figures.
- Published planning material for lot 107 describes an apartment-size distribution of 50% above 86 square metres, 25% from 61 to 85 square metres and 25% up to 60 square metres. This is a planning mix, not a current sales inventory.
- A current room-by-room inventory, exact unit schedule, parking allocation, storage allocation and apartment-specific plan list have not been published in a source approved for this package.

## Design and amenities

- The current developer page credits Rani Ziss Architects. The 2023 municipal design decision, which predates Dimri's acquisition, names both Rani Ziss Architects and Donsky Tzafnat Architects in the planning team. Use source-specific attribution and do not erase either public record.
- The developer presents Kelly Hoppen CBE as responsible for the interior concept or design of the public spaces and residences. This must be attributed to the developer and must not be rewritten as a bespoke Kelly Hoppen design entitlement for every delivered apartment.
- The developer presents a western facade with wave-like balconies.
- Planned amenities presented by the developer include a rooftop infinity pool, an indoor semi-Olympic pool, spa and treatment areas, wet and dry saunas, a gym, yoga or Pilates space, a chef kitchen and bar, workspace or library, lounge, children's room, music room and wine room.
- These are planned or developer-presented amenities. Their final specification, operating model, access rules, management fees, opening dates and inclusion in a buyer's legal rights have not been verified. Every draft must direct the buyer to the signed specification and management documents.
- The 2023 municipal design decision includes a public-easement component in the courtyard and open-space system. Do not describe the entire garden or courtyard as private residents-only land.

## Developer and acquisition history

- Developer group: Y.H. Dimri Construction & Development Ltd., founded in 1989 according to the company's public profile.
- The 2025 annual report describes effective 100% group rights, held 96% through Y.H. Dimri Sde Dov Ltd. and 4% through Y.H. Dimri Real Estate Ltd. The identity of the contractual seller must still be checked in the actual sale agreement.
- On 20 March 2024 the company reported an offer to acquire lot 107 rights from the Hanan Mor group debt arrangement. The acquisition agreement was signed on 24 July 2024 for NIS 1.1 billion plus VAT.
- The company reported completion of the acquisition on 3 December 2024 and an NIS 825 million financing agreement.
- This history may be explained as the route by which the rights reached Dimri. It must not imply that the current project or buyer funds are in distress.

## Sales and prices

- The developer does not publish a complete current price list or live unit inventory on the official public pages reviewed for this package.
- A public launch report dated 22 December 2025 described two-room apartments of approximately 56.5 square metres from NIS 3.75 million. This is a dated historical launch example only. It is not a current asking price, current availability or promise that an equivalent apartment remains for sale.
- The 2025 annual report recorded 32 sale contracts by 31 December 2025 and an average contracted price of about NIS 70,000 per square metre excluding VAT. It reported five further contracts between year-end and publication at an average of about NIS 64,000 per square metre excluding VAT.
- The Q1 2026 financial report recorded 41 cumulative contracted apartments and 1% completion by 31 March 2026. Business-press coverage interprets the company table as an average contracted value of approximately NIS 67,000 per square metre excluding VAT and a company forecast of approximately NIS 62,000 per square metre excluding VAT for unsold stock. These are dated corporate reporting and interpretation, not a consumer price list.
- The investor presentation dated 1 June 2026 repeated 41 sold apartments and reported four signed purchase requests totalling approximately NIS 23.643 million including VAT at that presentation date. Purchase requests are not completed sale contracts, and neither figure is live inventory.
- Four of the nine contracts signed in Q1 2026 involved Yigal Dimri or members of his family, under the approvals described in company reporting. This must always retain the denominator and period: four of nine Q1 2026 contracts. It must never be described as nearly half of all project sales.
- The live NadLan showroom's four apartment records, availability labels and NIS 66,000 to NIS 84,000 per square metre display are illustrative and are prohibited as factual inventory or current pricing.
- No independent current project-price estimate is approved. If a draft explains how to build an estimate, the label must be the exact target-language equivalent of `estimate, non-binding`, and the result must not be presented as a published DIMRI YAMA price.
- No rental yield, appreciation rate, resale liquidity or foreign-currency conversion is approved. Buyers may be shown how to model these variables, but no outcome may be promised.

## Environmental disclosure

- The 2025 annual report describes a March 2026 environmental review concerning PFAS at the site. It states that most of the plot was below the applicable threshold, while a small area exceeded it and required further investigation to define the affected limits and the necessary excavation or disposal treatment.
- Ministry of Environmental Protection material says PFAS was identified in groundwater and in limited soil samples across the former Sde Dov airfield, likely linked to historic firefighting foam use. It says the groundwater is not used for drinking and does not threaten drinking-water sources, while direct contact with affected soil and movement of contaminated soil require controls. This is district context and must not be projected uniformly onto lot 107.
- This is a current material project disclosure and must appear neutrally in every language. Do not claim that the matter is fully resolved, that the whole plot is contaminated or that it creates a proven health outcome. Ask for the latest environmental report, regulator instructions, remediation scope and effect on schedule and costs.

- These are district plans, not existing services at the project entrance. Every draft must distinguish what exists today from what is planned and must not invent school assignments, travel times or opening dates.

## Buying-process guidance

- Purchase tax depends on buyer status and the law in force on the transaction date. Link to the official calculator and NadLan purchase-tax calculator; do not state a universal rate.
- Mortgage eligibility and loan-to-value limits depend on the buyer and property classification. Bank of Israel public guidance gives general maximum LTV examples of 75% for a sole home, 70% for a replacement home and 50% for an investment property. These are ceilings, not an approval or an offer.
- For a new apartment, buyers should independently verify rights, permit and plan documents, the Sale Law guarantee, the technical specification, payment schedule, indexation, variations, delay provisions, management arrangements, inspection, handover and registration.
- A remote buyer should verify identity and source-of-funds requirements, independent Israeli representation, translations, the exact form and permitted scope of any power of attorney, signature authentication, bank transfer mechanics, construction notices, inspection and ongoing management. The process depends on the buyer's citizenship, residence and documents.
- This is general buyer information, not legal, tax or mortgage advice for an individual transaction.
