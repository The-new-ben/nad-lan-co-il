# ASHIRA Sde Dov - frozen fact and source ledger

Research date: 2026-08-04

Scope: common factual spine for the English, French, Russian and Arabic content drafts. This file is editorial evidence, not public page copy.

## Facts approved for all four languages

- Project: ASHIRA, a residential project by Avisror Moshe & Sons in Sde Dov, Tel Aviv.
- Planning location: lot 101 in the Eshkol planning unit. A final postal address has not been verified. Do not use "Levi Eshkol 29" as an official address.
- Lot plan: architectural and environmental development plan תע"א/תמ"ל 3001(101), approved by the municipality in May 2024.
- Site: approximately 7.4 dunams according to the municipal lot page.
- Approved composition: four residential buildings, comprising one 35-storey tower, one 16-storey building and two 8-storey buildings.
- Homes: 406 planned homes, allocated in the municipal plan as 202 in tower construction and 204 in perimeter or mid-rise construction.
- Public and open space: about 3,400 sqm of open space, approximately 46 percent of the lot, and 500 sqm planned for a kindergarten and municipal offices. These are planned uses, not evidence that the facilities operate today.
- Architecture: Yashar Architects, led by Avner Yashar. Interior design is presented by the developer as the work of Dana Oberson.
- Apartment mix: official marketing is internally inconsistent between 2 to 5 rooms and 2 to 6 rooms. Safe wording: current marketing presents apartments from 2 to 6 rooms and penthouses, while a dated current mix and live inventory have not been publicly published.
- Amenities: the developer presents an indoor semi-Olympic pool, children’s pool, jacuzzi, spa, salt room, collagen tanning, dry and wet sauna, gym, yoga or wellness space, cinema, children’s areas, resident lounge, bar, workspace and ground-floor commerce. Attribute these to the developer’s plans. Do not guarantee delivery, hours, fees or operating rules.
- Permit and status: a full building permit was publicly reported in March 2026. Public reporting in spring 2026 placed work at foundation and basement-raft stage. The project is presented as being marketed and under construction.
- Completion: public reports describe 2030 as a target. It is not a contractual delivery, occupancy or guaranteed handover date.
- Financing: published reports identify Mizrahi-Tefahot and First International Bank as project financiers, with financing above NIS 2.5 billion. They are financiers, not co-developers.
- Current prices: no current official comprehensive public price list or unit-by-unit inventory was found. State this plainly.
- Historical price context only: Ynet reported March 2024 launch examples of a 49 sqm two-room apartment from NIS 3.9 million, three rooms from NIS 5.4 million, four rooms from NIS 7.4 million, five rooms from NIS 9 million and a 160 sqm penthouse plus 67 sqm terrace at NIS 25 million. In February 2025 it reported two-room offers from NIS 4.1 million and some high-floor five-room marketing up to NIS 21 million. These are dated marketing examples, not current prices, availability, valuations or transactions.
- Developer: Avisror says it was founded in 1978 by Moshe Avisror and operates as a family company in development, management and construction. Current reports identify Avisror as both developer and executing contractor for ASHIRA.
- District: Tel Aviv Municipality describes Sde Dov under master plan TA/4444, approved in August 2020, across approximately 1,300 dunams, with about 16,000 planned homes and an estimated future population of about 40,000. Mixed uses and public infrastructure remain district-scale planning context.
- Eshkol: the wider plan was approved in May 2021 and provides 4,844 planned homes, about 45,000 sqm retail and about 145,000 sqm employment. These figures are not ASHIRA project figures.
- Transport: the Green Line and four stations along the Ibn Gabirol continuation are future infrastructure. NTA currently publishes 2028 for a southern section and 2030 for full commercial operation, subject to change.
- Environment: wider Sde Dov contamination and PFAS issues are governed by public guidance. The company stated that it holds approvals indicating no contamination concern on its lot, but the underlying approvals were not independently inspected. Attribute the statement to the company only.

## Conflicts resolved uniformly

- Floors: use 35, 16, 8 and 8 from the municipal approved plan and current permit coverage, not older 34, 15, 7 and 8 figures.
- Site area: use approximately 7.4 dunams from the municipality, not 7.3.
- Apartment mix: describe the official 2 to 5 versus 2 to 6 inconsistency, then state that current inventory is unpublished.
- District area: use approximately 1,300 dunams from the municipality, not the developer’s 1,500.
- Banks: use Mizrahi-Tefahot and First International Bank, supported by multiple current reports.
- Completion: describe 2030 only as a reported target.
- Milestones: May 2024 is municipal design-plan approval. March 2026 is the reported full building permit.
