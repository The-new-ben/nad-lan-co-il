# RECANATI RESIDENCE - independent source review

Review date: 5 August 2026

## Outcome

The independently checked sources support a stable core identity: RECANATI RESIDENCE is a completed demolition-and-reconstruction project in Ramat Aviv Gimel, Tel Aviv-Yafo, with 198 apartments in three buildings. Current Ashtrom materials use 16 floors and immediate occupancy. Enav describes the complex as occupied.

## Independent corrections

- Reject Neve Avivim as the project neighborhood.
- Reject Sde Dov, sea-facing or generic showroom language.
- Use Ashtrom Residences as the current brand; explain Ashdar as the former name.
- Attribute the project to Ashtrom Residences together with Enav. Do not invent current ownership percentages.
- Use 198 as the project total. Do not use the contractor page's isolated 66 as the total.
- Use 16 floors in current public copy. Earlier 17/18 counts are planning or counting-convention variants.
- Treat Recanati 5-15 as the historic planning site, not a guaranteed current entrance list.
- Treat every current price, availability and last-unit claim as volatile and unpublished unless reconfirmed from a dated direct unit sheet.

## Historical evidence accepted with dates

- 96 former apartments were replaced by 198 homes.
- 102 of the 198 were historically allocated to developer sales.
- The regulatory report records buildings 1 and 2 complete in March 2023 and building 3 in June 2023.
- A 2023 regulatory sale example may be used only as a dated large-unit transaction excluding VAT, never as a current asking price or price estimate.

## Rejected claims

- Current price per sqm or project price range.
- A current last penthouse or exact remaining-unit count.
- Guaranteed sea view, park view, storage or apartment-specific parking.
- Exact current postal entrances.
- Exact walking times, school assignment, management fee or municipal tax.
- A promised investment yield or appreciation rate.
- Demo unit sizes, floors, directions, model geometry, interior scenes or selectable availability from the existing page.
- The unsafe expired old mini-site URL.

## Editorial recommendation

Write the project as a completed-home decision: buyers can inspect the real building and apartment, but must still verify the specific lot, seller authority, current price, rights, building budget and handover mechanics. This is more useful and more accurate than repeating generic new-development copy.
