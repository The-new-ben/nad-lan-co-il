## Evidence boundary

This file records search language and buyer questions. It is not proof of a project fact, apartment, price, availability, view, school, return or legal conclusion. Google suggestions and competitor headings shape terminology and information architecture only. Every project claim remains controlled by `00-source-ledger.md`.

Direct Google search-result pages were rate-limited to Google's `/sorry/` page during the final Recanati pass. No People Also Ask statement or organic ranking was accepted from that blocked state. Google Autocomplete was still collected live in real Chrome through Google's public suggestions endpoint, with `hl=<target language>` and `gl=il`. Competitor-title themes were supplemented by direct public web pages and treated only as intent evidence.

Final live suggestions were captured at 06:49 Israel time on 5 August 2026 from the current Ashtrom Recanati page in Chrome. The endpoint sometimes omits the seed phrase from the returned array; the seed itself remains a real query, not a returned suggestion.
