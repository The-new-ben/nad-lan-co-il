## Identity lock

- Project: `RECANATI RESIDENCE`.
- Correct location: Ramat Aviv Gimel, Tel Aviv-Yafo.
- Safe location wording: on the western side of Recanati Street, beside Beit Tzuri Garden, on the urban-renewal site historically identified as Recanati 5-15.
- Project type: demolition-and-reconstruction urban renewal.
- Current official configuration: 3 completed residential buildings, 16 floors, 198 apartments.
- Published housing mix: 3-5 rooms, mini-penthouses and penthouses.

## Conflicts and decisions

### 16, 17 or 18 floors

The current official Ashtrom page and the 2023 regulatory report use 16 floors. Earlier planning and partner descriptions use 17 or 18, apparently because of design evolution or different treatment of the entrance, ground and technical levels. Current buyer-facing articles use 16. A short note may explain the counting difference if the reader encounters another number. Do not present all versions as simultaneous physical facts.

### 198 or 66 apartments

The current Ashtrom project page, statutory plan and regulatory filing establish 198 apartments. An Ashtrom Construction page shows 66, exactly one third of 198. It may refer to one building or be a content error. Do not use 66 as the project total.

### Immediate occupancy, occupied, or remaining unit

Immediate occupancy and occupied status can coexist in a completed complex. They do not prove that a particular apartment is for sale. Historic reports and stale indexed copy cannot establish August 2026 inventory. The articles must say that current availability and price require same-day confirmation.

### Recanati 5-15, 7, or 9

Recanati 5-15 is the verified historic planning site. Search and portal pages surface individual numbers, but the inspected current official project page does not provide the current entrance numbers. Use the street-side and garden description rather than a fabricated single postal address.

## Facts that must remain unpublished

- A current price, price list, financing offer or payment schedule.
- A current unit count or a claim that one penthouse remains.
- An apartment-specific area, floor, orientation, view, parking space or storage room.
- Current management fees, municipal tax, legal fees or fit-out costs.
- A promised delivery day, handover date or registration date for a current buyer.
- A guaranteed sea view, park view or unobstructed outlook.
- A numerical rental yield, capital-gain forecast or guaranteed investment return.
- Exact current entrance numbers without a current official source.
- A precise school assignment, walking time or travel time without a separate current check.

- Lead with the completed-project reality, Ramat Aviv Gimel identity, 198 apartments, 3 buildings, 16 floors, published mix and the current price/inventory gap.
