## Cultural differentiation

The factual contract is identical, while the buyer task differs by language:

- English prioritizes completed-versus-off-plan choice, relocation, remote purchase, financing, source of funds and currency exposure.
- French prioritizes appartement neuf versus achat sur plan, achat à distance, installation or aliyah planning, taxe d'acquisition, procuration and charges de copropriété.
- Russian prioritizes current price evidence, liquidity, holding costs, document checks, remote purchasing and currency risk without promising return.
- Arabic prioritizes family use, space, privacy, daily services, education verification, parking/storage rights, building charges and remote purchase in native RTL copy.

These are distinct native articles, not paragraph-aligned translations. No cultural emphasis changes a project fact.
