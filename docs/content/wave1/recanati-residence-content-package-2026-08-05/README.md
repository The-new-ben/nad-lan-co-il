## Locked identity and facts

- RECANATI RESIDENCE is in Ramat Aviv Gimel, Tel Aviv-Yafo, on the western side of Recanati Street beside Beit Tzuri Garden.
- It is a completed demolition-and-reconstruction urban-renewal complex with 198 apartments in three buildings.
- Current Ashtrom project material and the later regulatory report use 16 floors.
- The published mix is 3-5 rooms, mini-penthouses and penthouses.
- Project-level facts include more than 15 metres between buildings and three underground parking levels.
- The developer attribution is Ashtrom Residences, formerly Ashdar, together with Enav.
- The 2023 regulatory report records buildings 1 and 2 complete in March 2023 and building 3 in June 2023.
- Current Ashtrom material presents immediate occupancy; Enav describes the project as occupied.

## Information that remains unpublished

No inspected current official source publishes a current price table, dated apartment inventory, apartment-specific area/floor/orientation schedule, payment terms, current management charges, municipal tax, apartment-specific parking/storage rights or a guaranteed view. Every public article tells the buyer to obtain these fields for the exact unit rather than inventing them.

The historic planning site `Recanati 5-15` is not treated as a verified present entrance list. Current entrance numbers must come from the transaction file.

None of those elements is evidence for RECANATI RESIDENCE and none enters the four articles.

An older mini-site URL redirected to unrelated DHgate/Akamai content during real-Chrome verification. It is treated as unsafe and is excluded from all source and article links.
