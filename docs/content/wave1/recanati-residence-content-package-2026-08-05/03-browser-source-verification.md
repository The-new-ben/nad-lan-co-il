# RECANATI RESIDENCE - real Chrome and direct-source verification

Verification date: 5 August 2026, Asia/Jerusalem

## Browser and scope

The target page, the current official project pages, the official planning PDF and live Google intent endpoints were opened through the user's real Chrome session. This was read-only. No form was submitted and no page, post, code file, media item or URL was changed.

## Verified direct pages

### Existing Nad-Lan page

Opened: https://nad-lan.co.il/projects/recanati-residence/

The page loaded, but its visible content mixes RECANATI RESIDENCE with Neve Avivim, Sde Dov and generic showroom data. The actual content audit is in `existing-page-audit.md`. The page was not treated as factual evidence.

### Current Ashtrom project page

Opened in Hebrew: https://www.ashtrom.co.il/projects/recanati-residence

Opened in English: https://www.ashtrom.co.il/en/projects/recanati-residence

The direct current page and its live HTML established the current Ramat Aviv Gimel identity, 198 apartments, 3 buildings, 16 floors, immediate-occupancy status, 3-5-room mix, mini-penthouses and penthouses, more than 15 metres between buildings, 3 underground parking levels, location beside Beit Tzuri Garden, Canaan Shenhav and Dana Oberson.

The current direct page did not expose a price table or current unit schedule. Stale Google indexing had retained final-apartment wording that was absent from the direct live page. Current inventory was therefore classified as unpublished and volatile.

### Official statutory plan PDF

Opened: https://apps.land.gov.il/IturTabotData/takanonim/telmer/5007929.pdf

Chrome loaded the official 16-page PDF in its native PDF viewer. It is plan TA/3850/MH, ID 5007929. The document is used for the historic Recanati 5-15 planning scope and the 96-to-198 urban-renewal program, not as proof of each building's current postal entrance.

### Ashdar 2023 annual report

Direct filing: https://mayafiles.tase.co.il/rpdf/1580001-1581000/P1580697-00.pdf

Used as the primary regulatory record for the 96 replacement/102 market allocation, current 16-floor reporting convention, three basement levels and 2023 completion history. Dated historic sales remain historical and are never presented as a current price list.

## Live Google language research

Direct Google result pages were rate-limited to `/sorry/` during the final Recanati pass. No organic position, People Also Ask answer or result-count claim was recorded from the blocked pages.

Google's live autocomplete endpoint remained available through script injection inside the real Chrome page. A final 12-seed pass ran at 06:49 Israel time with Israel targeting and English, French, Russian and Arabic `hl` values. The exact accepted suggestions and limitations are preserved in `01-live-google-intent-ledger.md`.
