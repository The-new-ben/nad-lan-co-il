## Mandatory exclusions and corrections

- Do not repeat the existing nad-lan page's statement that Meier is at Shadal Street or that it is also `Shadal Tower`.
- Do not describe the project as combining residences with hotel and offices. The architecture source describes apartments on a retail base.
- Do not use the live page's stored coordinates, zero-unit showroom counters, demo model or generic apartment tour as factual evidence.
- Do not state that 40 floors is verified. The project sources conflict; use the architect-published 42 above-ground floors with the stated caveat.
- Do not state that a current apartment is available unless the exact listing is checked again on publication day and the seller's authority is established.
- Do not present NIS 75 million as the project price. It was a January 2026 asking price for a singular duplex penthouse.
- Do not promise a sea view, permanent open view, quiet, appreciation, yield, rent, liquidity, mortgage approval or tax outcome.
- Do not infer parking, storage, balcony, protected room, wine cellar, private lift or pool access for a particular apartment.
- Do not invent delivery dates, warranties, defects responsibility, management fees, current staffing or amenity hours.

## Required estimate language

If an article offers a buyer model, it must be labelled in the target language as the equivalent of `estimate, not binding`. It must show inputs rather than output a false valuation. Permitted input categories include the exact apartment's dated asking price, verified registered area, verified parking and storage rights, current management invoice, purchase-tax result from S11, lender offer, professional fees, renovation budget, vacancy, rent evidence and exit costs. If those inputs are unavailable, the model remains a checklist and not a number.
