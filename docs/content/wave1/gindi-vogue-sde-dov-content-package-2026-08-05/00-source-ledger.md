## 1. Exact entity lock

### 1.1 Public project identity

The exact project is the Sde Dov development whose official project site uses the title `VOGUE - גינדי שדה דב` and whose visible opening line includes `VOGUE Gindi Sde Dov Tel Aviv`.

Safe naming:

- `VOGUE` when quoting the official brand

- `Gindi Holdings` as the developer brand, when the statement is about the group presentation

Unsafe substitutions:

## 5. Official price-table control

Every future price statement must contain all four elements:

1. source category, such as official developer-site marketing table
2. capture or publication date
3. exact scope, such as room category and `equivalent area`
4. limitation, such as availability not guaranteed and not a binding offer

The current official table does not identify an apartment, building, floor, direction, balcony, parking, storage, contractual area, payment schedule, linkage, total price, or offer validity. It therefore cannot support a project-wide average, an available-apartments table, or a price promise.

The official page also displays a column labelled average price per sqm. It does not expose a calculation method or accepted comparison set in the accessible text. Do not reproduce those averages as independent market evidence.

The site's transaction tables appear to describe surrounding-area transactions. They are not accepted as exact-project transactions because the accessible table does not tie every row to GINDI VOGUE, a lot, a building, or a unit.

Historical media and broker snippets may lead to earlier launch-price reporting, including references to NIS 49,000/sqm. No such number may appear without its original article, publication date, offer scope, and exact attribution. The current nad-lan page's undated NIS 49,000 and NIS 59,000 claims are not accepted.

## 11. Research conclusion

The strongest public description is precise and more complex than the live page: GINDI VOGUE spans two Center Sde Dov lots, with 708 planned homes across nine structures. The approved municipal design schedule includes a 45-floor tower, three 20-floor towers, and five lower buildings. Both lot plans received conditional design approval on 21 January 2026. The project was still actively marketed on 5 August 2026, while the official sales site's own legal text described the presentation as pre-permit.

That distinction controls every future language product. Marketing prices, buyer counts, amenities, views, availability, and timelines must remain dated and attributed. The municipal plans control the project geometry and unit total. Contract documents and current official records must control any apartment-specific buying claim.
