# GINDI VOGUE Sde Dov apartments for sale - prices, plans and buyer checks

GINDI VOGUE Sde Dov is a residential project being marketed in Sde Dov, Tel Aviv, with 708 planned apartments across two planning lots and nine structures. A buyer looking at price, plans or the practical steps for buying should start with the municipal design records, not with a sample apartment or a single address. Those records show one 45-floor tower, three 20-floor towers and five lower buildings. Both design plans were approved subject to conditions on 21 January 2026. As of 5 August 2026, the official project site still described its presentation as taking place before receipt of a building permit. The site displayed a dated marketing price table, but it did not identify a currently available apartment, a total purchase price, a contractual area, a payment schedule or a delivery date. The useful question is therefore not simply "How much is GINDI VOGUE?" It is "Which exact lot, building and apartment is being offered, what documents define it, and what is included in the written price?"

## Overview

### The project is larger than one tower or one lot

The official project site uses the VOGUE brand and the line "VOGUE Gindi Sde Dov Tel Aviv" [S01]. For a buyer searching in English, GINDI VOGUE Sde Dov is the clearest way to keep the project separate from GINDI TLV, other Gindi developments and other projects in the Sde Dov district. The shared word Gindi is not enough to transfer a price, apartment plan, amenity or timetable from one project to another.

The strongest public description comes from two Tel Aviv-Yafo municipal design decisions for lots 2255 and 2206 in the Center Sde Dov plan, TA/4444/1 [S03]. Together, the two schedules contain 708 planned homes. Lot 2255 contains 382 and lot 2206 contains 326. The arithmetic is simple, but its meaning matters: 708 is the combined planning total for two lots. It is not the number of apartments now available, the number covered by one sale contract or a promise that every home is being marketed on the same terms.

The plans contain nine structures. Four are towers: one at 45 floors and three at 20 floors. Five are lower or perimeter buildings at 5, 7, 9, 9 and 9 floors. This municipal schedule controls the project-level geometry. If a current offer uses a different commercial floor convention, the seller should explain it in writing and reconcile it with the permit and sale plan. Until then, the municipal figures are the reliable reference.

Create a route sheet for the actual building. ### Design approval is not a building permit

A design plan organizes buildings, uses, massing, access and other planning matters. Approval subject to conditions means the committee accepted the design plan with conditions and coordination requirements. A building permit is a later legal and technical milestone. Construction start is a physical milestone. Contractual delivery is governed by the sale agreement and its dependencies. These milestones should not be collapsed into a progress bar.

If the seller says the permit position has changed after 5 August 2026, request the permit number, issue date, scope and relevant drawings. Confirm whether it covers the offered building and whether later changes affect the apartment. A news item, site activity or sales representative's statement is not the permit itself.

Registration after handover is another distinct stage.

The public sources do not establish the exact GINDI VOGUE process or timing for these events. They provide the planning and marketing baseline. The contract must provide the transaction-specific sequence.

### A comparison scorecard

Score each candidate apartment on evidence, not on the project name:

| Decision area | Questions to answer |
|---|---|
| Product | Does the plan work, and are every area and attached right defined? |
| Building | Which lot and building, which facilities, which access and which cost allocation? |
| Timing | What permit and delivery clauses govern the apartment? |
| Price | What is the written total, payment schedule, indexation and full acquisition budget? |
| Rights | Who sells, what is registered now and how will the apartment and attachments be registered? |
| Location | Which routes and services exist, and which remain planned? |
| Risk | What happens if approvals, design, public works, finance or delivery differ from expectations? |
| Exit | Who is the likely future user, and what evidence supports rent or resale assumptions? |

The best apartment is not automatically the one in the tallest building or the one with the lowest marketing price per square metre. It is the one whose layout, rights, timing, cost and risk fit the buyer after the documents are read.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is GINDI VOGUE Sde Dov one tower?

No. The municipal design plans cover lots 2255 and 2206 and contain nine structures. They include one 45-floor tower, three 20-floor towers and five lower buildings at 5, 7, 9, 9 and 9 floors [S03].

### How many apartments are planned?

The two municipal schedules total 708 planned homes: 382 on lot 2255 and 326 on lot 2206 [S03]. This is a planning total, not current sale inventory.

### How many floors does the main tower have?

The approved municipal design schedule lists Building 1 on lot 2255 at 45 floors [S03]. Any different commercial convention should be explained and reconciled with the current permit and sale plan.

### What is the project address?

The public municipal records identify two lots and their street boundaries, not one complete-project street number. Obtain the current official address and property identifiers for the exact building and apartment.

### Has a building permit been issued?

No permit issuance was verified in the public material. The design plans were approved subject to conditions on 21 January 2026, while the official project site still described the presentation as pre-permit on 5 August 2026 [S01][S03]. Ask for any later permit directly.

### Has construction started?

A construction-start date was not publicly verified. Design approval and marketing do not prove physical commencement. Request current official evidence for the offered building.

### Is a delivery date published?

No current public delivery date was verified. The contract for the exact apartment should state the delivery obligation, dependencies, delay mechanism and remedies.

### Is the official price table a current offer?

No. It is a developer-site marketing table captured on 5 August 2026. It lists room categories, equivalent areas and prices per square metre, but not an identified unit, total price, contractual area, payment terms or guaranteed availability [S01].

### Can the price per square metre be multiplied by the equivalent area?

The multiplication produces a number, not a verified apartment price. The public page does not define the equivalent-area field as the contractual area or identify the rights and adjustments behind it. Use a written unit offer and contract definitions.

### Are more than 700 apartments sold?

The official site stated more than 700 buyers and 700 apartments already sold on 5 August 2026 [S01]. These are dated marketing claims, not an audited current inventory. Do not subtract them from the 708-home planning total.

### Is the project 250 metres from the sea?

The official site publishes 250 metres from the coastline [S01]. It does not disclose the measurement method or route. The claim does not prove beachfront position, walking time or a sea view from an apartment.

### Does every building share the same pool and gym?

The design plans locate amenities in particular buildings. Lot 2255 places a shared pool and welfare areas on Building 8's roof and a residents' club in Building 1. Lot 2206 places pool, gym, relaxation and sports areas in Building 5 [S03]. Access, final specification, operation and fees require contract verification.

### Are four kindergarten classrooms guaranteed to operate?

The lot 2255 plan includes four kindergarten classrooms in Building 8 [S03]. This is a planned use. It does not guarantee opening, timing, operator or admission.

### Are parking and storage included?

No apartment-specific public schedule was verified. Ask for the numbered spaces, dimensions, access, plan and legal treatment in the sale and rights documents.

### Is a sea view guaranteed?

No. A view depends on the exact building, floor, direction, opening and present and future obstructions. Obtain unzoomed images, a continuous video, a north-marked plan and surrounding planning information.

### Who is the developer?

The professional project page names Gindi Holdings as client. The municipal design plans name Gindi Israel 2010 as developer or applicant [S03][S04]. The current legal seller for an apartment must be identified in its contract and rights documents.

### Who is the architect?

The municipal records name Kika Braz as lead architect, Baha Milhem as landscape architect and Amy Metom as traffic consultant [S03]. The Kika Braz page identifies VOGUE as project 669 [S04].

### Can a foreign buyer complete the process remotely?

Some preparation may be possible remotely, but no project-specific remote-signing or foreign-buyer service was publicly verified. Use independent Israeli legal advice, confirm identity and payments and check which steps require physical presence or formally accepted documents.

### What should be checked before paying a reservation amount?

Confirm the exact unit, seller, refund conditions, price, validity, payment account, planning and permit status, rights, security, specification, attached rights and contract path. Do not pay solely because a registration form or marketing table appears online.

### What information remains unpublished?

Current binding inventory, complete total prices and payment terms, a permit issue date, construction start, delivery, seller and rights for each apartment, bank accompaniment, executing contractor, management fees, parking, storage and apartment-specific views were not publicly verified. Request them for the exact offer.
