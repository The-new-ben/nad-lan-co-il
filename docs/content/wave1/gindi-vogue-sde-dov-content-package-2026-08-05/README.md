## Locked project boundary

GINDI VOGUE Sde Dov spans lots 2255 and 2206 under Center Sde Dov plan TA/4444/1. The municipal design schedules contain 708 planned homes across nine structures. They identify four towers and five lower buildings with a floor schedule of 45, 20, 20, 20, 5, 7, 9, 9 and 9 floors.

Both lot design plans were approved subject to conditions on 21 January 2026. That planning milestone is not a building permit. The official project site still described its presentation as pre-permit when captured on 5 August 2026.

The official site's buyer-count, coast-distance and price-table statements are dated marketing claims with explicit limitations. They do not establish current inventory, a binding offer, contractual area, a total apartment price, payment terms, parking, storage, a view, management costs, a construction-start date or a delivery date.
