# ZOHI Sde Dov - live Google Chrome intent ledger

Research date: 5 August 2026, Israel time  
Scope: content research for English, French, Russian and Arabic buyer pages  
Browser: the user's real Chrome session  
Location controls: `gl=il`, `pws=0`, with `hl=en`, `hl=fr`, `hl=ru` or `hl=ar`; Russian and selected Arabic checks also used the matching `lr` language filter

## What this ledger proves

This file records search wording and visible intent surfaces. It does not turn a Google result, snippet, related search, People Also Ask question or AI summary into a project fact. Project facts come only from `00-source-ledger.md` and the directly verified sources listed there.

The observations are a dated qualitative sample, not search-volume, ranking or keyword-difficulty data. Result composition can vary by time, device, account state and Google experiment. The useful evidence is the language buyers use and the questions the result page tries to answer.

## Cross-language decisions

- Keep `ZOHI Sde Dov` in Latin at the start of every Title and H1. Google attempted to correct the English exact-project query to ZOHO, so the brand may not be varied.
- Keep Tel Aviv immediately visible and keep the page transaction-led rather than turning it into a general district guide.
- Resolve current price status in the opening. The title promises coverage of price questions, not the existence of a public price list.
- Explain view, coast and distance as separate checks. No search phrase proves a project location, measured route or apartment view.
- Separate current official facts, dated planning or launch reporting and unknowns. Search language never changes the confidence level of a fact.
- English prioritizes relocation and overseas coordination; French prioritizes a controlled remote dossier; Russian prioritizes price classes, costs and liquidity inputs; Arabic prioritizes family use, privacy, education and daily access. These are reader paths, not assumptions about identity.
- Use the same five factual source URLs in the same order in all four Sources chapters.

## Content acceptance gate

The Title, H1 and first paragraph must let a reader identify ZOHI, Sde Dov, Tel Aviv, apartment-sale intent, current price status and the practical buying question without opening another section. Every secondary phrase must occur only where it helps answer a real buyer question. Repetition, borrowed competitor claims, unsourced superlatives and machine-like cultural stereotypes fail the gate.
