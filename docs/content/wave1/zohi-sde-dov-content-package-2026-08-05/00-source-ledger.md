## Decision summary

The four language articles are built from a smaller, stricter fact set than the current Hebrew page. The current page contains real project information, but it also mixes current first-party facts, dated launch reporting, a superseded planning configuration, generic showroom demo data and unverified estimates. Those categories cannot be treated as one evidence layer.

The locked current facts are: ZOHI is a residential project in Sde Dov, Tel Aviv; the official site publishes 230 apartments, a current mix of 2 to 6 rooms plus penthouses, 3 metre ceilings, a private urban garden of about 2.8 dunams, shared workspaces, a gym, yoga area and residents' lounge. The official project architecture is credited to Galor Fishbein Architects, and the interior-design material is credited to Tal Goldschmidt Fish. Levinstein's current project-stage archive lists ZOHI as "בשיווק", meaning marketed or on sale.

The current public first-party material inspected in Chrome does not publish a current apartment price list, current unit availability, a contractual delivery date, a verified construction-permit status, or a unit schedule. Those items remain unpublished or unverified. The language articles must say so plainly when the topic arises.

## Evidence classes

Every claim receives one of these labels before it can enter an article:

| Label | Meaning | Publication rule |
|---|---|---|
| LOCKED CURRENT | Directly supported by a current first-party project or developer source | May be stated as a current published project fact, while distinguishing marketing material from a sales contract |
| LOCKED PLANNING CONTEXT | Supported by an official statutory source or an attributable report about a planning lot | May be used for location and planning context, never as a postal address or permit certificate |
| DATED REPORT | Supported by an attributable publication with a clear publication date | Must carry its date and attribution; may not be converted into a current offer or guarantee |
| DISCOVERY ONLY | Found in Google results, a snippet, a secondary portal or an inaccessible document | May lead to a source check; may not support a final factual sentence by itself |
| REJECTED | Contradicted, unsupported, stale, synthetic, or liable to mislead a buyer | Must not appear as fact in any language |
| NOT PUBLISHED | No current source inspected publishes the value | Say that it has not been published or independently verified; do not fill the gap |

## Exact source precedence

Use sources in this order whenever two sources conflict:

1. A current, directly inspected statutory, municipal, Israel Land Authority, permit or registration document that identifies the exact project or planning lot.
2. A current official company filing that identifies the project and the precise claim.
3. The current official ZOHI project site and the current Levinstein project-stage archive.
4. Attributable news reporting with author, date and direct article URL.
5. Specialist district sites, marketplaces and marketing portals, used only for leads or clearly attributed context.
6. Google result snippets, autocomplete and People Also Ask, used for discovery and intent research only.

Recency does not automatically beat authority. A current official marketing page is strongest for the product currently being marketed, such as the apartment mix. A statutory document is strongest for a plan. A dated news article can report a launch term, but it cannot establish that the same term remains available today.

## Conflict resolutions

### Four buildings versus three buildings

The 2024 Globes planning report described four buildings, including two 16-storey and two 9-storey buildings. The January 2026 Mako launch report described three buildings, including two 15-storey towers and one 7-storey building above commerce. This indicates an evolved design or a different counting/configuration at launch. The official microsite text inspected in August 2026 uses generic terms such as textured construction, towers and smaller tower volumes but publishes no numerical count.

Resolution: do not state a current official building count. If the 2026 configuration is useful, introduce it as the configuration reported at the January 2026 launch. Do not combine elements from both versions.

### 2 to 6 rooms versus studios to 5.5 rooms

The current official site publishes 2 to 6 rooms plus penthouses. The January 2026 report described studios through 5.5 rooms plus penthouses.

Resolution: the current official mix controls. The older launch mix may be discussed only as dated reporting and should normally be omitted to avoid confusing buyers.

### Garden size of 2.8 versus about 3 dunams

The official site publishes about 2.8 dunams. The launch report rounded the internal garden to about 3 dunams.

Resolution: use about 2.8 dunams for current product text. The values are not materially inconsistent, but one exact normalization prevents drift across languages.

### Tender payment of NIS 634 million versus NIS 659.5 million

The January 2026 report described about NIS 634 million paid for land. The August 2024 report described about NIS 659.5 million including development costs.

Resolution: these figures use different cost definitions. Do not collapse them into one number. They are not necessary for the buyer article. If used as historical context, state the definition attached to each source.

### Allied versus Metropolis

Historical reporting names Allied, Levinstein and Mivne as the lot consortium. The official project site presents Levinstein, Metropolis and Mivne and explains that Metropolis leads Allied Real Estate's residential arm.

Resolution: describe both roles accurately. Do not present Allied and Metropolis as unrelated fourth and fifth partners, and do not call Metropolis merely an outside marketing agency without a current source for that narrow role.

## Mandatory wording rules for the four articles

1. The brand remains `ZOHI` in Latin letters in every language.
2. The title and H1 may lead with a culturally researched query, but the city must be Tel Aviv and the project identity must remain unmistakable.
3. Facts and certainty levels remain identical across English, French, Russian and Arabic.
4. A dated report must keep its date in every language. It must not become present tense through stylistic localization.
5. The standard current-price sentence is conceptually: current apartment prices were not published or independently verified in the current first-party sources inspected on 5 August 2026. Native phrasing may vary.
6. Any editorial market estimate created later must be labelled with the exact local equivalent of `estimate, non-binding` and must disclose its method. No such estimate is authorized by this ledger.
7. Lot 110 is always a planning lot, never a street address.
8. `בשיווק` is translated as a current marketed/on-sale category. It does not mean units are available.
9. Amenities are described as published or planned project amenities, with contractual verification reserved for the sale specification. This is buyer guidance, not defensive boilerplate.
10. No language may add a school, guaranteed sea view, immigration benefit, return, rental yield, financing approval, tax outcome, safety promise or delivery guarantee merely because that angle has local search demand.
11. The source chapter uses the same factual sources and the same order in every language. Anchor text is localized, URLs are unchanged.
12. The independent-site statement appears in native language, but it must not replace the opening project overview.
