### How to verify the exact position

Before comparing the location, obtain a plan that identifies the project parcel, every proposed building and the north direction. The sales document should identify the selected apartment within that plan. With that information, a buyer can measure practical routes and understand what may stand between the apartment and the places that matter.

Check four types of distance separately:

1. straight-line distance, which may help with orientation but says nothing about access;
2. walking distance from the actual entrance along open public routes;
3. driving distance under the current street network;
4. public-transport travel using services that operate on the date of the check.

Do not start a measurement from the centre of Sde Dov or from a marketing pin with no parcel reference. A difference of one block can change the route to a school, shops, the coast or a main road. A future street plan may also differ from the route available while the district develops.

For broader city context, the site's [Tel Aviv-Yafo page](https://nad-lan.co.il/cities/%D7%AA%D7%9C-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%95/) can help a buyer compare areas and project choices. ZOHI still requires its own parcel-level check. City-level descriptions should never be used as proof of a project amenity, school allocation, transit time or unit view.

### Daily life for a resident or relocating household

A relocation decision needs two maps. The first should show what operates today: shops, healthcare, schools, childcare, public transport, cycle routes and road access. The second should show facilities that are planned but not yet operating. Mixing those maps can make a future district look complete before the household arrives.

Visit the area at more than one time of day. Observe current access, noise, wind, sun exposure, active works in the wider district and how long basic errands take. A visit also helps test whether the route from the project position to established parts of north Tel Aviv suits a household's routine.

Families should not assume that a district plan assigns a child to a particular school or guarantees the opening date of a facility. Ask the municipality about current registration rules when the move becomes relevant. If the apartment is being bought years before occupation, record which decisions depend on future services and which still work with today's services.

An overseas buyer can conduct part of this work remotely. Request a dated, continuous video from the project position along the routes that matter. Ask the person filming to identify the starting point on a map and avoid edited clips that jump between locations. Remote footage is useful for orientation, but it cannot replace current planning documents or a local inspection of a selected apartment's setting.

### Direction, light and future outlook

To evaluate light and outlook, ask for the apartment plan with a north arrow and a plan of the full development. Record the direction of each main window and balcony. Then identify existing and planned buildings along those lines. The relevant questions concern separation distance, height, roof equipment, roads, commercial uses and future construction.

A higher floor does not automatically produce an open view. A west-facing apartment does not automatically face the sea. The building could sit behind another structure, or a future plan could change the outlook. Because the current first-party text does not publish a building or unit schedule, no ZOHI apartment should be described as having a sea view without a current apartment-specific document.

Sun and heat should be reviewed alongside view. Afternoon exposure, balcony depth, glass area and shading can affect comfort and energy use. Wind can also feel different at different heights. These are design questions for a specific unit, not claims that can be resolved from the project name.

### Price evidence is not interchangeable

Keep four categories separate:

1. a current first-party project price, which must come from a dated official offer or current project publication;
2. a private asking price, which belongs to a particular listing and may concern an assignment or resale;
3. a completed transaction price, which belongs to a recorded sale with its own date and property details;
4. an estimate, which requires stated inputs, method, date and limitations.

ZOHI currently has no public first-party unit price in the available official sources. A January 2026 media range remains historical. The available data does not support an independent estimate because the required unit information and a transparent comparable set are missing. Creating a precise-looking figure would hide rather than solve the evidence gap.

Price per square metre is only useful when the denominator is comparable. Ask whether the quoted area is internal, contractual, sale area or another calculation. Determine how walls, balconies and common portions are treated. A penthouse with a large terrace cannot be compared mechanically with a compact apartment merely because both have a price-per-metre figure.

### Building the full acquisition budget

The apartment price is one line in the purchase budget. A buyer may also need to allow for:

- purchase tax based on the buyer's actual status and transaction date;
- legal review and any translation or notarisation required for the buyer's process;
- mortgage valuation, bank fees and insurance if finance is used;
- currency conversion and transfer costs when funds are held outside Israel;
- contractual indexation if it appears in the signed agreement;
- specification upgrades and apartment changes;
- technical inspection and handover support;
- furniture, lighting and appliances;
- municipal charges, insurance and common expenses after handover;
- ongoing management if the owner will not live in Israel.

Purchase tax is not determined by the language a buyer speaks or by an assumption that an English-speaking buyer lives abroad. It depends on the law in force, the nature and value of the right, and the buyer's facts. The [purchase tax calculator](https://nad-lan.co.il/purchase-tax-calculator/) can help build an initial scenario, but it should be checked against current official rules and professional advice for the actual transaction.

If finance is needed, the [mortgage calculator](https://nad-lan.co.il/mortgage-calculator/) can test monthly payments under different amounts, terms and rates. It is a planning tool, not a bank offer. A lender will assess the buyer, source of funds, income, property, valuation and documents. Approval for one buyer or project cannot be inferred from a general financing limit.

For an overseas buyer, model the shekel obligation at every payment date. A single currency conversion made at the start can be misleading if payments are spread over years. Add transfer spreads and a buffer. If the contract includes indexation, keep that exposure separate from exchange-rate movement and mortgage interest.

Do not rank two apartments using one headline metric. A smaller apartment may have a higher price per square metre but a lower total cost. A lower price may exclude a right included elsewhere. ### Investment analysis without a return promise

An investment decision needs inputs that are not in the current public ZOHI material: the exact purchase price, expected rent for a genuinely comparable apartment, vacancy, common expenses, municipal tax, insurance, maintenance, management, finance, furnishing and transaction costs. Without those inputs, a yield cannot be calculated responsibly.

Interest in the wider Sde Dov district does not prove rental demand for a particular ZOHI plan. The project total of 230 apartments does not prove resale liquidity. A garden or shared workspace may be valued by future occupants, but the market effect cannot be converted into a number without evidence.

For a holding analysis, build at least a conservative case. Use a rent supported by comparable completed units, include periods with no tenant, and deduct recurring costs. Test what happens if completion takes longer than expected or the exchange rate moves against the buyer. Treat resale timing and price as unknown rather than as an automatic exit.

A buyer who plans to live in the apartment should separate financial return from personal value. Layout, location, light, outdoor space and proximity to family may justify a choice without producing a measurable investment gain. Combining those motives into one forecast can make a subjective preference look like market evidence.

Also pause if the purchase only works with a promised sea view, a future rent, a low management fee or maximum mortgage financing. None of those outcomes is established by the current published project material. A stronger document package is more useful than another broad estimate.
