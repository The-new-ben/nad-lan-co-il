## Overview

### What YOO Tel Aviv is today

YOO Tel Aviv is an existing residential complex in Park Tzameret. The physical towers are complete, homes have been occupied for years, and the decision is about a particular finished apartment. This distinction changes almost every buyer question. There is no common delivery date to await, no single developer specification that proves the current condition of every interior, and no authoritative project-wide price that can be applied to whatever apartment appears in an advertisement.

The original development was associated with the Habas Group and the YOO design brand, identified by the official portfolio as YOO Inspired by Starck. ### Resale evidence replaces launch material

A launch brochure can explain the original concept. It cannot prove the condition or rights of a resale apartment years later. The seller's current documents must answer who owns the property, what is registered, which parking or storage rights are included, whether alterations were approved, whether the apartment is occupied, and when possession can be delivered.

This is why a polished listing is only the start of the investigation. Its photographs may be current, old or digitally enhanced. The area may follow a marketing convention that differs from the registered or approved plan. The room count may not tell an international buyer how many bedrooms there are. A high-floor label says little about direction, heat, wind, noise, privacy or the future outlook unless the exact home is identified.

If the real objective is a home that is still being planned or built, compare the different contract and delivery questions through the [new-project buyer guide](https://nad-lan.co.il/new-projects/). YOO Tel Aviv belongs to a different category. Its advantages and risks come from inspecting an existing asset, checking its current rights and understanding the ongoing cost of a mature tower.

### The first apartment pack to request

Before arranging an expensive due-diligence process, ask the seller or broker for a dated apartment pack. A useful first pack should identify:

1. The current seller and the basis of the seller's authority.
2. The building address, apartment identifier and applicable subunit or rights-record reference.
3. The floor used in the current legal, approved-plan and management records.
4. The approved plan and any documented later changes.
5. Every published area figure and the measurement convention behind it.
6. The room layout, number of actual bedrooms and current use of each room.
7. The asking price in NIS and a written list of included items.
8. The legal and practical status of each claimed parking space and storage area.
9. The latest management invoice, known arrears, current budget and any notified special expenditure.
10. Occupancy, tenancy, possession terms and the proposed transaction timetable.

This pack does not complete the checks. It determines whether the offer is concrete enough to justify legal review, physical inspection, planning review, valuation work and banking preparation. If the seller cannot identify the apartment or show the basis of the rights being sold, the tower name is not a substitute.

### Project facts and apartment facts must stay separate

Two towers, a historical design collaboration and an original facilities program belong to the complex-level story. The apartment decision has a narrower evidence base. It asks whether the offered unit retains an original design direction, whether its layout has changed, whether the changes were approved, what the windows face, what is included in the price and what the buyer will pay each month.

The same separation protects both buyer and seller. A buyer should not assume that one old dispute describes the entire complex. A seller should not use general project prestige to avoid answering unit-level questions. A sound transaction connects current records, the apartment as inspected and the words of the proposed contract.

The word central does not promise a quiet bedroom. A high floor does not remove every road, mechanical or wind effect. ### Roads, arrival and daily movement

Test the complete route, not just the drive past the towers. Arrive by the transport modes you expect to use. Observe entry from Nissim Aloni, vehicle queuing, guest access, deliveries, bicycle storage and the journey from an underground parking level to the apartment. If a household member needs a step-free path, inspect that path from pavement or parking space through every door and lift.

Traffic conditions can change by hour. A short map distance can become a longer practical journey at peak time. The same is true for a pedestrian route exposed to crossings, heat or construction. No fixed travel time is published here because an honest time depends on entrance, destination, date and mode.

For relocation, build a weekly route map before choosing a unit. Include work, school or childcare if relevant, medical care, groceries, exercise, social visits and airport travel. A central tower can still be the wrong home if the repeated routes do not fit. It can also be a strong fit for someone whose essential journeys align with the district.

NTA's official M1 material includes planned stations named Namir-Pinkas and Tel Aviv Center. The word planned is essential. The source does not justify describing the metro as operational, assigning a guaranteed opening date or publishing a walking time from a YOO entrance.

A buyer can consider planned transport as part of the wider planning context, but should value the apartment on the access that exists and can be tested now. Future infrastructure may change convenience and construction conditions. It should not be converted automatically into a price premium, rent forecast or promised appreciation.

### Education and household services

A Tel Aviv municipal update documented expansion of the Tzamarot Ayalon school for the 2024-2025 school year. That dated record shows education planning in the area. It does not prove the current catchment, admission rules, capacity, teaching language or eligibility of a particular child.

Households should verify the current municipal assignment and the exact institution directly. Then test the route at the relevant hour. A school name nearby is not enough if admission is uncertain or the daily journey is impractical. Buyers without children can apply the same method to any service that matters to them, including health care, shopping, recreation or support for an older family member.

### View, light and privacy

No project-wide sea view or open skyline promise is supported. The towers' height and circular form can create varied orientations, but the result belongs to a specific window and floor. Ask for the precise direction of each main room. Stand at normal eye level in the living area and bedrooms. Look down as well as out. Check nearby towers, roads, roof equipment and future planning material.

Light and heat should be evaluated together. Broad glazing may bring daylight and also increase glare or cooling needs in a particular orientation. Visit when the relevant facade receives sun. Ask for energy bills if the seller is willing to provide them. Inspect shades, seals and glass condition. A sunset photograph is not a year-round comfort study.

Privacy changes after dark. Interior lighting can expose a room to neighboring windows even when the daytime view feels open. Test curtains or shading and look at the angles between buildings. These practical details can matter more than the marketing label attached to the floor.

For a purchase, the exact apartment must be located across the current documents. The public-company report described an original project of up to 297 apartments. That is not the same as an exact current registered total. The reviewed public evidence does not establish any different round number as a settled current count.

### Circular facades and documented materials

The original filing describes granite, aluminum and glass on the exterior. Circular geometry can shape rooms and views. It can also create curved perimeter conditions, changing furniture placement, wall lengths and window angles. Measure where essential furniture will sit. ### Flexible layouts and the four design directions

Flexible interior planning was part of the original concept, supported by a living-space approach with fewer internal columns. The original material named four interior design directions: Classic, Nature, Culture and Minimal. These names belong to the initial branded concept.

A resale apartment may preserve one of those directions, may have been substantially renovated, or may combine elements from different periods. Do not describe a current interior as an original Philippe Starck apartment merely because it stands in YOO Towers. Ask for old plans, renovation records, invoices, approvals and dated photographs where provenance affects the price.

Design authorship also needs careful wording. The official portfolio uses YOO Inspired by Starck. That supports the collaboration and brand association. ### Room count and area for an international buyer

An Israeli listing's room count should not be converted automatically into a bedroom count used in another country. The reported 2023 transaction concerned a 3.5-room apartment. That does not mean 3.5 bedrooms. Ask the seller to identify the living room, full bedrooms, half room or smaller space, kitchen arrangement, bathrooms, storage and balconies on the approved plan.

Area also needs a denominator. A listing may display one number while the rights record, approved plan, municipal account, appraisal or physical measurement uses another. Determine whether each figure includes walls, balconies, common shares, storage or another component. Do not calculate price per square metre until the price and area definitions are written beside each other.

Measure function as well as area. Check bedroom dimensions, circulation, storage, kitchen work surfaces, dining space, home-office options and accessibility. A relocation buyer should test whether existing furniture fits. An investor should test whether the layout matches the intended long-term tenant without assuming a guaranteed rent.

### Parking and storage

Three underground parking levels are documented at project level. That fact does not give every apartment a fixed number of spaces. For the offered unit, obtain the legal or contractual basis of every parking and storage claim. Record identifiers and inspect the spaces. Check access, dimensions, turning radius, charging arrangements if relevant, lift route, security and practical usability. A parking space can have value and still be awkward for the buyer's vehicle.

Storage deserves the same care. Confirm its identifier, location, dimensions, ventilation and physical condition. Decide whether it is a registered attachment, a contractual allocation or another form of use. The sale contract should describe exactly what is transferred.

### Alterations and the apartment as built

Commission a technical inspection for the exact apartment. The inspector should state what was accessible, what was tested and what remains outside the inspection. ### What to test during a physical visit

Walk slowly. Test windows, sliding doors, shading, plumbing fixtures, water pressure, drainage, electricity, air conditioning and ventilation. Look for moisture, cracking, repaired areas, uneven floors and signs of movement. Listen for lifts, pumps, generators, road traffic, neighbors and building services.

Trace the arrival sequence from street and parking to the apartment. Note every access control, lift change, narrow turn or service corridor. Inspect the rubbish route, delivery arrangements and renovation rules. Ask when contractors can work and how materials move through the building.

Visit at a second time if the price depends on light, quiet or view. Record the actual condition in dated photographs or video with the seller's permission. Compare the final inspection with the contract's list of included fixtures and with the condition at handover.

### How to build a useful comparable set

Start with completed transactions that can be matched to the correct address and apartment as closely as the records allow. The government real-estate information database supports searches by address, block and parcel, neighborhood, text and map. Its records still need interpretation. A nearby transaction is not automatically comparable. Then narrow the set by date, real building position, area definition, layout, orientation, view, balcony, parking, storage, renovation, occupancy and possession terms.

Do not mix a compact apartment, a combined unit and a large upper-floor home merely because they share the YOO name. Total price affects the buyer pool. A rare layout may take longer to sell even if its price per square metre appears attractive. A renovated interior can carry value for one buyer and a replacement cost for another.

Ask the seller or broker to explain the asking price in writing. Which completed transactions were used? How were areas normalized? What adjustment was made for date, condition, floor and included rights? If the explanation relies on a sea view, photograph and map the actual view from the relevant rooms and review nearby planning.

### Price per square metre needs one area definition

Price per square metre can look objective while combining incompatible inputs. If the price includes parking and storage but the denominator is only an apartment area, the ratio contains more than the interior. If one area includes walls or balconies and another does not, the comparison creates false precision.

Build a table with a source column for every number. Keep registered area, approved-plan area, advertised area and measured usable area separate. Do not choose the largest or smallest figure merely because it makes the price look better. Explain which one is used and why.

A valuation professional can help select and adjust comparables. The buyer still needs the source documents. An opinion based on an incorrect unit identifier or area convention will not become reliable because it is presented in a polished report.

### Buying with income or capital in another currency

The transaction is discussed in NIS. An overseas buyer may hold funds or earn income in another currency, but no default conversion is shown here. A dollar, pound or euro figure becomes stale as rates change and can distract from the seller's shekel price.

Map every expected payment by date. For each one, record the NIS amount, source currency, conversion route, quoted fees, bank documentation and safety margin. Ask the bank what source-of-funds material it will require before the contractual date becomes urgent.

Currency exposure continues after purchase if income and building costs use different currencies. Model management, repairs, tax, insurance and financing in the currencies actually paid. Do not treat a favorable exchange rate on the viewing day as a guaranteed purchase rate.

### Rent and investment analysis without a promised yield

No verified current rent, occupancy rate or net yield for a specific offered YOO apartment was established. An investor should begin with rent evidence for a genuinely comparable home and then subtract vacancy, management, maintenance, furnishing, insurance, tax, finance, currency and future sale costs.

The gross ratio is only a first line. The relevant result is the cash flow after realistic costs and downtime. A high acquisition price can limit yield even where monthly rent is substantial. A large or unusual apartment can also have a narrower tenant and buyer pool.

Test more than one scenario. Use a base rent supported by evidence, a lower rent, a vacancy period, a major repair and a slower sale. If the model works only when the apartment is continuously occupied and sold quickly at a higher price, the assumptions carry much of the investment case.

YOO's brand and location may interest some tenants or future buyers. They do not create guaranteed rent, appreciation or liquidity. The investment decision remains specific to the acquired home and its total cost.

### Negotiating from evidence

A defensible offer connects the apartment to completed comparables, the inspection, its approved and registered area, current costs and required work. It can recognize strong features without treating the seller's description as proof.

Keep commercial and legal questions separate. A lower price does not cure unclear rights, unapproved alterations or an uncertain parking claim. Resolve those issues through documents and contract terms rather than assuming a discount makes them acceptable.

Time can be part of the negotiation. A seller's possession needs, an existing tenancy, bank release process or document delay may affect the proposed schedule. Make sure the timetable is workable for an overseas buyer's banking, signatures, inspection and funds transfer before signing a binding commitment.

9. Negotiate a contract and payment schedule that reflects the verified facts and unresolved items.
10. Complete a final inspection, possession checklist, key transfer and required registration steps.

Do not let a booking form, reservation request or short memorandum run ahead of the checks merely because the apartment appears attractive. Ask the buyer's advisers what is binding before signing or transferring money.

### Remote transaction planning

A purchase can involve overseas documents and representation, but there is no one remote process for every buyer. Country, document form, bank, tax profile, seller and type of right can change the sequence. An independent Israeli lawyer should explain what must be signed, verified, translated or authenticated for the actual transaction.

Remote viewing should have clear instructions. Ask for a continuous dated video of the route from street or parking to the home, every room, window view, service area, parking and storage. Commission an independent physical inspection. Name a representative for the final walkthrough and possession if the buyer cannot attend.

Begin banking early. Confirm the receiving account, transfer path, source-of-funds evidence, currency conversion and timing before a contract creates fixed deadlines. A buyer who has sufficient wealth can still miss a payment date if the operational path was not prepared.

## Who it suits

### A relocation buyer

YOO Tel Aviv may suit a relocation buyer who wants an existing apartment that can be inspected before moving. The buyer can test the real commute, room layout, light, noise and building operation rather than wait for a planned home to be completed.

The fit depends on timing. Confirm when the apartment will be vacant, whether furniture is included, what work is required and whether building rules affect renovation or moving. Build a schedule for legal checks, banking, possession, furnishing and the household's move. A completed tower does not guarantee that a specific home is ready on the buyer's date.

Relocation also magnifies the need to understand room conventions. Ask for actual bedroom dimensions, storage, kitchen function, work space and access. A room count written for the Israeli market may not match expectations formed in another country.

### An own-use buyer in Tel Aviv

An own-use buyer may value the tower setting, known design identity and ability to inspect a mature building. The decision should still begin inside the apartment. Test the plan, privacy, window condition, mechanical noise, parking route and monthly cost.

Visit the surroundings during the hours that matter. Park Tzameret is a high-rise district near major road corridors, not a quiet coastal lane. Some buyers will value that urban connection. Others will decide that traffic exposure, tower management or the daily route does not suit them.

### A second-home or pied-a-terre buyer

A second-home buyer needs an operating plan for periods of absence. Ask what building staff may and may not do inside a private apartment. Confirm emergency access, leak response, deliveries, contractor access, insurance expectations and the handling of utilities and mail.

A managed tower does not automatically include private apartment management. If a separate service is needed, obtain its scope and cost. Inspect systems before each long absence and decide who will respond if management reports a problem.

### A buyer planning for family use

Family suitability is not proved by a large area or a pool. It comes from the exact plan, privacy, storage, bedroom separation, bathrooms, lift route, parking, outdoor access and the household's daily destinations. Measure the rooms and walk the real school, childcare or service routes.

The municipal education update is useful context but not an admission promise. Confirm the current catchment and requirements for the particular household. Do not assume language support or eligibility from the project's location.

### An investor

An investor may consider YOO Tel Aviv as a completed branded-tower asset. The analysis must begin with the purchase price of a real unit, verified rent evidence, current management costs, vacancy, maintenance, tax, financing, currency and exit costs.

The target tenant and future buyer depend on layout and total price. A highly individual renovation can narrow the market. A large unit can require a tenant with a substantial budget. A smaller apartment may have a different cost and demand profile. No project-wide yield or resale period was verified.

The investment case should survive weaker assumptions. Model lower rent, longer vacancy, a repair, higher operating costs and a slower exit. Do not count planned transport as guaranteed appreciation. Do not count a view that has not been verified from the home.

### A buyer for whom it may not fit

YOO may not suit someone who wants a current developer inventory, a fixed project price, a future delivery date or a standard new-build package. It may also be a poor fit for a buyer who needs very low common costs, will not review alterations, or expects a high floor to guarantee quiet and a permanent sea view.

The project can be appealing and still be the wrong purchase. Walking away after finding unclear rights, impractical layout, excessive costs or unapproved work is a valid result of due diligence.

### Decision grid

| Intended use | Evidence that matters most | Common assumption to avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Relocation home | Possession date, condition, actual bedrooms, routes, banking and move schedule | Completed building means immediate move-in |
| Primary home | Layout, light, noise, privacy, parking, storage and monthly cost | Brand name proves daily comfort |
| Second home | Absence management, emergency response, insurance, access and private-unit service | Building staff manage the apartment automatically |
| Family use | Usable plan, storage, lifts, parking and verified education route | Room count or pool proves family suitability |
| Rental investment | Purchase cost, verified rent, vacancy, fees, repairs, tax and exit | Prestige guarantees yield or liquidity |

### When to pause

Pause if the seller will not provide a current rights record or cannot reconcile the apartment identifier across documents. Pause if advertised parking or storage is absent from the legal or contractual material. Pause if the apartment differs from the approved plan and no satisfactory evidence explains the change.

Pause if the purchase depends on an assumed view, quiet, school admission, metro date, tax status, mortgage approval or management fee. Pause if the payment timetable is too short for the buyer's bank and source-of-funds review. The right response to missing evidence is more verification or a different apartment, not a stronger adjective.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is YOO Tel Aviv a new development?

No. YOO Tel Aviv is completed and occupied. The official YOO portfolio marks the original project Sold. A current purchase normally concerns a resale apartment offered by its present owner or another current rights holder, not a home in an active developer launch.

### Are apartments for sale in YOO Tel Aviv right now?

Individual resale listings can appear, but no complete authoritative current inventory was found as of 5 August 2026. Confirm that a specific apartment is still available, identify the seller and obtain a dated written offer before treating an advertisement as live inventory.

### What is the current price of an apartment in YOO Towers?

No authoritative current project-wide price list or defensible current range was found. Price depends on the exact unit, area definition, floor, orientation, view, condition, parking, storage, occupancy and possession terms. Ask for the current asking price and compare it with properly matched completed transactions.

### What completed transaction is publicly documented?

Globes reported on 3 June 2023 that a 3.5-room, 123-square-metre apartment on floor 12 of 37 at Nissim Aloni 21 sold for NIS 6.65 million, with three underground parking spaces and storage reported for that unit. It is one dated sale, not a current project price or proof of rights for another home.

### How many towers are in YOO Tel Aviv?

The historical public-company report and official portfolio describe two residential towers in Park Tzameret. A buyer should still identify which building and entrance contain the apartment being offered.

### How many floors do the towers have?

The original public-company filing described 41 and 37 floors above ground. Alum Eshet's project record uses 35 and 39, while a 2023 municipal document describes the building it calls YOO 2 at Nissim Aloni 19 as 40 floors. The sources count differently. Verify the offered apartment's floor in current records.

### How many apartments are there?

The original plan allowed up to 297 apartments. That is not an exact current registered total. A municipal record for one building and evidence of unit combinations show why original, approved and existing counts can differ. The current total and the number for sale were not verified.

### Which address is YOO 1 and which is YOO 2?

Publicly evidenced complex addresses include Nissim Aloni 19 and 21. A municipal document maps YOO 2 to Nissim Aloni 19, but the full current mapping is not treated as settled here because secondary references are inconsistent. Use the current legal and building records for the offered apartment.

### Did Philippe Starck design every apartment?

The official portfolio identifies the project as YOO Inspired by Starck. That supports the branded design collaboration. It does not prove that Starck personally designed every interior or that a resale apartment retains an original design package after later renovations.

### What were the original design concepts?

The historical filing named Classic, Nature, Culture and Minimal as four original interior directions. A seller should show evidence if an asking price relies on one of those original packages. Inspect the current interior and verify later work.

### Does every apartment have a sea view?

No project-wide sea-view fact was verified. View depends on the exact floor, direction, window, nearby buildings and future planning. Inspect the offered apartment and record what is visible from its main rooms. Do not use a photograph from another unit.

### Does every apartment include parking and storage?

No. The project had three underground parking levels, but the rights of each apartment are specific. The 2023 reported sale included three parking spaces and storage for that unit only. Check identifiers, documents and physical spaces for the apartment being bought.

### Are the pool, spa and gym operating now?

The original program documented a pool, spa and gym or club, and a 2023 report referred to a pool, club and gym. Current operation, hours, rules, condition and access were not verified. Ask building management for current written information and inspect the facilities.

### How much are the management fees?

No reliable current project-wide schedule was found. Request the latest invoice for the exact apartment, the allocation basis, annual budget, arrears, reserve information and any planned special expenditure. Do not use a fee quoted for another unit.

### How should I check renovations or combined units?

Compare the apartment as built with the approved plan and current rights record. Request permits and professional documentation for later changes. Commission an inspection. A 2026 report about one apartment illustrates the issue but is not a project-wide finding.

### What does a 3.5-room apartment mean?

Do not translate it directly into a bedroom count used in another country. Ask for the approved plan and a plain description of the living room, full bedrooms, smaller space, bathrooms, kitchen, storage and balconies. Measure the usable layout.

### Can I buy from abroad?

Build the process with an independent Israeli lawyer, the proposed bank and qualified advisers for the buyer's actual country and circumstances. Confirm seller authority, rights, documents, signatures, source of funds, tax, inspection and possession arrangements. Do not rely on a video tour alone.

### Can a foreign buyer obtain a mortgage?

No approval can be inferred from language, nationality or the project name. A lender must assess the buyer, income, source of funds, property and valuation under its current requirements. Obtain written terms before signing a payment schedule that depends on financing.

### What purchase tax applies?

The result depends on the transaction date and the buyer's actual facts. Use a current calculation and obtain appropriate advice before committing. Do not assume first-home, resident, new-immigrant or foreign-buyer treatment from a listing or general article.

### Is the planned M1 metro operating?

No operational claim is made here. NTA's official material identifies Namir-Pinkas and Tel Aviv Center as planned M1 stations. It does not support a guaranteed opening date or a fixed walking time from the towers.

### Is a place at a nearby school guaranteed?

No. A municipal update documented expansion of the Tzamarot Ayalon school for the 2024-2025 school year. Current catchment, admission, capacity and eligibility for a particular household must be confirmed directly with the municipality or school.

### Is YOO Tel Aviv a guaranteed investment?

No. No guaranteed yield, rent, appreciation or resale period was verified. Evaluate the exact purchase price, documented rent evidence, vacancy, management costs, maintenance, tax, finance, currency and exit assumptions.

### What should I request before making an offer?

Request a current Land Registry extract or other applicable rights record, seller authority, approved plan, area definitions, alteration documents, current asking price, parking and storage evidence, management invoices, arrears information, possession terms and access for an independent inspection. An official extract is date-specific. For an apartment in a registered joint house, the correct sub-parcel must be identified and is not necessarily the apartment number. Then decide whether the apartment justifies a formal offer.
