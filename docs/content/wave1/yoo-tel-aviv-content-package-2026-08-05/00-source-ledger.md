Research date: 2026-08-05

Scope: shared evidence ledger for the English, French, Russian, and Arabic articles in this package. This file does not itself authorize publication. The live Hebrew page, WordPress records, code, media, URLs, and existing language content were not changed.

## Editorial baseline

The safe public description is:

YOO Tel Aviv, also known as YOO Towers or מגדלי YOO, is a completed and occupied two-tower residential complex in Park Tzameret, Tel Aviv. It was developed historically by the Habas Group in collaboration with the YOO design brand associated with Philippe Starck. The original public-company report described up to 297 apartments, towers of 41 and 37 floors above ground, and three underground parking levels. Current purchases are generally resale transactions for individual apartments, not purchases from a new-project developer inventory. Current availability, asking prices, management charges, apartment specifications, parking, storage, alterations, views, and legal registration are unit-specific and must be verified for the apartment offered.

This baseline must remain identical in substance across all four language products. Cultural adaptation may change question order, examples, and vocabulary. It may not change the facts.

## Price and availability status

### What can be said

- The official YOO project page is marked Sold.
- Apartments can appear on the resale market through private sellers and brokers.
- A single completed sale reported in June 2023 can be used as a dated example: NIS 6.65 million for the specific apartment described in F23.
- Current asking prices found in broker results may be mentioned only if captured with the listing date, exact unit, and "asking price" label. They must not be promoted to a project price.
- A future numerical estimate must be labeled in every language as an estimate and non-binding. It needs a transparent method, date, sample, and exclusions.

### What cannot currently be said

- No verified current project-wide price list was found.
- No verified current developer inventory was found.
- No defensible current project-wide NIS-per-square-metre average was established in this research pass.
- No current list of available apartments was established.
- No current management-fee schedule was found.
- Search-result snippets and AI summaries are not evidence of a completed price or of an available unit.

Recommended future public label: "Current availability and asking prices were not published by an authoritative project source. Each resale apartment must be checked individually."

## Contradiction register

### Apartment count

- The 2007 Habas filing says up to 297 apartments.
- The current nad-lan.co.il structured data repeats 300 accommodation units.
- Unit mergers and planning changes mean an original maximum is not necessarily the current registered total.

Resolution: use "up to 297 apartments in the original plan" and state that the exact current registered total was not verified. Do not use 300 as an exact fact.

### Floor count

- Habas filing: 41 and 37 floors above ground.
- Alum Eshet: 35 and 39 floors.
- A 2023 Tel Aviv planning document: one building described as 40 floors.

Likely causes include different treatment of entrance, roof, technical, and mechanical floors, plus possible source error. Resolution: anchor the complex description to the primary filing and explicitly acknowledge that public sources count floors differently. For an apartment listing, use the building and floor designation in the current legal and management records.

### Tower names and addresses

- The municipal record associates YOO 2 with Nissim Aloni 19.
- Secondary sources do not consistently map YOO 1 and YOO 2 to numbers 19 and 21.

Resolution: name both publicly evidenced addresses but do not publish a tower-number mapping until verified from a definitive current building record.

### Completion date

- Occupancy began in the first tower by the end of the 2007 reporting period.
- Professional project records commonly list 2009.

Resolution: describe staged occupancy beginning in 2007 and completion around 2009. Do not collapse those into a single unsupported delivery date.

### Area figures

- The existing nad-lan.co.il article uses approximately 100,000 square metres, including 70,000 above ground and 30,000 below ground.
- A professional contributor page refers to approximately 58,000 square metres.
- Alum Eshet's approximately 25,000 square metres concerns curtain walls and windows, not total construction area.

Resolution: omit a project-wide built-area number until the measurement scope and primary source are verified. Never combine these figures.

### Geographic coordinates

- The current page's structured data uses 32.0853, 34.781806.
- Search verification associates those coordinates with an unrelated Afeka-area location, not the YOO Towers site.
- Secondary map results place Nissim Aloni 19-21 near 32.0906, 34.7965, but these are not accepted here as authoritative coordinates.

Resolution: remove the current coordinates from future content output and recalibrate the map pin from an authoritative municipal or cadastral source before publishing exact geo schema.

## Claims from the current page that are not accepted as facts

- "300 units" as an exact current total.
- Approximately 100,000 square metres with 70,000 above ground and 30,000 underground.
- A project-wide apartment range from roughly 80-90 square metres to a 750-square-metre penthouse.
- Any implication that an active project inventory or floor selector exists.
- Any implication that this is a Sde Dov or beachfront project.
- Any statement that buyers are purchasing a new apartment from a contractor.
- Any current facilities, fee, price, availability, view, or legal-right claim that is not tied to a specific apartment and dated source.

## Source-use rules for the writing stage

- Keep every number attached to its date and scope.
- Distinguish planned maximum, historical status, current registered fact, asking price, completed transaction, and estimate.
- Do not cite a competitor listing as proof of a building-wide fact.
- Do not use an AI summary as a source.
- Do not translate a legal or planning claim more strongly than the source supports.
- A current unit listing must be separately verified immediately before publication because inventory changes.
- A buyer-facing due-diligence section should ask for the land-registry extract or current rights record, house documents, management-fee statement, approved plan, permits for alterations, apartment measurements, parking and storage identifiers, and an inspection of the apartment as built.
- Purchase-tax discussion must send the buyer to the current official simulator with the actual transaction date, value, property type, and eligibility facts. Do not repeat tax brackets as evergreen copy.
- Mortgage discussion must distinguish a regulatory LTV ceiling from a bank's approval. Do not infer financing treatment from the reader's language or origin.
- A transaction comparison should begin with the government database but must exclude or qualify units that are not genuinely comparable.
- The independent-site statement belongs in each future language product, written natively and briefly. It must not interrupt the first sentence or replace the factual opening.

## Release blockers for future article writing

There is no blocker to writing source-grounded long-form content with the accepted baseline. The following items are blockers only if the article tries to make the corresponding claim:

- Exact current unit count requires a current authoritative register.
- Exact tower-to-address mapping requires a definitive current building or cadastral record.
- Current prices or price-per-square-metre require a dated, transparent transaction dataset.
- Current facilities and monthly charges require building-management evidence.
- Exact geo schema requires authoritative map calibration.
- School assignment requires a current municipal catchment check.
