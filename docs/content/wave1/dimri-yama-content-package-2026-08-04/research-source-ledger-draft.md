# DIMRI YAMA שדה דב - research and source ledger draft

**Scope:** evidence base for future multilingual buyer-facing content only. This document is not a finished article and does not authorize publishing or changing the existing Hebrew page.

**Research access date:** 2026-08-04

**Target Hebrew page reviewed:** <https://nad-lan.co.il/projects/dimri-yama-sde-dov/>

## 1. Editorial baseline

The safest current public description is: **DIMRI YAMA שדה דב, תל אביב** is a Y.H. Dimri mixed-use project on **Plot 107 in the Eshkol neighborhood of the Sde Dov district**, in the southern part of the district and northwest of the Levi Eshkol-Shai Agnon junction. Y.H. Dimri's 2025 annual report describes a 39-floor tower, a 16-floor tower, and two 8-9-floor contextual buildings, above five shared basement levels, with **458 apartments for sale, 70 hotel rooms, and commercial space**. The company's Q1 2026 report classifies the project as under construction, records a 2025 construction start, a planned 2031 completion, 1% completion as of 31 March 2026, and 41 cumulative apartment sales by that date. No authoritative source found in this research establishes a current August 2026 apartment price, current availability, a binding handover date, or an exact finalized postal address.

This baseline deliberately excludes the existing page's prototype 3D-unit records, the internal NIS 75,000-per-square-metre estimate, and any presentation of the December 2025 launch price as a current offer.

## 2. Naming and entity reconciliation

### Recommended public project name

- **Primary brand:** `DIMRI YAMA`.
- **Search-friendly extended name:** `DIMRI YAMA שדה דב, תל אביב`.
- **Hebrew transliteration encountered:** `דמרי ימה`.
- **Campaign variant encountered:** `DIMRI YAMA TLV`.
- **Corporate-report labels:** `דמרי בשדה דב` and `YAMA שדה דב`.

The Latin brand should remain `DIMRI YAMA` in every language. The district and city may be localized. Do not silently turn the corporate project label into a different consumer brand.

### Developer and ownership

- Public developer brand: **Y.H. Dimri Construction and Development Ltd.**, commonly presented in Hebrew as **י.ח. דמרי**.
- The 2025 annual report states that the rights are held effectively at 100% by the group: 96% through **י.ח. דמרי בשדה דב בע"מ** and 4% through **י.ח. דמרי נדל"ן בע"מ**.
- The Water Authority notice names **י.ח. דמרי בשדה דב בע"מ** as applicant for the Plot 107 groundwater-reinjection plan.
- The municipal architectural-design decision predates Dimri's acquisition and therefore names Hanan Mor as the then-developer. That is historical planning context, not the current developer identity.
- The official developer site says the company was founded in 1989 by Yigal Dimri. The 2025 annual report states incorporation on 9 July 1989 and public-company status since May 2004.

## 12. Environmental due-diligence context

This topic is material and should neither be suppressed nor sensationalized.

### District-wide official context

The Ministry of Environmental Protection reported PFAS in groundwater and limited soil samples at the former Sde Dov airfield, likely linked to historical firefighting foam use. The ministry stated that this groundwater is not used for drinking and does not threaten drinking-water sources. It also identified possible direct-contact and soil-movement concerns and established continued investigation and handling requirements.

### Plot-specific company disclosure

Y.H. Dimri's 2025 annual report says that in March 2026, following investigation of the plot, the ministry confirmed that most of Plot 107 was below the relevant PFAS threshold, while a small area exceeded it. The company said further investigation would define the affected boundaries and, if needed, quantities for excavation and removal to an authorized destination.

### Safe buyer-facing treatment

State the dated official facts and recommend review of current environmental documents, permit conditions, contractual disclosures, and professional advice before purchase.
