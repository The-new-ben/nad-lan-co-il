# DUO Tel Aviv frozen source ledger

Research cut-off: 4 August 2026

This is the shared factual contract for the English, French, Russian and Arabic articles. Language and emphasis may change. Project facts, dates, qualifications and source order may not.

## Publishable project baseline

- Project name: DUO Tel Aviv. Africa Israel Residences also identifies it in corporate reporting as DUO Somail South.
- City and area: central Tel Aviv, in the Somail South area at the junction of Ibn Gabirol, Arlozorov and Ben Saruk.
- Municipal licensing addresses: Arlozorov 85 and 87, and Ibn Gabirol 112A and 118, Tel Aviv-Yafo. These are public licensing references, not a promise that one address is the entrance or legal description for every apartment.
- Developer: Africa Israel Residences Ltd, company number 520034760.
- Physical composition: two residential towers above a shared mixed-use development.
- Floors: each tower has 54 total floors, including 50 residential floors. Marketing and architecture pages that call them 50-storey towers are using the residential-floor convention.
- Apartment count: 668 apartments in the whole project.
- Marketed pool used in the developer's sales tables: 510 apartments within the partners' marketable rights. The 510 figure is not the whole project and the 668 figure is not the developer's current sales inventory.
- Publicly described apartment mix: 2-, 3-, 4- and 5-room apartments and penthouses. In English, the project site describes the same broad mix as 1- to 4-bedroom apartments and penthouses. Sample plans do not prove current availability.
- Mixed uses: residential towers, a three-level commercial complex, underground parking and a public or civic component.
- Parking basements: five underground levels in the current municipal and contractor records.
- Architecture: Yashar Architects. The municipal record names architect Avner Yashar.
- Lobby and public-area design: Orly Shrem Architects, according to the official project presentation.
- Main construction: a joint execution arrangement involving Danya Cebus and Electra Construction under an agreement signed on 14 August 2022.
- Full permit milestone: reported on 30 December 2021. A later 2025 municipal licensing decision approved changes subject to conditions.
- Marketed resident facilities: residents' club, 24/7 security, an outdoor infinity pool, toddler pool, seating and sunbathing areas, and a fitness room. These are features presented by the official project site and remain subject to final plans, specifications, agreements and operating rules.
- Commercial component: the project site presents an intended retail and leisure mix. No verified signed-tenant schedule was found, so no retailer, restaurant or operator may be described as confirmed.

## Dated construction and sales evidence

The following are dated disclosures, not live inventory or promises:

- At 31 December 2025, the developer described DUO as under marketing and construction and reported 76% financial completion.
- For the period ending 31 March 2026, Africa Israel Residences reported 82% financial or engineering completion and forecast completion and the start of occupancy during 2027.
- Danya's live Q1 2026 TASE filing is retained as current contractor context. It does not provide a project-specific DUO completion percentage or handover quarter used in the public articles.
- Electra's project page still stated December 2026. Later developer and Danya material points to 2027, so the Electra date is treated as stale.
- No reviewed source establishes the contractual delivery date, grace period or delay provisions for a specific apartment. A 2027 corporate forecast is not a buyer's contractual handover date.
- During 2025, the company reported 13 contracts covering 1,281 sqm, at an average NIS 69,900 per sqm excluding VAT.
- At 31 December 2025, the company reported 361 cumulative contracts and 149 units not contracted within the 510-unit marketable pool.
- During Q1 2026, the company reported nine contracts with total consideration of NIS 80.147 million excluding VAT, an average NIS 64,800 per sqm excluding VAT and an average apartment consideration of NIS 10.508 million including VAT.
- At 31 March 2026, the company reported 366 cumulative contracts and 144 units not sold within the 510-unit marketable pool.
- Later reported transactions mean that 144 is not current availability as of 4 August 2026.
- No verified public live price list or current unit-by-unit availability schedule was found at the research cut-off.

## Price rules

- Every price must be attached to its period, transaction count and VAT basis.
- NIS 69,900 per sqm excluding VAT for 2025 becomes approximately NIS 82,482 after adding 18% VAT. This substantially reconciles the NIS 82,500 figure quoted by the developer's CEO. It does not make either number a current list price.
- NIS 64,800 per sqm excluding VAT for Q1 2026 becomes NIS 76,464 after adding 18% VAT. This substantially reconciles the approximately NIS 76,500 figure reported by Calcalist. The press article does not explicitly label that project's VAT basis in the same sentence, so this reconciliation is an arithmetic inference.
- A Globes report dated 19 April 2026 described one five-apartment family transaction and cited an average above NIS 82,000 per sqm. It is a dated transaction example, not a project-wide price, current inventory statement or independent appraisal.
- Accounting forecasts for unsold inventory are not offers or price lists.
- No current starting price, current per-square-metre price, current incentive, payment schedule or financing campaign may be invented.
- If a writer introduces any estimate rather than a source figure, it must be identified in the target language as an estimate, non-binding. New numerical estimates are not needed for these articles.

## Location and transport rules

- NTA describes the planned Arlozorov West Green Line station beneath the Somail complex and Century Tower, with an eastern entrance in Somail at Ibn Gabirol and Arlozorov.
- The reviewed NTA evidence supports proximity to planned light-rail infrastructure. It does not support saying that the station or line is operating.
- The developer describes a direct connection from basement level -1. NTA independently supports a planned Somail entrance, but the reviewed NTA page does not establish a private internal DUO connection in the same terms. Any direct-connection reference must be attributed to the developer and made a document-check question.
- NTA published a notice on 23 July 2026 for Purple Line track-laying and infrastructure works scheduled for the nights of 26 to 30 July on Arlozorov up to Ibn Gabirol. This is evidence of recent works and the potential for changing access arrangements, not proof that the same closure remained active on 4 August. No opening year is adopted as a shared fact.
- No walking time, driving time or distance may be invented. A reader should test the route from the actual entrance.

## Rights, areas and legal descriptions

- The annual report divides project rights among Africa Israel Residences directly, Ram-Nah Ltd, Afriram Ltd, Migdaley Somail Ltd and other rights holders.
- The company's approximately 70% figure applies to its economic share within the approximately 510 apartments in the partners' marketable rights. It does not mean 70% of all 668 apartments.
- The approximately 52% figure uses a broader project-wide rights basis. Public articles may omit these percentages rather than burden readers with denominators. If included, the denominator must be explained.
- The current licensing application identifies block 6213, parcel 1468. Separate municipal property records discuss parcels 1471 and 1472 and historic lots 111 and 112. These are different planning, licensing and title layers and must not be collapsed into one ownership statement.
- Published project-area numbers use different conventions: principal area, gross area, architect scope and contractor scope. They may not be added together or used as substitutes for sellable apartment area.
- An individual buyer must verify the exact tower, entrance, apartment, legal description, parking, storage and rights in the offer, sale agreement, plans and registration material.

## Unpublished information

The following must be stated as unpublished, unavailable in the reviewed public material or a buyer question when relevant:

- A live price list current to 4 August 2026.
- Current availability by tower, floor, layout and direction.
- Binding apartment and balcony areas for current inventory.
- Current incentives, payment schedule, indexation formula or finance campaign.
- The contractual delivery date and delay terms for a specific apartment.
- Management fees, reserve arrangements and operating costs for the pool, gym and club.
- The parking or storage attachment for each offered apartment.
- Final elevator allocation, EV charging allocation and visitor parking.
- Confirmed commercial tenants and opening dates.
- A guaranteed sea view, protected view corridor or protection from future obstruction.
- Exact noise, wind, shading and traffic conditions for an apartment.
- A final private/internal connection from DUO to the Green Line station.
- An occupancy certificate or Form 4.
- Foreign-buyer, remote-signing, mortgage, currency-transfer, tax or residency terms specific to DUO.

## Mandatory demo and media quarantine

The live interactive experience contains prototype data. It is not a verified inventory feed.

Do not publish prototype unit codes, demo floors, demo apartment areas, demo balconies, demo directions, demo availability labels, zero prices, model-estimated prices or model-derived views. Do not treat a clickable unit as purchasable. Do not describe a model facade as approved contractual architecture. Do not use a Rainbow-derived interior image as if it depicts DUO.

The 3D experience may be described only as an illustrative visualization. Current availability must come from a dated developer sales schedule or direct sales-office response.

## Mandatory buyer checks

The articles should turn evidence gaps into concrete questions:

1. Which tower, entrance, floor, address and legal description appear in the offer and agreement?
2. What are the binding apartment and balcony areas, and which measurement method applies?
3. Does the quoted sum include VAT, parking, storage, legal expenses, indexation and upgrades?
4. Which parking and storage rights are legally attached to the apartment?
5. What is the contractual handover date, grace period and delay-compensation mechanism?
6. Which part of the price is indexed, to which index and until when?
7. Which facilities and finishes are contractual rather than illustrative?
8. What approvals remain before occupancy?
9. What management fee, operating budget and reserve arrangements are expected?
10. What is the final approved route between the project and planned transport infrastructure?
11. What current and future works may affect access, noise, light or views?
12. For a remote or foreign buyer, which documents, powers of attorney, tax steps, banking checks and currency arrangements apply?
