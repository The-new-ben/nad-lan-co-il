# DUO Tel Aviv research and source ledger

Research cut-off: 2026-08-04

Purpose: frozen factual baseline for four future language-specific buyer articles. This file is an internal research ledger, not publishable copy, a current inventory sheet, a price list, legal advice or a substitute for the sale agreement and its appendices.

Scope: the live Hebrew project page, official developer and project materials, public-company disclosures, municipal licensing and property records, contractor disclosures, architect material, NTA transport material and dated market reporting. The research explicitly separates verified project facts from forward-looking estimates, dated sales evidence, marketing descriptions, unresolved discrepancies and unpublished information.

## 1. Evidence rules for the future articles

Use the following hierarchy when two sources differ:

1. A current statutory filing or municipal licensing record for a legal, permit, ownership or dated financial fact.
2. The latest dated public-company report for construction, sales and forecast information.
3. The official project site for apartment mix, marketed resident facilities and design language.
4. The architect or contractor's official project page for its own scope and area convention.
5. A named news report for dated transaction context or a clearly attributed executive statement.
6. Listing portals and aggregators only as discovery aids or secondary context.

Every number that can change must carry its evidence date. Every price must state whether VAT is included or excluded when the source provides that basis. A marketing statement must not be rewritten as a contractual promise. A planning or corporate completion forecast must not be called a contractual delivery date.

The factual baseline must be identical across English, French, Russian and Arabic. Cultural adaptation may change questions, examples, order and explanation, but may not change the project facts.

## 4. Developer, ownership and project entity map

### 4.1 Developer identity

Africa Israel Residences Ltd is the public company associated with the development. Its public company number is 520034760. The official project site identifies Africa Israel Residences as the developer, while the public filings provide the more precise rights structure.

### 4.2 Rights disclosed in the 2025 annual report

The annual report's project table divides the 668 apartments and 9,620 sqm of principal commercial area among several rights holders:

| Rights holder row in the filing | Apartment allocation shown | Principal commercial area shown | Africa Israel Residences ownership in entity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Ram-Nah Ltd | 135 | 1,862 sqm | 57.5% |
| Afriram Ltd | 101.6 | 1,444 sqm | 40% |
| Migdaley Somail Ltd | 67.7 | 962 sqm | 50% |
| Africa Israel Residences directly | 205.7 | 3,005 sqm | 100% |
| Other rights holders | 158 | 2,347 sqm | Not applicable |
| Total | 668 | 9,620 sqm | Not one ownership percentage |

The decimal apartment figures are weighted disclosure allocations. They are not fractional physical apartments and must not appear as an apartment inventory in public copy.

The report separately says the company directly and indirectly holds rights in approximately 510 apartments, of which its economic share is approximately 358. The report also states that a purchasing group holds approximately 25% of the residential-designated areas. The numerical `other rights holders` row may appear consistent with that wider structure, but the filing does not expressly say that every one of those 158 units belongs to the purchasing group. Do not make that inference.

### 4.3 The 70% versus approximately 52% denominator issue

Two percentages in the company's disclosure can look contradictory when stripped of their denominator:

- Approximately 70% describes the company's economic share within the approximately 510 apartments in the partners' marketable rights. Approximately 358 divided by 510 is about 70%.
- Approximately 52% in the detailed project table describes a different project-wide rights basis. The company's approximately 358-unit economic share divided by all 668 project units is about 53.6%, close to the disclosed project-wide rounded basis.

Future copy should either explain the denominator or omit the percentage. It must not say simply that Africa Israel owns `70% of the 668 apartments`.

## 5. Physical program, buildings and area conventions

### 5.1 Consistent core facts

The official filings and municipal decision consistently support:

- Two residential towers.
- 54 total floors in each tower, including 50 residential floors.
- 668 apartments in the whole project.
- A common mixed-use development with commercial buildings/levels, parking basements, public development and a civic/public component.
- Five underground parking levels in the current municipal and contractor descriptions.

### 5.2 Why the area figures differ

The following figures are not interchangeable:

| Source and convention | Figure | What it means |
|---|---:|---|
| Africa Israel annual report, principal residential area | 65,107 sqm | Planning/accounting principal residential area |
| Africa Israel annual report, principal commercial area | 9,620 sqm | Planning/accounting principal commercial area |
| Africa Israel annual report, commercial complex gross | About 13,000 sqm | Gross commercial complex including commerce at basement level -1 |
| Africa Israel annual report, parking basements | About 47,000 sqm | Basement/parking gross description |
| Yashar Architects | 106,000 sqm above ground and 47,000 sqm underground | Architect's project-area convention |
| Electra | 169,000 sqm | Contractor portfolio's total project-area convention |
| Danya | About 100,000 sqm residential, 12,000 sqm commercial and 40,000 sqm parking | Contractor-scope convention |
| Municipal public-building agreement | 2,140 sqm gross public building | Gross municipal public-building obligation |
| Africa Israel planning table | About 1,070 sqm principal public building | Principal area, not the same as municipal gross area |

Do not add these figures together, use one to `correct` another or describe a gross figure as sellable area. When area detail is not necessary for buyer intent, the safest public formulation is that the project combines residential towers, a multi-level commercial component, underground parking and a public/civic component.

### 5.3 Apartment mix and plans

The official Hebrew project site presents 2-, 3-, 4- and 5-room apartments and penthouses. Its English convention presents 1- to 4-bedroom apartments and penthouses. This is a room-count language convention, not a factual conflict.

The official site contains sample plans. They establish that the marketed mix includes those categories, but they do not establish:

- Which layouts are available now.
- Which tower or floor still has a particular type.
- The binding net or sale area.
- A price for that layout.
- The direction or guaranteed view from an apartment.
- The specification delivered under a particular contract.

The future articles may link to or discuss sample plans only as examples and should tell a serious buyer to request a current, dated schedule and the contractual plan.

### 5.4 Marketed resident facilities

The official project presentation markets a residents' club, 24/7 security, an outdoor infinity pool, a toddler pool, seating and sunbathing areas, and a fitness room. The pool area is presented on the lobby structure connecting the towers. A wellness complex is presented as accessible directly from the lobby. The main lobby is described as approximately seven metres high, with public-area design by Orly Shrem Architects.

Use `the official project presentation includes` or an equivalent formulation. Do not promise operating hours, exclusivity, opening date, equipment, staffing level, service standard or cost. Those details were not found in the reviewed public sources and may be governed by the final specification, management agreement and house rules.

### 5.5 Commercial component

The project site describes a retail and leisure mix that may include flagship stores, cafés, restaurants, a supermarket, design and lifestyle businesses and boutiques. This is an intended commercial character. It is not evidence that a named tenant has signed, opened or will operate. No verified tenant schedule was located in this research round.

## 6. Location, addresses, parcel references and Somail context

### 6.1 Location that can be stated

The project occupies the Somail South area at the junction of Ibn Gabirol, Arlozorov and Ben Saruk in central Tel Aviv. The 2025 municipal licensing decision describes its boundaries as Arlozorov Street to the south, Ibn Gabirol Street to the west, the Givat Hamoreh public open space to the north and Ben Saruk Street to the east.

The licensing decision lists Arlozorov 85 and 87 and Ibn Gabirol 112A and 118. These are suitable as public municipal reference addresses. A future buyer article should avoid claiming that one address is the definitive entrance for every tower, apartment, parking space or registered sub-parcel.

### 6.2 Parcel and title caution

The current licensing application identifies block 6213, parcel 1468 and a site area of 8,338 sqm. Separate municipal property material discusses parcels 1471 and 1472 in block 6213 and historic owners' lots 111 and 112 in relation to the public-building agreement. The annual report describes multiple rights-holding companies and other rights holders.

These records describe different administrative, planning and title layers. Public copy should not collapse them into a statement such as `the entire project is registered as parcel 1468 and wholly owned by the developer`. A buyer needs the exact legal description in the sale agreement, land-registry material and legal appendices for the apartment being considered.

### 6.3 Somail planning context

The secondary planning index for plan TA/MK/2988A describes Somail South as a detailed plan of about 24.091 dunams that became effective on 2 July 2013 and included re-parcellation. Its historic plan figure of 534 housing units is not the current DUO apartment count. The current project count of 668 comes from later project and permit disclosures.

A 2019 Tel Aviv municipal infrastructure tender recorded coordinated public works in the wider Somail area between Ibn Gabirol, Jabotinsky, Arlozorov and Ben Saruk and referred to Somail South construction involving underground parking, commerce and two residential towers. This is historical context, not evidence of the current construction percentage or delivery date.

### 7.2 Current status formulation

The safest current formulation is:

> Africa Israel Residences reported that DUO was under marketing and construction at the end of 2025. In its report for the period ending 31 March 2026, the company reported 82% financial/engineering completion and forecast completion and the start of occupancy during 2027. These are a corporate reporting metric and a forward-looking forecast, not apartment-specific readiness or the contractual handover date for a specific apartment.

Do not convert the 82% reporting measure into a percentage of finished apartments or a handover countdown. Its scope does not establish the readiness of a chosen apartment, shared systems, public works or occupancy approvals.

### 7.3 Completion conflict

Electra's portfolio page still states expected completion in December 2026. Later sources are more current:

- Africa Israel's Q1 2026 report: completion and start of occupancy expected in 2027.
- A Globes interview published 19 April 2026: the developer's CEO said a Q2 2027 Form 4 was realistic.

Therefore December 2026 is quarantined as a stale contractor-page forecast. Q2 2027 can be mentioned only as a dated executive forecast from the April 2026 interview. The broad 2027 company forecast is the preferred common baseline.

No reviewed source establishes the contractual delivery date, grace period, compensation terms or handover conditions for a particular apartment. Those must be checked in the current sale documents.

### 8.3 VAT reconciliation

The apparently conflicting press and filing numbers reconcile when VAT is handled correctly:

- 2025 official average: NIS 69,900 per sqm excluding VAT.
- NIS 69,900 multiplied by 1.18 is approximately NIS 82,482 per sqm including VAT.
- The developer's CEO told Globes that the 2025 average was NIS 82,500 per sqm. The arithmetic strongly indicates an including-VAT presentation of the same broad figure.

For Q1 2026:

- Official average: NIS 64,800 per sqm excluding VAT.
- NIS 64,800 multiplied by 1.18 is NIS 76,464 per sqm including VAT.
- Calcalist reported approximately NIS 76,500 per sqm. The article does not explicitly label the DUO figure's VAT basis in the same way as the company table, so the match should be described as a strong arithmetic inference, not an independently stated VAT fact.

The official Q1 transaction volume also reconciles:

- NIS 80.147 million excluding VAT divided by 9 contracts is about NIS 8.905 million excluding VAT per apartment.
- Multiplying by 1.18 gives about NIS 10.508 million including VAT per apartment, matching the company report.

Future articles must never put NIS 69,900 excluding VAT beside NIS 82,500 without explaining that the two figures are substantially reconciled by VAT.

### 8.4 April 2026 five-apartment transaction

Globes reported on 19 April 2026 that one businessperson bought five apartments for family members: four approximately 160 sqm five-room apartments and one approximately 100 sqm four-room apartment, each with parking. The executive quoted in the article described the apartments as west-facing with sea views and said the average exceeded NIS 82,000 per sqm. The article did not clearly establish the VAT basis for that transaction figure.

This evidence may be used as a dated transaction example. It must not be used to claim:

- That five-room apartments are currently available.
- That every west-facing apartment has an unobstructed sea view.
- That NIS 82,000 per sqm is today's project price.
- That every apartment includes parking.
- That the transaction represents an independent market appraisal.

The same article quoted two-room inventory from NIS 4 million and said penthouses were also available. Those were April 2026 executive statements, not a current, independently verified price list.

### 8.5 Inventory count conflict after 31 March 2026

The official Q1 report recorded 144 unsold apartments at 31 March 2026. Five later sales alone would imply 139, while the Globes interview said 138 remained. The one-unit difference may reflect an additional transaction, timing or a different reporting cut, but the public evidence reviewed does not resolve it.

Do not publish 138, 139 or 144 as the current count on 4 August 2026. The correct formulation is that the latest official quarter-end disclosure located in this research recorded 144 unsold apartments within the 510-unit marketable pool at 31 March 2026, with later reported sales meaning that figure is no longer current.

### 8.6 What `price` means in future copy

Every price paragraph should separate:

- Signed-sale average versus asking price.
- Per-square-metre average versus total apartment price.
- Excluding VAT versus including VAT.
- One reporting period versus cumulative history.
- Project-wide marketable pool versus a specific layout, floor, direction or tower.
- A dated company figure versus a current sales-office quote.

No source reviewed provides a verified public price list current to 2026-08-04.

## 9. Transport and public-realm evidence

### 9.1 Green Line

NTA's official station page describes Arlozorov West as the northernmost of the Green Line's four underground stations. It is planned beneath the Somail complex and Century Tower. NTA describes an eastern entrance in Somail at Ibn Gabirol and Arlozorov and a western entrance at the former Super Yuda site, Ibn Gabirol 103. The page also notes proximity to the Purple Line's Ibn Gabirol station.

This supports describing DUO as next to planned light-rail infrastructure and a planned station entrance in the Somail complex. It does not establish that the station or either line is operating.

### 9.2 Direct connection claim

The developer and a press interview have described a direct connection from basement level -1 to the Green Line. NTA independently confirms a planned Somail entrance but the reviewed NTA source does not verify a private, internal DUO connection with the same wording.

If used, phrase this as the developer's presentation and tell buyers to verify the final approved access arrangement. Do not present the private direct connection as an independently established NTA fact.

### 9.3 Purple Line and current works

NTA published a notice on 23 July 2026 for Purple Line track-laying and infrastructure works scheduled for the nights of 26 to 30 July on Arlozorov Street in the section extending to Ibn Gabirol. That defined window ended before the 4 August research cut-off. The notice is evidence of recent construction activity and changing access arrangements, not proof that the same closure remains active. Buyers should recheck current notices rather than writing only about future convenience.

No current official opening date was adopted in this ledger. Future articles should avoid an opening year unless it is rechecked against an official NTA source at drafting time.

### 10.1 Africa Israel Residences

Africa Israel Residences is a public residential developer. For this project, the annual and quarterly reports are the authoritative source for the rights structure, sales denominator, dated sales, construction metrics and corporate forecasts. Brand descriptions from the developer site may be summarized sparingly, but they are not substitutes for project-specific evidence.

Yashar Architects lists DUO as an in-progress, mixed-use project for Africa Israel. It gives 106,000 sqm above ground and 47,000 sqm underground, two 50-story residential towers and 668 apartments. ### 10.3 Orly Shrem Architects

The official DUO site credits Orly Shrem Architects with the public-area and lobby design. This does not establish the interior designer, finishes or included upgrades for every private apartment.

The main construction agreement was signed with Danya Cebus and Electra Construction. The contractor agreement describes approximately 52 months from the work order to Form 4/development, followed by additional periods for public portions/apartment delivery and a contractual grace period. Those are terms of the construction contract. They must not be presented as the apartment buyer's contractual delivery schedule.

Electra's portfolio describes 16 high-speed residential elevators, 12 commercial elevators, eight escalator sets and four service robots. These are contractor portfolio scope claims. Unless the current buyer specification confirms them, they should not be elevated into guaranteed project features.

## 11. Governance, related parties and legal matters

### 11.1 Contractor transaction

The Danya/Electra construction transaction was disclosed and approved as a related-party matter because Danya and Africa Israel Residences were under a common control chain. The annual report records shareholder approval on 19 July 2022 and contract signing on 14 August 2022. This is a governance disclosure. It is not, by itself, evidence of improper conduct.

### 11.2 Related-party apartment purchases

An official TASE filing dated 14 January 2021 reports board approval, following audit-committee approval, for DUO apartment purchases by the controlling shareholder/then chair Jacob Luxenburg, his son and a daughter of deputy chair Ariel/Erik Shapir. A later official group report records that the January transaction covered three units for approximately NIS 13.7 million before VAT and a second September 2021 related-party approval covered three units for approximately NIS 21.8 million before VAT.

If mentioned, this belongs in a transparent governance note, not a sensational buyer-facing claim. The evidence does not by itself prove a bargain, overpayment, conflict breach or current price benchmark.

### 11.3 Betterment levy

The 2025 annual report discusses a municipal betterment-levy assessment connected with parcels 1471 and 1472. A decisive appraiser's decision dated 5 March 2025 reduced the development-side levy by approximately NIS 78 million. Both the municipality and the developers appealed, and no decision on those appeals was reported by the annual-report approval date of 24 March 2026.

This is a project/legal financial matter. It should not be translated into an apartment-buyer tax estimate. Purchase tax, VAT treatment and buyer expenses depend on the transaction and buyer profile.

### 11.4 Historic land/title litigation

The annual report says older litigation dating from 2015 was settled through mediation, the settlement received judgment on 3 December 2024 and the claims were dismissed. Do not describe that historic dispute as an active unresolved lawsuit.

## 20. Research conclusion

DUO can be described confidently as a large mixed-use Somail South development by Africa Israel Residences, with two towers, 54 total floors in each tower, 50 residential floors per tower and 668 apartments across the whole project. Public-company sales reporting, however, applies to a 510-unit marketable pool rather than all 668 units. The latest official quarter located for this ledger recorded 366 cumulative contracts, 144 units not sold in that pool and 82% developer-reported financial/engineering completion at 31 March 2026, with a forward-looking 2027 completion/start-of-occupancy forecast.

The central editorial risk is false currency. Prices, remaining-unit counts, progress percentages and completion dates have all changed and use different denominators or VAT conventions. The second risk is contaminating factual copy with prototype 3D inventory. The source rules, reconciliations and quarantine in this ledger are mandatory controls for the future four-language content products.
