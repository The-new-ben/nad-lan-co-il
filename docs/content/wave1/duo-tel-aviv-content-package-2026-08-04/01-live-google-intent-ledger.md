# DUO Tel Aviv - live Google Chrome intent ledger

Research date: 2026-08-04

Research surface: the user's connected Google Chrome browser. Every query was loaded in the visible Chrome session on Google with Israel targeting (`gl=il`), no personalization (`pws=0`) and the target interface language. Russian and Arabic searches also used `lr=lang_ru` or `lr=lang_ar` when useful. Google identified the location as Tel Aviv or Tel Aviv-Yafo from the user's IP.

Scope: evidence for the four future content-only products. Search result language and rankings are a dated sample, not a permanent market measurement. AI Overviews and listing snippets were recorded as evidence of questions and competing claims, not adopted as project facts.

Primary phrase selected: `DUO Tel Aviv apartments`.

It must not imply that foreign nationality creates a universal legal, tax or lending result.

Competitor risk: Google exposed listing prices, `last unit` language and AI-generated project price ranges. These are not a substitute for a current official unit schedule. The page must clearly separate dated reported sales, third-party resale listings and unpublished developer inventory.

## Shared editorial implications

- DUO has a strong official multilingual footprint in English, French and Russian. New pages must exceed it by adding dated, independently sourced buyer-decision detail, not by copying its marketing language.
- The project query is price-sensitive in every language. Current price and inventory must be requested, while every published price is tied to its source date, apartment context and tax basis.
- Central-city location is a primary intent. The article should explain Arlozorov and Ibn Gvirol, existing services, construction disruption and planned transit separately.
- Tower views are unit-specific. A high floor, project rendering or west-facing direction does not prove a permanent sea view.
- The two towers, commercial component, public realm, resident facilities and surrounding Somail development must be described as one buyer context, with access and operating costs examined.
- Overseas purchase, tax, financing and investment questions belong in the project article only to the extent that they help evaluate a DUO apartment. Broad legal, tax, city and mortgage intent remains with the site's dedicated guides and calculators.
- Google exposed no stable project-specific FAQ set common to all four languages. FAQs must be built from the real buyer decisions above and the verified source ledger, never fabricated as People Also Ask evidence.
