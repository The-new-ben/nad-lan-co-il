## Arabic quality and cultural-fit review

The body is fluent public-facing Modern Standard Arabic, not an English fallback or a sentence-by-sentence literal translation. It uses buyer vocabulary naturally, including الشقة المحددة, قائمة أسعار, التوفر, عقد البيع, ضريبة القيمة المضافة, ضريبة الشراء, الرهن العقاري, رسوم الإدارة and موعد التسليم التعاقدي. Brand names and official entity names remain in Latin script where useful.

The family and education treatment is careful. Schools, nurseries, registration, walking time, protected-room configuration and Arabic-language service are presented only as checks for the actual apartment, address and year. No school, catchment, distance or service level is invented. Remote buying, translation, powers of attorney, apostille, banking, source-of-funds and currency exposure are framed as buyer tasks whose exact requirements depend on the person, document, bank and jurisdiction.

The tone stays directed to a buyer deciding whether and how to purchase. It converts evidence gaps into concrete requests without filling the public article with internal production language or defensive audit commentary.
