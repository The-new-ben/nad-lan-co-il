# DUO Tel Aviv existing-page audit

Audit date: 4 August 2026

Page reviewed: [https://nad-lan.co.il/projects/duo-tel-aviv/](https://nad-lan.co.il/projects/duo-tel-aviv/)

Scope: read-only content and presentation audit. No field, page, code, model, media item or URL was changed.

## What the current page gets right

The current Hebrew article contains several useful core facts that are supported by stronger public sources:

- DUO is in central Tel Aviv in the Somail South area, around Ibn Gabirol, Arlozorov and Ben Saruk.
- The project has two towers.
- Each tower has 54 total floors, including 50 residential floors.
- The whole project contains 668 apartments.
- The publicly presented product range includes 2-, 3-, 4- and 5-room apartments and penthouses.
- Africa Israel Residences is the developer associated with the project.
- The project combines residences with commercial space, underground parking and public or civic space.
- A 2027 completion and occupancy period appears in current company reporting, provided it is clearly described as a forecast rather than a contractual handover date.

These points may be retained only through the primary sources in the frozen ledger, not by copying the existing article.

## Above-the-fold and information-order problems

The public page places a large interactive showroom before the long-form article. The article H1 and the verified project explanation appear later in the rendered page, after interface material and unrelated shell elements. For search relevance and buyer comprehension, the new language articles therefore begin directly with a project-first H1 and a factual opening paragraph. They do not repeat interface instructions or expose the research process.

The showroom header also displays a Sde Dov and sea-facing label that does not belong to DUO's central Somail location. This is a shared presentation contamination, not a project fact. None of that wording is reused in the language articles.

The page title identifies two 54-floor towers in the Somail complex. That formula is factually stronger than the generic showroom label, but the title and opening should also answer the buying questions: total apartments, current price status, dated construction stage and the distinction between a corporate forecast and contractual delivery.

## Interactive showroom audit

The live record exposes an interactive model with five prototype apartment entries. The interface gives floors, room counts, areas, directions and labels resembling availability. The underlying project data marks the experience as a demo or prototype, and the model disclaimer states that the building, facade and apartment presentation is illustrative rather than approved inventory.

The prototype records must not be used as article facts. Specifically, the new language versions exclude:

- Prototype apartment codes.
- Demo floor numbers.
- Demo room counts attached to a particular floor.
- Demo apartment and balcony areas.
- Demo directions and sea-view implications.
- Labels such as available, reserved or priority.
- Zero prices and model-derived price fields.
- Model clicks as evidence that an apartment can be bought.

The default interior media associated with the experience is not verified as a DUO apartment and points to material associated with another project. It cannot be captioned, described or given alt text as a DUO interior. The facade visual is also identified as concept material rather than approved developer material. The article's image-alt suggestions therefore describe the type of verified image that should be used and never authenticate the existing demo asset.

## Data and denominator problems

The current page mentions project and sales figures without consistently protecting the denominator and date. The language products use the following distinctions:

- 668 means all apartments in the physical project.
- 510 means the marketable pool covered by the company's sales tables for the partners' rights.
- 361 contracts and 149 not contracted refer to the 510-unit pool at 31 December 2025.
- 366 contracts and 144 not sold refer to the same pool at 31 March 2026.
- Later transactions mean that 144 is not current availability on the audit date.

The gap between 668 and 510 is not a hidden inventory count. It reflects the disclosed rights structure. Likewise, a 70% economic-share figure and an approximately 52% project-wide figure use different denominators. The new articles either explain that distinction or omit the percentages.

## Price presentation problems

The current page discusses dated averages from company reports and press coverage. Those can help a buyer understand scale, but only when period, sample and VAT are explicit.

- The 2025 average of NIS 69,900 per sqm is excluding VAT and covers 13 contracts.
- The corresponding approximately NIS 82,500 figure substantially reconciles after 18% VAT and is not a separate current price level.
- The official Q1 2026 average of NIS 64,800 per sqm is excluding VAT and covers nine contracts.
- The approximately NIS 76,500 press figure substantially reconciles after 18% VAT. The VAT treatment in the press sentence is inferred from the arithmetic, not expressly stated there.
- The Q1 2026 average apartment consideration of NIS 10.508 million includes VAT and is a nine-contract average, not a unit quote.
- An April 2026 five-apartment transaction is a dated example, not a live price list.

No reviewed primary source supplies a public price list current to 4 August 2026. The new articles say so plainly and direct the buyer to request a dated, unit-specific offer.

## Construction and delivery problems

The current page contains or can be read alongside several progress and completion figures. They need separation:

- Africa Israel Residences reported 82% financial or engineering completion at 31 March 2026.
- The live Danya Q1 2026 TASE filing is retained for current contractor context, but no separate project-specific Danya completion percentage is used in the articles.
- Electra's older portfolio page lists December 2026.
- Later company reporting forecasts completion and the start of occupancy during 2027.
- The earlier Danya presentation URL that appeared in Google results was found to return 404 during final link QA, so its project-specific quarter forecast was removed from public copy.

The new articles use 2027 only as the latest broad corporate forecast. They do not call it the contractual delivery date for any apartment. The actual date, grace period, conditions and compensation mechanism belong in the sale agreement.

## Address, title and planning problems

The current page lists Arlozorov 85 and 87 and Ibn Gabirol 112A and 118. Those addresses are supported by the 2025 municipal licensing decision. They should be described as licensing addresses, not as one guaranteed tower entrance or the complete legal identity of every unit.

The current permit application refers to block 6213, parcel 1468. Other municipal property material discusses parcels 1471 and 1472 and historic lots 111 and 112. These are different administrative and title layers. The new content does not flatten them into a simplistic ownership statement and tells buyers to inspect the legal description for the specific apartment.

## Amenities and commercial claims

The official project presentation supports a residents' club, 24/7 security, an outdoor infinity pool, toddler pool, seating and sunbathing areas, and a fitness room. It does not publicly establish the final operating hours, fees, service level or access rules. The new articles describe these as presented facilities and turn costs and rules into document questions.

The project's intended commercial mix is not a signed tenant list. The articles do not name a shop, restaurant or operator as confirmed unless supported by a current tenant announcement, which was not found in this research round.

## Transport claims

NTA supports a planned Arlozorov West Green Line station beneath the Somail area and an eastern entrance around Ibn Gabirol and Arlozorov. It does not establish that the line is operating. The developer presents a direct link from basement level -1, but the reviewed NTA page does not independently confirm a private internal DUO connection in the same language.

The new articles distinguish planned future access from present conditions. NTA was publishing Purple Line work notices near the junction in July 2026, so current disruption is part of the buying assessment. No unverified opening year or invented walking time is used.

## Duplicate-content and language rule

The new EN, FR, RU and AR files do not copy the Hebrew article paragraph by paragraph. They use the same frozen facts, then rebuild the explanation around the target audience's search language and decisions. Each has its own project-first title, opening, examples, practical questions and calls to action. Shared numbers remain identical. Cultural framing does not alter the evidence.

The existing Hebrew page and any existing suffix page are not edited by this content-only package. Publishing, replacement, canonical and hreflang behavior are outside the package and require a separate authorized workflow.

## Reuse decision

Retain through primary evidence: project identity, location, two towers, floor convention, 668 apartments, broad apartment mix, developer, mixed-use program, current dated construction and sales data.

Rewrite completely: title, opening, buyer journey, price explanation, timeline qualification, remote-purchase guidance, FAQs and image-alt suggestions.

Exclude completely: Sde Dov labels, demo inventory, prototype directions and areas, model-derived availability, borrowed interiors, unverified present prices, live-stock claims, guaranteed views, guaranteed transport access and delivery promises.
