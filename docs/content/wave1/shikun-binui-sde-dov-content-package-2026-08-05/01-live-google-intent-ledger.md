## Evidence boundary

This ledger records wording and result behavior observed in Google Chrome. It does not establish a fact about a Shikun Binui project, the Sde Dov district, an apartment, a price, a building, a view, availability, a timeline or a developer commitment. Search-result titles, advertisements, snippets, related searches and Google interface suggestions are intent evidence only. They may identify a question that deserves research. They may not answer it.

The working entity label in this research is `Shikun Binui Sde Dov`, taken from the assigned slug and query brief. A live Hebrew-page review corrected the content owner: the assigned entity is being treated as long-term rental housing on planning lot 109, not apartments for sale. This is a working entity correction pending the authoritative, date-stamped fact ledger. Before article writing, that ledger must establish the exact public project name, the lot relationship and the relationship to the corporate entities written as `Shikun Binui`, `Shikun & Binui`, `שיכון ובינוי` and `שיכון ובינוי נדל"ן`.

The earlier sale-query observations remain below because they document a real SERP mismatch. They are no longer primary keyword signals and must not drive the Title, H1, opening or calls to action.

This is a dated qualitative sample. It is not search-volume, ranking, traffic, conversion, keyword-difficulty or audience-demographic data.

## Chrome conditions

- The original sale-mismatch pass used 10 searches. The rental correction added 13 focused searches, for 23 recorded searches in total. All ran in the user's real Chrome session.
- Every query used `gl=il` and `pws=0`.
- English and French rental searches rendered as `en-IL` and `fr-IL`. During the rental correction, Google's signed-in Israel session rewrote the Russian and Arabic queries into Hebrew after initial load and exposed `html lang="iw"`, despite the requested `hl`, Russian `lr=lang_ru` and Arabic `lr=lang_ar` controls.
- Arabic rendered with `dir=rtl`.
- No CAPTCHA, unusual-traffic page or robot challenge appeared on any recorded query. One concise Russian retry switched to a Google apps widget before organic extraction; it is recorded as unusable rather than interpreted.
- The English exact-entity result footer visibly located the session in `Tel Aviv-Yafo - From your IP address` and stated that results were not personalized.
- The Chrome session was visibly signed in. `pws=0` was still present, but a single signed-in observation cannot represent an anonymous rank distribution.
- Language mixing remained material. English results included Hebrew advertising; French results included English titles; Arabic results included English, Hebrew, hotel and holiday-rental material.
- The Arabic `lr=lang_ar` retry did not improve entity precision. It produced largely irrelevant Arabic pages. The quoted Arabic entity retry without `lr` also failed to resolve a useful project result set.
