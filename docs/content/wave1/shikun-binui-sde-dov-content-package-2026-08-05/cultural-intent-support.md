# Shikun Binui Sde Dov multilingual cultural-intent support

Research date: 5 August 2026, Israel time  
Scope: English, French, Russian and Arabic intent strategy for future long-term-rental project content  
Output type: research and editorial brief only  
Live evidence companion: `01-live-google-intent-ledger.md`  

This is not a project article. It establishes no project fact. It converts wording observed in localized Google Chrome results into a decision-oriented editorial strategy. All future factual statements must come from a separate, authoritative and language-neutral source ledger.

## Working entity correction

A live Hebrew-page review corrected the intended content owner: the assigned entity is being treated as long-term rental housing on planning lot 109, not apartments for sale. This is a working correction, not a replacement for the authoritative fact ledger. That ledger must prove the tenure, exact lot, public project name and responsible legal entity before article writing.

The earlier sale-query research remains useful only because it documents how Google can misclassify this entity as a for-sale development or generic Sde Dov project. It must not drive the Title, H1, opening, FAQ or call to action. The primary action is tenant information or an application-interest request, never `buy`, `purchase`, `à vendre`, `покупка`, `للبيع` or an apartment reservation.

## Evidence labels

- **Observed evidence**: wording or result behavior directly visible in the recorded Chrome session.
- **Editorial inference**: a proposed way to answer the observed questions. It is not a demographic claim.
- **Fact gate**: a rule preventing a search result, competitor claim, portal number or cultural assumption from becoming project copy.
- **Unknown**: information not established by the live search observation.

A reader's language does not establish nationality, residence, legal status, income, religion, family structure, investment objective or migration plan. The four language versions should offer different decision pathways without assigning an identity to the visitor.

## Query-owner contract

The intended owner is one project-specific long-term-rental page per language for the entity currently labeled `Shikun Binui Sde Dov`. It should help a prospective tenant:

1. identify the exact project and distinguish it from the wider Sde Dov district;
2. understand only the published building and rental-apartment facts;
3. resolve current rent, availability, eligibility, lease-term, planning, construction and occupancy status;
4. evaluate location, orientation, plans and daily-life fit;
5. know what remains unpublished and what must be verified before applying or signing a lease;
6. reach a request-for-details action without false urgency or a claim that contact confirms eligibility, availability or acceptance.

The page must not become:

- a corporate profile of Shikun & Binui;
- a directory of every Sde Dov project;
- a generic Tel Aviv sale or rental portal;
- a Plot 109, rental-complex or neighboring-project page;
- a hotel, holiday-rental, serviced-apartment or relocation-service page;
- a promise of sea view, beachfront position, below-market rent, rent control, subsidy, eligibility, acceptance, occupancy date or current inventory;
- legal, benefits, eligibility, lease or immigration advice;
- an official developer page if the publisher is independent.

### Editorial inference

The English rental page should support three optional pathways:

1. a Tel Aviv household comparing long-term-rental options;
2. a relocating household that needs district and daily-life orientation;
3. a remote applicant who needs an application and lease-document checklist.

The page should use plain Israeli rental English. Define local planning and lease terms once. Keep monthly rent and charges in ILS. A foreign-currency illustration may appear only as dated, sourced and non-binding. Explain that Israeli room counts may not equal bedroom counts when plans are published.

For remote renting, answer practical questions without giving legal advice: who may apply, which documents are requested, whether income or guarantor checks apply, how identity and signatures are handled, which deposit or guarantee is required, what is included in the monthly rent, how indexation and renewal work, and how handover defects are recorded. Unknown conditions must remain unknown.

### English decision questions

- What exactly is Shikun Binui Sde Dov?
- Is it officially a long-term-rental project?
- Have current monthly rents and available apartment types been published?
- Which buildings, layouts and sizes are verified?
- How is the location different from the wider district label?

### English no-go language

Do not use `below-market rent`, `rent-controlled`, `subsidized`, `eligible for everyone`, `available now`, `no deposit`, `furnished`, `pets allowed`, `fixed rent`, `guaranteed move-in date` or `accepted applicant` without exact current evidence. Do not call a request for details an application, approval or apartment hold.

### Inférence éditoriale

La version française doit être utile à un locataire francophone en Israël comme à un ménage qui prépare son installation à distance. Elle ne doit supposer ni nationalité française, ni alyah, ni statut administratif, ni éligibilité particulière.

Le dossier locataire doit être concret: source des plans, loyer publié ou non, durée du bail, indexation, dépôt ou garantie, justificatifs, garant éventuel, charges, stationnement, animaux, renouvellement, préavis, état des lieux et remise des clés. Chaque condition inconnue reste explicitement inconnue. Il ne s'agit pas d'un conseil juridique.

Les surfaces restent en mètres carrés. Le loyer reste d'abord en ILS par mois. Une illustration en euros doit être datée, sourcée et qualifiée de non contractuelle. La rédaction distingue `pièces` et `chambres`; un nombre de pièces israélien ne devient pas automatiquement un nombre de chambres français.

### Наблюдаемый паттерн

Предыдущая выдача по продаже усиливала темы новостроек, покупки, инвестиций и вида на море. Теперь это только свидетельство ошибочного намерения. В арендном проходе Google перевел результаты на иврит даже при `hl=ru`, `lr=lang_ru` и Google.ru, поэтому органические русские фразы не были приняты.

### Редакционный вывод

Русская версия должна помогать арендатору сравнивать проверяемые условия. В начале нужны точная сущность проекта, подтвержденный долгосрочный формат, статус публикации ежемесячной платы и доступности, а также только подтвержденные числа.

Следует отдельно показывать базовую арендную плату, индексацию, депозит или гарантию, коммунальные платежи, эксплуатационные сборы, парковку, кладовую, срок договора, продление и досрочное прекращение, но только при наличии источника. Районный диапазон аренды не является ставкой проекта. Частное объявление не доказывает официальный свободный фонд.

`У моря` описывает контекст района. `Вид на море` относится к конкретному дому, этажу и ориентации. Эти вопросы остаются полезными для арендатора, но не меняют основной арендный интент.

### النمط الذي ظهر في البحث

كان البحث العربي الأقل استقرارًا. كانت عبارات البيع السابقة دليلًا على سوء تصنيف الكيان فقط. في البحث المصحح للإيجار، ترجم Google النتائج إلى العبرية رغم `hl=ar`، وأدى `lr=lang_ar` إلى ضوضاء غير مرتبطة. هذا لا يثبت غياب الطلب العربي، بل يفرض تعريفًا واضحًا جدًا للكيان وفصلًا صارمًا بين الإيجار السكني طويل الأمد والإقامة السياحية.

### الاستنتاج التحريري

يجب كتابة النسخة العربية بلغة عربية معيارية حديثة وطبيعية، مع عرض RTL كامل. يبقى `Shikun Binui Sde Dov` باللاتينية في بداية Title وH1، ثم يمكن تعريف `سدي دوف` بالعربية في النص. ينبغي تثبيت كتابة `تل أبيب` وعدم التبديل بين صيغ متعددة إلا إذا كان المصدر الرسمي يحتاج إلى `تل أبيب-يافا`.

مسار الأسرة يجب أن يكون عمليًا وليس عاطفيًا. يمكن مناقشة المساحة، توزيع الغرف، الخصوصية، الشرفة، المخزن، الموقف، المصاعد، الدخول بعربة أطفال، المساحة المحمية، المدارس، الحدائق، الرعاية الصحية، التسوق والنقل فقط إذا نشرت مصادر موثوقة معلومات تخص الموقع أو المشروع. وجود كلمة `للعائلات` في الاستعلام لا يثبت أن المشروع مناسب لكل أسرة.

الخصوصية تُشرح من خلال بيانات قابلة للفحص: عدد الشقق في الطابق إذا نُشر، اتجاه النوافذ، علاقة الشرفات، مسار الدخول، المرافق المشتركة، الضوضاء المحتملة، وطريقة الوصول إلى الموقف. لا تُستبدل هذه البيانات بعبارات ثقافية عامة.

يجب ألا يفترض النص أن القارئ مواطن إسرائيلي أو مقيم أو أجنبي. الأهلية والمستندات والضمانات قد تختلف حسب الجهة المديرة ووضع المتقدم. لذلك تُعرض الأسئلة التي ينبغي التحقق منها من دون تعميم.

يبقى الإيجار الشهري بالشيكل أولًا. إذا لم تُنشر القيمة أو مدة العقد أو التأمين أو الرسوم، يقال ذلك بوضوح. تستخدم الجداول نظام أرقام واحدًا بصورة متسقة.

### الإشارات الرئيسية

- **استعلام الملكية:** `Shikun Binui Sde Dov تل أبيب شقق للإيجار طويل الأمد`
- **Title:** `Shikun Binui Sde Dov تل أبيب - شقق للإيجار طويل الأمد والشروط والمخططات`
- **H1:** `Shikun Binui Sde Dov تل أبيب - شقق للإيجار طويل الأمد ودليل المستأجر`
- **المفردات الثانوية:** `شقق للإيجار طويل الأمد`, `الإيجار الشهري`, `عقد الإيجار`, `المخططات`, `طلب المستأجر`, `التأمين`, `الرسوم`, `شروط الأهلية`

### أسئلة القرار باللغة العربية

- ما هو مشروع Shikun Binui Sde Dov تحديدًا؟
- هل المشروع مخصص رسميًا للإيجار طويل الأمد؟
- هل نُشرت قيم الإيجار الشهرية والشقق المتاحة؟
- ما المباني والمساحات والمخططات التي تؤكدها المصادر؟
- من هو المالك أو المدير ومن هو طرف عقد الإيجار؟
- كيف يمكن التحقق من الموقع والاتجاه والإطلالة والمسافة إلى البحر؟
- هل تلائم المساحات والخدمات المنشورة احتياجات أسرة بعينها؟
- ما المستندات والضمانات وشروط الأهلية التي ينبغي التحقق منها قبل تقديم الطلب؟

### عبارات عربية ممنوعة من دون دليل

لا تستخدم `إيجار أقل من السوق`, `إيجار مدعوم`, `متاح للجميع`, `شقق متاحة الآن`, `من دون تأمين`, `مفروش`, `الحيوانات مسموحة`, `إيجار ثابت`, `موعد سكن مضمون` أو `تم قبول الطلب` من دون مصدر حديث ودقيق. طلب المعلومات ليس طلب استئجار ولا موافقة ولا حجزًا. تجنب كلمات الحجز السياحي والأسعار الليلية وتقييمات النزلاء ومرافق الفنادق.

## Cross-language fact contract

All four future articles must be built from one neutral fact ledger. Cultural adaptation may change emphasis, explanation, examples, FAQ order and calls to action. It may not change the facts.

### Mandatory fields for every publishable fact

1. canonical fact ID;
2. exact entity and scope: company, project, building, floor, apartment type, unit, district or city;
3. value and unit;
4. evidence class;
5. primary URL;
6. publisher and document title;
7. publication date and verification date;
8. exact excerpt retained for audit;
9. status: verified, conflicting, superseded, unpublished or estimate;
10. allowed wording and prohibited extrapolation;
11. four-language rendering check.

### Source order

1. municipal, planning, permit, cadastral and statutory records for legal and planning status;
2. an exact official project, owner or managing-party source for rental tenure, plans, amenities, application rules, lease conditions and availability status;
3. public-company filings where applicable;
4. attributable reputable reporting for dated events or statements;
5. current portals and brokerage listings only as labeled market or listing evidence, never official project availability or lease terms;
6. an editorial estimate only when its date, inputs, method and scope are visible.

Google titles, advertisements, AI summaries, snippets, related searches, autocomplete, social captions and copied portal text are excluded as project-fact sources.

### Unknown wording

- English: `This information has not been published in the sources reviewed.`
- French: `Cette information n'a pas été publiée dans les sources consultées.`
- Russian: `Эта информация не опубликована в проверенных источниках.`
- Arabic: `لم تُنشر هذه المعلومة في المصادر التي تمت مراجعتها.`

### Estimate labels

- English: `Estimate, non-binding.`
- French: `Estimation non contractuelle.`
- Russian: `Оценка, не является обязательной.`
- Arabic: `تقدير غير ملزم.`

The label must sit beside the number. An estimate needs a date, inputs, method, scope and a clear reason for inclusion. Otherwise it should be omitted.

### Rent evidence classes

- official current project monthly rent;
- official historical launch rent;
- current private-listing asking rent;
- published signed-lease rent when lawfully available;
- district-level published rent range;
- editorial rent estimate.

Never merge these classes into one range. Never write `rent from` from a competitor, a single portal listing or a historical campaign. Deposit, guarantee, parking, management charges and utilities remain separate from base rent.

### View and distance gates

`Sea view`, `vue mer`, `вид на море` and `إطلالة بحرية` are apartment, floor or orientation claims unless an exact source defines a wider scope. A coastal district does not prove a view. A rendering does not prove a permanent view. A distance needs a verified project origin, destination, route type and measurement date.

## Opening-paragraph gate

Before publication, every language opening must:

1. begin naturally with `Shikun Binui Sde Dov` and Tel Aviv;
2. make long-term residential-rental intent explicit;
3. distinguish the exact project from the company and district;
4. state current rent, availability, eligibility and lease-term publication status immediately;
5. include only verified decision-relevant numbers;
6. tell the reader what tenant decision the article will help resolve;
7. avoid SEO commentary, internal QA language, research disclaimers and application urgency.

If no reliable numerical fact is available, do not invent one to make the paragraph appear data-rich.

## Native-language quality gates

### English

- Use natural Israeli property English and define local terms once.
- Keep resident, relocation and overseas routes optional.
- Check for copied competitor adjectives and unsupported claims about rent, eligibility or availability.

### French

- Use native French syntax and real property vocabulary.
- Keep `pièces`, `chambres`, `surface intérieure`, balcony and storage categories distinct.
- Make France, alyah and installation references conditional; do not turn a rental into a second-home ownership pathway.
- Require native review for calques and contract vocabulary.

### Russian

- Use consistent transliteration and clean Latin-Cyrillic spacing.
- Keep `арендная плата проекта`, `плата в объявлении`, `районный диапазон` and `оценка` distinct.
- Require native review for case endings, hyphenation and financial phrasing.

### Arabic

- Render the whole article, tables, FAQ, forms and captions RTL.
- Use fluent Modern Standard Arabic, not translated Hebrew sentence order.
- Test bidi punctuation around the Latin project name.
- Use one consistent spelling of `سدي دوف`, one digit convention per table and no Hebrew fallback.
- Require a native Arabic editor to remove machine language and hospitality drift.

## Pre-writing acceptance gates

No full article should begin until all of these pass:

1. The exact project identity is connected to an authoritative project or statutory source.
2. The role of Shikun & Binui is explicitly verified.
3. The long-term-rental tenure and planning-lot 109 relationship are proven, while neighboring sale and rental projects remain excluded.
4. A single four-language fact ledger exists.
5. Every number has an evidence class, scope and date.
6. Current rent, availability, lease length, eligibility, deposit, fees and application status are known or explicitly unpublished.
7. Building, floor and apartment counts are reconciled or marked conflicting.
8. The project map point is verified before distance claims.
9. View wording is scoped to the correct building, floor or apartment.
10. Planning, permit, construction, rental launch, application and occupancy status remain separate.
11. Title and H1 are project-first in all four languages.
12. Every opening resolves entity, long-term-rental intent, rent status and availability status.
13. The same facts and source set appear in every language.
14. Native review removes language leakage, literal translation and cultural stereotypes.
15. The Arabic page contains no hotel, holiday-rental or booking drift.
16. The French page does not assume France residence or alyah.
17. The Russian page promises no below-market rent, acceptance or fixed future payment.
18. The English page separates remote-applicant questions from universal project facts.

## Final editorial direction

The strongest corrected pattern is project first, Tel Aviv immediately visible and long-term-rental intent explicit:

- `Shikun Binui Sde Dov Tel Aviv long-term rental apartments`
- `Shikun Binui Sde Dov Tel Aviv location longue durée`
- `Shikun Binui Sde Dov Тель-Авив долгосрочная аренда квартир`
- `Shikun Binui Sde Dov تل أبيب شقق للإيجار طويل الأمد`

English should prioritize relocation, application documents and lease-condition clarity. French should prioritize a controlled `dossier locataire` and room-count conventions. Russian should prioritize rent classes, deposits and the full monthly-cost picture. Arabic should prioritize clean entity resolution, full RTL native prose, household-use evidence and a hard boundary against hotels, serviced apartments and holiday rentals.

These are decision pathways, not stereotypes. Search evidence supplies the vocabulary and questions. Authoritative sources must supply every answer.
