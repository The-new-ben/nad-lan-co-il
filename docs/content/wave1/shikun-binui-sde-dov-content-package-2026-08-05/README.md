## Scope

This is a content-only package for four foreign-language project articles. It does not publish or modify WordPress posts, the Hebrew project page, plugin or theme code, shared engines, media, redirects, canonicals or hreflang output.

The intended public article files are:

- `shikun-binui-sde-dov-en.md` for slug `shikun-binui-sde-dov-en`
- `shikun-binui-sde-dov-fr.md` for slug `shikun-binui-sde-dov-fr`

The alt suggestions remain outside article body copy and may be used only with matching rights-cleared imagery. No manual canonical or hreflang values are supplied because the platform derives the language cluster from the suffix slugs.

## Locked project identity and facts

- The entity is a Shikun & Binui group long-term-rental project at planning lot 109 in Sde Dov, Tel Aviv-Yafo. Lot 109 is a planning designation, not a postal address.
- The published program contains 324 rental homes in three buildings.
- The Tel Aviv-Yafo municipal record dated 8 April 2025 describes a 20-year long-term-rental program, with 50% of the homes at regulated rent for eligible tenants and 50% at free-market rent. The 20-year period belongs to the approved project program and is not the promised length of an individual lease.
- The Shikun & Binui exchange disclosure dated 17 March 2026 states that a full building permit existed, excavation and shoring had been completed, and structural work had begun through a main contractor.
- Published height descriptions conflict. The April 2025 municipal record describes a 40-floor tower and two 9-floor buildings under its stated counting conventions, while December 2025 reporting describes a 38-floor tower and two 8-floor buildings. The inspected March 2026 disclosure does not resolve the count. Every language must preserve the conflict rather than choose one figure.

## Unpublished information and exclusions

No verified current project source publishes monthly rents, available-unit inventory, individual lease duration or renewal terms, project-specific application and eligibility procedures, a postal street address, or a guaranteed apartment view. These fields must remain unpublished until a current authorized source supplies them.

The selectable apartment cards, sample unit details, generic model, interior tour and view tools on the existing page are illustrative demonstrations. They are not evidence of real units, availability, plans, areas, prices, floor-specific views or the exact project building. None may enter the four articles as a project fact.

Lot 109 must not be mixed with lot 106, its environmental information, or the separately reported 511-home Shikun & Binui project.
