## Source register and permitted use

### S1 - Tel Aviv-Yafo Licensing Authority protocol

- Authority: primary municipal record.
- Document date: 8 April 2025.
- Permit request: 1109-24; building file 2436-014; information request 23-01607.
- Site reference: plan lot 109 in planning unit 8 of the new Sde Dov quarter; the record also lists parcel/lot data 15/6634 and an application area of 5,221 sqm.
- Applicant named in the record: `ריט ש.ב נדל"ן (נכסים) בע"מ`.
- Program in the licensing request: long-term rental for 20 years; 50% of homes at regulated rent for eligible tenants and 50% at free-market rent.
- Configuration described in the request: three separate buildings over four shared basement levels; tower A with 40 floors including ground and technical floors and 229 homes; building B with 9 floors including ground and roof floors and 44 homes; building C with 9 floors including ground and roof floors and 51 homes; 324 homes in total, with residential and commercial uses.

## Unresolved conflict: 40/9 versus 38/8

This must remain visible in every language if exact heights are discussed.

- Primary municipal record dated 8 April 2025: tower A 40 floors including ground and technical floors; buildings B and C 9 floors including ground and roof floors.
- Current architect page: 40-floor tower and two contextual buildings.
- Two reports dated 11 December 2025: 38-floor tower and two 8-floor buildings.
- Current March 2026 exchange disclosure confirms a full permit and structural work but gives no floor count.
- It is possible that counting conventions or a later permit revision explain the difference, but that is an inference, not a verified fact. Do not choose a number merely because two secondary reports repeat it. Safe wording: "The April 2025 municipal record describes a 40-floor tower and two 9-floor contextual buildings, while December 2025 permit reporting described 38 and 8; the current full-permit floor schedule was not available in the inspected official disclosure."

## Rental-versus-sale lock

- The project must be written for prospective renters or readers assessing a rental asset, not as a normal contractor apartment-sale page.
- Press discussion of a corporate petition or possible future structural transaction does not create apartment inventory for sale.
- Until an official current sale offer appears, reject purchase CTAs, purchase price estimates, "choose your apartment to buy", mortgage calculators, purchase-tax framing and buyer-availability claims on this project page.
- The queue requires four standard internal resources in every language. On this rental project, the mortgage calculator, purchase-tax calculator and new-project buying guide may appear only once each inside a clearly general rent-versus-buy comparison. They must never be presented as tools for purchasing a unit in lot 109. The city page may be used for broader Tel Aviv context, not as proof of a project fact.
