## Shared fact contract

The English, French, Russian and Arabic articles publish the same underlying Park Bavli facts:

- Official brand page: `parkbavli.co.il`.
- Official-site presentation: The Plaza International and Gal Nauer as `Architect & Developer`, plus the four-elements design concept.
- Tower 1 municipal identity: HaRav Nissim 9, plan 1770A, building file 1315-009, 44 storeys and 153 units after an approved 2022 merger.
- Municipal-party names remain tied to their cited municipal context rather than being generalized as the seller of every apartment.
- Dated January 2025 media description: two 44-storey towers and about 340 apartments.
- Dated exceptional transaction: approximately NIS 43 million, roughly 360 square metres built, about 145 square metres of terraces and floor 44 of the second tower.
- Dated inventory context: about 28 new units remained in the first tower in 2020; in January 2025 one other penthouse and about 30 apartments with four rooms or more remained according to the cited report.
- Dated May 2026 promotional statement about operating common facilities, clearly identified as promotional rather than an independent inspection.
- No current project-wide inventory, asking-price list, fee schedule, unit schedule, delivery statement, parking table, storage table or view guarantee was verified.

The different figures remain attached to their tower, document and date. No article combines them into a false total or presents historical stock as current stock.

## Legitimate cultural differences

Parity does not mean identical paragraphs. The language versions answer different verified buyer questions:

- English gives more space to relocation, foreign-buyer coordination, financing and investment scenarios.
- French gives more space to a France-to-Israel purchase file, remote documents, representation and controlled decision stages.
- Russian gives more space to yield inputs, liquidity, operating costs, currency exposure and distance checks.
- Arabic gives more space to family routines, usable space, education, access, privacy and exact apartment rights.

These differences change emphasis and vocabulary only. They do not change the project facts.
