# Park Bavli - locked source ledger

Research cut-off: 2026-08-05, Israel time  
Scope: locked fact contract for the four multilingual content files. It does not establish current inventory, a current price list or publication status.  
Core rule: every number is tied to the entity and date supported by its source. A number about one tower, one apartment, the wider Bavli-Dekel plan or a different branded tower must not be generalized to Park Bavli.

## 1. Entity resolution

### 1.1 Official brand owner for the article

The live project-brand page found in Google and opened successfully in Chrome is:

- [BAVLI - PARK BAVLI](https://parkbavli.co.il/)

It presents "Park Bavli Private Residences" in Tel Aviv, attributes the presentation to The Plaza International, names Gal Nauer as "Architect & Developer", and describes the four-elements apartment-design concept. It does not publish an address, tower count, floor count, unit count, completion date, delivery date, current asking price or current apartment inventory.

`https://parkbavli.com/` is not an equivalent source. Real-Chrome navigation on 2026-08-05 ended at `https://parkbavli.com/lander` with the title `parkbavli.com/lander`. Treat the `.com` domain as parked or otherwise non-authoritative. Do not link to it as the project site.

### 1.2 Municipal identity for Tower 1

A Tel Aviv-Yafo licensing decision dated 2022 identifies:

- "Tower 1 in the Park Bavli project"
- a 44-storey residential tower
- a post-merger total of 153 housing units
- the governing Bavli-Dekel plan, 1770A
- Elad Israel Residences Ltd. and A.M.T.S.H. Investments Ltd., referred to together in that municipal agreement context as the Elad Group

A separate municipal licensing record identifies the address as HaRav Nissim 9, Bavli, Tel Aviv-Yafo, building file 1315-009, and records a 7,912 square metre plot across the listed block/parcel interests. The address and plot data belong to the municipal building file for Tower 1, not automatically to every tower marketed within the wider Park Bavli or Bavli-Dekel area.

### 1.3 Two-tower marketing and transaction scope

A dated Ynet report published in connection with a January 2025 penthouse transaction described a project of two residential towers, each 44 storeys, with approximately 340 apartments in total. That is a dated media description, not a current municipal inventory certificate. It must not be combined with the 153-unit Tower 1 municipal figure to produce an invented total.

### 1.4 Wider Bavli-Dekel complex is not the same fact unit

Search results repeatedly use "Park Bavli" for a wider development area that also contains or is planned to contain separately branded towers and separate lots, including Beresheet Bavli and Masterpiece Bavli. Planning and press sources variously discuss six or seven towers and changing unit totals for the wider plan. Those figures are not accepted as facts about the official Park Bavli brand page.

The future four-language articles must define their subject in the opening as the named Park Bavli residences and must label Tower 1, Tower 2, a resale listing, the wider Bavli-Dekel plan and a neighboring branded tower separately.

## 6. Price and transaction discipline

- Never convert the reported NIS 43 million penthouse sale into a project average.
- Never present a listing asking price as a completed transaction.
- Never compare apartments solely on a simple NIS-per-square-metre division when terrace area, finish, floor, rights and date differ.
- Any current comparable set must be rebuilt from the government database using the exact address or block/parcel and a stated search date.
- A registry extract and the government transaction database serve different functions: the extract checks registered rights and encumbrances; the transaction database provides reported sale information.
- Any foreign-currency calculation must name the exchange-rate source and date and be labelled as a calculation, not a price quote.
- Any future estimate must be labelled exactly as follows: English `estimate, non-binding`; French `estimation non contractuelle`; Russian `оценка, не является обязательной`; Arabic `تقدير غير ملزم`.

## 7. Buyer-process fact gates

Before a future article states that an apartment is available or suitable, obtain or verify:

1. exact tower and municipal address
2. exact apartment and floor
3. seller identity and authority to sell
4. current Land Registry extract or the applicable rights document
5. contractual area and the definition of every published area
6. registered or contractual rights to parking and storage
7. balcony or terrace area and legal status
8. finish, occupancy and renovation status
9. current building-management agreement, budget and charges
10. current asking price, payment terms and what is included
11. recorded transactions selected with a documented comparable methodology
12. purchase-tax result for the buyer's actual legal circumstances
13. financing approval for the buyer, not a generic national ratio
14. source-of-funds, transfer and remote-signing requirements confirmed by the buyer's professionals

## 9. Research conclusion

The strongest verified public fact is not a project-wide tower or unit total. It is the municipal identity of Tower 1: HaRav Nissim 9, plan 1770A, 44 storeys and 153 units after the 2022 approved apartment merger. The official brand page supports the Park Bavli name, Plaza International presentation, Gal Nauer attribution and design concept. Dated media supports a two-tower sales context and specific transactions, but not current 2026 inventory.

Future content should therefore lead with exact entity scope, not a grand total. Current availability, prices, unit mix, management charges and delivery status remain unpublished or unverified as of the research cut-off.
