# Park Bavli Tel Aviv apartments for sale - prices, apartment details and buyer checks

Park Bavli Tel Aviv apartments for sale belong to a residential development with built and occupied evidence, but the verified status must be read by tower and date. Municipal records identify Tower 1 at HaRav Nissim 9 as a 44-storey building with 153 homes after an approved apartment merger in 2022. A January 2025 report described two 44-storey towers with about 340 apartments in total, yet that media figure is not a current inventory certificate. The official project site did not publish a live apartment list or a project-wide price list as of 5 August 2026. Buying at Park Bavli therefore starts with one exact unit: rooms, documented area, floor, direction, balcony, parking, storage, condition, seller and registered rights. Park Bavli is also not a name for every property in the wider Bavli neighborhood. Whether the purchase is for relocation, a primary home, a second home or investment, the asking price only becomes useful after those details are verified.

## Overview

### What Park Bavli means in a current apartment search

The official site uses the Latin name Park Bavli and the description Park Bavli Private Residences. It places the residences in Tel Aviv, presents The Plaza International, and identifies Gal Nauer as "Architect & Developer" [S01]. Those statements establish the brand language used by the project site. They do not identify the seller of a particular apartment, prove that a unit is currently available, or replace the legal documents for a sale.

The word Bavli creates an immediate comparison problem. It can refer to the neighborhood, the Bavli-Dekel planning area, Park Bavli, or a separately branded development nearby. A listing headed "Bavli apartment" may have no connection to Park Bavli. A high-rise apartment elsewhere in northern Tel Aviv is not Park Bavli inventory merely because a portal places it in the same search results. Before comparing price, ask for the tower name, municipal address, apartment identifier and seller. If those basic fields are missing, the rest of the listing is difficult to verify.

The strongest public building facts concern Tower 1. A Tel Aviv-Yafo licensing decision dated 14 November 2022 calls it Tower 1 in the Park Bavli project. The decision records a 44-storey residential tower and 153 housing units after approval to combine two existing units [S02]. A separate municipal record connects the building file to HaRav Nissim 9, Bavli, plan 1770A, building file 1315-009 and a plot entry of 7,912 square metres across the listed block and parcel interests [S03].

Each of those numbers has a boundary. The 153-unit figure belongs to Tower 1 after the approved merger described in the 2022 decision. The 7,912-square-metre entry belongs to the municipal building-file context. It is not the apartment area, a private residents' park, or a figure that can be divided among apartments. HaRav Nissim 9 is supported for Tower 1. It should not be presented as the proven address of every building associated with the Park Bavli name.

A Ynet report published in the context of a January 2025 penthouse transaction described Park Bavli as two residential towers, each 44 storeys, with approximately 340 apartments in total [S04]. That is useful dated project context. It is not a current municipal register, and the word "approximately" matters. The 153 homes in Tower 1 must not be added to the reported 340. One figure describes a particular tower after a specific planning change; the other describes a two-tower project in a media report.

### Built evidence does not answer the seller question

Park Bavli should not be reduced to the labels "new project" or "resale project." The first tower was described as occupied in a 2020 report, and a 2026 branded feature presented common facilities as operating [S05][S06]. At the same time, an apartment offered today could be held by a private owner, a company or another rights holder. It could be previously occupied, never occupied, renovated, finished to an earlier specification, or sold in another condition. The transaction type must come from the seller's authority and the proposed contract, not from the age of the tower alone.

That distinction changes the evidence a buyer needs. A current developer or professional-seller offer may involve specifications, statutory protections, payment arrangements and representations that differ from a private resale. A resale requires careful review of the seller's rights, alterations, possession, existing occupants, fixtures and building liabilities. When the seller category is unclear, do not let the phrase "new apartment" settle it. Ask who signs, under what authority, and what exactly will be transferred.

The official project site does not publish a current unit schedule. It also does not publish a current project-wide starting price, current management charges, a complete unit mix, or a delivery date for each possible offer. This is not a reason to fill the gaps with portal averages. It is a reason to request a complete unit file before deciding whether the apartment deserves a visit.

### The apartment file that makes a listing useful

A serious first response should identify the tower, exact address, floor, apartment number or other identifier, room arrangement, every published area and its definition, balcony or terrace, direction, current condition, occupation status, parking, storage, asking price, payment expectations and seller type. Photographs should be current and tied to that unit. A floor plan should be legible and matched to the home being offered.

For a local buyer, these details prevent wasted visits. For an overseas buyer, they form the minimum remote-screening package. A polished video can show light and finishes, but it cannot prove title, area, parking rights or permission for alterations. A document can establish a right, but it may not reveal water damage, noise or poor workmanship. Legal review and physical inspection answer different questions, and both matter.

If an offer is presented as remaining first-sale inventory, the buyer can also use the [guide to buying an apartment from a developer](https://nad-lan.co.il/new-projects/) to organize contract and specification questions. The guide does not decide whether a Park Bavli unit is developer inventory. That status must be established first.

The most useful early question is not "How much is Park Bavli?" There is no single home called Park Bavli. Ask instead: "What am I buying at this price?" A complete answer should describe the unit, the rights, the condition, the attached spaces, the ongoing costs and the seller's obligations. Only then can another apartment become a meaningful comparison.

## Location and surroundings

### Tower 1 has a supported municipal address

The municipal record for Tower 1 identifies HaRav Nissim 9 in Bavli, Tel Aviv-Yafo [S03]. This is the clearest public address anchor in the reviewed material. Keep its scope intact. An offer in another building may use a different entrance or address. Ask the seller to reconcile the marketing name with the municipal and registration records before relying on a map pin.

Park Bavli sits within the urban context of Bavli and the Bavli-Dekel plan area. Other branded towers and planning lots exist in the broader area. Their floors, apartments, facilities and schedules cannot be borrowed for Park Bavli. This matters when a map, broker page or neighborhood portal groups nearby developments under one heading. The buyer needs the route to the actual entrance, not the center point of a broad project label.

The official Park Bavli site publishes three distances: 1.4 km to the city center, 2.7 km to Tel Aviv Beach and 0.6 km to HaYarkon Park [S01]. The site does not explain the measurement method, starting entrance, route or travel mode. Treat them as distances stated by the project site, not guaranteed walking times. A buyer should test the journey from the entrance and parking access that the apartment actually uses.

### Park access and daily use

The Tel Aviv-Yafo Municipality identifies HaYarkon Park as one of the city's major parks [S08]. Access to a large open space may be relevant for walking, exercise, time with children or simply a less enclosed daily environment. It does not prove that an apartment faces the park. A Park Bavli address and a park view are separate facts.

Test park access on foot rather than from a sales map. Start at the residential lobby. Note crossings, slopes, shade, pavement condition and the route after dark. Repeat the test with the practical burden that matters to the household, such as a stroller, bicycle, dog, groceries or reduced mobility. A nominally short distance can feel different when the route crosses traffic or requires a detour.

The same method applies to food shopping, healthcare, schools, public transport and regular appointments. A relocation buyer may need to understand the first weeks before a car, local account or familiar routine is in place. A second-home owner may care more about airport transfers, deliveries, building access during an absence and nearby services that operate when the apartment is used. An investor needs to know what a plausible tenant would value rather than projecting the owner's habits onto the market.

For broader city context, the [Tel Aviv-Yafo city page](https://nad-lan.co.il/cities/%d7%aa%d7%9c-%d7%90%d7%91%d7%99%d7%91-%d7%99%d7%a4%d7%95/) can help frame other neighborhoods and projects. The apartment decision still depends on the actual route, exposure and services around the building.

### Community services and schools

The municipal Bavli Community Center says it serves the neighborhood and surrounding area with education, enrichment, culture, arts, sports and programs for different ages [S07]. That supports a local-service context. It does not guarantee a place in a course, a particular language of instruction or a fixed schedule. Buyers who expect to use it should check the current program, enrolment conditions and route.

School claims require greater care. The municipality explains that first-grade assignment is connected to a child's registered residential address. A transfer is a separate request and is not guaranteed [S09]. It would be misleading to advertise automatic access to a named school on the strength of the project name. A household should verify the exact apartment address, intended school year, registration dates and current municipal rules.

This is especially relevant to a family relocating from abroad. Citizenship, immigration status, previous schooling and arrival date may affect the practical process, but the project does not answer those questions. The apartment can be assessed for layout and location while education eligibility is confirmed directly with the appropriate authority. Keeping the two workstreams separate prevents a housing deposit from being used as a substitute for school confirmation.

### Roads, current transport and planned transport

An apartment in northern-central Tel Aviv can offer useful access while still exposing the buyer to traffic, noise and variable travel times. Drive the morning and evening routes that matter. Test the exit from the underground parking, the point where a taxi can stop, the delivery route and the pedestrian approach. A map estimate does not show queueing inside a garage or the time needed to reach the street from a high floor.

NTA lists Tel Aviv lines and stations and includes Namir-Pinkas and Tel Aviv Center among planned M1 stations [S10]. Planned is the operative word. The reviewed public material does not support a guaranteed opening date, a fixed walk from Park Bavli, or a promised effect on apartment value. The purchase should work with transport available now. Future infrastructure can be monitored as a possible benefit, not priced as certain.

Check the apartment's exposure at more than one hour. Traffic sound can change between daytime, evening and the weekend. Height may alter the character of noise rather than eliminate it. Wind, glazing, ventilation openings, mechanical equipment and neighboring construction all affect comfort. Open and close windows during the visit. Stand in bedrooms as well as the living room. A quiet lobby tells little about a high-floor facade.

### Beach, city and view language

The project's stated 2.7 km distance to Tel Aviv Beach places the coast within the wider city setting, but Park Bavli is not established by the reviewed sources as a beachfront building. If a listing promises a sea view, ask for unzoomed photographs from the exact apartment, the direction of each main window and a visit at normal eye level. A thin strip of water from one corner is not the same product as an open view from the principal living space.

The same discipline applies to park and skyline views. Record what is visible from the living room, primary bedroom, other bedrooms and outdoor area. Note structures below, neighboring towers, undeveloped lots and cranes. Review planning around the relevant direction with qualified advisers. No one can guarantee that a city view remains unchanged merely because it is open on the viewing day.

Location fit is personal. Park access may matter more than beach access. A quiet residential setting may matter more than being above a retail street. A buyer who expects to walk everywhere should test that assumption in August heat, winter rain and evening traffic. A buyer who drives should understand the actual parking right and garage circulation before treating road access as an advantage.

### A location test for an overseas buyer

Remote viewing can narrow the list, but it should be structured. Ask for one continuous video that starts outside the correct entrance, enters the lobby, uses the lift, reaches the apartment and then shows the route back to the assigned parking or storage space. Request separate daytime and evening window recordings when light or noise drives the price.

The person filming should state the date, apartment, floor and direction without revealing unnecessary personal information. Ask them to show the street name, building number, lift panel and view from each main room. This does not replace an in-person visit or professional inspection. It prevents a montage from blending another lobby, a model apartment and a more attractive view into one presentation.

Once the apartment passes that screen, test four trips: park, weekly shopping, a key work or family destination, and the beach or cultural area the buyer expects to use. The goal is not to prove that Park Bavli is "central." It is to learn whether this address supports the buyer's own week.

## The towers and apartments

### Tower counts must remain scoped

Tower 1 has the clearest municipal profile: 44 storeys and 153 housing units after an approved combination of two existing units in 2022 [S02]. The municipal file ties that tower to HaRav Nissim 9 and plan 1770A [S03]. Ynet's January 2025 report described a two-tower project, both towers at 44 storeys, with approximately 340 apartments across the project [S04].

These statements can coexist because they describe different scopes. The municipal figure is a dated count for Tower 1 after a particular approval. The media figure is an approximate project description. It would be wrong to turn them into 493 apartments, to divide 340 evenly between the towers, or to claim that 340 is the exact registered total today. It would also be wrong to import tower counts from the wider Bavli-Dekel plan or neighboring brands.

If a listing says Tower 2, ask for its official address and registration details. If it says only "Park Bavli," do not assume the building from the photographs. Exterior angles can be confusing, and a listing portal may reuse a project photograph for several apartments. The contract and rights record must identify the building and unit without relying on branding.

### Room count is not a bedroom count

Israeli listings commonly count the living room within the total number of rooms. International buyers should not translate a four-room apartment into four bedrooms. Request a written schedule that names the living room, full bedrooms, smaller rooms, protected room if present, bathrooms, kitchen, utility spaces, circulation, balcony, terrace and internal storage. Compare that schedule with the approved plan and the apartment as built.

Usability matters more than the headline count. Measure bedrooms after fixed cabinetry, door swings and circulation are considered. Check whether a smaller room can support the intended use. Look at privacy between sleeping and living areas, bathroom access, kitchen ventilation, laundry placement and storage for everyday items. A large published area can still contain long corridors or awkward geometry.

Changes made after construction need their own file. Compare the current layout with the approved plan. Ask who performed the work, whether approvals were needed, what documentation exists and whether building systems were affected. A beautiful renovation is not evidence that walls, wet rooms, glazing or mechanical work were authorized or well executed.

### Every area needs a definition

An advertisement may show one large square-metre figure without explaining what it includes. That number might refer to built area, an internal measurement, a contractual measure, a municipal figure, a registry entry, a balcony or terrace added separately, or a commercial convention. There is no safe conversion rule.

Create an area table for the unit:

| Area field | Document or measurement to request | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Interior usable area | Current measured plan with method stated | Shows how much space can actually be furnished and occupied |
| Built or contractual area | Sale agreement, specification or seller document defining the term | Supports comparison with the price and seller's representation |
| Registered area | Current applicable rights document | Helps identify what the legal record states |
| Municipal area | Current municipal record or charge basis where relevant | May use a different measurement method |
| Balcony or terrace | Approved plan, rights documents and measurement | Separates outdoor space from enclosed living area |
| Parking and storage | Registration or contractual identifiers and plans | Confirms attached rights rather than assumed access |

Numbers may differ without one of them automatically being fraudulent. The task is to understand the definitions and resolve material discrepancies before signing. A lender or valuer may also use a different basis. If the purchase depends on financing, provide the exact documents early enough for the bank's assessment.

### Floor, direction and actual view

A high floor is not a complete description. Confirm how the floor is identified in the building, municipal file, approved plan, rights documents, lift and listing. Entrance levels, service floors and local conventions can create inconsistent labels. The apartment's vertical position should be traceable across the documents.

Direction affects daylight, heat, wind, noise and view. Ask for a marked plan showing north and the orientation of every principal opening. Then stand at each window without zoom. Record the view at normal eye level and from seated height where relevant. A balcony view may not be visible from the sofa or bedroom.

No source supports a claim that every Park Bavli apartment has a park, city or sea view. A high floor can face another tower. An open facade can change as nearby construction proceeds. A lower floor may offer a green outlook that suits one buyer better than a distant panorama. Price the actual exposure, not a project-wide adjective.

### Balcony, parking and storage

Outdoor space should be described with the same care as the interior. Establish whether it is a balcony or terrace, its measured area, access points, orientation, shading, wind exposure, drainage, approved status and permitted use. Check what is included in the sale. Furniture or planters shown in photographs may belong to the seller and may not remain.

Parking cannot be inferred from the building's market position. Ask for the exact parking-space identifier, location, dimensions, access method, registration or contractual status, and any mechanical system or usage restriction. Test the route with the vehicle likely to use it. Tandem, narrow, accessible, electric-charging and visitor arrangements are different products.

Storage needs the same proof. Identify the storage unit, measure it, inspect humidity and access, and match it to the rights documents. A cupboard inside the apartment is not equivalent to a registered external storeroom. A shared room is not a private right. If an asking price relies on parking or storage, their status belongs in the written offer and contract.

### Interior condition and four-elements branding

The official Park Bavli site presents apartment concepts inspired by fire, air, water and earth [S01]. This is a distinctive part of the brand presentation. It does not prove that the offered apartment was completed in one of those schemes, remains in its original condition, or includes furniture and art shown in promotional material.

Describe the actual interior as shell-condition, finished, occupied, renovated or another documented state. Avoid "designer apartment" unless the seller provides enough detail to understand what was designed, by whom, when, and what is included. A branded concept can be commercially relevant, but the buyer will own the physical condition delivered under the contract.

Inspect stone, woodwork, cabinetry, sanitary fittings, glazing, shading, lighting, air-conditioning, electrical capacity, plumbing and waterproofing. Check maintenance records for systems that serve the apartment. If the home has stood empty, test it as carefully as an occupied resale. Unused equipment can age, seals can fail and hidden moisture can develop without obvious daily symptoms.

### Common areas and facilities

A Ynet Ymag promotional feature dated 19 May 2026 described the pool, spa and common areas as operating and presented the project as available without a construction wait [S05]. This is a dated branded statement, not an independent technical inspection and not a promise that every service operates on the buyer's preferred schedule.

Ask building management for the current facility list, access rules, opening hours, guest policy, staffing, maintenance closures and cost allocation. Visit the actual spaces. Check whether a facility is included in ordinary charges, billed separately or limited by rules. A photograph of a pool says nothing about current access, temperature, supervision, repair plans or the share of cost borne by the unit.

Common areas affect both enjoyment and budget. Review the condition of lobbies, lifts, corridors, parking levels, service areas and external landscaping. Ask about lift maintenance, major contracts, reserve planning, insurance and recent or proposed capital work. A premium lobby can be attractive while still carrying significant long-term costs.

### A complete apartment inspection

Begin outside. Confirm the entrance, security process, delivery route, waste arrangements, parking access and path to storage. Time the lift journey. Note waiting time, lift changes, service-lift availability and what happens during maintenance. For a high-floor home, vertical circulation is part of daily life.

Inside the apartment, compare every wall and opening with the plan. Test water pressure, drainage, hot water, electrical outlets, shutters, windows, air-conditioning zones and accessible appliances. Look for staining, cracking, condensation and signs of repairs. Listen with mechanical systems on and off. Ask an appropriately qualified inspector to assess matters beyond ordinary observation.

Visit at least twice when light, wind, traffic or view materially affects the price. A midday visit can hide evening glare or road noise. A still day says little about a high terrace in stronger wind. A staged interior can make circulation look easier than it is. Bring measurements for beds, tables and storage rather than relying on wide-angle photography.

## Prices and estimates

### No verified current project-wide price

The official Park Bavli site did not publish a current project-wide price list or a complete current apartment inventory as of 5 August 2026 [S01]. That means there is no responsible single answer to "What does an apartment in Park Bavli cost?" The price depends on the exact unit, seller, date, legal rights, area definitions, floor, direction, outdoor space, parking, storage, condition, occupation and payment terms.

An asking price is a seller's request. It is not a completed transaction, an independent valuation or proof of what a lender will recognize. The same home can appear through several intermediaries with different descriptions. A listing may also remain visible after its commercial status changes. Record the date, source, seller or representative and exact apartment whenever an asking price enters the comparison.

Ask for the price in shekels and a written list of inclusions. If furniture, art, appliances or a renovation package affects the number, identify each item. If the apartment is occupied, clarify whether possession will be vacant, whether a tenancy continues and how deposits or prepaid sums are handled. If payment depends on finance, align contract dates with a realistic lending process before treating the asking price as affordable.

### One exceptional transaction does not set the market

Ynet reported a Park Bavli penthouse transaction in January 2025 at approximately NIS 43 million [S04]. The report described a roughly 360-square-metre built area, about 145 square metres of terraces, and a position on floor 44 of the second tower. This is a specific, dated and exceptional apartment. It is not a project starting price, an average apartment price or a benchmark that can be applied without adjustment.

The terrace illustrates why a simple price-per-square-metre division can mislead. Interior built area and exterior terrace area are not necessarily valued, measured or used in the same way. The sale also needs context for finish, direction, parking, storage, contractual rights and date. Those fields were not established as a complete comparable package for an ordinary unit merely by the headline transaction.

The same report included a January 2025 sales snapshot of one other penthouse and about 30 apartments with four rooms or more remaining at that time [S04]. That historical snapshot does not prove inventory on 5 August 2026. A remaining-unit count can fall, rise through cancellations, or include categories that do not match a buyer's unit. Current supply must be requested again.

### How to build a comparable set

Start with the government real-estate database, which allows searches by address, map, block and parcel [S13]. The database can help identify reported transactions, but a result is not automatically comparable. Match the correct building and distinguish Tower 1 from any other address. Confirm transaction date, registered area and floor where available. Investigate whether the record reflects parking, storage, terrace, related-party circumstances or another feature that changes the comparison.

Use a written inclusion test:

| Comparison field | Question to resolve |
|---|---|
| Building | Is this the same tower and address? |
| Date | How far is the transaction from the proposed purchase date? |
| Area | Which area definition appears in each record? |
| Floor | Is the vertical position genuinely similar? |
| Direction and view | Are exposure and obstructions comparable? |
| Outdoor space | Is there a balcony or terrace, and how is it documented? |
| Condition | Shell, original finish, renovated or furnished? |
| Attached rights | Which parking and storage rights are included? |
| Occupation | Vacant, owner-occupied or tenanted? |
| Transaction type | Completed sale, asking price, option, related-party deal or another category? |

One matching field is not enough. A same-building transaction can still be a poor comparable if it is on another facade, in another condition or carries different rights. A similar floor area elsewhere in Bavli may provide neighborhood context but not a Park Bavli tower comparison. Keep the sets separate and explain why each entry was included.

An independent valuer can test the evidence when the purchase is material or finance is involved. The buyer should give the valuer the actual plan, rights documents, attached spaces and known alterations. A valuation based on an inflated marketing area or the wrong tower cannot rescue a weak comparison.

### Total acquisition budget

The purchase price is only the first line. A useful budget also considers purchase tax, independent legal work, registration and document costs, valuation, technical inspection, lending expenses, insurance where applicable, currency conversion, bank transfer costs, immediate repairs, furnishing, moving, management charges and a reserve for known building work.

The following budgeting method is an **estimate, not binding**. It is a collection framework, not a tax result, mortgage approval, valuation or return forecast.

**Total acquisition budget = agreed purchase price + buyer-specific purchase tax + verified transaction costs + finance costs + immediate apartment work + initial building obligations**

Do not fill the formula with generic percentages copied from a listing. The Israel Tax Authority simulator uses the transaction and buyer's actual circumstances [S12]. The [purchase tax calculator](https://nad-lan.co.il/purchase-tax-calculator/) can organize an initial check, but the result should be confirmed for the legal position and purchase date before signing.

Currency deserves its own line for an overseas buyer. Keep the contractual price in shekels. If income or savings are in dollars, pounds, euros or another currency, model the amount at more than one exchange rate and include transfer timing. A foreign-currency illustration must name its rate source and date. It is not a revised Park Bavli price.

### Financing without assumptions

The Bank of Israel explains approval in principle and standardized comparison information for mortgage offers [S14]. It does not approve a particular applicant or apartment. Nationality, residence, income source, existing property, loan purpose, valuation and bank policy can affect the process. Obtain a written approval based on the buyer's actual facts before accepting a contract schedule that relies on borrowing.

The [mortgage calculator](https://nad-lan.co.il/mortgage-calculator/) can help test payment scenarios. Stress the payment against rate changes, currency movement, other housing costs and periods of lower income. Building charges, municipal charges, insurance and maintenance continue alongside the loan.

For a non-resident or a buyer paid abroad, banking preparation often starts before the property choice is final. Ask the proposed bank which identity, income, tax-residence and source-of-funds documents it will require. Confirm how originals, translations or certifications are handled in the buyer's circumstances. Do not assume that wealth alone guarantees a transfer will arrive on the contract date.

### Ongoing costs and management charges

No reliable current Park Bavli-wide management-fee schedule was published in the reviewed public material. Request the latest invoices for the exact apartment, the current budget, the allocation method and information about arrears. Ask which services are included, which are separately billed, and whether a change has been approved for the coming year.

High-rise operating costs can include lifts, security, cleaning, common electricity, mechanical systems, pool or spa operations, landscaping, insurance and professional management. This is a checklist, not a statement that each cost has a particular Park Bavli amount. The management agreement and current accounts should show the actual structure.

Look beyond the regular monthly charge. Request information on reserve funds, major contracts, pending disputes, insurance claims, planned repairs and special assessments. A unit with a lower asking price can become the more expensive choice if it carries arrears or near-term capital work. The contract should allocate pre-completion and post-completion liabilities clearly.

### Investment analysis without a yield promise

No guaranteed rent, occupancy, appreciation, liquidity or yield was verified for Park Bavli. An investment case must start with the exact purchase price and a realistic tenant profile. Gross rent alone does not answer whether the apartment works as an investment.

Build at least three scenarios using buyer-provided or independently checked inputs:

| Scenario | Inputs to test |
|---|---|
| Personal use | Purchase budget, management charges, travel or commuting, furnishing, maintenance and value of actual use |
| Long-term rental | Achievable rent for a comparable unit, vacancy, management, repairs, insurance, tax and non-recoverable building costs |
| Later resale | Acquisition costs, holding costs, likely buyer pool, selling costs, condition at exit and time needed to market |

Net rental analysis should subtract vacancy, management, maintenance, insurance, taxes where applicable and owner-paid building costs from collected rent. It should not assume full occupancy or rising rent. A resale scenario should not assume appreciation. The value of an exceptional penthouse transaction cannot be spread across ordinary apartments.

If the investment relies on a park or sea view, confirm that the exact unit has it and examine planning in that direction. If it relies on corporate tenants, ask for actual comparable leases rather than a general statement about Tel Aviv demand. If it relies on short-term occupation, check the building rules and applicable law instead of assuming that a residential tower allows the intended use.

## The developer

### Brand presentation, municipal parties and seller are different roles

The official project site says The Plaza International introduces Park Bavli Private Residences and labels Gal Nauer "Architect & Developer" [S01]. This is the correct way to describe the site's presentation. It should not be expanded into a claim that either name is automatically the legal seller, registered owner or contracting party for every apartment offered today.

The municipal licensing decision identifies Elad Israel Residences Ltd. and A.M.T.S.H. Investments Ltd. as parties to the cited municipal agreement and refers to them together as the Elad Group in that document's context [S02]. Those legal names help trace the planning record. They do not prove who currently owns a particular apartment or who owes the buyer contractual obligations.

Media reports may use wider group names or ownership descriptions. Marketing may use a project brand. Building management may be a separate entity. A private owner may be the seller. The purchase file should map each role rather than compressing them into one familiar name.

### What to verify about the contracting party

Ask for the seller's full legal name, identification or company details, authority to sell, and connection to the registered rights. A current Land Registry extract can show registered owners, mortgages, liens, attachments, orders and restrictions as of the date it is generated [S11]. Where another rights system applies, the buyer's lawyer should identify the correct document and obtain it from the proper source.

If a representative signs or negotiates, verify the authority and scope of representation. If the seller is a company, review the company details and signing authority appropriate to the transaction. If the apartment is jointly owned, inherited, mortgaged, occupied or subject to another right, the contract must address the path to a valid transfer and vacant possession where promised.

Do not treat a logo, email domain or branded brochure as authority to receive money. Payment instructions belong in a controlled legal and banking process. Confirm the receiving account and any release conditions through independent channels before transferring funds.

### Design authorship and the apartment delivered

The four-elements concept and Gal Nauer attribution are part of the official brand story [S01]. A current apartment should still be judged on its own plans, finishes and condition. Later owners may have renovated. A unit may have been completed to a different package. Furniture in a photograph may be staging.

If design provenance affects the price, request dated specifications, invoices, plans and a precise inclusion list. The contract should state what remains. A general association with a designer or concept does not establish authorship of every later alteration, and it does not guarantee present quality.

The practical developer question is therefore twofold: what does the public project history support, and who is responsible for this sale now? Buyers need both answers. One explains the development. The other determines the enforceable transaction.

## Project stages

### A dated record, not one universal delivery story

Public evidence shows development and occupation over time, but it does not establish one handover date for every apartment that may be offered in 2026. Tower, seller and unit status can differ. The following chronology keeps each source in its own period.

| Date | Publicly supported event | Meaning for a buyer now |
|---|---|---|
| 2020 report | Calcalist described the first 44-storey tower as occupied for about two years and said about 28 new apartments remained then [S06] | Historical evidence of occupation and a dated supply statement, not current inventory |
| 14 November 2022 | Municipal decision approved combining two units in Tower 1 and recorded 153 homes in the 44-storey tower [S02] | Strong scoped count for Tower 1 after that decision |
| January 2025 | Ynet reported an approximately NIS 43 million penthouse sale and described two 44-storey towers with about 340 apartments; it also reported a dated remaining-inventory snapshot [S04] | Transaction and marketing context for that date, not a 2026 price list |
| 19 May 2026 | A branded Ymag feature described the pool, spa and common areas as operating and promoted purchase without a construction wait [S05] | Dated promotional status evidence that still requires exact-unit and current-facility checks |
| 5 August 2026 check | The official brand site did not publish a current apartment count for sale, project-wide price list, unit schedule or delivery date [S01] | Request current information from the seller and verify it independently |

The 2020 number and the 2025 number should not be combined into a sales-rate calculation. They may refer to different towers, seller categories and definitions. The public material does not provide a consistent inventory series. Any statement about how quickly apartments sold would therefore go beyond the evidence.

The 2022 merger is also a warning against treating an original unit count as permanent. Apartments can be combined or otherwise changed through approved processes. Current registered and approved information for the home matters more than a launch total.

### How to classify a current offer

Before discussing "delivery," place the offered unit in one of the categories that the documents actually support:

- completed and previously occupied resale
- completed but represented as never occupied
- completed unit sold by a professional or company seller
- renovated resale
- shell-condition or partly finished unit
- occupied investment sold with a tenancy or other possession arrangement

The list is a classification tool, not a claim that each category is currently available. The seller should provide the evidence for the selected category. A current photograph and keys do not prove legal transferability. A contract does not prove physical quality. Both tracks need to meet.

If possession is promised quickly, inspect the apartment, confirm it is vacant or understand the occupant's rights, agree the delivered condition and write the handover mechanics into the contract. If work remains, identify who performs it, to what specification, under which approval, on what schedule and with what remedy for delay or defects.

### Current availability stays unit-specific

Historical reports show that apartments were marketed at earlier dates. They do not show what remains today. A private resale can appear even when original inventory is exhausted, while a company-held unit can remain after much of a tower is occupied. Ask for a dated availability confirmation and seller identity. Treat any verbal statement as a lead until documents support it.

A buyer should also ask whether the same unit is being marketed by several agents, whether an offer has been accepted, whether a deposit has been placed, and what authority the contact has. Do not transfer a reservation payment merely because the apartment is described as scarce. Independent legal review should come before a binding commitment.

## Who it suits

### Buyers who value a specific northern Tel Aviv setting

Park Bavli may appeal to buyers who want a high-rise home in Tel Aviv, access to the Bavli area and practical proximity to HaYarkon Park. It can work for someone who prefers a residential setting over a beachfront address or a dense retail street. The fit depends on the exact facade, floor, layout and daily route.

A primary-home buyer should test the apartment through an ordinary week. Bedroom privacy, storage, lift time, parking, waste handling, deliveries and management rules will matter after the first impression fades. A household with children should confirm the layout and current school process. A buyer with reduced mobility should trace every threshold and backup arrangement, not rely on a general accessibility label.

A second-home buyer may value building services and a lock-and-leave routine, but should examine absence management. Ask who can enter in an emergency, how leaks are detected, whether deliveries are accepted, how maintenance is authorized and what insurance expects. Building staffing cannot replace a clear plan for the private apartment.

### Relocation and overseas buyers

An English-language search often brings a buyer who is comparing Israel from abroad. The best early work is documentary. Obtain the exact apartment pack before booking travel: tower and address, seller, plan, areas with definitions, floor, direction, current condition, price in shekels, parking, storage, management charges, occupation and proposed possession.

Then arrange a remote walkthrough with a defined route. It should show the building entrance, lobby, lifts, apartment, views, balcony, parking and storage. Ask independent professionals to review documents and condition within their own expertise. A sales representative's video is useful evidence of appearance, but it is not independent verification.

Remote purchase should not be promised as universally possible. Identity checks, signatures, banking, tax, source of funds and document formalities depend on the buyer's circumstances and the institutions involved. Some steps may be possible remotely; others may require a local appearance, original document or specific certification. Confirm the route before the contract fixes dates.

An independent Israeli lawyer should review the rights, seller authority, encumbrances, contract, payment protections, registration path, taxes and possession terms as applicable. The buyer should also obtain advice in any other jurisdiction that affects tax, inheritance, marital property, reporting or finance. An Israeli apartment can create consequences outside Israel.

### Personal use, second home and investment are different purchases

For personal use, judge whether the apartment supports the buyer's actual routine. A beautiful terrace may be less useful than an extra bedroom or better storage. A high floor may offer a wider outlook but add lift dependence and wind. A finished interior may save time but carry design choices the buyer would pay to replace.

For a second home, model fixed annual costs against expected use. Management charges, municipal charges, insurance, maintenance and utilities do not disappear during an absence. Add travel, local support and currency exposure. Compare the cost with alternatives without assuming future appreciation.

For investment, separate rent from resale. Verify building rules, likely tenant profile, comparable leases and owner-paid costs. Use vacancy and maintenance assumptions. Do not describe Park Bavli as liquid, high-yield or guaranteed to appreciate without evidence. A premium asking price can compress yield even when rent is high.

### When Park Bavli may not fit

Park Bavli may be a poor match for a buyer who needs a published project-wide price, a guaranteed developer inventory list or one standard delivery date. It may not suit someone who wants direct beachfront living, a low-service building, minimal common costs or a dense commercial street immediately outside the lobby.

It may also be wrong for a buyer who will not inspect alterations, verify attached rights or review the building budget. High-rise ownership requires tolerance for shared systems and collective decisions. A buyer who expects a view never to change, a facility never to close or a remote process never to require travel is placing too much weight on assumptions.

The decision should be apartment-first. A strong Park Bavli candidate has a clear seller, coherent documents, a layout that works, a defensible price, understood costs and an acceptable physical inspection. Brand recognition cannot compensate for a weak unit file.

### A practical request before scheduling

Ask for the tower, municipal address, apartment identifier, floor, current plan, room schedule, all area figures with definitions, direction, balcony or terrace, parking and storage rights, current condition, occupation, asking price in shekels, management charges, seller identity and available rights documents. Request dated photographs and one continuous video.

This request does not reserve an apartment or confirm an appointment. It creates enough information to decide whether deeper legal, financial and physical work is justified. A complete response saves time for both local and overseas buyers.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Park Bavli the same as the Bavli neighborhood?

No. Park Bavli is the official Latin brand used by the project site for private residences in Tel Aviv. Bavli is the wider neighborhood, and Bavli-Dekel is a broader planning context. Listings for nearby homes or separately branded developments are not automatically Park Bavli apartments.

### What is Park Bavli's verified current status?

Public evidence shows built and occupied residential use. A 2020 report described the first tower as occupied, and a May 2026 branded feature described operating pool, spa and common areas. The exact status of a unit offered today still needs verification, including whether it is resale, company-held, occupied, vacant, renovated or unfinished.

### How many towers are in Park Bavli?

The official brand site does not publish a tower count. A Ynet report in January 2025 described two residential towers, each 44 storeys. That is a dated media description. It should not be merged with tower counts from the wider Bavli-Dekel area or neighboring projects.

### How many storeys does Tower 1 have?

A Tel Aviv-Yafo municipal licensing decision dated 14 November 2022 records Tower 1 as a 44-storey residential tower. Ynet also described two 44-storey towers in its January 2025 report. Verify the offered apartment's floor across the lift, plan and rights documents because floor labels can differ.

### How many apartments are there?

The 2022 municipal decision records 153 homes in Tower 1 after approval to combine two existing units. Ynet described approximately 340 apartments across two towers in January 2025. Do not add these numbers. One concerns Tower 1 after a specific approval; the other is an approximate two-tower media figure.

### What is the address?

The municipal building file identifies Tower 1 at HaRav Nissim 9, Bavli, Tel Aviv-Yafo. The reviewed evidence does not establish that address for every tower associated with the brand. Ask for the exact building and apartment address in the proposed transaction.

### Are Park Bavli apartments available now?

No complete current official inventory was publicly verified as of 5 August 2026. Reports from 2020 and January 2025 contained historical remaining-unit counts, but those are not current availability. Private resale offers can also appear independently of original project inventory.

### Is there an official starting price?

No current project-wide starting-price list was found on the official brand site. Request a dated asking price for the exact apartment and a written statement of what is included. Compare it with properly matched recorded transactions, not a neighborhood average or an undated portal result.

### What does the NIS 43 million transaction show?

Ynet reported that approximate price for a penthouse transaction in January 2025. The report described about 360 square metres of built area, about 145 square metres of terraces and floor 44 of the second tower. It is an exceptional unit-specific transaction, not the price of Park Bavli generally.

### Does every apartment have a park or sea view?

No project-wide view guarantee was verified. View depends on the exact tower, floor, direction, window, neighboring buildings and future planning. Ask for unzoomed photographs and video from each main room, mark the direction on a plan and inspect the apartment.

### Are parking and storage included?

No complete public schedule proves parking or storage for every home. For the offered unit, obtain the identifiers, legal or contractual basis, plans and physical access. Inspect the actual spaces and state them in the written offer and contract.

### Are the pool and spa operating?

A branded Ymag feature dated 19 May 2026 described the pool, spa and common areas as operating. That is dated promotional evidence. Ask building management for current operation, access rules, hours, closures and costs, and inspect the facilities before relying on them.

### What are the current management charges?

A reliable current project-wide schedule was not publicly verified. Request the latest invoices for the exact unit, the management agreement, current budget, allocation basis, arrears, reserve information and planned special expenditure. Charges can vary by unit and change over time.

### How close is Park Bavli to HaYarkon Park and the beach?

The official project site publishes 0.6 km to HaYarkon Park and 2.7 km to Tel Aviv Beach. It does not state the measurement method, entrance or route. Test the journey from the actual building access. Those distances do not prove a park or sea view.

### Is a place at a nearby school guaranteed?

No. Tel Aviv-Yafo states that first-grade assignment is connected to the child's registered residential address, while transfer follows a separate process and is not guaranteed. Confirm the exact address, current catchment and registration rules directly for the relevant school year.

### Is the planned metro already operating near Park Bavli?

No operational claim is made. NTA lists Namir-Pinkas and Tel Aviv Center among planned M1 stations. The reviewed evidence does not support a guaranteed opening date, walking time from the tower or effect on apartment value.

### Can a foreign buyer purchase an apartment?

The answer depends on the buyer, seller, property, financing, tax position and required documentation. Use an independent Israeli lawyer and confirm banking, identity, source-of-funds, signing and registration steps for the actual circumstances. Do not assume that nationality alone determines the result.

### Can the entire purchase be completed remotely?

Do not assume so. Many tasks can begin remotely, including document collection, video inspection and professional review. Signatures, identification, banking or certifications may have specific requirements. Confirm every step and travel need before a contract creates deadlines.

### Can an overseas buyer get a mortgage?

No approval or financing ratio can be inferred from the project name or the buyer's language. A lender must assess the applicant, income, residence, source of funds, property and valuation. Obtain written terms for the real case and allow enough time for the bank's checks.

### What purchase tax applies?

Purchase tax depends on the law and the buyer's actual facts on the transaction date. Use the Israel Tax Authority simulator and appropriate professional advice. Do not assume first-home, resident, new-immigrant or foreign-buyer treatment from a general description.

### Which title document should be checked?

Start with a current Land Registry extract where that is the applicable record. It can show owners, mortgages, liens, attachments, orders and restrictions as of generation. The lawyer should identify the exact subunit and any other rights record needed. An apartment number is not automatically the legal sub-parcel number.

### How should area be checked?

Request every area figure with its source and definition. Compare the approved plan, sale document, current rights record, municipal data where relevant and an independent measurement. Separate interior, built, registered, balcony, terrace and storage areas rather than promoting one unexplained total.

### Is Park Bavli suitable for rental investment?

That depends on the exact price, achievable rent, vacancy, management, maintenance, tax, finance, building rules and resale assumptions. No guaranteed yield or appreciation was verified. Build net scenarios from comparable leases and real costs, and do not use the exceptional penthouse sale as an ordinary-unit benchmark.

### What should be requested before a viewing?

Request the tower, address, floor, plan, room schedule, defined areas, direction, balcony, parking, storage, condition, occupation, asking price, management charges, seller identity, rights documents and dated photographs. For a remote screen, add one continuous walkthrough from the correct entrance to the unit and attached spaces.

### What should be checked during the visit?

Trace street and parking access, security, lobby, lifts, common areas and storage. In the apartment, compare the plan, measure key rooms, test windows, water, drainage, electrical systems and climate control, and record noise and view. Return at another time if light, traffic or wind materially affects the decision.

### What information remains unpublished?

No complete current official inventory, project-wide price list, unit-by-unit area schedule, general parking and storage allocation, current fee table, guaranteed view list or universal delivery date was verified. These facts are specific to the tower, unit, seller and date and should be obtained before an offer.
