# Park Bavli live Google intent ledger

Research date: 2026-08-05, Israel time  
Surface: user's connected real Google Chrome browser  
Market: Google Israel, `gl=il`, separate `hl` per language, `pws=0`, desktop  
Purpose: search-language and intent evidence only. Search results, snippets, listing counts, Google AI Overview and local panels are not project-fact sources.

## 1. Method and limitations

- Eight localized searches were recorded: one exact project-sale query and one culture-specific decision query for each language.
- The original observation window was 2026-08-04 22:10:40 to 22:12:09 UTC, equal to 2026-08-05 01:10:40 to 01:12:09 Israel daylight time.
- The Chrome session was signed in. `pws=0` and Google's non-personalized footer reduce but do not eliminate account, device, experiment, location and history effects.
- English and French exact-project results were independently reopened and confirmed in the real browser earlier in this task. Russian and Arabic exact-project results were reopened again after reconnecting the Chrome tab. No CAPTCHA was present.
- Google sometimes honored the query language but rendered interface or organic text in Hebrew. This was especially visible in Russian and Arabic. The mixed-language output is evidence of a native-content gap, not vocabulary to imitate.
- AI Overview headings are retained only as question and topic signals. Its prices, facilities, legal steps, financing ratios, yield, safety and tax statements are rejected as facts.
- Portal inventory counts and adjectives such as luxury, exclusive, gorgeous or invested are volatile or promotional. They are not reusable claims.

## 4. Four distinct intent products

| Language | First decision lens | Required native emphasis | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| English | Exact-unit comparison plus overseas due diligence | asking price versus recorded transaction, remote inspection, rights, source of funds, relocation/second-home/investment split | US-only `condo`, `HOA`, `escrow` or automatic remote-completion claims |
| French | Purchase dossier that can be reviewed from France | `pièces`, area definitions, full shekel budget, independent Israeli legal review, EUR/NIS exposure | importing `VEFA`, `acte authentique` or `frais de notaire` as false equivalents |
| Russian | Status and scenario audit | new build versus resale, developer versus individual seller, documents, charges, transparent own-use/rental/resale inputs | `ДДУ`, `214-ФЗ`, `ЕГРН`, guaranteed yield, liquidity or appreciation |
| Arabic | Practical exact-apartment fit in full native RTL | layout, privacy, access, parking/storage rights, safe-room verification, school-address rule, family use as one scenario | mixed script, Gulf legal assumptions, safety or family-suitability promises |

## 5. Shared search-intent rules

1. Define the named Park Bavli entity before using broad Bavli-neighborhood language.
2. State current project and inventory uncertainty plainly. Do not call every unit new.
3. Lead with the exact apartment fields people compare: rooms, documented area, floor, orientation, balcony, parking, storage, condition, charges and asking price.
4. Separate asking price, recorded transaction and non-binding estimate.
5. Treat a view as an exact-unit fact, never as a project-wide benefit.
6. Use AI Overview only to discover questions; answer from the locked source ledger.
7. Do not assume nationality, citizenship, residence, tax status, aliyah/repatriation status, family structure or financing eligibility.
8. Keep Park Bavli in Latin characters in every language for entity consistency.
9. Full Arabic RTL and zero Hebrew fallback are release requirements.
10. Current availability, management charges, taxes and financing must be dated and buyer-specific.

## 6. Evidence conclusion

The SERP owner is transactional and exact-unit led in all four languages. The unmet need is not another luxury description. It is a source-led buyer file that distinguishes Park Bavli from the Bavli neighborhood and the wider tower complex, explains the exact unit, separates asking prices from recorded transactions, and answers each language community's purchase questions without importing foreign legal concepts.
