# KikarHamedinaWorld v104.1: the phone's first screen

**Design system version 104.1 (30.9.2026, P9a of the Kikar Hamedina loop, HAD-375).** It builds on v104 (`KikarHamedinaWorld-v104-README.md`). The coordinator publishes it as Claude Design v104.1 BEFORE release 1.72.371.

**What it fixes:** on a phone (390x844), the site's WhatsApp bar, the page top's three buttons and the world's tabs covered each other on the first screen of the Kikar pages.

## The problem, measured on the live pages (1.72.370)

The live pages were measured in a real Chrome at 390x844 (2x, a phone's user agent), read-only. The tool reads `getBoundingClientRect` at scroll 0, 300 and 700 px, in all five languages. Evidence: `docs/research/2026-09-30-kikar-hamedina/p9a-shots/before-live370-*`.

| Page | Scroll 0 | Scroll 300 | Scroll 700 |
|---|---|---|---|
| he | **on the third button + 3 tabs** (5,117 px²) | clear | clear |
| en | clear | **on the full-screen button** (968 px²); the bar dropped BELOW its resting place | clear |
| fr | clear | **on the third button** (9,361 px²) | clear |
| ru | clear | **on the third button** (10,471 px²) | clear |
| ar | **on all three buttons** (9,520 px²) | clear | clear |

**Total: 10 overlaps, 35,437 px².**

**Why it happened:**
1. **The bar only knew the stage's controls, not the page top's buttons.**
   - It rose above the world's tab bar (its top + 10 px).
   - There was no room between the third button and the tabs (11 px), so it landed on the third button: "סיור וירטואלי בכיכר", "Visite virtuelle de la place", "Виртуальная прогулка по площади".
2. **In Arabic the lead is longer**, so the buttons themselves sit at the bar's resting place.
3. **The lift was a bottom offset that could be SMALLER than the resting one** (55 px against 78 px). In English that moved the bar down, onto the full-screen button.

## Three ways considered, one chosen

| Option | Verdict |
|---|---|
| (a) The third button as a half-width button on row 2, leaving the bar's column free | **Rejected.** The bar is 239-283 px wide on a 390 screen, 61-72% of the width. A button that fits beside it is at most 110-119 px, too narrow for "סיור וירטואלי בכיכר", and far too narrow for the French and Russian words. |
| (b) One row of two buttons + the third as a text link | **Rejected.** In en, fr and ru the two main buttons do not fit side by side (they stack full width today). A text link is still a control the bar could cover. It also demotes the world's entry, which is the page's signature. |
| (c) The bar treats the page top's buttons as controls, and the page gives it a place to park | **Chosen.** Nothing is hidden, nothing is removed, and every language gets the same rule. |

## What changed (v104.1)

1. **The bar's rule: the free place nearest its resting place.** This is in `inc/conversion-cta.php`, for phones up to 600 px, on the whole site.
   - **The obstacles:** in its own column, the bar treats these as places it never covers:
     - the page top's buttons (`.nlps-hero__cta`, as one block);
     - every stage control, each climbed to its card as in v91.
   - **Where it goes:** it takes the free place **nearest** its resting place, a little lower or higher.
   - **The limits:** never closer than 16 px to the screen's foot (plus the safe area), and at most 70% up.
   - **Never lower than allowed:** a bottom offset below the resting one cannot land on a control any more, which was the English bug.
   - **What it ignores:** the world's 3D map labels (`.nlw-labels`). They move with the camera, so the bar floats over the map as over a picture instead of chasing them.
   - **No flicker:** nothing in the rule depends on where the bar itself is.
   - **Late buttons:** it measures again 1.2 s and 3 s after the page's load, because a stage paints its own buttons after load. Without this, DUO's card sat under the bar on the first screen.
   - **Unchanged:** the bar is never hidden, and one tap still opens WhatsApp.
2. **The Kikar landing lane.** This is in `inc/project-stage.php`, on phones only.
   - The world starts 50 px lower: `.nlps-page--world > .nlps-stagebox { margin-top: 50px }`.
   - The lane between the third button and the world's tab bar is now 73 px (measured in Hebrew at scroll 0: the button ends at 719, the tab bar starts at 792), up from 21 px. That fits the bar, 50 px, with 10 px of air on each side.
   - While the world's tabs pass the bar's resting place, the bar parks in the lane, between the buttons and the tabs, in every language and at every scroll.
   - On a phone, the lane is also the visual break between the page's text top and the world.
3. **No button was removed and none was shortened** (the additive law). The three buttons keep their words, their order and their size.

## Also in v104.1 (the same release)

- **Degrees in Hebrew are a word ("1.25 מעלות").**
  - Next to a Hebrew word the sign read "ב-°1.25".
  - A bidi isolate (`<bdi dir="ltr">` or LRI/PDI) does NOT change that in Hebrew; this was measured in Chrome: `p9a-shots/bidi-variants-chrome.webp`.
  - Arabic has used "درجة" since P8.
  - Where the sign stays in a sentence of an RTL page (the window's bearing line), it becomes a word in Hebrew and Arabic.
  - Alone in its facing chip, the sign keeps an LTR isolate.
- **The window view names the Mediterranean once.**
  - The label where the line of sight meets the water, with its real distance, replaces the landmark's fixed point.
  - Before, both showed.
- **Russian in the house type.**
  - Each site font gains the Cyrillic of its own design family:
    - Assistant takes Source Sans 3;
    - Noto Serif Hebrew and Frank Ruhl Libre take Noto Serif;
    - Heebo takes Roboto.
  - The rules:
    - only on /projects/hamedina-ru/;
    - only the Cyrillic range (unicode-range; about 18-25 KB per family);
    - font-display swap.
  - Before, Russian fell back to Segoe UI, Georgia and Arial on Windows, and to the phone's system fonts.
- **Day, sunset and night in the floor view.**
  - They are three moments of the same sun clock, and the clock now runs to 22:00:
    - sunset is 20 minutes before the sun sets on the chosen date, from the sun's path over the plot;
    - night is 1 h 40 min after.
  - At night the city's windows glow (about a third of them). This is labelled in the panel and in the caption as an illustration, not information about residents.
  - The towers themselves stay dark, because no one lives there yet.
  - On a phone the switch sits right under the sheet's title. The site's accessibility button covers the sheet's lowest corner.
  - No extra draw call.
  - Night is faster than day, because it has no shadow pass.
