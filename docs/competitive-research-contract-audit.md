# Competitive research — Israeli online contract-review ("ביקורת חוזה דירה") marketplaces

**Prepared for:** nad-lan.co.il marketplace (Fiverr-for-Israeli-real-estate-lawyers)
**Date studied:** June 2026
**Method:** live visits to each site's product/pricing/profile pages + SERP for commercial Hebrew queries + Fiverr/RocketLawyer mechanics.
**Honesty notes:** where a site does not publish a figure, it says **"not public"**. Hebrew is quoted verbatim, untranslated. Two priority sites (lawyers.co.il, psakdin.co.il) were not opened within the time budget — see note in the table; they are well-known **directory/database** sites in the same category as din.co.il, not productized checkouts, but I did not verify their pages so I make no specific claims about them.

---

## 0. The single most important finding

**There is no true "Fiverr-for-lawyers" productized contract-review checkout in the Israeli market.** The Israeli "marketplaces" (din.co.il / lawguide.co.il and peers) are **lead-generation directories + content portals** — no price, no cart, no fixed-scope product, no escrow. Individual law-firm pages are **SEO articles + a lead form**, with prices either hidden ("contact us") or quoted as a range inside body text. The only place a buyer can actually *click-buy a fixed-price real-estate contract review with a named deliverable and SLA* is **Fiverr** (which already operates in Hebrew and in ₪).

**Implication:** nad-lan's productized model is differentiated in Israel. The mechanics to copy come from **Fiverr** (packaging, SLA, reviews, profile); the **legal-structure / disclaimer** patterns come from din.co.il + RocketLawyer; the **price anchor** comes from the Israeli SERP (₪500–₪1,500 for rental-contract review).

## 2. Patterns I see (what the leaders do consistently)

1. **Three price tiers is the default** for productized legal work (Fiverr Basic/Standard/Premium; RocketLawyer Standard/Plus/Pro). A single price is rare; two-tier exists but three is the norm, with the **middle tier flagged "Most Popular."**
2. **Tiers are framed by *who it's for*, not by feature lists** — Fiverr's "Ideal for…" rows (basic transaction → personalised → complex/high-value) do the selling. Buyers self-select by transaction complexity.
3. **Delivery SLA is stated in days and is a first-class selling point**, with a **paid "rush/faster delivery" upsell** (Fiverr +₪147–₪206 for 1-day). RocketLawyer promises "**within 1 business day**." Israeli directories have **no SLA at all** — a gap.
4. **Reviews carry price + duration + repeat-client signal**, not just stars. Fiverr shows the ₪ band paid and days taken on every review; din shows 5★ + name + date. The *aggregate sub-scores* (communication / quality / value) are persuasive.
5. **Response-time is shown on the profile** ("Avg. response time: 1 Hour", online/away). It's a conversion lever the Israeli directories also lean on (instant lead-form + call-back).
6. **Trust = volume counters + named human reviews + (where possible) authority logos.** din leads with "5,565 עורכי דין / 340,901 שו"ת / 14,067 המלצות"; RocketLawyer with "20M+" and a press wall.
7. **The disclaimer is short, sits at the foot of the profile/article, and disclaims "this is not legal advice / not a substitute for a lawyer."** Everyone uses a near-identical line.
8. **Israeli pricing anchor is consistent:** rental-contract review ≈ **₪500–₪900**, draft/review up to **~₪1,500 + VAT**, rule-of-thumb "**half-to-one month's rent + VAT**." Fiverr undercuts heavily (₪74–₪265) because it's global freelancers, not Israeli-bar lawyers.
9. **Escrow + on-platform payment is what makes a marketplace trusted** (Fiverr holds funds until delivery). The Israeli lead-gen sites have **no payment rail at all** — the entire transaction happens off-platform after the call-back.
10. **Lead-gen monetises the lawyer, not the buyer** (din: lawyers pay for placement). A true marketplace monetises the **transaction** (Fiverr fee). nad-lan's "platform fee + lawyer issues חשבונית" sits between these and is the right call.

## 3. Outliers worth stealing (smart things only one site does)

1. **Fiverr — "Ideal for…" buyer-segmentation rows on each tier.** It removes "which package do I need?" friction without a sales call. Steal verbatim structure: tier = *who + transaction type*, not feature count.
2. **Fiverr — price-band + days-taken stamped on every review** ("₪600–₪1,200 · 4 days · Repeat Client"). This is the strongest trust artifact I saw anywhere — it proves real money changed hands and the SLA held. Build this into nad-lan's review schema from day one.
3. **din.co.il — real lawyer-answered Q&A threads embedded on the profile.** Each profile becomes an SEO + trust asset: prospects read the lawyer *actually answering* questions about מחיר למשתכן, הערת אזהרה, etc. This is content marketing welded onto the storefront. nad-lan already has the forum/Q&A muscle — bolt answered questions onto each lawyer profile.
4. **din.co.il — the "סוג פניה" intent dropdown** (*הצעת מחיר / תיאום ייעוץ / הערכת סיכויים / שאלה / פניה אישית*). It routes and qualifies the lead in one field. Useful even in a productized flow as a "what do you need?" pre-step.
5. **RocketLawyer — "Upload any contract and get AI insights backed by Legal Pros"** — a hybrid: instant AI red-flag pass *then* human lawyer. A cheap automated "first look" lowers the entry price and feeds the paid human audit. A future nad-lan upsell ladder.

## 4. Mistakes to avoid (broken / confusing / legally sketchy)

1. **No price = high drop-off.** Every Israeli player hides price behind a lead form ("contact us"). It maximises sales control but kills self-serve conversion and trust. nad-lan's whole edge is **showing the price**. Don't blink on this.
2. **No SLA = no accountability signal.** Israeli pages never promise a turnaround. Buyers reading "we'll get back to you" assume slow. State hours/days explicitly.
3. **Call-tracking proxy numbers + pure lead-gen feel "salesy."** din's 055-453xxxx numbers and pay-for-placement model make it a *broker*, not a *service*. Avoid anything that reads as "we sell your details to whoever paid most."
4. **Reviews without context are weak.** "עורך דין תותח" (one line, 2015) on a profile is thin. Don't ship a review system that captures only stars + a sentence — capture service-bought, price band, date, and verified-purchase.
5. **Vague/over-broad disclaimers that don't address the lawyer–client relationship.** The generic "אין באמור ייעוץ משפטי" line is fine for *articles*, but a **transactional** product needs an explicit statement of **who the client's lawyer is, that the engagement/representation is directly with that lawyer (who issues the חשבונית), and that the platform is an intermediary** — otherwise you risk implying the *platform* is practicing law (a לשכת עורכי הדין problem). RocketLawyer's explicit "not a substitute for an attorney / not a referral service" line is the model to adapt.

> **Israeli-Bar (לשכת עורכי הדין) note:** I did **not** find an official, verbatim לשכת עורכי הדין-mandated disclaimer on the studied pages — sites use their own "אין באמור ייעוץ משפטי" wording. Treat the wording in §5 as drafting guidance to run past a lawyer, **not** as an official Bar text. The relevant live constraint to verify with counsel: platform must not appear to *practise law*, *share fees with* or *solicit on behalf of* lawyers in a way the Bar rules prohibit.

## 5. Recommended starting point for nad-lan.co.il /contract-audit/

### 5a. The product page (`/contract-audit/`)

**Headline (steal the SERP winner's urgency):** `בדיקת חוזה דירה — לא חותמים עד שבודקים` (mirrors yanovsky's proven H1 "לא חותמים עד שבודקים").
**Sub-head:** value-prop in one line, e.g. `עורך דין מקרקעין עובר על החוזה שלכם, מסמן את הסעיפים הבעייתיים, ומחזיר חוות דעת כתובה — תוך 3 ימי עסקים.`

**Three tiers (mirror Fiverr's "Ideal for" structure; price anchored to the Israeli ₪500–₪1,500 norm, positioned *below* a full lawyer engagement):**

| tier | price (₪, + מע"מ) | "מתאים ל…" | scope (what's IN) | SLA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **בסיסי / Quick Read** | ~₪390 | שוכרים/משכירים, חוזה שכירות סטנדרטי עד ~8 עמודים | מעבר על החוזה + רשימת **דגלים אדומים** כתובה (עד 10 הערות) | **3 ימי עסקים** |
| **מלא / Full Audit** ⭐(הכי נבחר) | ~₪690 | רוכשי/מוכרי דירה יד-שנייה, חוזה מכר | חוות דעת כתובה מלאה, סעיף-סעיף, + **שיחת טלפון 15 דק'** להסבר | **2 ימי עסקים** |
| **קבלן / Premium** | ~₪1,200 | רכישה מקבלן / עסקה מורכבת / מסמכים נלווים | ביקורת חוזה קבלן + מפרט + נספחים, חוות דעת מלאה + **שיחת זום 30 דק'** + סבב שאלות המשך | **2 ימי עסקים** |

- **Rush upsell** (Fiverr pattern): `מסירה מהירה — תוך 24 שעות (+₪200)`.
- **"מה לא כלול":** ניסוח/עריכת חוזה חדש, ייצוג מול הצד השני, ליווי עד רישום — אלה שירותים נפרדים (link to the lawyer's profile / consult). State OUT-of-scope explicitly to prevent disputes (the §4 lesson).
- **CTA button:** `הזמינו בדיקה` → checkout (WooCommerce), **not** a lead form.
- **On-page social proof:** review count + ⭐ + "X חוזים נבדקו" counter (din's counter pattern) + 3 newest reviews with **price-band + turnaround + verified-purchase** (Fiverr pattern).

### 5b. Lawyer profile / storefront pages (anatomy to ship — merge din + Fiverr)

Photo · full name · **מספר רישיון / חבר לשכת עורכי הדין** · years' experience · city · specialties (icons) · **languages** · ⭐ rating + review count · **"זמן תגובה ממוצע"** · short bio · **3 productized services with prices + "הזמינו"** · gallery (sample redacted opinion) · **answered Q&A block** (din pattern — bolt the forum answers here) · reviews (with price-band/turnaround) · the §5e disclaimer in the footer.

### 5c. Intake form (post-"הזמינו", before/at checkout)

Exact fields:
1. **העלאת קובץ החוזה** (PDF/DOCX/JPG, drag-drop) — required.
2. **סוג העסקה** — שכירות / מכר יד-שנייה / רכישה מקבלן / אחר (routes to the right tier).
3. **תפקידך** — שוכר / משכיר / קונה / מוכר.
4. **מועד חתימה צפוי** (date) — drives urgency / rush suggestion.
5. **מספר עמודים בחוזה** (number) — validates tier.
6. **3 שאלות / חששות מרכזיים** (free text, optional) — Fiverr "Requirements" pattern.
7. **טלפון + אימייל** for delivery + the 15/30-min call.
8. Consent checkbox: `קראתי ואני מסכים/ה לתנאי השירות ולמדיניות הפרטיות` + the lawyer-client acknowledgement (§5e).

### 5d. Refund policy (write it on the page — nobody else does, it's a trust win)

- **לא נמסר בזמן (SLA חרג ביותר מ-X ימי עסקים) →** ביטול והחזר מלא, או 50% הנחה לבחירת הלקוח.
- **אי-התאמת היקף** (קיבלת בסיסי וצריך מלא) → תשלום הפרש בלבד, ללא קנס.
- **אי-שביעות רצון מהותית** → סבב תיקון אחד ללא תשלום; אם עדיין לא תקין → בירור מול הפלטפורמה (Resolution Center pattern).
- Make clear the platform **holds the payment until delivery** (escrow assurance, even if implemented as delayed payout to the lawyer).

### 5e. Disclaimer / legal-structure wording (draft — verify with counsel before publishing)

> `נדל"ן חכם היא פלטפורמה המחברת בין לקוחות לבין עורכי דין עצמאיים. הבדיקה מתבצעת על-ידי עורך הדין שבחרתם, והקשר המשפטי (יחסי עורך דין–לקוח) הוא ישירות מולו; עורך הדין מנפיק חשבונית מס ונושא באחריות המקצועית לחוות הדעת. הפלטפורמה אינה משרד עורכי דין, אינה מעניקה ייעוץ משפטי ואינה תחליף לייעוץ משפטי אישי. חוות הדעת מתייחסת למסמכים שנמסרו בלבד.`

This adapts RocketLawyer's "not a substitute for an attorney / not a referral service" structure to the Israeli reality (lawyer = client's lawyer + חשבונית issuer; platform = intermediary). **Run past a lawyer for לשכת עורכי הדין compliance before going live.**
