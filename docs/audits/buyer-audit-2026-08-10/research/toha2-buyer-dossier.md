# ToHa2 — buyer/tenant due-diligence dossier

**Audience:** US startup evaluating an Israeli headquarters lease  
**Evidence cut-off and access date:** 2026-08-10  
**Scope:** ToHa2, Tel Aviv; public-source verification only  
**Decision status:** **Not yet lease-decision-ready.** Public evidence is sufficient to establish the project, broad scale, location, Google commitment, construction stage and strong transit proposition. It is not sufficient to price, engineer, fit out, insure or sign a particular floor.

## 1. How to read this dossier

The labels below are deliberate:

- **Verified:** supported by a primary, statutory, municipal, transport, owner financial-report or project-team source, with scope stated.
- **Owner claim:** published by an owner/project-team marketing page, but not independently certified or not precise enough for lease reliance.
- **Derived:** calculated from a cited dataset; method and limitations are stated.
- **Missing:** no decision-grade public answer was found. A named owner and exact requested document are supplied.
- **Contradicted:** authoritative/public sources disagree, or a page mixes scopes. The website must not silently choose one value.

This is not legal, tax, engineering or accessibility advice. A tenant should rely only on executed lease exhibits, stamped drawings, official assessments and final certificates.

## 4. Delivery, construction and occupancy

The most current owner financial evidence found is Amot’s Q1-2026 report. It says the structural skeleton was complete, finishing/envelope/system works were progressing, and Form 4 was expected by the end of 2026. Other project pages advertise either Q4-2026 completion or Q1-2027 occupancy. Google’s lease commencement is also described as Q1 2027 after construction.

These dates can all refer to different milestones—construction completion, Form 4, landlord handover, fit-out access, rent commencement and business opening—but public pages do not define them consistently.

### Required milestone model

The website and RFP must show these separately:

1. Structure complete.
2. Watertight/envelope complete.
3. Permanent power and base-building systems energized.
4. Early access for tenant contractors.
5. Form 4 / occupancy certificate.
6. Base-building substantial completion.
7. Floor-specific landlord-work completion.
8. Tenant fit-out completion.
9. Rent commencement.
10. Operational opening.

Each needs an owner, current forecast, contractual date, evidence, dependency, cure period and long-stop remedy. Until Form 4 and floor-specific handover occur, “Available now” would be false.

## 5. Leasing, commitments and real availability

### 5.1 What is verified

- Amot reports approximately **156,000 sqm** of above-ground marketed area for ToHa2 and a 50% Amot share.
- As of the Q1-2026 reporting date, signed agreements covered approximately **45% of leasable area**. The report also describes negotiations for the remaining space and warns there is no certainty they will mature.
- The Google agreement covers approximately **60,000 sqm** of non-partitioned office space in the upper part of the tower plus several hundred parking spaces, for ten years with a one-time exit after five years. The current Amot report gives approximately **NIS 120 million annual rent for the whole package**, linked to the index, in shell/core condition. An older owner article used NIS 115 million.
- An owner article describes Google’s area as roughly 24 floors, but no public floor numbers or demised plans were found.

### 5.2 What cannot be inferred

Google’s 60,000 sqm is about 38.5% of 156,000 sqm, while signed agreements are reported at about 45%. It is arithmetically tempting to infer that another ~6.5% is contracted. That calculation does **not** identify the other tenant, exact area, floor, legal status or current availability, so it must not become a website fact.

Likewise, “upper part” cannot be turned into specific floors. Sky lobbies, technical levels, changing floor plates, bridge levels and owner measurement conventions make a simple 60,000 ÷ typical-floor-area calculation unreliable.

### 5.3 Availability truth model

Only the joint owner/leasing CRM should originate availability. Its private workflow can retain the following ingestion states, but they are **not** a second public/runtime enum:

- `available_verified`
- `available_divisible_verified`
- `soft_hold`
- `loi`
- `legal_negotiation`
- `signed_not_public`
- `leased_public`
- `owner_withheld`
- `public_or_amenity`
- `technical`
- `not_yet_released`
- `unknown`

The adapter must map them deterministically into the canonical public enum:

| Private CRM ingestion state | Canonical public status | Public reason/handling |
|---|---|---|
| `available_verified`, `available_divisible_verified` | `verified_available` | Divisibility remains a separate verified field. |
| `soft_hold` | `soft_hold` | Never green; expiry and approver remain visible. |
| `loi` | `under_loi` | No counterparty identity is exposed. |
| `legal_negotiation` | `under_offer` | Private negotiation detail stays private. |
| `signed_not_public` | `not_marketed` | Reason is confidential/signed; do not leak tenant or transaction. |
| `leased_public` | `leased` | Only after owner authorizes public disclosure. |
| `owner_withheld`, `public_or_amenity`, `technical`, `not_yet_released` | `not_marketed` | Preserve the private reason; do not present as available. |
| `unknown` | `unknown` | Request a live owner check. |

The remaining canonical statuses `delivered` and `unavailable` may be supplied only through explicit owner workflow states; they must not be inferred. Every mapped status needs `as_of`, source-system ID, responsible leasing approver and its own four-state evidence envelope. Public availability expires automatically after 24 hours unless revalidated.

## 8. Rent, taxes and total occupancy cost

### 8.1 No verified ToHa2 asking rent

No public decision-grade asking rent was found for a currently available ToHa2 floor. The Google package is not a comparable unit quote: it includes a very large upper-tower commitment and several hundred parking spaces, is described in shell/core condition, is index-linked and has negotiated lease rights. Dividing NIS 120 million by 60,000 sqm would incorrectly assign all package value to office rent and ignore parking, measurement and index details.

Cushman & Wakefield Inter Israel’s H2-2025 survey reports an average asking rent of approximately **NIS 129/sqm/month** for the Yigal Alon vicinity and about **NIS 120/sqm/month** for the CBD. The report says surveyed asking rents generally reflect fitted space and average landlord participation of about NIS 2,000/sqm for new offices and NIS 500/sqm for existing as-is space. These are market benchmarks, not a quote or valuation for ToHa2.

### 8.2 Arnona (municipal tax)

The Tel Aviv 2026 arnona order lists standard non-residential rates of NIS 449.78/sqm/year in zone 1, NIS 375.07 in zone 2 and NIS 307.74 in zone 3. It lists NIS 196.49/sqm/year for qualifying software houses whose principal activity is software production. The only Yigal Alon exception seen in the order is 49–51, suggesting—but not conclusively proving—that 114 would use zone 1 for a standard office.

Derived monthly equivalents are approximately NIS 37.48/sqm for zone-1 standard office and NIS 16.37/sqm for qualifying software-house classification. These are **not ToHa2 assessments**. Municipal measured area can differ from lease area, and a startup is not automatically entitled to the software-house classification.

Required: a sample/current municipal assessment for the relevant unit, classification ruling/precedent, measured area, zone map result and tenant tax advice.

### 8.3 One-click total-cost model

For every candidate suite, the website should display a low/base/high scenario and the source of each input:

`monthly occupancy cost = base rent + indexation + service/management charge + arnona + parking + utilities + after-hours HVAC + generator/critical-load charge + telecom + security/access add-ons + fit-out amortization + insurance + VAT/tax treatment`

Show separately:

- Rentable and usable area.
- Base rent and currency.
- CPI/index base, cap/floor and review dates.
- Management/service charge budget and prior-year reconciliation method.
- Parking quantity and per-space price.
- Arnona classification and assessed area.
- Electricity, water and HVAC metering/rates.
- Tenant-improvement allowance, landlord works, rent-free and fit-out period.
- Deposit, bank guarantee and parent guarantee.
- VAT inclusion/exclusion and other taxes.
- Broker/legal/project-management/permit costs.
- Restoration/reinstatement and end-of-term cost.

An empty price field must say “Commercial terms not published—request a timestamped term sheet,” not “Contact us” without explaining what will be returned.

## 9. Technical due diligence: public facts and critical unknowns

### 9.1 Vertical transport

Electra Times (April 2026) describes **70 elevators and four escalators** for the project, three vertical sections with two-storey lobbies, double-deck shuttle elevators up to four tonnes and 8 m/s, 36 MRL elevators at 2.5 m/s, two large high-speed freight elevators, and dedicated parking/service/bicycle elevators. It also describes OTIS OEO emergency-evacuation functionality integrated with fire detection. An older Electra item referred to 60 elevators; the latest 70 figure should supersede the older marketing number, subject to the final as-built schedule.

This still does not answer a tenant’s floor-level questions: assigned passenger bank, transfer requirement, handling capacity, destination-dispatch logic, accessible controls, freight booking, after-hours bank, security zoning or measured wait time.

Required: final elevator schedule, vertical-transport study, traffic assumptions, floor-bank matrix, accessible operating specification, commissioning results and freight/after-hours operating manual.

### 9.2 Structure and floor plate

The planning protocol’s maximum typical employment-floor gross height of 3.8 m is a planning control, **not** verified clear finished ceiling height. Public sources do not provide live load, concentrated load, vibration criteria, slab thickness, penetration zones, raised-floor capacity, column grid or structural reserve for compact storage/labs.

Required from owner and structural engineer: stamped tenant structural criteria, floor-by-floor load schedule, vibration criteria, slab/beam/post-tension drawings, penetration procedure and current core/grid dimensions.

### 9.3 Power, resilience and critical loads

The approved-design material places electrical facilities and private dry transformers in upper basements. It does not publish tenant W/sqm, transformer capacity, dual-feed topology, generator coverage, N/N+1 arrangement, runtime, refueling, UPS, tenant critical-power allocation, metering, EV charging or black-start/test protocol.

Required: basis of design, final one-line diagrams, utility letters, generator/ATS/UPS schedules, fuel and runtime calculation, critical-load allocation matrix, load-bank and commissioning reports, tenant metering specification and outage/maintenance SLA.

### 9.4 HVAC, air quality and after-hours use

No public decision-grade source was found for HVAC type, cooling/heating capacity, outside-air rate, filtration, humidity, acoustics, zoning, normal hours, after-hours cost, 24/7 server-room cooling, heat-rejection limits, BMS access/API or commissioning status.

Required: mechanical basis of design, floor capacity schedule, air/water schematics, ventilation and filtration specification, tenant supplemental-cooling rules, after-hours tariff, BMS points list/API policy, acoustic criteria and TAB/commissioning reports.

### 9.5 Telecom and fiber

No carrier list, meet-me-room locations, riser capacity, diverse physical routes, cross-connect charges, service lead time or mobile coverage/DAS specification was publicly verified.

Required: carrier-neutrality statement, carrier LOAs, MMR/riser/diversity drawings, route-separation evidence, cross-connect rate card and SLA, DAS/Wi-Fi/cellular survey and current order lead times.

### 9.6 Life safety, protected spaces and business continuity

The elevator source’s OEO claim is useful but does not replace the fire/life-safety strategy. Public sources did not establish protected-space/shelter capacity and travel distance for each floor, evacuation/refuge arrangements, fire command procedures, tenant warden requirements, emergency power scope or earthquake/business-continuity plan.

Required: approved fire-safety strategy, fire authority approvals, protected-space schedule and plans, egress/occupant-load analysis, OEO sequence, evacuation/accessibility plan, emergency-power matrix and tested incident procedures.

## 11. Sustainability: claim, certificate and scope

Gav-Yam’s current page says ToHa2 “meets LEED Platinum standards.” This wording is not the same as a final GBCI certification. No public final certificate, scorecard or exact ToHa2 project ID was located in this research. The USGBC project directory can omit confidential projects, so directory absence is not proof that the project is unregistered or uncertified.

The official Israel green-building dataset was also searched by project/address/block terms without locating an obvious 80-floor ToHa2 record. That is not conclusive while a project is under construction and names/scopes can differ.

Separately, an Israel Green Building Council ToHa tour describes LEED Platinum, double-skin facade, night ventilation and related performance—but that item concerns the completed **ToHa1** building. Those facts must not be copied into ToHa2.

Likewise, the Gav-Yam ToHa2 page’s “55,000 sqm / four parking floors / 950 stalls / 30% AC and lighting / 50% water” block appears to describe ToHa1 or the existing complex. It is a high-priority content-contamination defect.

Required:

- Final GBCI certificate, project ID, rating system/version, certification date and scorecard.
- Exact boundary/scope statement: ToHa2 only, shared podium/parking, or entire complex.
- Israel Standard 5281 Stage A/B certificates and laboratory details.
- Energy model, embodied-carbon/LCA scope, water model, metering plan and commissioning reports.

Until supplied, the website should say **“Owner states the project is designed to meet LEED Platinum standards; final certification document not supplied to this audit.”** It should not display an unqualified certification badge.

- Step-free route and road-crossing details.
- Official category, current hours, phone/site, last verified date and source.
- Open-now state, Friday/Saturday constraints and late-night availability.
- Public versus tenant-only access.
- Opening status: open, temporarily closed, under construction, planned or unverified.

Minimum categories for a corporate relocation decision: varied lunch/coffee, grocery, pharmacy, urgent care and hospital, gym, hotel, childcare, bank/ATM, courier/post, print/IT support, event space, employee wellbeing, police/fire, green space and late-night food. The map needs a “what is missing within 5/10/15 minutes” view, not only positive pins.

## 14. Amenities: plan versus operating reality

No reliable current operator roster, opening schedule, service level or tenant-access policy was found for ToHa2 amenities. Before a buyer can value them, the owner must identify:

- Operator name and executed/opening status.
- Location and capacity.
- Public, tenant-only or paid membership access.
- Hours, Friday/Saturday policy and holiday closures.
- Booking system, price and tenant allocation.
- Accessibility and emergency arrangements.
- Whether amenity cost is included in service charge.
- Remedy/substitute if opening is delayed.

The floor and building pages should visually distinguish **open now**, **contracted/opening by date**, **planned without operator**, and **concept only**.

## 17. Floor-click UX: the answer must be on the same page

### 17.1 Desktop interaction

When a buyer selects a floor, keep the 3D model visible while opening a persistent, resizable fact panel. Do not navigate away or reset selection. The panel should contain:

1. **Identity:** floor ID, permitted/as-built program, availability state, last verified time and source.
2. **Space:** selectable suite polygons, usable/rentable/gross area, measurement standard and core factor.
3. **Orientation:** true north, georeferenced plan, facade directions, view cones and surrounding obstructions.
4. **Technical:** clear height, grid, live/concentrated load, power, HVAC, critical cooling, fiber, elevator bank and protected space.
5. **Condition/delivery:** shell/CAT A/fitted state, actual photos/360, landlord works, earliest access and occupancy milestone.
6. **Cost:** base rent, service charge, arnona scenario, parking, utilities, TI/rent-free and total monthly/one-time estimate.
7. **Operations:** access hours, loading, freight elevator, security, accessibility, amenity rights and bicycle/parking allocation.
8. **Evidence:** downloadable area plan, technical sheet, certificate index and document revision.
9. **Action:** save/compare, export one-page fact sheet, request exact term sheet, ask a floor-specific question and book a visit.

Every value should expose provenance on hover/click: status, source, revision, date, scope and whether contractual. Unknown values should remain visible in the expected location with a one-click request for the named document.

### 17.2 Mobile interaction

On mobile, do not add another bottom sheet. Nadlan already tested a 46vh three-snap sheet and it preserved the nested-scroll/coverage problem. Selecting a floor should atomically replace the theatre with one decision viewport: a live model strip, exact floor identity/status, exposure scene, evidence facts, four tool doors and the commercial CTA. Heavy evidence opens as a body-level full-screen tool and returns in one action to the same floor, camera, focus and page position. Required behavior:

- 44 px or larger touch targets; no hover-only data.
- Persistent floor ID and availability state in the one-screen selected-floor header.
- A clearly visible “Back to building” control that preserves filters.
- Save, Compare, Request documents and Ask question must fit without covering the model, map, legal copy or each other.
- No draggable sheet and no internal fact-card scroll. Normal page scroll is allowed; full-screen tools own the viewport while open.
- Tables must become labeled cards; no clipped horizontal content.
- Document download, map routing and contact forms must work without pop-up dependence.
- Low-bandwidth static plan fallback with the same facts and deep link.

### 17.3 Unknown-data component

Bad: `Power: —` or `Call us`.

The request button should prefill building, floor, missing field, document name and buyer identity. It should create a trackable requirement—not a generic lead with lost context.
