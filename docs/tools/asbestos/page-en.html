Asbestos is a group of fibrous minerals found in Israel as asbestos-cement and friable asbestos. Intact, undisturbed asbestos-cement is not dangerous while it remains unbroken, unburnt and not crumbling. Never drill, cut or break it. For standard removal, use only a licensed asbestos contractor working under a Ministry of Environmental Protection permit; narrow legal exemptions are explained below. Report asbestos hazards to the Ministry hotline, *6911. Before buying, renovating or arranging disposal, establish what the material is and whether it is damaged.

What asbestos is and why it is still on Israeli roofs

Asbestos describes six fibrous minerals. For property decisions, the useful distinction is between asbestos-cement and friable asbestos. Asbestos-cement is a rigid mixture of cement and asbestos, usually containing about 10% asbestos fibres. It was used for roof sheets and many other building components, according to the Ministry of Environmental Protection (3.2025).

Friable asbestos contains more than 1% asbestos by weight and can crumble or become powder under hand pressure when dry. That is a technical definition, not a test to perform yourself. Its uses included thermal and acoustic insulation, industrial equipment and certain household heat-resistant products.

Why the ban did not make existing roofs disappear

The Ministry reported that most asbestos-cement roofs were installed during the 1960s to the 1980s. The law prohibited new uses when it entered into force in August 2011, but asbestos-cement products installed beforehand may continue in use. Seeing an old asbestos roof therefore does not, by itself, establish an offence. Its condition and any work being done to it matter.

Friable asbestos has a different legal history, including a removal deadline of August 2021. According to the Knesset Research and Information Center (7.2023), friable asbestos was generally not used in private buildings, and the Ministry was not aware of private buildings with sprayed acoustic friable asbestos. That does not establish that an individual home contains no asbestos. Roofs, pipes, outbuildings and household products still need consideration.

How to spot asbestos in a flat, a building and a yard

Look beyond the flat's interior when arranging an assessment. The Ministry's published product list includes materials on roofs, along walls and in yards, as well as water infrastructure. A renovated room tells you little about a separate shed or an older pipe. Ask the surveyor to state which areas and components were inspected.

Identification requires more than a visual impression

Definitive identification comes from analysis of a material sample by an accredited laboratory. According to Ministry guidance (4.2025), the result should state whether asbestos is present, its type and its concentration. Until established otherwise, treat suspect material as asbestos. Do not break off a corner to settle an argument about what a roof contains.

The Ministry allows sampling under its published guidelines or by an asbestos surveyor. Those sampling instructions are not reproduced here. For a private property, arrange a licensed surveyor's paid opinion and ask how any required sampling will be handled. Public environmental inspection services are described as inspecting public buildings; do not assume that they provide a free private-home survey.

When asbestos is dangerous and when it is not

The principal exposure route is breathing airborne fibres. The Ministry identifies friable products and asbestos-cement that is damaged, broken, burnt or crumbling as sources of concern. Fibres can also be released around asbestos-containing buildings during demolition or renovation. An intact sheet left undisturbed is a different situation from the same sheet being cut for building work.

Never sand, polish, drill, saw, cut or grind asbestos-cement. A proposed repair should begin with identifying what will be disturbed. Do not let an ordinary renovation task become asbestos work without the appropriate professional and permission process.

Maintenance and preventive removal

Ministry guidance (9.2025) recommends maintaining intact asbestos-cement and describes painting it with water-resistant paint containing a binder, following its painting guidelines. This does not authorise abrasive preparation. If material is damaged or must be dismantled, ask a licensed asbestos contractor to assess the work.

The Ministry also recommends preventive roof removal to reduce exposure if a roof is later damaged by fire, collapse or a blast. That recommendation should not be confused with a blanket legal deadline to remove every intact private asbestos-cement roof.

During nearby removal, the Ministry advises avoiding the immediate area, closing windows and doors facing the work, and avoiding air-conditioning units that draw outside air. Ask to see the work permit or the documented basis for an applicable exemption. A general safe distance for every asbestos incident has not been published.

Asbestos and health: diseases, latency and when to see a doctor

All six main forms of asbestos are carcinogenic to humans, according to the World Health Organization (8.2026). The recognised diseases include asbestosis, lung cancer and mesothelioma, as well as cancers of the larynx and ovaries. The relevant concern is exposure, especially inhalation of released fibres, rather than the mere knowledge that a building contains an intact asbestos-cement component.

DiseaseDescription and latency in Ministry guidance, 4.2025
AsbestosisLung disease associated with significant exposure over some years. First signs may appear at least 10 years after initial exposure.
Lung cancerThe guidance describes a latency of 25 to 30 years after exposure begins. Smoking increases the risk further.
MesotheliomaCancer affecting the lining of the lungs, abdomen or heart, with a described latency of 20 to 40 years. About 80% of cases are linked to asbestos. It can follow light-to-moderate environmental exposure.

These descriptions are not a personal diagnosis or a timetable for an individual. The Israel Cancer Association (12.2024) says most people do not become ill after occasional exposure, while advising anyone concerned about exposure to consult a family physician or occupational physician.

What to discuss with your doctor

Explain what happened, whether material was damaged and whether your concern relates to work or your home. Symptoms listed by the Israel Cancer Association include breathlessness, wheezing or hoarseness, a persistent worsening cough, blood in sputum, chest pain, difficulty swallowing, facial or neck swelling, unintended weight loss and fatigue. Ask the doctor to assess your circumstances. A universal screening schedule for residents living near an asbestos roof has not been published.

What about water pipes and tanks?

The Ministry, citing the WHO position adopted by Israel's medical committee, says asbestos fibres in drinking water are not considered to cause malignant or other digestive-tract diseases. Its guidance allows asbestos-cement pipes and tanks to remain in use provided their surfaces are not rubbed or polished. Drinking-water quality is the responsibility of the Ministry of Health.

What Israeli law requires

The main statute is the Prevention of Asbestos and Harmful Dust Hazards Law, 5771-2011 [חוק למניעת מפגעי אסבסט ואבק מזיק, התשע״א-2011]. It entered into force in August 2011. It prohibits manufacture, import, trade and new uses of asbestos, regulates asbestos professionals and work, establishes maintenance obligations and provides enforcement powers.

Private property and an asbestos hazard

Private buildings are subject to the asbestos-work rules even though they do not have a general asbestos-inventory or mapping duty. According to the Knesset Research and Information Center (7.2023), there is no comprehensive national inventory of asbestos in private buildings. An address being absent from a record is therefore not proof that it is asbestos-free.

Section 10 prohibits causing an asbestos hazard. Section 11 applies to someone who caused a hazard, or has reasonable grounds to believe one exists at premises they own or hold. Immediate duties include stopping fibre release, restricting access except for rescue or inspection, preventing objects and waste from being removed, and reporting to the commissioner.

The statutory seven-day deadline is for submitting an application for a permit to remove the hazard. It runs from learning of the hazard. It is not permission to postpone immediate protective action. The Ministry's FAQ uses removal wording, but the statutory provision specifies the permit application.

Public places have additional maintenance duties

The statutory category of public places is broad. It includes educational, cultural, religious and sports premises, community centres, restaurants and public parks. Do not assume that every shared residential area belongs to this category merely because several residents use it.

Under Section 8, owners and holders of public places must keep existing asbestos-cement intact, paint it according to Ministry guidance and renew the paint. The original painting deadline was within three years of the law's commencement. Visual inspection is annual, or every three months until painted. A surveyor is required where the total area is 1,000 m² or more, and in certain public places. A maintenance log must be displayed prominently.

Cracking affecting up to one third of the asbestos-cement in a building must be repaired and sealed within 30 days. Above one third, the requirement is immediate reporting and removal and landfilling within three months, subject to the director's power to defer for up to one year. These are public-place provisions, not a universal timetable for every private roof.

Penalties and monetary sanctions

Section 58 provides administrative monetary sanctions. The following amounts were recorded in the legal text on 3.11.2026. They are periodically indexed and were not verified against the current official indexed text. Confirm the applicable official amount before relying on a figure. An administrative sanction is distinct from a criminal fine.

The higher tier includes causing a hazard, failing to take immediate measures, failing to apply for a hazard-removal permit, hiring an unlicensed person, unlawful transport or disposal, and work performed without a required licence or permit. The middle tier includes specified failures to remove friable asbestos or maintain or remove damaged asbestos-cement in public places.

Section 54 also provides potential criminal penalties: six months' imprisonment for specified lower-tier offences, one year for intermediate offences, and two years for offences including unlawful asbestos work or disposal. Aggravated circumstances can carry three years. Criminal fines refer to the Penal Law scale; continuing offences can add 5% of the fine per day. There is no single fixed fine covering every illegal removal.

Asbestos removal step by step

Choose the licence that matches the work

The Ministry's public registry records licence categories, expiry dates, conditions and disciplinary information. Check the person or business you are hiring, not just a general claim of experience. The Ministry's professional-routing guidance (9.2025) distinguishes the following roles.

TaskProfessional route
Building survey and condition assessmentAsbestos surveyor, friable-asbestos inspector or appropriately authorised worker in charge
Asbestos-cement removalAsbestos-cement contractor or friable-asbestos contractor
Friable-asbestos workFriable-asbestos inspector and friable-asbestos contractor
Fire, collapse or widespread asbestos-cement destructionFriable-asbestos contractor, or friable-asbestos inspector together with an asbestos-cement contractor
Air sampling and sample analysisSampling laboratory for collection; analysis laboratory for analysis
Waste disposalSite licensed for the relevant asbestos waste

From assessment to completion documents

  1. Establish the scope. Ask for identification of the material, its condition and the components to be removed.
  2. Confirm the permission route. For ordinary permitted work, the client applies through a licensed asbestos contractor. Obtain the permit before work starts. If an exemption is proposed, ask for its precise basis.
  3. Agree the work arrangements. Ask who is responsible for site restrictions, notifications, packaging, transport and disposal.
  4. Have the licensed team carry out the work. Do not drill, cut or break sheets yourself to prepare the site or reduce the load.
  5. Confirm the waste destination. Ask for the licensed receiving site and disposal documentation.
  6. Collect the completion record. Ministry guidance describes air-fibre measurements by an accredited laboratory on completion. Ask for the sampling results required for your job and the final report.

According to the Knesset Research and Information Center (7.2023), the statutory decision period for a permit is 30 days. The Ministry then reported practical processing of about a week and a half, or the same day for a hazard. Those reported processing times are not a guarantee for your application.

What may be exempt?

Regulations allow defined limited work and disposal without a permit or contractor, subject to their conditions. A 2024 amendment also describes low-risk work by licensed contractors using advance notice rather than a permit. Its commencement date was not verified, so ask the Ministry to confirm the applicable route.

The amendment describes dismantling up to 500 m² of sheets or 100 m of pipe in a building intended for demolition or an open shed. It also describes removal of specified intact waste piles, up to 500 m² of sheets or 5 tons of another asbestos-cement product. These are conditional categories, not general permission for an owner to dismantle a roof.

The notification requirements include at least two photos, a site sketch showing the surroundings within 50 m, and a signed declaration by the contractor's worker in charge. Notices must be posted at the property and properties within 50 m at least two days before work. Within 50 m of a public place, work must be timed when nobody is there. The notification radius is not a universal safe distance.

Nor does being below 50 m² automatically make a job exempt: Tel Aviv-Yafo expressly requires a licensed contractor even below that area. Ask for the complete applicable conditions rather than relying on an area threshold quoted in isolation.

Packing and disposal

For its specific asbestos-cement product disposal route, such as a planter weighing up to 700 kg, Ministry guidance specifies wetting, two layers of 0.15 mm polyethylene, tape sealing, an asbestos warning label and delivery to a licensed asbestos waste site, or instructions from the local authority. Confirm that the route applies before handling an item. It is not a procedure for clearing fire debris.

A separate Ministry instruction covers certain asbestos-containing household heat products: stop using them and dispose of them with household waste in thick, double, well-sealed plastic packaging. This limited instruction does not apply to roof sheets, construction rubble or pipe debris.

Free removal and state funding: what exists and for whom

Assistance for qualifying owners with an existing hazard

The Knesset Research and Information Center (7.2023) described Ministry-funded removal without charge under a procedure for qualifying owners. It applies to an existing asbestos hazard, not simply an intact roof you would prefer to replace. The published conditions concern a private, non-corporate owner who did not cause the hazard and who signs consent.

The owner must receive one of the specified benefits: income support, a nursing benefit, an elderly disability pension or hostile-action disability compensation. The Ministry's contractor removes the hazard; the Ministry does not install a replacement roof. Current availability and application arrangements have not been published. Ask for the low-income asbestos-hazard removal procedure and the documents needed to establish eligibility.

Geographically targeted programmes

In September 2024, the Ministry announced NIS 12 million for northern local authorities replacing asbestos-cement roofs on public and residential buildings in border areas. This was support directed through local authorities, not a published nationwide grant for any homeowner.

For Gaza-envelope localities, the Knesset reported in December 2023 that NIS 60.5 million had been allocated and NIS 45 million used, with owner participation. About 315,000 m² had been removed in Hof Ashkelon, Sdot Negev, Eshkol and Sha'ar HaNegev. These are dated programme figures, not a statement of funds still available.

A May 2021 announcement approved NIS 11 million to encourage roof removal and solar-panel installation; the current tender status has not been published. A separate NIS 50 million proposal for public buildings was published as draft support criteria in December 2020. It should not be treated as an active residential entitlement.

Buying an older flat, renovating, TAMA 38 or pinui-binui

Before committing to building work, establish whether asbestos is present and who will arrange any required survey, removal and replacement. For an overseas owner, ask for the documents and their practical meaning in English. Do not accept an unexplained assurance that the matter will be dealt with during demolition.

TAMA 38: Israel's national plan for strengthening older buildings against earthquakes, usually in exchange for added floors or flats. Haifa names it in its asbestos-survey policy.

Pinui-binui: urban renewal in which old buildings are demolished and replaced with new, larger ones. Demolition is exactly when asbestos must be found and removed first.

Check municipal requirements for the actual property

Tel Aviv-Yafo requires an asbestos survey before renovation in buildings constructed up to 31.12.2000, as part of the renovation licence request. Its guidance also requires licensed contractors for asbestos dismantling, including work below 50 m².

Haifa makes surveys a condition for permits on plots with a high likelihood of asbestos and for starting work on specified suspect buildings. These include urban-renewal and TAMA 38 buildings, and buildings in the Check Post area, the port, Hadar and downtown. If asbestos is found, Ministry approval of removal is a condition for starting work.

A universal national requirement to attach an asbestos survey to every demolition or renewal application has not been published. Ask the local planning authority what applies to your building and proposed work. Keep the municipal requirement separate from the Ministry's asbestos-work permission process.

Buyer and renovation contract checklist

  • Request existing asbestos surveys, sample results, maintenance records and previous removal documents.
  • Ask the surveyor to identify the inspected areas, any excluded areas and suspect components requiring further assessment.
  • Record who arranges and pays for survey work, removal, transport, disposal and replacement roofing.
  • Specify the required licence categories and the applicable permit or exemption documentation.
  • Agree what happens if additional suspect material or damage is discovered during work.
  • Identify which work must wait for asbestos removal and the required completion documents.
  • Require delivery of the relevant laboratory results, disposal records and final report.
  • For remote ownership, nominate the person who receives notices, communicates with the contractor and keeps the records.

These are points to agree with your lawyer and contractor. They do not assert a standard seller warranty, automatic price reduction or nationally fixed allocation of costs, none of which has been published.

A neighbour's roof, a shared building or a rented flat

When the material belongs to a neighbour

An intact roof and an active hazard call for different responses. If you see breaking, burning, collapse or exposed asbestos waste, report the concern to the local authority or Ministry hotline. Do not enter the property or collect a sample to prove your report. Provide the location and describe the condition or activity you observed.

For nearby removal, ask about the permit or documented exemption and follow the Ministry's window, door and outside-air guidance. If dismantling appears unauthorised, the environment hotline expressly accepts such urgent reports.

Shared buildings, or bayit meshutaf

In a shared building, ask the residents' representatives to arrange an assessment covering the affected common areas. Request a written scope, the licence details and a clear decision on arranging the work. Keep the survey and completion records available to owners, including those abroad.

No source publishes a universal rule dividing asbestos-removal costs among apartment owners. Ask your lawyer to establish responsibility for the particular component and building arrangements. Do not allow a disagreement over payment to obscure an immediate hazard report.

Rented flats

Notify the landlord in writing and request assessment of suspect or damaged material. Describe the location and condition, and ask who will organise licensed work if needed. Do not commission ordinary drilling or roof repairs that would disturb asbestos.

Section 11 refers to owners and holders of premises, not only the person who holds title. If there is an active hazard, report it promptly rather than waiting for a tenancy dispute to be resolved. Automatic rent reductions, termination rights and repair-cost allocation are not published; obtain advice about your agreement and circumstances.

After a fire, a collapse or a missile strike

Burning asbestos-cement, collapse involving asbestos components and exposed asbestos-waste piles are recognised hazard situations. Blast damage can also turn an existing roof into a removal problem. Report suspected damage and keep people away while the appropriate professionals assess it.

  1. For a life-threatening hazardous-material emergency, contact police on 100 or fire and rescue on 102.
  2. Report the environmental hazard to *6911, extension 5, or 073-2733200, and notify the local authority.
  3. Restrict access except for rescue or inspection, and prevent objects or waste from being removed from the affected area.
  4. Contact a friable-asbestos contractor, or a friable-asbestos inspector working with an asbestos-cement contractor, following the Ministry's routing guidance.
  5. Arrange the immediate protective response and the required permit application. Do not treat the seven-day application deadline as a waiting period.

Do not approach this as ordinary rubble clearance. Ask the asbestos professional to define the affected area, permission route, disposal arrangements and required completion evidence. An area that looks tidier is not a substitute for the job's completion record.

The Knesset's December 2023 report recorded 13 war-related asbestos hazards known to the Ministry at that time. That is a dated incident count, not a current total. No universal re-entry time after a fire or strike has been published.

Western Galilee: the history and the soil clean-up project

The Western Galilee has a specific legacy of asbestos waste in soil. The asbestos-cement products plant in Nahariya was established in 1952 and supplied sheets and pipes across Israel. Industrial waste was sold or supplied for roads, paths, yards and agricultural ground cover. Waste also reached the adjacent beach, according to Ministry project information (3.2025).

Waste was distributed within a 15 km radius of the plant, including areas in Mateh Asher Regional Council, Nahariya Municipality and Mazra'a Local Council. That describes the project's historical geography; it does not establish contamination at every property within the area.

What the published clean-up figures mean

The Ministry's December 2025 implementation report describes Stage A, from 2011 to 2017, as removing about 80% of the waste at a cost of NIS 325 million. Its March 2025 project page gives about 130,000 m³ removed from 342 sites, with a NIS 327 million budget and a 70% to 80% estimate. The publications use different budget and completion figures, so they should not be combined into a calculated total.

For Stage B, the December 2025 report described continuing work at dozens of sites, a budget of more than NIS 70 million and expected completion during 2026. Confirmed completion has not been published. The same report records 45,000 tons of contaminated soil removed from the beach north of Nahariya at a cost of about NIS 100 million.

What a buyer or owner should request

A historical government map layer created in 2018 distinguishes sites cleaned with no known asbestos left from sites cleaned where asbestos remained under infrastructure, and sites not yet cleaned. Those labels describe the recorded status at that time. They are not a current clearance certificate for your property.

Request records for the actual site: surveys, the scope of removal, any material recorded as remaining, and relevant sampling or completion documents. If friable asbestos in soil is suspected, the Ministry directs you to a friable-asbestos inspector or contractor. Ask before arranging ground work; a regional clean-up announcement does not answer a parcel-specific question.

Frequently asked questions

Is asbestos dangerous?

Released airborne asbestos fibres are hazardous. Intact, undisturbed asbestos-cement is not dangerous while it remains unbroken, unburnt and not crumbling. Friable material, damaged products and work that releases fibres require a different response. Never drill, cut or break suspect asbestos.

What was asbestos used for in Israel?

Asbestos-cement was used for roofs, wall panels, pipes, gutters, chimneys, planters and tanks. Friable asbestos had insulation and industrial uses. Certain household heat-resistant items also contained asbestos, including heater wicks, heat plates, fire blankets and gloves, according to Ministry guidance.

How do I get rid of asbestos?

Establish what the material is, then use the appropriate licensed professional and permission route. Standard removal involves a Ministry permit and licensed disposal. Defined exemptions exist for particular limited work and products, but do not assume they apply to your roof or damaged waste.

How can I tell if something is asbestos?

Definitive identification requires analysis of a material sample by an accredited laboratory. Treat suspect material as asbestos until established otherwise. Arrange professional assessment rather than breaking off a sample yourself. Ask for a result stating presence, asbestos type and concentration.

How is an asbestos roof removed?

The licensed contractor establishes the scope and applicable permission process, then handles dismantling, packaging, transport and lawful disposal. Ask for the required sampling results and final documentation. Price the replacement roof separately unless the quotation expressly includes it.

Is there free asbestos removal in Israel?

Free hazard removal has been described for qualifying private owners who meet specified benefit and other conditions. It is not a general service for replacing intact roofs, and it excludes a replacement roof. Confirm current availability and eligibility with the Ministry.

Does the state fund asbestos removal?

Yes, published programmes include targeted northern and Gaza-envelope roof replacement, Western Galilee soil remediation and assistance with qualifying private hazards. Their budgets, dates and conditions differ. A historical funding announcement does not establish a current entitlement for your property.

Can I remove asbestos myself?

Only specifically defined limited work or product disposal may qualify for exemption, subject to all applicable conditions. A small area alone does not establish permission. Confirm the route with the Ministry and local authority before acting, and never drill, cut or break asbestos.

What is the fine for illegal asbestos removal?

There is no single amount. The law provides administrative sanctions and criminal penalties. The higher administrative tier in the text recorded on 3.11.2026 was NIS 487,220 for an individual and NIS 974,430 for a corporation. Amounts are indexed and depend on the offence.

Where does asbestos waste go?

Construction asbestos waste goes through the authorised route to a site licensed for the relevant material. Ask your contractor to identify that site and provide disposal records. The Ministry's separate household-product instruction must not be applied to roof sheets or building rubble.

What do I do about an asbestos roof in a rented flat?

Notify the landlord, request professional assessment and avoid disturbing the material. Report an active hazard to the Ministry or local authority without waiting for a tenancy dispute. Ask a lawyer about responsibility and remedies under your agreement; no published rule sets an automatic rent reduction.

How long does a removal permit take?

The statutory decision period is 30 days. In the July 2023 Knesset report, the Ministry described about a week and a half in practice, or the same day for a hazard. These are reported processing times, not a guaranteed appointment or completion date.

Should I worry about asbestos when buying an apartment in Israel?

Include it in your property checks without assuming that every older flat has a hazard. Establish what material is present, its condition and whether planned work will disturb it. Request the relevant survey and records, check municipal requirements and agree responsibility for any necessary work.

This is general information, not legal or medical advice.
