  in the centre. Plan 2500 (1983-2000): three 25-floor towers, 387 homes, 10,800 m² of commerce and 6,000 m² of public space.
  Plan 2500A (2008): three 40-floor towers of 153 m, about 450 homes, no ground-floor commerce.
- The long road (one sentence, as a story of persistence): in 2008 a Green Party petition to keep the square green halted
  plan 2500A; in October 2009 the owners asked to cancel it because 40-floor towers could block IDF communication beams
  (Hebrew Wikipedia, via a search summary, 30.9.2026); the beam problem was resolved in 2012 (TheMarker Labels, 1.6.2021); the
  works required moving a Ministry of Defence antenna (TimelapseIT, undated).

### The towers: floors, height, form
- Three residential towers: two of 40 floors and one of 37 (Ashtrom; Electra; project pages read 30.9.2026).
- Towers A and C: 160 m, 40 floors. Tower B: 157 m, 37 floors (Hebrew Wikipedia, "Kikar HaMedina Towers", read 30.9.2026).
- Municipal buildings data (read 30.9.2026): A: 40 floors, 160.0 m; B: 40 floors, 158.2 m; C: 40 floors, 160.0 m (mean
  height from the municipality's surface survey). The building-site record mentions a request for "2 more floors for building
  B", and its text lists 40 residential floors and 453 homes.
- Every floor turns 1.25° relative to the floor below (Hebrew Wikipedia; Mako, 24.9.2026; Alum Eshet project page).
- Total twist: 50° on the 40-floor towers, 46° on the 37-floor tower (English Wikipedia, "List of twisted buildings", read
  30.9.2026).
- Because of the circular core, the exits sit in a different position on each floor (Hebrew Wikipedia).
- The architects' concept: each residential floor is set back from the floor below and rotates in a circular movement. The
  façades are "a modern interpretation of the current building fronts": a repeating system of floor slabs and white curtain
  walls. The inspiration is the circular form of the square (Yaski Mor Sivan Architects project page, read 30.9.2026).
- The façade: glass and white aluminium curtain walls with prominent floor slabs (Mako, 24.9.2026).
- Alum Eshet's façade package (project page, undated): 85,000 m² in total; 35,000 m² of column and beam cladding; 4,000 m of
  balcony railings; 3 mm aluminium sheet; 300 mm deep shading fins; floor-to-ceiling unitised curtain wall; insulated glass
  with built-in electric shading; full-height pivot windows with inner railings. Aluminium consultant: Landmann.
- Elevators (Electra's Hebrew page, read 30.9.2026): 29 in total. 15 high-speed elevators (4-6 m per second) serve the homes,
  7 are panoramic elevators in the lower section, and 7 are construction freight elevators (two cars per shaft). Air
  conditioning: Daikin VRF.
- Entrance doors and garbage chutes: Rav Bariach (the chutes contract about ₪4 million) (ICE, 4.6.2025).

### Construction: method and pace
- All three towers are built at the same time, not in phases, because all the homes belong to the landowners (Globes,
  16.9.2023).
- The 2023 plan: 52 months of construction and completion in December 2026 (Globes, 16.9.2023).
- Method: three imported slip forms for the circular spiral cores, rising about 1.5 m per working day, which is 5-7 floors a
  month per tower. The municipality limited concrete pours to daytime. The first use in Israel of a perimeter safety system
  for aluminium work without external scaffolding (CivilEng, undated).
- Engineering: 3 reinforced rafts anchored on piles; a circular turning core built with slip forms; a 24-segment perimeter
  system for the aluminium and finishing works (Ashtrom).
- About 7 floors a month, against about 3 in conventional design (Globes English, 25.9.2025).
- The Tel Aviv-Yafo municipality building-site record (file 61-1-2018-0391, read 30.9.2026): the recorded stage is "frame
  completed" (גמר שלד), dated 23.4.2026; works started 6.6.2022; permits 20181007, 20220484, 20220688, 20220958, 20221436,
  20241393, 20241394, 20251961, 20251962.
- September 2026: "the three spiral towers are already visible on the skyline" (Mako, 24.9.2026).
- Ashtrom (Facebook video, undated): the project "continues to progress steadily".
- Progress video: "Kikar HaMedina Towers, September 2025" (YouTube, channel "Ma Bonim Po?", September 2025).

### General law: purchase tax and mortgages (not project facts; copied from the verified fact list of the H Infinity page, 28.9.2026; mark every rate [to be confirmed by the site owner, a lawyer])
- Purchase tax 2026, an Israeli resident's single apartment: 0% up to ₪1,978,745; 3.5% up to ₪2,347,040; 5% up to
  ₪6,055,070; 8% up to about ₪20 million; 10% above (Bizportal, 6.7.2026, which reports the brackets frozen until January
  2028).
- An additional apartment, or a foreign resident: 8% from the first shekel up to ₪6,055,070, 10% above (same source).
- New immigrants (olim): reduced rates for one apartment, from one year before aliyah to seven years after. Sources conflict
  on the exact brackets: say that there is a benefit and send the reader to the Tax Authority simulator
  https://www.misim.gov.il/svsimurechisha/startPage.aspx .
- Mortgages: up to 50% of the value for a non-resident and for an investor; up to 75% for an Israeli resident's first home;
  up to 70% for a resident replacing a home (Bank of Israel, Proper Conduct of Banking Business Directive 329).
- Remote purchase: a power of attorney to an Israeli lawyer, and a non-resident bank account (professional guides, 2026).
- Buying property gives no residency and no citizenship.
