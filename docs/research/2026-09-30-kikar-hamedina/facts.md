Planner: Yaski Mor Sivan Architects — https://xn--5db8awa.com/p/69614 and https://www.tabanow.co.il/תבע/תל-אביב/תא/מק/2500/א (read 30.9.2026)

- Provisions of 2500/א include: land consolidation and division without the owners' consent, road widening, building-line changes, redistribution of main floor area, a raised maximum number of units, service roads and parking structures, and surface and underground easements — https://xn--5db8awa.com/p/69614
- Block (גוש): 6213. Parcels (חלקות) in 2500/א include 184, 189, 209-220, 273, 275, 322-340 and many more; parcels 557-558 were cancelled — https://xn--5db8awa.com/p/69614
- Amending plan: 507-0193490, תא/2500/ב, "כיכר המדינה - שינוי מערך התנועה", in force (published 30.3.2018), district committee, 77.745 dunam. Parcels (all in block 6213): partly 184, 211, 212, 273, 322-325, 327-329, 354, 897, 899, 901-907, 937; wholly 326, 898, 900 — municipal decision 4.8.2021 (URL in 1.1)

### 1.4 Towers: floors, heights, form

- Three residential towers: two of 40 floors and one of 37 — https://www.ashtrom.co.il/projects/kikar-hamedina ; https://www.electra.co.il/en/electra_building/projects/kikar_hamedina_towers (both undated, read 30.9.2026)
- Towers A and C (north and south): 160 m, 40 floors. Tower B (east): 157 m, 37 floors — https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/מגדלי_כיכר_המדינה (read 30.9.2026)
- Every floor turns 1.25° relative to the floor below — same Wikipedia page; also https://www.mako.co.il/living-architecture/local/Article-1b24331b3cec0a1026.htm (24.9.2026) and https://www.alumeshet.co.il/en/projects/%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%94/ (undated)
- Total twist: 50° on the 40-floor towers, 46° on the 37-floor tower — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_twisted_buildings (read 30.9.2026)
- Because of the circular core, the exits sit in a different position on each floor — https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/מגדלי_כיכר_המדינה
- Architect's concept: each residential floor is set back from the floor below and rotates in a circular movement. The façades are "a modern interpretation of the current building fronts": a repeating system of floor slabs and white curtain walls. The inspiration is the circular form of the square — https://m-y-s.com/Kikar-Hamedina (undated, read 30.9.2026)
- Façade: glass and white aluminium curtain walls with prominent floor slabs — https://www.mako.co.il/living-architecture/local/Article-1b24331b3cec0a1026.htm (24.9.2026)
- Façade package by Alum Eshet: 85,000 m² in total; 35,000 m² of column and beam cladding; 4,000 m of balcony railings; 3 mm aluminium sheet; 300 mm deep aluminium/glass shading fins; floor-to-ceiling unitised curtain wall; insulated glass with built-in electric shading; full-height pivot windows with inner railings. Aluminium consultant: Landmann — https://www.alumeshet.co.il/en/projects/%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%A8-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%94/ (undated)
- Lifts (Electra Hebrew page): 29 in total. 15 high-speed lifts (4-6 m/s) serve the homes, 7 are panoramic lifts in the lower section, and 7 are construction freight lifts (two cars per shaft). Air conditioning: Daikin VRF — https://www.electra.co.il/%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%94_%D7%91%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%92/%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%98%D7%99%D7%9D/%D7%9E%D7%92%D7%93%D7%9C%D7%99_%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%A8_%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%94 (undated, read 30.9.2026). The English page says "6 double-deck freight elevators" — see CONFLICTS.
- Entrance doors and garbage chutes are supplied by Rav Bariach (chutes contract about ₪4M) — https://www.ice.co.il/realestate/news/article/1067848 (4.6.2025)

### 1.12 Construction method, progress and timeline

- 1942: first plots bought; 1997: owners' company formed; 2000: architects chosen; 2017: current plan approved; December 2022: building permit; 18.12.2022: first concrete pour — https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1001432722 (14.12.2022)
- Excavation and shoring permit: December 2018. Local committee approval: August 2021. Building permit: December 2022 — https://project-tlv.info/buildings/kikar-hamedina/kikar-hamedina-project/ (read 30.9.2026). Project TLV gives "construction start May 2023" — see CONFLICTS
- All three towers are built at the same time, not in phases, because all the units belong to the landowners — https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001457634 (16.9.2023)
- Construction period: 52 months, completion December 2026 (as planned in 2023) — same Globes article (16.9.2023)
- Method: three imported slip forms for the circular spiral cores, rising about 1.5 m per working day, which is 5-7 floors a month per tower. The municipality limited concrete pours to daytime. It was the first use in Israel of a perimeter safety system for aluminium work without external scaffolding — CivilEng page (undated)
- Engineering challenges: 3 reinforced rafts anchored on piles; a circular turning core built with slip forms; a 24-segment perimeter system for the aluminium and finishing works — https://www.ashtrom.co.il/projects/kikar-hamedina
- About 7 floors a month, against about 3 in conventional design — https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-tel-avivs-kikar-hamedina-undergoes-transformation-1001522524 (25.9.2025)
- The works required moving a Ministry of Defence antenna — https://timelapseit.co.il/en/project-story-kikar-hamedina-towers-tel-aviv/ (undated). The IDF communication-beam problem was resolved in 2012 — https://www.themarker.com/labels/2021-06-01/ty-article-labels/00000180-8db8-d30a-a5ae-afbca44d0000 (1.6.2021)
- Status in September 2026: "three spiral towers already visible on the skyline". No topping-out date or Form 4 status was found — https://www.mako.co.il/living-architecture/local/Article-1b24331b3cec0a1026.htm (24.9.2026)
