Date: 8.11.2026. Basis: the unit map in amit-vered-map.md (365 units; YES 94, PARTIAL 90, NO 181). Unit ids refer to that table. Briefs are instructions for original writing; nothing here is copy. Every official source below must be opened and confirmed on the day of writing; section numbers marked "verify" are from memory of the law and were not fetched (no web access in this task).

## Priority order for the writer

1. The post-signing chain they cover end to end and we skip: warning note, buyer tax report, mortgage execution, seller-mortgage clearing, municipal certificate, tax deposit, handover, registration (units in sections 6 to 10 below).
2. Zikaron devarim (memo of understanding) legal effect, the single biggest legal trap they cover and we mention in one clause.
3. Contract chapters and the ten security mechanisms.
4. Seller identity fraud, spouse consent, joint-house file and plan, use-designation check: all missing from our rights section.
5. Small numbers we lack: 0.2% weekly penalty, 5% margin, 15% ancillary ratio, 39 NIS file fee, 140 sqm levy exemption.
