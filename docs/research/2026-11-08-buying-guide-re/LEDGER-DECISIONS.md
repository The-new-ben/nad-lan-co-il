# Ledger decisions (9.11.2026): how the writers state facts where sources disagree

Read with `sources-ledger-gov.md` (49 rows) and `sources-ledger-law.md` (64 rows). Where this file and a ledger row differ, this file wins.

1. **The advance on the seller's capital-gains tax (מקדמה על חשבון מס השבח):** section 15(ב) of the Real Estate Taxation Law. 7.5% of the price when the seller bought on or after 7.11.2001, and always when the seller is a company; 15% when the seller bought up to 6.11.2001. The buyer transfers it only after more than 40% of the price has been paid (80% when buying from a contractor). It does not apply when the seller has asked for the single-home exemption, or when the seller shows a Tax Authority certificate. Never "section 16".
2. **Additional home rates 8% / 10%:** state them "לרכישות עד 10.2.2027" with the Tax Authority notice of 12.10.2026 as the source. Single-home brackets: frozen until 15.1.2028. Link /purchase-tax-calculator/ for the brackets; do not print a second bracket table in the chapters.
3. **Fines for lateness (purchase tax):** a late declaration costs a fixed sum per two weeks (310 ₪ in 2026, section 94א). For late payment write the rule WITHOUT a rate: "קנס פיגורים לכל שבוע של איחור, נוסף על הפרשי הצמדה וריבית" (the Tax Authority's 2026 guide says 0.2% a week; the law text read says 0.5%; unresolved). The 5% administrative fine may be stated with the gov.il source. An assessment difference is due within 15 days (section 91(א)).
4. **Delivery delay by a developer (contracts signed from 7.7.2022, amendment 9):** compensation runs from the end of the first month after the contractual date: rent of a similar flat ×1 up to month 4, ×1.25 for months 5 to 10, ×1.5 from month 11. Older contracts: the older rule; do not detail it. The ministry's buyer guide still shows the old rule (updated 10.5.2022); cite the amendment 9 page and the law.
5. **Linkage to an index in a developer deal (amendment 9):** only the construction input index; at most half of each payment linked; the first 20% of the price not linked; no linkage after the contractual delivery date.
6. **Sale Law securities:** bank guarantee, insurance policy, warning note with conditions, and the other forms in section 2. A 2026 change lets more licensed bodies issue guarantees (Economic Program Law 2026); its start day is not confirmed, so write "since 2026 the law allows more licensed bodies to issue a guarantee; check who signed it" without a date.
7. **Mortgage limits (Bank of Israel directive 329, version of 30.6.2026):** loan-to-value 75% single home, 70% replacement home, 50% additional home; repayment up to 50% of income; up to 30 years; variable-rate share up to 66.66%. Do NOT write a separate prime cap.
8. **Bank of Israel rate:** 3.25% since 3.9.2026 (read 9.11.2026).
9. **Land Registry fees 2026 (valid from 1.1.2026):** sale registration 44 ₪; warning note 188 ₪; mortgage 188 ₪; cancelling a warning note 127 ₪; online extract 18 ₪; condominium file copy 39 ₪. Online warning note: lawyers with a smart card, up to 3 business days. Online sale registration: up to 16 business days.
10. **Israel Land Authority:** rights confirmation 81 ₪; for a flat run by a housing company, the ILA issues only a free, non-binding notice. Transfer time and fee at the ILA: NOT confirmed, do not state.
11. **Lawyer fees:** the Israel Bar's recommended minimum tariff exists and is not binding (2026: 1% of value up to 633,967 ₪, 0.75% above). The only binding cap: the developer's lawyer fee charged to the buyer, the lower of 0.5% of the price or 5,915 ₪, plus VAT, when the price is up to 4,642,750 ₪.
12. **VAT:** 18% since 1.1.2025.
13. **Developers who refuse to register flats:** the live list is https://www.gov.il/he/Departments/DynamicCollectors/sarvanirishum (17 entries on 9.11.2026); the buyer guide's own link is dead.
14. **CBS dwelling price index:** down 1.8% year on year (release of 15.10.2026, July to August 2026).
15. **Selling the old flat (replacement home):** keep the rule already on the live page (bsell, verified 8.11.2026); do not restate a different window.
16. **Not confirmed, never state as fact:** property-tax clearance for flats unsold since 31.12.1999; ILA transfer time and fee; the project account details beyond "the payment voucher goes to the project's supervised account"; that building files are kept by the local committee (say "בוועדה המקומית לתכנון ולבנייה או במחלקת ההנדסה" as general practice only if needed, without a source line); the pledge registration fee; the electricity name-change fee; water meter reading rules; whether heirs may sell before registration (say "the heirs need an inheritance order or a probated will, check with the lawyer"); the practice of a spouse's signature; what a buyer inherits from a signed urban-renewal deal (say "ask for the signed renewal agreement and read it with the lawyer").
