## Priority order for the writer

1. Developer purchase (chapter 11 below): the biggest block, nearly all of it absent from our page. Start with guarantees (bank guarantee versus policy versus warning note), index mechanics, the request form, the changes annex, delay compensation, and the handover chain (settlement, Form 4 trap, protocol).
2. Signing day with a developer, and the section 6B registration duties of a developer.
3. Second-hand legal gaps: pledges registrar, planning printout, contractual shields against violations, appraisal gap, the bank steps after signing.
4. Tax gaps: levy mechanics, deferral that passes to the buyer, relief populations.
5. Conflicts to settle before writing: advance rate on seller tax (15% / 7.5%), 18 versus 24 months for selling the old flat, equity treatment of borrowed money, amendment numbers of the Sale Law, online extract fee (15, 17, 18 NIS in different pages).
