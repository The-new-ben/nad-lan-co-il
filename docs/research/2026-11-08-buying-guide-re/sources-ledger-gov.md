## Surprises and things the writers must know

1. **The Tax Authority contradicts itself on the additional-home rate.** The 12.10.2026 notice extends 8% / 10% to purchases up to 10.2.2027. The 2026 guide's own table (chapter ט) labels the same 8% / 10% as running "16.1.2024 to 15.01.2028". Use the notice (it is later and specific) and say plainly that nothing is published for purchases from 11.2.2027.
2. **The advance has two rates, not one.** Section 15(ב): 7.5% for a seller who bought from 7.11.2001 (and always when the seller is a company), 15% for a seller who bought up to 6.11.2001. It is due only after more than 40% of the price is paid (80% from a contractor) and not before day 30, and it does not apply when the seller claims the qualifying-home exemption.
3. **Registering a sale costs 44 ₪**, far less than a warning note or a mortgage (188 ₪ each); cancelling a warning note 127 ₪; the online extract 18 ₪. All 2026 amounts change every 1 January by the index, so the page needs a yearly check in January 2027.
4. **The ministry's buyer guide is out of date on delay compensation.** It still says two months' grace, then 150% of rent and 125% after eight months (page updated 10.5.2022). The amendment 9 page says: from the second month of delay 100%, months 5 to 10 125%, from month 11 150%, for contracts signed from 7.7.2022. Write amendment 9; do not copy the guide's paragraph.
5. **The buyer guide's link to the developers who refuse to register is dead.** It points to an old land.gov.il address that now lands on the Israel Land Authority home page. The live list is on gov.il (DynamicCollectors/sarvanirishum), 17 entries on 9.11.2026.
6. **Discount-housing eligibility changed in 2026.** Eligibility now depends on not being listed by the army as an unsettled draft-liable person (High Court ruling plus Land Council decision), and new certificates were paused until 5.10.2026.
7. **A private buyer cannot file a warning note online alone.** Online filing is only for lawyers with a smart card (Comsign or Personal ID); a buyer without a lawyer files at the office by appointment (Sunday and Wednesday mornings) or by post.
8. **Printed extracts are not official.** Both the Land Registry extract and the Israel Land Authority rights confirmation (81 ₪) count as originals only in digital form. For a flat run by a housing company, the authority gives only a free "notice on registration of rights", which is not binding; the binding document comes from the managing body.
9. **No 1999 property-tax rule found on gov.il.** The guide only says the Land Registry needs a certificate of no debts in capital gains, purchase and property tax. Keep the 31.12.1999 note off the page until a source is found.
10. **The fee for registering a pledge is not stated on the pledge service page** (it depends on the chosen period on the payments site), so do not print a number for it.
