## Surprises and corrections

1. Sale Law guarantees are no longer bank-only. The 2026 Economic Program Law (ס"ח 3510, 31.3.2026, section 31) lets "נותן ערבות אחר" (licensed non-bank credit providers, stable-payment providers, insurers) give the guarantee. Chapter ו starts six months after the law's commencement. Pages that say "ערבות בנקאית בלבד" are out of date.
2. Directive 329 has no separate prime cap. Version 13 (30.06.2026) caps only the variable-rate share at 66.66%. Versions 12 and 13 also added: PTI now counts other loans on the same property, non-purchase loans up to 70% LTV (excess over 50% up to 200,000 NIS), reduced-price valuation cap 2.1M NIS (was 1.8M).
3. Delay compensation (contracts from 7.7.2022) is counted from the end of the first month after the contractual date, not from the contractual date. Rent x1 to month 4, x1.25 months 5 to 10, x1.5 from month 11. The old 60-day rule with x1.5 from day one applies only to contracts signed before 7.7.2022. Wikisource prints the start date as 7.7.2023 in section 11(ד); the official gazette gives 7.7.2022.
4. The buyer's advance on the seller's tax is section 15 (not 16): 7.5% of the price, transferred only after more than 40% is paid, and not required when the seller asked for the qualifying-residence exemption.
5. Late-filing fine is a fixed sum per two weeks (310 NIS in 2026 for a section 73 declaration); the late-payment fine is 0.5% per week (section 94ב). Kolzchut's purchase-tax page gives 30 days to pay an assessment difference, while section 91(א) says 15 days (30 only for the undisputed part after an objection).
6. Bank of Israel rate is 3.25%, in force since 3.9.2026 after the 1.9.2026 cut. 2026 path: 4% from 8.1, 3.75% from 28.5, 3.5% from 9.7, 3.25% from 3.9.
7. The 140 sqm betterment-levy exemption: the 4-year residence condition is in 19(ג)(2) and governs the sale of such a flat; a first-time safe room adds to the exempt area (kolzchut). Section 19 carries a תשפ"ו amendment tag whose content was not checked.
8. The Bar's recommended minimum tariff still exists with 2026 amounts (1% of value up to 633,967 NIS, 0.75% above) and is expressly non-binding. The only binding cap found is on the buyer's share of the developer's lawyer: 0.5% or 5,915 NIS (2026), the lower, plus VAT.
9. The certificate rule for local councils is in section 21 of the Local Councils Ordinance, not in an order (צו).
10. Housing prices: CBS release of 15.10.2026 shows a 1.8% fall year on year (July-August 2026).
