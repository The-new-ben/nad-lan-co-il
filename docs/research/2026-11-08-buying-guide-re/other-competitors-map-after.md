# Other competitors: information-unit map AFTER the new page (re-grade of other-competitors-map.md)

- Date: 9.11.2026. Ticket: Linear HAD-686. Read-only re-grading. No web access, no git.
- Method: every one of the 658 units in other-competitors-map.md was re-graded against the NEW page (page-9-v3.txt, 18 steps, FAQ, glossary, sources) plus the text the tools render. The same strict rules as the first map: YES only when the new page states the same fact (wording may differ); PARTIAL when it touches the topic but misses the specific fact, number or nuance; NO when absent. A topic that appears only as a link label or a chip counts as PARTIAL at best.
- Two extra labels: "YES (updated)" = our page states the current official rule where the competitor claim is outdated, simplified or unofficial. "NO (left out: no official source)" = our page deliberately declines to state it because no official source confirms it (the page states it in S14: no uniform official time is published for the whole path or for the municipal and tax approvals; LEDGER-DECISIONS.md item 16 is the general rule that unconfirmed facts are never stated as fact).
- What was read: the old map (all 22 sections, 658 rows); the whole new page text page-9-v3.txt (all 18 steps, the FAQ, the glossary head and the sources head; the long steps by full reading, the rest by targeted searches); the ten existing tools from the old live extraction 42115af023.json (equity, offer price, comparison, bridge-loan, benefits, visit sheet, who pays what, dates, step-by-step table); the five new tools from buying.json (pay, sign, keys, reg, memo); LEDGER-DECISIONS.md. The tools add only a little to the page: the signing checklist (documents to bring), the payment table, the handover meter and key list, the ten registration steps and the six memorandum questions.
- "Where on the new page": S1 to S18 are the step numbers of the guide (S1 budget and costs, S2 financing, S3 city and area, S4 visit and physical check, S5 rights and extract, S6 seller and occupants, S7 condo file and planning, S8 taxes, S9 memorandum and negotiation, S10 contract and lawyer, S11 payment schedule, S12 signing day, S13 warning note, S14 reporting, approvals and mortgage, S15 handover, S16 registration, S17 buying from a developer, S18 special cases). FAQ = the questions block, glossary = the terms block.
- Honest limits: the first map graded the old page of about 6,800 words; the new page is far larger, so many NO rows became YES because a whole new chapter (S17) and the signing, warning-note, payment, handover and registration steps were added. I did not copy from the competitor pages; units are the same short paraphrases as before. Every legal point is as the new page states it; I did not re-verify the law. Where a competitor figure differs from ours (for example 18 months vs 24 months, 70% vs 75%, 17 vs 18 NIS) the row is YES (updated) or marked in the "where" column.
- Reasons for the rows still not YES: gap (an official fact we could add), detail (minor nuance or one competitor example), unofficial (practitioner figure or practice with no official source), scope (outside the guide purpose).
