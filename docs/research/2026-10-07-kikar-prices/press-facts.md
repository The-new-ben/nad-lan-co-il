# Kikar HaMedina / Rova 4: price facts from the press and official sources

Internal research note, 7.10.2026. Facts are paraphrased; numbers are as published. Nothing here goes on a page
without its claim id. Labels: **DEAL** = closed transaction (or an estimate of closed transactions, marked "est."),
**ASKING** = listing / marketing price, **REPORTED** = "according to publications" (celebrity buys), **OFFICIAL** =
government statistics, **ESTIMATE** = professional estimate, **PLAN** = developer filing or planning number.
Article dates are publication dates; the deal date is given when the source states it.
