# Buying costs in Israel, valid October 2026 (inputs for the Kikar price calculator)

Internal research note, retrieved 7.10.2026. Every number has a source. "OFFICIAL" = Tax Authority / Bank of Israel
document; "PRESS" = reported by a news site; "MARKET" = typical range, not a legal rule. Not legal or tax advice:
the page must tell buyers to confirm with a lawyer, and must not present the calculator result as a tax assessment.

## 1. Purchase tax (מס רכישה): official source

Source (OFFICIAL): Israel Tax Authority, הוראת ביצוע מיסוי מקרקעין 1/2025, 16.1.2025,
https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-01-2025-3/he/realestate_inst-01-2025-3.pdf

Freeze: under the Economic Efficiency (Arrangements) Law for 2025, the purchase-tax amounts are **not indexed in tax
years 2025-2027**. The brackets in force from 16.1.2025 "will remain relevant until 15.1.2028" (same document).
So the 16.1.2026 annual update did not change them. Brackets normally update every 16 January.

### 1a. Single residential apartment (דירת מגורים יחידה), Israeli-resident individual, s. 9(ג1ג)(3)
Valid 16.1.2025 → 15.1.2028 (frozen).

| part of the price | rate |
|---|---|
| up to ₪1,978,745 | 0% |
| ₪1,978,745 - ₪2,347,040 | 3.5% |
| ₪2,347,040 - ₪6,055,070 | 5% |
| ₪6,055,070 - ₪20,183,565 | 8% |
| above ₪20,183,565 | 10% |

### 1b. Apartment that is not the buyer's only one (דירה נוספת), temporary provision s. 9(ג1ו)
Per the same document: 8%/10% brackets, and the temporary provision was extended **until 31.12.2026**.

| part of the price | rate |
|---|---|
| up to ₪6,055,070 | 8% |
| above ₪6,055,070 | 10% |

### 1c. New immigrant (עולה) buying a single apartment, regulation 12א (in force for purchases from 15.8.2024)
Sources: OFFICIAL 1/2025 instruction (confirms the reg. 12א amounts follow s. 9 and are frozen);
amounts as stated by capitax (Alex Shapira & Co., tax advisers): https://www.capitax.co.il/content/2/2986
- Apartment value must not exceed ₪20,183,565; benefit used once.
- 0% up to ₪1,978,745; 0.5% from ₪1,978,745 to ₪6,055,070; above ₪6,055,070 the single-apartment brackets apply (8%).
- Eligibility window ("התקופה הקובעת" of reg. 12): from one year before first entry to 7 years after it (capitax;
  bshcpa). Verify the window with the immigrant's lawyer before showing it as a rule.
- Older reg. 12 track (also for a business property): 0.5% up to ₪1,988,090, 5% above, for 16.1.2025-15.1.2026
  (OFFICIAL 1/2025). The 2026 value of this one threshold was not verified.

## 2. VAT

- **18%** from 1.1.2025 (was 17%). OFFICIAL: Tax Authority interpretation notice 5.12.2024,
  https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/represent-info-051224-2/he/vat_represent-info-051224-2.pdf ;
  government decision https://www.gov.il/he/pages/dec1270-2024 . No change for 2026 was found (search on 7.10.2026);
  the Tax Authority rates page (https://www.gov.il/he/departments/general/vat-rate-amount-new) returned HTTP 403 to our
  fetcher, so the "still 18%" check rests on the absence of any change notice.
- VAT applies to lawyer, broker and appraiser fees (below). A new apartment bought from a developer has VAT inside the price.

## 3. Typical buyer fees (MARKET ranges)

Main source (PRESS): Bizportal, "העלויות הנלוות שמפילות תקציבים", 6.8.2026, https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/20038031
Second source: protocol.co.il (undated), https://protocol.co.il/apartment-buying-costs/

| item | typical value | source | type |
|---|---|---|---|
| Buyer's lawyer, second-hand apartment | 0.5%-1% of price + 18% VAT (minimum a few thousand ₪) | Bizportal 6.8.2026; protocol.co.il | MARKET |
| Developer's lawyer fee charged to a buyer of a new apartment | capped at ₪5,915 + VAT or 0.5% of price, the lower (applies up to ~₪4.64M price), 2026 figure | Bizportal 6.8.2026 | PRESS (regulated cap; verify against the regulation before publishing) |
| Broker fee (buyer side) | customary 2% + VAT = 2.36%; 1%-1.5% common when the market is slow | Bizportal 6.8.2026 | MARKET |
| Bank appraiser | ₪350-₪950 | Bizportal 6.8.2026 | MARKET |
| Private appraisal / full inspection | ₪1,500-₪3,500; engineer inspection ₪1,500-₪2,500 | Bizportal 6.8.2026 | MARKET |
| Mortgage file opening fee | 0.25% of the loan, max ₪360 | Bizportal 6.8.2026 | PRESS |
| Mortgage adviser | ₪3,500-₪8,500 | protocol.co.il | MARKET |
| All buying costs together | 4%-9% of the price | Bizportal 6.8.2026 | ESTIMATE |
