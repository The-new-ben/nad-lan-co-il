# סרט הסבר: כך עובדת חברה מארגנת עם חדר הניהול

אורך: 66 שניות ברוחב מסך, 68 שניות לטלפון. בלי קריינות: את הקול מקליטים בנפרד.
כל מה שרואים בסרט צולם מהחדר עצמו, על נתוני דוגמה, והתווית "נתוני דוגמה" מופיעה בכל פריים.
המספרים בכתוביות הם המספרים שהחדר מחשב על נתוני הדוגמה.

## Production notes (English)

- **Files (silent drafts):** `renewal-room-he-16x9.mp4` and `renewal-room-en-16x9.mp4` (1920x1080, 65.6 s), `renewal-room-he-9x16.mp4` and `renewal-room-en-9x16.mp4` (1080x1920, 67.5 s). H.264 High, 30 fps, no audio. Not published anywhere.
- **Source:** the local preview only (`build/preview/server.php`): the plugin's own `/my-renewal/` route and REST code over the sample data of `build/tests/fixture.php`. No live site, no stock footage, no third-party footage, no music.
- **Numbers:** taken from the sample data, and they stay consistent through the film. The complex opens at 26 of 48 (6 short of two thirds). The save signs apartment 15, which makes it 27 of 48. The update typed on screen therefore says "5 left", and the neighbor then sees 56%. The state is reset before every take, so each cut shows the same story.
- **Design:** captions and cards in NadLan Instruments: ink #12202A, sea #2F6F86, deep #1F4B5C, IBM Plex Sans Hebrew and Plex Mono. The red ring marks the tap point (the survey red, used for a pointer only). A "נתוני דוגמה" / "Sample data" tag is in every frame.
- **Not done:** voice (Ben's session), music, a real organizer's data. The captions are burned into the picture: a voice session should follow the timings above, or ask for a clean (caption-free) cut, a one-line change in `capture.py`.
- **Rebuild:** from the renewal-room folder, `php -S 127.0.0.1:8765 build/preview/server.php`, then `python film/build/capture.py 16x9 he` (also `9x16 he`, `16x9 en`, `9x16 en`). Frames go to the scratchpad; the mp4 lands in `film/`.
