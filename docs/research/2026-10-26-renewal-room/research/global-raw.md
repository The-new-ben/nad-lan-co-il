# Renewal room: global competitor evidence (raw)

- Date read: 26.10.2026 (all pages below, unless marked otherwise).
- Method: WebSearch + WebFetch of public pages only. Nothing submitted, no account, no trial, no demo, no contact.
- Public UK G-Cloud (Digital Marketplace) listings were used for published prices and security answers where vendor sites are silent.
- Evidence classes:
  - **seen** = seen on the page named in that section (26.10.2026).
  - **snippet** = only seen in a search-engine result summary, the page itself was not read in full (or was gated).
  - **inferred** = my reasoning from the product's scope, not stated by the vendor.
  - **not stated** = the pages read do not say.
- Scores: **Y** = yes, **P** = partial, **N** = no (only where the product's scope clearly excludes it; marked "inferred"), **?** = not stated.
- Quotes are short (under 15 words), taken from the fetched page text.
- Vendor claims are marketing claims; none were tested.

## Capability rows (used in every matrix)

1. Consent/signature status per unit (owner-by-owner tracking toward a threshold)
2. Multi-building complex in one project/community
3. Portfolio view across many projects (management firm)
4. Owners per unit, incl. co-owners and heirs/estates
5. Document checklist per unit (and whether the tool STORES files)
6. Stage ladder/project timeline, with legal clocks or deadlines
7. Majority/threshold calculator by law or bylaws
8. Per-unit change log/audit trail
9. Role-based privacy (resident sees only own unit)
10. Board/committee report (printable/PDF)
11. Resident updates feed + push/SMS/email/WhatsApp
12. Languages (Hebrew, Russian, Arabic, RTL)
13. Phone use (native app or mobile web)
14. Price for residents; price for the managing firm
15. E-signature built in
16. Meetings/voting/minutes
17. Staff task management/CRM
18. Privacy/security certification (SOC 2, GDPR, hosting)
