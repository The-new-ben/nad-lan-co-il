## 1. Purchase costs

### 1.1 Transfer tax (FMA)
- Rate **3%** of the taxable value + municipal levy of **3% of the tax** = **3.09%** effective. Paid by the buyer before signing. Base = **the higher of the contract price or the objective (tax-assessed) value**. Source: Ta Nea, 17 Jun 2023, https://www.tanea.gr/2023/06/17/economy/o-timokatalogos-stis-metavivaseis-akiniton/ - HIGH (matches every later source, e.g. Global Property Guide Sep 2026 https://www.globalpropertyguide.com/europe/greece/taxes-and-costs).
- **Proposed 15% for non-EU** (see watch list). Official DBP 2027 wording: "tax residents of third countries outside the EU", **from July 2027**, 15.45% incl. municipal levy. Source: https://minfin.gov.gr/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Greece_DBP_2027_final.pdf (PDF created 15 Oct 2026) - HIGH.
  - Bill "Strengthening disposable income, savings and investment", 48 articles, posted on opengov 18 Oct 2026, consultation to **1 Nov 2026**; art. 16 = 15% rate, test = **tax residence, not citizenship**, residential only, definitive contracts from 1 Jul 2027. Source: aludata.gr, 19 Oct 2026 (link above) - MEDIUM (secondary summary; I could not open opengov, HTTP 403).
  - Ministers on 8 Sep 2026: companies/legal entities excluded; ethnic Greeks and long-term residents excluded; commercial premises and plots excluded; start moved from 1 Jan 2027 to 1 Jul 2027. Sources: Protothema 8 Sep 2026 https://www.protothema.gr/economy/article/1875396/foros-metavivasis-15-gia-agorastes-apo-trites-hores-poioi-exairoudai-i-ekkremotita-me-tin-golden-visa/ ; Newmoney 8 Sep 2026 https://www.newmoney.gr/roh/palmos-oikonomias/oikonomia/akinita-se-pious-epivallete-o-foros-metavivasis-15-ti-allaxe-meta-tis-antidrasis/ - MEDIUM.
  - **Sources disagree:** September statements said "citizens of non-EU countries"; the October DBP and bill say "tax residents". Also open: golden-visa EUR 250k conversions (industry asked for 8% or exemption), preliminary contracts signed before July 2027, dual nationals, new builds under VAT suspension. Ballas Pelecanos law firm, 14 Sep 2026 https://www.ballas-pelecanos.com/legal-insights/greece-announces-15-property-transfer-tax-for-non-eueea-homebuyers--p263.html - MEDIUM.

### 1.2 VAT on new buildings (24%)
- New buildings are subject to **24% VAT** unless the developer files for the **suspension**; then the buyer pays the 3.09% transfer tax instead. Covers permits from 2006 onward, incl. land-for-flats (antiparochi); all unsold units under the permit. Source: iefimerida, 8 Jul 2026 https://www.iefimerida.gr/oikonomia/akinita-anastoli-fpa-apallagi-foro-yperaxias-2027 - MEDIUM.
- Law 5246/2025 (gazette 11 Nov 2025): option to elect suspension extended to **31 Dec 2026**; existing suspensions due to expire 31 Dec 2025 extended to 31 Dec 2026. Source: KPMG TaxNewsFlash, 21 Nov 2025 https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2025/11/tnf-greece-vat-suspension-on-real-estate-extended-to-december-31-2026.html - HIGH.
- DBP 2027: suspension "will be formally extended until 31 December 2027". Bill art. 17: existing suspensions extended automatically one year, no new application. Sources: DBP 2027 (15 Oct 2026); aludata (19 Oct 2026). Status: **planned, not law** - HIGH that it is planned.
- Practical: always ask the developer for the AADE suspension filing for the specific permit. Unknown how the proposed 15% rate interacts with VAT-suspended new builds bought by non-EU residents (not stated anywhere).

### 1.3 Notary
- Scale (KYA 111376/2011, FEK B 13/11.1.2012, first bracket amended by decision 72386/8.10.2015): **0.8% to EUR 120,000; 0.7% 120,000.01-380,000; 0.65% 380,000.01-2,000,000; 0.55% 2-5 million**, + EUR 20 fixed, on the declared or (if higher) assessed value. Sources: https://www.dsanet.gr/Epikairothta/Nomothesia/ya111376_12.htm (2012) and https://www.taxheaven.gr/circulars/21981/ariom-72386-8-10-2015 (2015) - HIGH for the text; I found no later amendment but cannot rule one out.
- **+24% VAT** on the fee; extra deed pages ~EUR 5 each, copy pages ~EUR 4. Source: Ta Nea 17 Jun 2023 - MEDIUM.
- Sources quote totals from 0.65-0.8% (private.law wiki) to "up to ~1.5% + VAT" (Ktimatoemporiki 30 Sep 2026). Calculator should use the scale above.
- Law 5293/2026 (FEK A 57/07.04.2026) makes the notary a one-stop shop for transfers (incl. paying cadastre fees; notary withholds inheritance/gift tax from the price under art. 17). Sources: odigostoupoliti.eu https://www.odigostoupoliti.eu/simantikes-allages-stis-metavivaseis-akiniton-topografiko-diagramma-forologiki-enimerotita/ ; DBP 2027 p.82 - MEDIUM/HIGH.

### 1.4 Hellenic Cadastre (land registry) fee
- Decision 2/12-1-2026, **FEK B 64/13.1.2026**, in force from 13 Jan 2026: sale registration **0.5% (5 per mille)** of value + EUR 20 per act (+ EUR 3 fixed listed separately); **0.6%** for certain cadastral-book entries (art. 10 Law 2664/1998). Mortgage registration **0.8%** (0.9% in the same special case) + EUR 20. Source: Taxheaven 14 Jan 2026 https://www.taxheaven.gr/news/72587/kthmatologio-h-nea-apofash-me-ta-pagia-kai-analogika-telh ; Odigos tou Politi 19 Jan 2026 https://www.odigostoupoliti.eu/pagia-kai-analogika-teli-gia-to-ktimatologio-kai-to-ilektroniko-mitroo-enechyron/ - HIGH. VAT on this fee: **unknown** (the decision summary does not mention it; a 2023 article said 24%).
- Older figure 0.475% at old mortgage registries (Ta Nea 2023) is superseded.

### 1.5 Lawyer
- Not mandatory since 1 Jan 2014, but standard for foreigners; a Greek mortgage bank requires a Greek-bar lawyer with power of attorney for non-Greek borrowers. Typical fee **0.5%-1.5% + 24% VAT** (sources range 0.4% to 2%). Sources: Ta Nea 2023; Ktimatoemporiki 30 Sep 2026 https://ktimatoemporiki.gr/news/367623-the-real-cost-of-buying-property-in-greece-in-2026-line-by-line (up to ~1.5% + VAT, LOW); Global Property Guide Sep 2026 (1-2%, MEDIUM); private.law wiki 30 Sep 2026 (0.4-1%, LOW). **Sources disagree** - use 1% + VAT as default, editable.

### 1.6 Real-estate agent
- Typically **2% + 24% VAT** (= 2.48%), negotiable; buyer-side commission is common but not universal. Sources: Ktimatoemporiki 30 Sep 2026 (LOW); Ta Nea 2023 (MEDIUM); GPG lists agent 1-2% optional.

### 1.7 Engineer, energy certificate, municipal clearances
- Engineer's legality certificate (unauthorised-works check) + owner's declaration must be attached to **every** transfer deed since 3 Nov 2017; restated in art. 368 of the new Spatial Planning Code, Law 5306/2026. Source: https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5306/2026/arthro/368 - HIGH. Normally obtained by the seller; buyer can hire his own engineer.
- Electronic Building Identity cost reported at EUR 300 for simple cases up to EUR 1,000 for complex ones (Protothema, undated) - LOW. Whether the E-ID is already compulsory for every transfer in 2026: **not verified**.
- Energy Performance Certificate: required for sale; seller provides. Cost: **unknown** (not verified).
- TAP certificate from the municipality (no unpaid municipal property fee) required before transfer since 2017 (Pilea-Hortiatis municipality notice, 4 Apr 2017, https://pilea-hortiatis.gr/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Aftodioikisi_Μεταβίβαση-ακινήτων_-Επανέρχεται-η-βεβαίωση-ΤΑΠ-από-τους-δήμους_04.04.2017.pdf) - MEDIUM; seller's side.
- Topographic diagram requirement abolished for transfers by law (odigostoupoliti.eu, 2026) - MEDIUM, conditions not read.

### 1.8 AFM (tax number) and bank account
- AFM is free; issued by the Tax Office for Foreign Residents (Athens) in person, by myAADE live video, or through a representative with an apostilled power of attorney + Greek translation. Source: lookuptax.com Greece tax-ID guide (2026) and Protothema English 2 Dec 2025 (AADE guide for Greeks abroad) - LOW/MEDIUM. **Whether a tax representative is mandatory for non-EU residents: unknown** (sources present it as optional).
- Non-resident bank account: 2-6 weeks (youroverseashome/heregreece, 2026) - LOW.

### 1.9 Border zones - prior permit for non-EU buyers (affects Israelis)
- Law 1892/1990 arts 24-26: non-EU/EEA natural and legal persons need **prior approval** of a Decentralised Administration committee to buy in border areas; a deal without it is void. Areas listed: **entire Dodecanese (Rhodes, Kos, Patmos, Symi...)**, Lesvos, Chios, Samos, Limnos, Samothraki, **Thira (Santorini)**, Skyros, Diapontia islands (not Corfu island itself); mainland Evros, Xanthi, Rodopi, Kilkis, Kastoria, Florina, Thesprotia, Preveza and parts of Drama, Ioannina, Pella, Serres. Takes months; refusals said to be rare. Source: private.law wiki, last modified 30 Sep 2026 https://wiki.private.law/en/greece-property-purchase - MEDIUM; Hellenic Parliament question (Feb 2024) https://www.hellenicparliament.gr/UserFiles/c0d5184d-7550-4265-8e0b-078e1bc7375a/12134845.pdf confirms arts 24-26 - MEDIUM. **Verify the exact area list against the law text before publishing.** Crete, Athens, Thessaloniki, Halkidiki, Corfu island are not on the list found.

## 2. Annual holding costs

### 2.1 ENFIA (annual property tax) - how it is computed
Legal basis: Property Tax Code, **Law 5219/2025, art. 11** (text on Taxheaven https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5219/2025/arthro/11) - HIGH. Owner on **1 January** pays for that year; bills issued mid-March, lump sum or **12 monthly instalments** from end-March.

- **Main tax (buildings)** = area m2 x base tax by zone x age coef. x floor coef. (detached house 1.02) x facade coef. (x 0.10 for auxiliary spaces, x 0.40 unfinished/never-electrified).
- Zone price (EUR/m2) -> base tax (EUR/m2): 0-750 -> 2.00; 751-1,500 -> 2.80; 1,501-2,500 -> 3.70; 2,501-3,000 -> 4.50; 3,001-3,500 -> 7.60; 3,501-4,000 -> 9.20; 4,001-4,500 -> 11.10; 4,501-5,000 -> 13.40; 5,001+ -> 16.20.
- Age: 26+ yrs 1.00; 20-25 1.05; 15-19 1.10; 10-14 1.15; 5-9 1.20; up to 4 1.25; built before 1930 0.80; over 100 years 0.60.
- Floor: basement 0.98; ground/1st 1.00; 2nd-3rd 1.01; 4th-5th 1.02; 6th+ 1.03. Facades: none 1.00; one 1.01; two+ 1.02.
- **Additional value tax (Section G)** for individuals whose total property value exceeds **EUR 300,000**, on the value of each right: 0% to 400k; 0.2% 400-500k; 0.3% 500-600k; 0.4% 600-700k; 0.5% 700-800k; 0.6% 800-900k; 0.7% 900k-1m; 0.9% 1-2m; 1.0% above 2m.
- **Surcharge on the main tax (Section E)** when total property value exceeds **EUR 500,000**: +5% (to 650k), +10% (to 800k), +15% (to 1m), +20% (above 1m).
- Legal entities supplementary tax: 0.55% general, 0.1% own business use (art. 12) - HIGH.
- **Insurance discount (2025-2026):** 20% where the owner's total residential objective value <= EUR 500,000, 10% above; home insured against earthquake, fire and flood for at least 3 months of the prior year (pro rata), insured value at least EUR 1,000/m2 of building; applies to main + supplementary tax. Source: capital.gr 10 Jan 2026 https://www.capital.gr/tax/3967310/enfia-pos-kleidonei-i-ekptosi-eos-20-to-2026-gia-akinita-asfalismena-gia-fusikes-katastrofes/ - MEDIUM.
- Small-settlement main-residence relief (50% in 2026, exemption from 2027, threshold rising to 2,000 inhabitants) applies to main residences of Greek tax residents only, Attica excluded - **not relevant to Israeli non-residents**. Source: DBP 2027 - HIGH.
- National scale: ENFIA 2026 = EUR 2.297 bn on 6,171,359 liable taxpayers (average ~EUR 372); insurance discount to 428,147 owners. Source: AADE figures via Naftemporiki, 15 Mar 2026 https://www.naftemporiki.gr/finance/economy/2085853/aade-anartithikan-ta-ekkatharistika-toy-enfia-2026-oi-prothesmies-oi-tropoi-pliromis-kai-oi-ekptoseis/ - MEDIUM.
- The zone price of a specific address must be looked up (AADE / zone tables); not known per area here.

## 3. Rental income - Greek tax and short-term rental rules

### 3.1 Greek income tax on rent (individuals, residents and non-residents)
- Non-residents are taxed on Greek-source income incl. rent from Greek property; rent is taxed on its own scale with a flat **5%** deduction instead of actual expenses. Source: Global Property Guide, Sep 2026 https://www.globalpropertyguide.com/europe/greece/taxes-and-costs - MEDIUM.
- **Scale for rent earned from 1 Jan 2026 (Law 5246/2025 art. 8):** 0-12,000 **15%**; 12,000.01-24,000 **25%** (new); 24,000.01-36,000 **35%**; above 36,000 **45%**. Until 2025: 15% / 35% (12,001-35,000) / 45%. Source: KPMG Greece, Nov 2025 https://kpmg.com/gr/en/insights/2025/11/tax-updates-20112025.html - HIGH; confirmed by DBP 2027 p.82 - HIGH. (GPG table prints "45% on the portion over EUR 35,000" - typo, flag.)
- Rent not paid through a bank loses the 5% deduction from 1 Jan 2026. Source: To Vima (2025) https://www.tovima.com/society/renting-in-greece-cash-payments-end-on-jan-1-2026/amp/ - MEDIUM.
- Examples (computed): gross EUR 12,000 -> tax EUR 1,710; EUR 24,000 -> EUR 4,500; EUR 40,000 -> EUR 9,900.
- Long-term-let incentive: 36-month income-tax exemption for homes up to 120 m2 (+20 m2 per child beyond two) let for 3+ years after 3 vacant years (or STR-only the prior year); leases signed 8 Sep 2024 - 31 Dec 2026; bill extends signing to end-2027. Sources: KPMG Greece Nov 2025; aludata 19 Oct 2026 - HIGH/MEDIUM. Whether non-residents qualify: **not verified**.

### 3.2 Short-term rental (Airbnb) rules
- Definition: furnished lease under 60 days; every unit needs an **AMA** registration number from AADE shown on every listing (art. 111 Law 4446/2016). Source: buygreece.us, reviewed Oct 2026 - LOW; registry rule itself confirmed by stegasi.gov.gr - HIGH.
- **Business threshold:** individuals with **3 or more** STR properties are treated as running a business (business income, social security registration) and charge **13% VAT**; up to 2 properties = property income, outside VAT; legal entities always business + 13% VAT. Law 5073/2023, from 1 Jan 2024. Source: Zepos & Yannopoulos newsletter 18 Dec 2023 https://www.zeya.com/sites/default/files/2023-12/real_estate_tax_newsletter_18_december_2023.pdf - MEDIUM.
- **Climate resilience fee per night** (Law 5162/2024 art. 24, stays from 1 Jan 2025; paid by guest, collected by host): STR unit **EUR 8 (Apr-Oct) / EUR 2 (Nov-Mar)**; STR detached house over 80 m2 **EUR 15 / EUR 4**; hotels 1-2* 2/0.5, 3* 5/1.5, 4* 10/3, 5* 15/4. Source: law text on Taxheaven https://www.taxheaven.gr/law/5162/2024/arthro/24 - HIGH. 2024 levels for reference: STR 1.50/0.50, detached >80 m2 10/4 (decision A.1217/2023). A Greek City Times article of Mar 2026 still quotes EUR 10 for detached houses - **outdated, sources disagree; statute says 15**. No change for 2027 found.
- **Freeze on new registrations:** Athens **1st district** (Plaka, Monastiraki, Syntagma, Omonia, Kolonaki, Exarcheia, Ilisia, Neapoli, Koukaki), **2nd** (Mets, Neos Kosmos, Agios Artemios, Pagrati), **3rd** (Petralona, Thiseio, Gazi, Votanikos, Metaxourgeio, Rouf): no first-time registrations to **31 Dec 2026** (KYA 225563 EX 2025, FEK B 7200/31.12.2025). Thessaloniki **1st Municipal Community**: **1 Jul - 31 Dec 2026** (art. 5 Law 5313/2026). On a sale, gift or parental transfer in these areas the AMA is **deleted** and cannot be re-registered while the freeze lasts - a buyer cannot take over an existing Airbnb there. Source: Housing Policy portal https://stegasi.gov.gr/programs/prosorinoi-periorismoi-stis-vrachychronies-misthoseis/ - HIGH; district names: GTP 26 Jan 2026 https://news.gtp.gr/2026/01/26/short-term-rentals-in-greece-new-restrictions-take-effect-in-2026/ - MEDIUM.
- 2027: DBP 2027 lists "extension of the restriction on new short-term rentals in three municipal districts of Athens and one of Thessaloniki for 2027" - HIGH (planned, not yet enacted). Earlier reports of a 1 Mar 2026 Thessaloniki start were superseded by the 1 Jul 2026 law.
- Fines: letting without AMA 50% of STR income, min EUR 20,000; repeat in same year 100% of rents, min EUR 40,000 (stegasi.gov.gr - HIGH). Safety/insurance rules (Law 5170/2025) breaches from EUR 5,000 (press, GTP Jan 2026 - MEDIUM).
- Golden-visa properties may not be let short-term at all (see section 6).

## 4. Israeli side

### 4.1 Rent from a Greek flat - Israeli Tax Authority (ITA) rules
Source for this sub-section: ITA guide "Know your rights" for tax year 2025, published 2026, section 25 p.44 https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/income-tax-guide-knowyourright/he/Guides_IncomeTax_da-2025.pdf - HIGH.
- An Israeli resident with rent from property abroad is taxable on it, **subject to tax treaties**.
- **15% track (section 122A):** 15% on the rent **after allowed depreciation only**; no other expenses, no loss offset, **no credit for Greek tax**, no exemption. Reported in form 1301 fields 345/285/225; if this is the only reason to file, it can be paid via the "short report" without an annual return.
- **Regular track:** marginal rates on net rent (actual expenses + depreciation), with **foreign tax credit** (form 1324 = appendix D to 1301), by income basket; credit only for final tax paid within 24 months after the tax year; excess credit carried forward up to 5 years (CPI-linked); municipal taxes and purchase/transfer taxes are **not** creditable (so ENFIA and Greek transfer tax are not creditable).
- The Israeli **exemption track and 10% track** apply only to residential rent **in Israel**, not abroad (the guide lists foreign rent only under the 15% / regular options) - HIGH.
- Example: EUR 12,000 rent, no depreciation: Greek tax EUR 1,710 + Israeli 15% track EUR 1,800 = EUR 3,510 (29%) **if** Israel taxes it; see 4.4.
- "Dirot meshukenot" (Israeli rented-flats tracks): not applicable to a Greek flat (above).

### 4.2 Capital gain on selling the Greek flat - Israel
- A foreign flat is an "asset" under section 88 of the Income Tax Ordinance; the Land Appreciation Tax Law does not apply. Gain taxed as capital gain: real gain accrued to end-2011 up to 20%, later gain up to **25%**; report to the assessing officer within **30 days** of the sale; foreign tax paid is credited, no refund of excess. Source: Ben Shushan CPA, 15 Jul 2024 https://www.bshcpa.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%92%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9C/ - MEDIUM. Form for non-securities capital gains: **1399** (ITA guide 2025) - HIGH.

### 4.4 Israel-Greece double tax treaty
- Convention signed **Jerusalem, 24 Oct 1995**; **in force 6 Mar 1998** (applies to taxes for years from 1 Jan 1999); Greek ratification Law 2572/1998 (FEK A 12/13.1.1998). Source: UN Treaty Series vol. 2274, No. 40499 https://treaties.un.org/doc/Publication/UNTS/Volume%202274/v2274.pdf - HIGH.
- **Art. 6 (income from immovable property), English text:** income of a resident of one state from immovable property in the other state "shall be taxable only in that other State" - i.e. **only Greece**. Covers letting and any other use. HIGH (treaty text).
- **Art. 13(1) (gains from immovable property):** taxable **only** in the state where the property is (Greece). HIGH.
- Art. 22: general credit method. Final clause: Greek, Hebrew and English texts equally authentic; **English prevails** on divergence.
- **Conflict flagged:** Israeli practitioners report that the **Hebrew** text of art. 6 reads "may be taxed" (not exclusive); they conclude English prevails, so Israel should not tax Greek rent or Greek sale gains. Sources: Asnafy law office 5 Sep 2018 https://asnafy.co.il/%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C-%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%99-%D7%91%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%9C%D7%90%D7%95%D7%9E/ ; Doron Tikotzky 12 Sep 2019 https://www.dt-law.co.il/articles.asp?ArticleID=12209 - MEDIUM. **No ITA ruling or circular found** - the ITA guide only says "subject to treaties". Pages must present this as "the treaty text points to Greek-only taxation; confirm with an Israeli tax adviser", not as settled.
- Double non-taxation risk to flag: Greek capital gains tax is suspended (to end-2026, planned end-2027) while art. 13 bars Israel - unresolved how ITA treats it.

## 6. Golden visa (residence by real-estate investment), 2026
Main source: IBA article by Drakopoulos law firm (Athens), 9 Sep 2026 https://www.ibanet.org/the-greek-golden-visa-after-2024-2026-overhaul - MEDIUM; legal basis Law 5100/2024 art. 64 amending art. 100 of the Migration Code (Law 5038/2023).
- **EUR 800,000**: Attica, regional unit of Thessaloniki, Mykonos, Santorini, and islands with **over 3,100** inhabitants. **EUR 400,000**: rest of Greece. (One Turkish press report used 3,000 and named Rhodes/Crete - less reliable.)
- **Single property** only (no aggregation of units). Built property (or with issued permit) must have **main areas of at least 120 m2** (storage/parking not counted toward area).
- **EUR 250,000 routes:** (a) commercial/industrial converted to residential, change legally completed before filing and after 5 Apr 2024, engineer's certificate; no 120 m2 rule; (b) restoration of a **listed building** - full restoration before first renewal, no sale before, failure = revocation + **EUR 150,000** fine.
- **Short-term letting banned** for golden-visa properties (and subletting): breach = revocation + **EUR 50,000** fine; long-term lease allowed; not retroactive to properties under the old regime/transition. **Sources disagree** on scope: one reports an April 2026 circular limiting it to sub-60-day lets without hotel services (greektriplanner.me - LOW).
- Permit **5 years**, renewable while the property is held; **no minimum stay**. Law 5275/2026 (new Migration Code, from 6 Feb 2026): the 5-year clock starts when the card is issued (globallawexperts / greektriplanner - LOW).
- Family: spouse/partner, children under 21, parents of both spouses (secondary guides - MEDIUM).
- Fees: **EUR 2,000** electronic fee + **EUR 16** card (Migration Ministry page https://migration.gov.gr/en/golden-visa/ - official but outdated text, MEDIUM); EUR 150 per family member (secondary - LOW).
- Timeline: 4-6 months for a complete file in 2026 (secondary - LOW); backlog 33,051 pending at end-May 2026 (stacker.com https://stacker.com/stories/international/greece-cut-its-golden-visa-queue-7-two-months - LOW, via search summary).
- Data: H1 2026 new applications **2,551** vs 4,553 in H1 2025 (-44%); 4,919 permits granted H1 2026 (+21%); 5,730 pending cases in Attica. Source: GreekReporter 26 Aug 2026 https://greekreporter.com/2026/08/26/greece-golden-visa-applications-drop/ - MEDIUM. Valid permits Jan 2026: 81,561 (28,589 main investors) - Greek City Times 17 Mar 2026 - LOW (its percentages do not reconcile).
- Interaction with the proposed 15% transfer tax: no golden-visa exemption announced; EUR 250k conversions unresolved (Protothema 8 Sep 2026).

## 9. Process, timeline and risks

### 9.1 Steps (typical; timings are estimates - LOW unless noted)
1. **AFM** tax number (same day in person / about a week remote) - see 1.8.
2. **Greek bank account** (2-6 weeks for non-residents) - optional but useful for traceable payments; rent must flow through banks to keep the 5% deduction.
3. **Power of attorney** to a Greek lawyer if not present: apostille + certified Greek translation (secondary sources - MEDIUM).
4. **Lawyer due diligence** (about 2-4 weeks): title chain in the Cadastre (KAEK), encumbrances/mortgages/seizures, building permit, engineer's legality certificate (unauthorised additions), forest-map status, co-ownership/inheritance, unpaid ENFIA/TAP/common charges, STR status (AMA) if relevant, border-zone permit need.
5. **Reservation / preliminary contract** with deposit (often ~10% in market practice - **not verified**, no official norm).
6. **Taxes paid** (transfer tax before signing) -> **notarial deed** (notary as one-stop shop under Law 5293/2026).
7. **Registration at the Hellenic Cadastre** (notary files; registration is the proof of ownership).
8. After: declare the property in E9/myPROPERTY, energy/utility contracts, insurance (for ENFIA discount), annual Greek tax return if letting.
- Overall: about **6-12 weeks** from offer to deed without mortgage or border permit (estimate; no authoritative source). Border-zone permit adds **months**.

### 9.2 Risks and the check that prevents each
- **Unauthorised constructions** (enclosed balconies, extra floors, semi-open spaces): engineer's certificate mandatory in every transfer (Law 4495/2017, art. 368 Law 5306/2026 - HIGH); buyer's own engineer recommended.
- **Cadastre gaps / errors:** 99% of the territory has posted cadastral data with KAEK (RE+D Magazine 26 May 2026 https://ered.gr/en/greeces-cadastre-nears-completion-with-99-coverage/ - MEDIUM); over 71% of rights (27.85 m) registered in the operational cadastre per Court of Audit report (ered.gr, Apr 2026 - MEDIUM); full digital cadastre targeted end-2026. Check KAEK record vs title deed and actual boundaries.
- **Forest maps:** properties wrongly shown as forest; 2026 reform lets owners correct cadastral records/remove from forest map under conditions (To Vima, 2026 https://www.tovima.com/society/new-rules-aim-to-untangle-greeces-property-registry-maze/amp/ - MEDIUM, read via search summary only; Court of Audit flags missing forest maps, ered.gr Apr 2026 https://ered.gr/?p=75700 - MEDIUM). No 2026 ratification % found (**unknown**). Check the forest-map status of plots and villas, especially on islands.
- **Inheritance / co-ownership:** many older properties are in undivided shares among heirs; all co-owners must sign; unaccepted inheritances block transfer. Check title chain and acceptance-of-inheritance deeds (general practice - MEDIUM).
- **Energy certificate / building ID:** require the seller's EPC and engineer's certificate before signing.
- **Border zones (non-EU):** prior committee approval or the deal is void (Law 1892/1990) - see 1.9.
- **STR plans:** in Athens 1st-3rd districts and Thessaloniki centre an existing AMA dies on sale; golden-visa properties cannot be let short-term at all.
- **Tax-law timing:** if the 15% rate passes, the date of the **definitive** deed (not the preliminary contract) is decisive per the bill summary; transitional rules unknown.
