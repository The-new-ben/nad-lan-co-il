The cost of buying

What it costs to buy: Israeli purchase tax and mortgages

Your purchase budget needs to cover the apartment price, purchase tax and the professional fees you agree. Purchase tax is the buyer’s tax on acquiring the property. For an overseas buyer, the distinction to settle early is between the Israeli-resident single-apartment schedule and the schedule for an additional apartment or a foreign resident. Being a foreign resident with no other apartment in Israel does not, by itself, put you on the Israeli-resident single-apartment schedule.

The lawyer and the broker’s fee

Ask your real-estate lawyer to examine the seller’s rights, identify the obligations included in the transfer and review the contract and its appendices. Agree the scope of the lawyer’s work and the fee in writing. For a rights purchase, make the treatment of outstanding construction payments part of that review, so the legal description and your budget describe the same transaction.

A real-estate broker is entitled to a fee only if licensed, holding a signed written order from the client and the effective cause of the deal. The fee is the amount stated in that written order. Budget for the service you have agreed rather than inserting an assumed standard percentage.

New immigrants

Olim, or new immigrants, have a reduced purchase-tax route subject to legal conditions. Ask a tax advisor to check eligibility and compare the applicable treatment before committing to a calculation. Keep this assessment specific to your circumstances rather than assuming that the standard foreign-resident example settles the tax position for every overseas buyer moving to Israel.

Step by step

How to buy an apartment in Kikar Hamedina Towers, from Israel or from abroad

Foreign buyers can purchase an apartment here, and the purchase can be handled from abroad through a lawyer acting under a power of attorney. Buying property does not itself give you Israeli residency. The guide to buying property in Israel from abroad provides a starting point for organizing the transaction around where you live. :chatgpt-content-reference{index="2"}

  1. Choose a position on this page. Select the tower, floor and apartment by direction, then write down your preferred layout and outdoor-space requirements. Use that shortlist to focus the conversation on actual homes that fit, rather than beginning with every apartment mentioned in an advertisement.
  2. Request the owner’s offer. Contact the owner or the owner’s broker for the apartment’s availability and proposed sale terms. Establish whether the offer concerns rights or a finished apartment, and ask for the full financial description before treating the headline price as your purchase budget.
  3. Collect the apartment documents. Ask for the plan of the home being offered, its specification appendix and the parking and storage allocation. Keep the documents together so the apartment you choose, the one priced in the offer and the one described in the contract can be compared directly.
  4. Put legal representation in place. Appoint a real-estate lawyer to examine the transaction and agree the scope of the work. For a remote purchase, arrange the power of attorney with that lawyer and clarify which actions the lawyer is authorized to take on your behalf.
  5. Settle the funding plan. Bring the lender’s decision, available equity, tax calculation and agreed professional fees into the same budget. Ask your lawyer to compare that funding plan with the proposed payment schedule, including any construction amounts that remain payable under a rights purchase.
  6. Agree and sign the apartment-specific contract. Have the agreed property description, price, payment obligations, specification and delivery date recorded together. Give your lawyer the final offer and all agreed changes, so the signed documents reflect the purchase you have chosen rather than an earlier advertisement.
  7. Keep the agreed record up to date. After signing, retain the contract, appendices, payment records and correspondence in a shared file with your representative. Ask for proposed changes to be explained in writing and reviewed against the agreement before you approve them from abroad.
  8. Arrange delivery and the apartment inspection. Plan who will represent you locally and compare the delivered home with the agreed documents. Discuss how to record any unfinished items and follow them through. The guide to taking delivery of an apartment from abroad addresses this final stage.

For sale and for rent

Kikar Hamedina Towers apartments for sale and for rent

A home here is bought from an individual owner, not selected from a central sales inventory. About 250 rights holders own the homes. There is no project sales office or official price list; owners resell their apartments, usually through real-estate brokers. Your search therefore needs to identify an owner who is offering a suitable home, rather than assume that every apartment in a preferred position can be bought.

How much is expected to come onto the market?

An April 2025 estimate put the expected sale supply at at most 200 to 250 apartments, because many owners planned to live in the towers themselves. That is a projection of potential supply, not a count of homes currently available. It does not identify which tower positions or layouts will be offered, or establish that they will be marketed together.

For a focused search, give the person handling it a clear brief: the features you need, the features you can compromise on and your complete purchase budget. Ask for the current offer for each suitable home, with the seller identified and the advertised details checked. This makes it easier to compare a small group of relevant opportunities without assuming that a published example remains available.

The directory of licensed real estate brokers is a route to finding someone to handle that search. Ask whether the contact represents the owner and whether the apartment is still being offered on the stated terms. Keep your preferred home distinct from similar descriptions until its tower, floor and apartment have been matched to the owner’s documents.

Renting in the towers

Rentals begin only after occupancy, and rents for the towers are not published. A published sale price cannot establish the rent for the same home. For someone planning to lease rather than buy, the useful inquiry is whether an owner intends to let the apartment, when it can be occupied and what terms will be offered.

For an investment purchase, keep the rental decision separate from the advertised purchase price. Ask for an actual rental proposal when one becomes available rather than treating an assumed rent as an agreed income stream. For a future tenant, request a follow-up tied to the specific apartment’s availability, not simply to the project’s general construction progress.

How construction has progressed

The foundations followed the excavation of about 300,000 m³ of earth, with three reinforced rafts on piles beneath the towers. All the towers are being built at the same time, rather than in phases. Their circular cores rose using imported slip forms, with reported progress of about 1.5 m per working day.

That method supported a pace of 5 to 7 floors per month per tower. The published comparison was about 7 floors a month against about 3 with conventional construction. This describes the structural work: use it to understand how the towers rose, rather than extrapolate it into a timetable for the remaining apartment and shared-area work.

Reading the different estimates

The builders’ published estimates include 2026 and April 2027. A September 2025 estimate from the financier referred to completion within about two years, pointing to about 2027. An earlier estimate, published in April 2025, referred to the end of 2028. These are different dated estimates, not a single confirmed appointment for taking possession.

When discussing a particular home, ask the seller to connect the proposed handover to the apartment’s contractual obligations. Keep that answer with the specification and payment schedule, so the timing you use for your own move comes from the transaction rather than a general project estimate.

The public works have a separate timetable

A September 2026 estimate placed completion of the square’s public works, including the park and He Be’Iyar Street, at the end of 2027. That timetable concerns the public works, not the towers. Discuss shared residential areas separately through the guide to handover of the common property, and ask which spaces will be available when your apartment is delivered.

Timeline

From the plots of 1942 to the frame of 2026

The circle first appeared in a city plan in 1939. The milestones below trace the landownership, planning and construction history behind the present residential project.

  1. 1942 Private investors buy the plots. Those holdings form the basis of the landownership from which the apartment project later takes shape.
  2. 1969 The square’s plan is drawn with Oscar Niemeyer. Israel Lotan and Abba Elhanani design the ring buildings, built mostly in the early 1970s.
  3. 1997 The owners form an association, bringing their interests together in the organization that later becomes the landowners’ company.
  4. 2000 Yaski Mor Sivan Architects is chosen through an architectural competition, establishing the design team for the towers.
  5. 24 June 2013 The compound plan takes effect. It allows 453 homes, about 59,175 m² of housing and 6,000 m² of public buildings across about 78 dunam.
  6. March 2018 The traffic amendment to the plan takes effect, adding a further planning milestone before the construction permits.
  7. December 2018 The excavation and shoring permit is issued, authorizing the preparatory ground works ahead of the later building permit.
  8. November 2021 Electra Construction and Ashtrom are selected jointly to build the towers after a competition involving 6 bidding groups.
  9. December 2022 The building permit is issued, a separate authorization from the earlier permit for excavation and shoring.
  10. 18 December 2022 The first concrete pour provides a precise construction milestone within the month in which the building permit is issued.
  11. 23 April 2026 The frame is recorded as completed, the latest recorded structural milestone in this timeline.
