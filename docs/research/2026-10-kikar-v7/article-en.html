Tel Aviv’s Kikar Hamedina Towers (מגדלי כיכר המדינה) comprises three towers with 453 apartments beside a public park of about 40 dunam. The landowners’ buildings rise 40, 40 and 37 floors, with each floor turning 1.25 degrees. The frame was recorded as completed on 23 April 2026. Three published deals from April to December 2024 ranged from ₪9.58 million to ₪10.63 million. Your choice comes down to the apartment’s position, layout and contract, including the specification, what the asking price covers and the seller’s agreed delivery date.

Tower, floor and direction

Which tower, floor and direction to choose at Kikar Hamedina Towers

Begin with the tower’s position on the plot. Tower A stands to the southwest, Tower B to the southeast and Tower C to the northeast. The tower positions are about 75 to 78 m from the plot center. A compass direction alone does not describe the whole choice: compare the tower, floor and apartment orientation together. You can choose tower, floor and apartment by direction on this page, then ask for the plan of the specific home you are considering.

What the turning floors change

The buildings are also called the Spiral Towers. Each residential floor steps back slightly and turns relative to the floor below, following the circular character of the square. The architects’ treatment of the floor slabs and white façades is a modern interpretation of the buildings around the ring. The 1.25-degree turn accumulates to about 50 degrees over 40 floors, or about 46 degrees over 37 floors. This makes the exact floor relevant to how a balcony is aligned, and to the direction you face when looking out from it. Read its position from that apartment’s plan rather than assuming that a balcony farther up the building faces precisely the same way.

Insulated glass includes built-in electric shading; assess sun and shade at the hours you expect to use the balcony.

The lawyer and the broker’s fee

Ask your real-estate lawyer to examine the seller’s rights, identify the obligations included in the transfer and review the contract and its appendices. Agree the scope of the lawyer’s work and the fee in writing. For a rights purchase, make the treatment of outstanding construction payments part of that review, so the legal description and your budget describe the same transaction.

A real-estate broker is entitled to a fee only if licensed, holding a signed written order from the client and the effective cause of the deal. The fee is the amount stated in that written order. Budget for the service you have agreed rather than inserting an assumed standard percentage.

New immigrants

Olim, or new immigrants, have a reduced purchase-tax route subject to legal conditions. Ask a tax advisor to check eligibility and compare the applicable treatment before committing to a calculation. Keep this assessment specific to your circumstances rather than assuming that the standard foreign-resident example settles the tax position for every overseas buyer moving to Israel.

How to buy an apartment in Kikar Hamedina Towers, from Israel or from abroad

  2. Request the owner’s offer. Contact the owner or the owner’s broker for the apartment’s availability and proposed sale terms. Occupancy

When will Kikar Hamedina Towers be ready?

The frame was recorded as completed on 23 April 2026. That gives buyers a dated construction milestone, but not an official move-in date. Published occupancy estimates span 2026 to 2028, and an official Form 4 date is not published. For the apartment you are buying, the delivery date is the one written into the seller’s contract.

How construction has progressed

The foundations followed the excavation of about 300,000 m³ of earth, with three reinforced rafts on piles beneath the towers. All the towers are being built at the same time, rather than in phases. Their circular cores rose using imported slip forms, with reported progress of about 1.5 m per working day.

That method supported a pace of 5 to 7 floors per month per tower. The published comparison was about 7 floors a month against about 3 with conventional construction. This describes the structural work: use it to understand how the towers rose, rather than extrapolate it into a timetable for the remaining apartment and shared-area work.

Reading the different estimates

The builders’ published estimates include 2026 and April 2027. A September 2025 estimate from the financier referred to completion within about two years, pointing to about 2027. An earlier estimate, published in April 2025, referred to the end of 2028. These are different dated estimates, not a single confirmed appointment for taking possession.

When discussing a particular home, ask the seller to connect the proposed handover to the apartment’s contractual obligations. Keep that answer with the specification and payment schedule, so the timing you use for your own move comes from the transaction rather than a general project estimate.

The public works have a separate timetable

A September 2026 estimate placed completion of the square’s public works, including the park and He Be’Iyar Street, at the end of 2027. That timetable concerns the public works, not the towers.   1. 1942 Private investors buy the plots. Those holdings form the basis of the landownership from which the apartment project later takes shape.
  2. 1969 The square’s plan is drawn with Oscar Niemeyer. Israel Lotan and Abba Elhanani design the ring buildings, built mostly in the early 1970s.

  6. March 2018 The traffic amendment to the plan takes effect, adding a further planning milestone before the construction permits.
  7. December 2018 The excavation and shoring permit is issued, authorizing the preparatory ground works ahead of the later building permit.

  9. December 2022 The building permit is issued, a separate authorization from the earlier permit for excavation and shoring.

The square

Kikar Hamedina, Tel Aviv: luxury shops, cafés and life around the square

Kikar Hamedina is considered the largest square in Israel. HaMedina Square means “State Square,” but the shape is a circle, with the shops and surrounding homes arranged around its ring. For someone looking to buy property in Tel Aviv, that setting is part of the choice: the apartment sits within an established neighborhood rather than at an address defined only by the towers.

Luxury shopping on the ring

Published shopping lists name Gucci, Saint Laurent, Givenchy, Dior, Valentino, Chloé, Burberry, Fendi and Dolce & Gabbana among the houses on the ring. A reopened Gucci store was reported in January 2025, and a watch and jewelry lounge of about 250 m² opened in December 2025. These dated examples describe the square’s luxury-retail character without treating the shop list as an unchanging directory.

For an apartment buyer, walk the section of the ring nearest the proposed home as well as the better-known shopping stretches. Consider where you would stop on an ordinary weekday and how that route connects with the entrance you expect to use. The appeal of a famous shopping address and the convenience of your own daily errands are related, but they are not the same question.

Coffee, food and everyday errands

Bakery Kikar Hamedina, Lechem Erez, Nuchi, Open and Nespresso appear among the published café and food addresses on the ring, about a minute’s walk away. The local list also names City Market and Victory supermarkets and a Super-Pharm pharmacy on Weizmann Street. That gives your neighborhood visit a practical focus: try the walk for groceries, coffee and a pharmacy stop rather than limiting it to a tour of the apartment.

A 2023 neighborhood count recorded about 13,300 residents in about 6,000 households. The square’s shops therefore belong to a residential setting as well as a destination for visitors. For buyers dividing their time between Israel and another country, consider which nearby errands you would want to handle on foot during a short stay.

An architectural setting with a public history

The circular plan shaped with Oscar Niemeyer and the ring buildings designed by Israel Lotan and Abba Elhanani give the square its architectural identity. Its center has had very different uses: the Medrano circus pitched its tent on the sand in the 1950s and 1960s. On 3 September 2011, about 300,000 people gathered here for the March of the Million, part of the social-justice protests.

Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

How tall are Kikar Hamedina Towers?

Tower A and Tower C have 40 floors, while Tower B has 37; the buildings reach up to about 160 m. Their floors turn by 1.25 degrees relative to the level below. For your choice, match the floor number with the tower and apartment direction, because a high-floor label alone does not describe the home’s orientation or the outlook from its rooms.

What do apartments in Kikar Hamedina Towers cost?

Three published deals from April to December 2024 ranged from ₪9.58 million to ₪10.63 million, averaging about ₪9.93 million. They concern 140 m² apartments on upper floors. Published asking prices include ₪10 million for a 132 m² home and ₪43 million for a penthouse. Establish whether any lower headline offer buys rights with further construction payments or a finished apartment.

What is the price per square meter in Kikar Hamedina Towers?

About ₪65,000 per m² is the published average for tower deals, while high floors and penthouses are quoted at ₪80,000 to ₪150,000 per m². These comparisons were published in September 2025 and September 2026. Use them as benchmarks rather than a fixed price list, and compare each apartment’s floor, direction, interior area, outdoor space, specification and the full sale terms before judging the offer.

Who is building the towers in Kikar Hamedina?

Electra Construction and Ashtrom are building the towers jointly for the landowners. The construction contract is about ₪1.4 billion, and the builders were selected in November 2021. Yaski Mor Sivan Architects designed the towers, with Waxman Govrin Geva managing the project. Apartments are sold by individual owners, usually through brokers, rather than through a central sales office with an official price list.

What is Kikar Hamedina in Tel Aviv?

Kikar Hamedina is a circular square in north Tel Aviv, surrounded by He Be’Iyar Street and known for the luxury-fashion shops on its ring. The towers stand inside the square, beside its public park and civic buildings. For a buyer, the location combines an established neighborhood of shops and services with new residential buildings, rather than placing the apartments in a separate shopping complex.

When will Kikar Hamedina Towers be ready for occupancy?

There is no published official occupancy date; estimates for the towers range from 2026 to 2028. The frame was recorded as completed on 23 April 2026. For an individual purchase, use the seller’s contractual delivery date. The separate public-works estimate points to the end of 2027 for the park and He Be’Iyar Street, not a confirmed move-in date for the apartments.

How many apartments and parking spaces are there?

There are 453 apartments and about 1,620 parking spaces on 3 underground levels. About 906 spaces are private for apartment owners and about 720 are public, including 14 for disabled drivers. Those project totals do not identify what accompanies an individual home. Ask for the apartment’s parking and storage allocation, including the bay locations and their route to the residential lifts.

Can foreigners buy an apartment in Kikar Hamedina Towers?

Yes, foreigners can buy an apartment in Kikar Hamedina Towers and handle the purchase from abroad through a lawyer with a power of attorney. Buying the apartment does not give Israeli residency. Start by identifying a specific owner’s offer, then have your lawyer examine the rights, agreement and payment obligations. The guide for foreign buyers helps organize the purchase around your circumstances.

How much purchase tax does a foreign buyer pay?

Under the 2026 brackets, a foreign resident pays 8% on the portion up to ₪6,055,070 and 10% above that amount. For a ₪10 million apartment, the tax is about ₪879,000. These are not the Israeli-resident single-apartment rates. An eligible new immigrant may have a different route under legal conditions, so establish the applicable category before finalizing the purchase budget.

Can a non-resident get an Israeli mortgage?

Yes, a non-resident can seek an Israeli mortgage of up to 50% of the property value. On a ₪10 million apartment, that means at least ₪5 million in own equity, before purchase tax and agreed fees. The ceiling is not a loan approval: ask the lender to confirm the borrowing amount and how it fits the seller’s payment schedule.

Do the apartments have a sea view?

Some published listings describe a sea view, including a high-floor apartment facing northwest; a sea view is not confirmed for every home. The nearest Mediterranean waterline is about 2 km northwest of the plot. Ask for the exact tower, floor and direction, then assess the outlook from the living room, bedrooms and balcony rather than treating a tower-wide description as apartment-specific.

What does "4 rooms" mean in Israel?

A 4-room apartment has 3 bedrooms and a living room, not 4 bedrooms. Published examples in the towers have different areas and layouts within the same room count, and one 168 m² listing is described as 4 rooms, planned as 5. Ask which plan is being sold and whether its bedroom arrangement suits the way your household will use the home.

Which light rail lines serve Kikar Hamedina?

The Red Line provides the operating light-rail connection via Arlozorov station, about 11 minutes on foot. The planned Purple Line’s Ichilov station is about 2 minutes away, with opening planned for 2028. The Green Line’s Arlozorov station on Ibn Gabirol is about 9 minutes away, with the full line planned for 2030. Keep planned lines separate from the service available for an immediate commute.
