## 2. Who can get it (S2, S3)

- "משרד הבינוי והשיכון מעניק לזכאים חסרי דירה משכנתא לרכישת דירה, לבנייתה, להרחבתה או להתאמתה לצרכים מיוחדים" (S2).
- The definition of "חסרי דירה" (no rights in a home in the 10 years before the request, with exceptions) appears on Kol Zchut, citing "נוהל 08/01". The official procedure text was **not read** (unverified).
- Timing (S2): a new flat from a developer, if 36 months have not passed since signing and it is not fully paid; a second-hand flat, if one year has not passed since signing and it is not fully paid; self-builders, within 36 months of the building permit. The aid never exceeds the balance owed to the seller.
- Families veteran with up to 599 points: "לא יהיו זכאים למשכנתא על פי הניקוד. אלא רק לתוספות סיוע הניתנת ביישובים" (S3).

## 5. Other profiles (points only unless stated)

**Veteran singles (S4):** aid from age 21 in priority zones א' and ב' for those who served in the IDF or national service; from 30 everywhere. Age 21 to 30: 30 points; 31: 250; 32 to 45: +50 a year (32: 300 ... 45: 950); one-time +200 from 45 (46: 1,200, 47: 1,250, 48: 1,300 as shown); maximum 1,690. Plus the siblings table above.

**Single-parent veteran families (S5):** "נקבע לפי 2,200 נקודות והוא עומד על סך 161,665 ש"ח".

**Olim families (S11, updated 30.03.2026):** seniority in Israel since receiving oleh status (0: 30, 1: 250, 2: 300, 3: 350, 4: 400, 5: 450, +50 a year) plus the children table (child = unmarried and under 25 at aliyah, plus children born after aliyah and a pregnancy from week 24); a fixed +730 points; floors 1,700 points in the first year and 1,800 from the second year. Families more than five years after aliyah get a supplement at 3% interest for 28 years: 5 to 6 years 19,630 ₪, 7 years 28,370 ₪, 8 years or more 43,630 ₪. Olim may choose the veterans' track instead.

**Olim singles (S12):** from age 21 by years in Israel; fixed +600 points at 21 to 45, +400 above 45; cap 1,690; a single oleh above 45 who has been in Israel eight years also gets 10,895 ₪ on eligible-loan terms. The seniority ladder for singles is not printed on S12 (unverified: probably S11's seniority table).

**Disability (S10):** +200 points. Floors: veteran families with 75% permanent disability 1,600; wheelchair-bound, or a couple each with 75%: 2,200. Veteran singles, degree א': at least 1,500 and at most 1,890; degree ב': 2,000 (the page's own example then says 2,200: an internal inconsistency on the official page, flag it). Olim families: degree א' at least 1,900; degree ב' or a couple each 75%: 2,200. Olim singles: as veteran singles.

## 7. Loan terms (S2, S17)

- Interest: "0.5% פחות מהריבית הממוצעת על הלוואות צמודות מדד של הבנקים למשכנתאות, שאותה מפרסם בנק ישראל, אך לא יותר מ-3%" (S2). S2 links the BoI page "הריבית הממוצעת על משכנתאות צמודות למדד" (https://www.boi.org.il/information/rates/הריבית-הממוצעת-על-משכנתאות/). **That BoI page answered with a Radware CAPTCHA in this session; it was not solved and not read. The current eligible-loan rate is therefore unverified.** Mashcantaman shows "ריבית זכאות" 2.31% (10 years) to 2.72% (30 years) with no date or source: do not copy.
- Term up to 30 years, shortenable to 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 15, 20 or 25 years (S2). Kol Zchut lists only 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 (conflict; follow S2).
- CPI-linked: "סכומי ההחזר החודשי ההתחלתי צמודים למדד המחירים לצרכן הידוע בעת מתן ההלוואה"; payments exclude property and life insurance (S2).
- Total cap: "ככלל, הסכום הכולל של המשכנתא (כולל משכנתא בנקאית משלימה) לא יעלה על 75% ממחיר הדירה הנרכשת" (S2). Kol Zchut (updated 25.6.2025) says 98% in priority zone א', 95% elsewhere and 99% for Ethiopian-origin programmes: **conflict with S2, unverified, do not publish either beyond S2's 75% without a newer official text**.
- Money goes straight to the seller's or developer's account; self-build in stages (S2).
- Early repayment: no fee of any kind on a directed (eligibility) loan (S17, section 2; already in `boi-mortgage.json` fees.directed_no_fee).
- The aid can be taken at any mortgage bank, not only the one that issued the certificate (S2).

## 8. Process and documents (S2)

1. Registration at any mortgage bank branch; fee "60 ש"ח" (S2). Kol Zchut says 70 ₪ (conflict; follow S2). Leumi accepts a digital request (S2).
2. Check against government databases (for example מס רכוש) and investigation companies.
3. The ministry issues "תעודת זכאות"; validity one year from the request date; can be extended at the branch; apply before signing any contract (S2).
4. Use the aid at any mortgage bank.
5. Appeal: ועדת אכלוס עליונה, ועדת אכלוס ציבורית, through the bank branch with supporting documents (S2).

Documents (S2): ID with appendix; pregnancy confirmation; disability confirmations; "מהלך שירות צבאי" or "אישור חובת שירות סדיר"; "אישור משרת מילואים פעיל שש שנתי" (מוקד המילואים *6535); national-civic service confirmation; ID copies of all siblings or an affidavit; marriage certificate or registration; divorce certificate and agreement; death certificate; common-law affidavit signed before a lawyer (or the branch manager with the named partnership certificates); oleh certificate.

Mortgage banks listed (S2): בנק אגוד, בנק דיסקונט, הבנק הבינלאומי למשכנתאות, בנק ירושלים, בנק לאומי למשכנתאות, בנק מזרחי טפחות, בנק מרכנתיל דיסקונט, בנק משכן, פועלים משכנתאות.

## 9. What the official calculator asks (S16, fields only)

Profile buttons: משפחה · משפחת עולים · משפחת נכים · משפחה שבראשה עומד הורה עצמאי (חד הורית) · נכה רתוק לכסא גלגלים · נכה רווק · רווק/ה · עולה חדש/ה.
Fields: שנות נישואין (or a marriage date that fills it) · מספר ילדים · אחים לבעל · אחים לאישה · שנים בארץ · האם גם משפחת עולים? · חודשי שירות חובה בעל / אישה (the code caps the value at 36) · מספר חודשי מילואים · סך חודשי שירות בתא המשפחתי · גיל · מספר אחים · רוכשים באזור עדיפות לאומית? (671 localities) · תוכנית 10+ · זכאות חוק הנגב (197 localities).
Outputs: "סך הכל נקודות", "סך הכל זכאות למשכנתה מועדפת", a status line and a details block. It computes on the server (every keystroke is POSTed), shows no date, no table and no source.

## 10. Spec for an honest calculator on our side

- Inputs: profile (the eight above), the fields of section 9, priority-zone locality from S15 (to be fetched and dated), Negev locality (amount per locality unverified), 10 פלוס conditions as yes/no questions with the S9 income table.
- Outputs, each with its source line and date: points by component (marriage or seniority, children, siblings per applicant, disability floor, olim fixed points), the band, the S3 basic amount, the S7 service supplement, reserve supplement (labelled with the S2 versus S7 wording), the S8, S9, S11 and S12 supplements, the total, and a note that "הזכאות ותנאי ההלוואות המחייבים ... מופיעים בתעודת הזכאות" (S1).
- The rate: compute "ממוצע צמודות של בנק ישראל פחות 0.5%, עד 3%" only after the BoI series is identified and dated; until then show the rule in words and let the user type the rate from the certificate. Our Mortgage 2.0 already has a "הלוואת זכאות" track with no early repayment fee: feed it the computed amount.
- Runs entirely in the browser (unlike S16), no lead form, no data sent.
- Never show: zakaut's undated 2018 amounts, Mashcantaman's undated eligible rates, Kol Zchut's 95% to 99% caps.
- Where: see delta.md row A2 (a tool in the main page switcher, because "מחשבון משכנתא משרד האוצר" belongs to the head-term intent in keywords.md; a separate page needs Ben's word and the URL word audit: the token "eligibility" is free today, `url_word_audit.py --token eligibility` returned 0 URLs on 6.11.2026).
