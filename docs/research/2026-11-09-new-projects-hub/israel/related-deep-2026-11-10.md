# New-projects family, related phrases: Google Israel read by hand (10.11.2026)

Method: one Chrome window, own tab, `https://www.google.co.il/search?q=<query>&hl=iw&gl=il`, page 1 read with the
page-text tool. A second load of every query (about two minutes later) was used only to collect the full result URLs
from the links; where the order changed between the two loads this is written down. Winners were opened in the same tab
and measured with a small read-only DOM script (title, H1, text after H1, headings, tables, selects, map element,
JSON-LD types, keyword counts). No CAPTCHA, no consent wall on Google. No ad label ("ממומן") was seen on any page.
The browser sits in the Tel Aviv area, so Yad2 results resolve to `tel-aviv-area` and `yad2.co.il/yad1` itself
redirects to the Tel Aviv area page.

Times (Israel, 10.11.2026): first pass of all 14 queries 15:18 to about 15:23; second load for URLs about 15:24 to 15:25;
winner pages 15:25 to 15:28.

On three queries (7, 9, 10) Google showed an owner-only Search Console insight box for this site ("גלוי רק לך").
Its numbers are deliberately not recorded here (public repo).

Hebrew label of the AI box: "תקציר מ-AI". "בתהליך חשיבה" means the AI box started and did not render; counted as absent.
"אנשים שאלו גם" (People also ask) did not appear on any of the 14 queries (also checked in the DOM on queries 3 and 14).
"Find related products & services" appears in Hebrew as "חיפוש מוצרים ושירותים רלוונטיים" (chips, followed by
"החיפושים האלה מאפשרים לך למצוא מבצעים רלוונטיים של מפרסמים").

Legend: position. full URL | title verbatim | first sentence of the snippet.
