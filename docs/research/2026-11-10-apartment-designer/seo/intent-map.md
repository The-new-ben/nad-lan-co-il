## About volumes (read before the table)

**No Google Ads volume was measured in this run.** The DataForSEO balance is $0 (`40200 Payment Required` on 10.11.2026), the owner's Google account has no Google Ads account (Keyword Planner shows "You do not have any Google Ads accounts"; creating one is the owner's call), and nad-lan has no impressions on these heads. So the "volume" column shows:

- `measured` only where an earlier paid DataForSEO run on this machine already holds a Google Ads number (9 long-tail keywords; file named in keywords.csv);
- otherwise **"not measured"** plus a demand signal that is real but relative: **AC** = in how many of the 888 autocomplete probes the phrase surfaced (more probes = Google offers it more often), and whether Google autocompletes the bare head.

The exact Google Ads request for the 1,000 strongest keywords is ready in `serp-raw/volume-request-body.json`. After a top-up, one call (about $0.09 for up to 1,000 keywords, per the google-israel-manual-search skill notes) fills the column: `node C:/Users/777/Documents/agent-tools/dataforseo/dfs.cjs POST /v3/keywords_data/google_ads/search_volume/live serp-raw/volume-request-body.json serp-raw/volume-response.json` (run from Git Bash with `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1`, otherwise `/v3/...` is rewritten into a Windows path and the call fails).

## Why one hub, and where the line is

- Clusters 1 to 10 and 14 to 19 are answered by the same reader journey: draw or import the plan, see it in 3D, generate looks with AI, then learn what a designer or a consultation costs and whether you need one. Google currently splits these across magazines, SaaS tools and designer blogs; no single page on page 1 does all of it.
- The price SERPs are identical across four phrasings, so prices get one section, not four pages.
- Clusters 11, 12, 13, 20 (trends), 21 and 22 already have nad-lan owners. The hub links to them and does not repeat their heads in headings.
- Child pages (kitchen, living room, small apartment, designer cost) are reserved, not created. They need their own evidence (volume after the top-up, and a SERP that does not overlap with the hub) before they get a URL.
