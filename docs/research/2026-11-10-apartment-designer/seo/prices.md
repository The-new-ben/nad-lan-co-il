# Israeli prices for interior designers, design consulting, home styling and renderings

Collected 10.11.2026 from the pages that rank on page 1 of google.co.il for the price queries (`מעצב פנים מחיר`, `כמה עולה מעצב פנים`, `מחיר מעצבת פנים למטר`, `ייעוץ עיצוב פנים מחיר`) plus the pages Google's AI block cites for them. Every range below is quoted from its source as written; nothing is averaged or invented. Raw HTML and extracted snippets: `price-pages/` and `competitor-pages/`.

## Read this first

- **These are published ranges, not measured market prices.** Almost every source is a designer, a design college or a lead-generation site selling the service. Midrag adds a few individual paid examples from its customers. No public statistical body (CBS, Tax Authority) publishes designer fees.
- **VAT basis is rarely stated.** Only midrag says its numbers include VAT; only bethameatzvot says its list is before VAT; ziratadrichalut explains that a licensed dealer (עוסק מורשה) adds VAT and an exempt dealer (עוסק פטור) does not. All other sources say nothing, so treat their numbers as "VAT basis unknown". The general VAT rate is 18% from 1.1.2025 (Tax Authority; confirm before publishing).
- **Dates matter.** Midrag's designer price page is from 23.2.2023; the home-styling page from 2022; goldistudio from 2020 to 2021. The 2026 guides are newer but are sales pages.
- **Units differ.** Per hour, per consultation meeting (2 to 3 hours), per m², per room, per apartment, and percent of the execution budget. The page must show the unit on every number.

## What the page should do with this

1. Show prices as **ranges with unit, source, date and VAT basis** on every row. Never one blended "average".
2. Split by **unit**: hour, meeting, m², percent, apartment type, room, styling, render. That is also how the AI blocks structure their answers.
3. Put the **VAT rule** next to the table: licensed dealer adds 18%, exempt dealer does not; ask the quote to state it (ziratadrichalut, midrag Tip 228).
4. The per-m² spread is wide (150 to 800 ₪/m²). A calculator that takes m², rooms, level of service (design only, full accompaniment, premium) and returns the range from each cited source, side by side, would be new on this SERP. It must say these are published ranges, not quotes.
5. Refresh dates: midrag 2022 to 2023 rows are the only ones with a stated VAT basis and real paid examples; they are also the oldest. Label them as such.
