## The schematic generators we already have (fallback when no plan exists)

All four build a layout from the room count and the area. None of them is a real plan, and each labels itself as an illustration.

1. `plugins/nadlan-config/inc/property-showroom.php` `nadlan_pshow_plan_svg()` (line 131): rooms, mamad, balcony, sqm into an SVG. Live on property pages, for example https://nad-lan.co.il/properties/balfour-batyam-35r-demo/ (screenshots 23, 25).
2. `plugins/nadlan-config/inc/interior-fp.php` `nadlan_ifp_rooms()`: the same facts into a room list with metres and the window wall from the listing's direction (salon 40%, kitchen 12%, bedrooms share 42%).
3. `plugins/nadlan-config/assets/showroom-engine/studio.js` `planRooms()`: two bands, salon 42%, kitchen 13%, bedrooms 36%, bath 9%; geometry revision `schematic-v1:<rooms>:<sqm>`.
4. `plugins/nadlan-config/inc/global-worlds.php` `planSvg()` (line 1260): open living plus bedrooms plus bath plus a balcony strip, for international projects.

## What this means for the new engine

- Only Ashira (4 types) has real plans we have already turned into usable room geometry. That is the right first project.
- Utopia (7 PDFs) and SIX-8 (2 JPGs) have real plans that still need tracing into the same room-polygon format.
- Rainbow, DUO, Dimri and Kikar need the developer's sales plans. Until then the engine needs an honest "example layout" mode, like the Kikar derived floor.
- The Plan Studio's room format (`{id, n, d, p: SVG path in cm, cls: wet|bal, main}` plus `glaze` lines and `face` bearing) is the best existing seed for a plan data format.
