# Broker + developer package: research (12.10.2026)

Scope: Meital Katsir (licence 3131540) brokering for new projects (Israel Canada Rainbow, Ashira, Sde Dov), with nad-lan.co.il charging the developer a fixed monthly fee. Web research only. Nothing was sent, signed or published.
Every URL below was retrieved on 2026-10-12 ("ret. 2026-10-12"). Where a page blocked the fetch, this is said next to the claim.

## סיכום בעברית פשוטה (מה לאמץ)

1. תקופת הגנה: 12 חודשים ארוכה מכל מדיניות של יזמים שמצאנו בעולם (30 יום, 60 יום, 6 חודשים). בישראל לא מצאנו נוהג מפורסם. המלצה: 6 חודשים מיום הרישום, ועוד 6 חודשים רק אם יש פעילות מתועדת עם הלקוח (פגישה, סיור, חדר צפייה). סך הכול עד 12 חודשים.
2. חלון התנגדות: לא מצאנו מספר מקובל. אצל יזמים בחו"ל מערכת הלקוחות של היזם היא שקובעת. המלצה: 2 ימי עסקים (ראשון עד חמישי) במקום 48 שעות, וההתנגדות תקפה רק עם רשומה מתוארכת אצל היזם שקודמת לרישום שלנו.
3. קדימות: מי שרשם ראשון זוכה, ופנייה קודמת ומתועדת של הלקוח ליזם גוברת. כך כתוב במדיניות של יזמים בארצות הברית.
4. ראיות: מייל רישום לכתובת קבועה של היזם עם אישור קבלה, רשומה מתוארכת באתר, וליווי של מיטל בפגישה הראשונה (גם בחדר הצפייה בווידאו). בחו"ל ליווי בביקור הראשון הוא תנאי כמעט קבוע, וסיור וירטואלי נחשב ביקור.
5. ההסכם החתום עם היזם הוא עצמו "ההזמנה בכתב" שהחוק דורש. בית המשפט המחוזי בירושלים דחה מתווך שתבע חברה קבלנית בלי הזמנה חתומה. מייל רישום לבדו לא מחליף אותה.
6. חתימה: מאז תיקון 2018 חתימה אלקטרונית תקפה אם היא משיגה את מטרת החתימה ברמת ודאות מספקת. מי שבחר את שיטת החתימה צריך להוכיח שהצד השני חתם, ולכן שומרים קוד חד פעמי לנייד ולמייל, חותמת זמן, כתובת רשת, טביעת אצבע של המסמך, ועותק שנשלח לשני הצדדים. וואטסאפ לבד הוא ראיה תומכת בלבד.
7. הקונה חותם בקישור לנייד על טופס קצר: מיטל מגלה בכתב שהיזם משלם לה (חובה לפי תקנות האתיקה מ-2025), והקונה מסכים שפרטיו יועברו ליזם.
8. עמלה בישראל: אחרי אוקטובר 2023 יזמים שילמו למשווקים ולמתווכים כ-1%. בתל אביב בשנת 2026 הם מוכנים ל-2% ויש שמוכנים ליותר (כלכליסט, מאי 2026). בפסק דין מכפר סבא המתווכת המחליפה קיבלה 1% ועוד מע"מ. המלצה: לבקש 1.5% עד 2% ועוד מע"מ, ולא לרדת מ-1%.
9. בעולם: בונים בארצות הברית משלמים 2.5% עד 3%, בטורונטו 4% עד 5%, בדובאי 2% עד 8% על מכירה על הנייר.
10. מחירי השוואה: מחירונים של לוחות הנדל"ן ליזמים בישראל לא מפורסמים, לא נמצאו. מערכת תלת ממד למכירה על הנייר עולה לפי ספק אחד 8 אלף עד 250 אלף דולר לפרויקט. אתר אמריקאי גובה מהבונה 750 עד 1,000 דולר לכל מכירה שנסגרה. צילום 360 מעלות לדירה בישראל כ-800 עד 1,000 ש"ח.
11. תלת ממד: הנתונים חלשים. היצרן טוען למכירה מהירה ב-31%, אבל מחקרים עצמאיים מצאו השפעה קטנה או אפסית על המחיר, וקיצור זמן רק בחלק מהשווקים. מול היזם נאמר "עוזר לקונה מרחוק להחליט", בלי מספרים של היצרן.
12. קונים מחו"ל: ברבעון הראשון של 2026 קנו אמריקאים 238 דירות, צרפתים 130, בריטים 57. בתל אביב: 28 צרפתים ו-10 אמריקאים. באפריל 2026 תושבי חוץ היו כ-1.5% מכלל העסקאות. צרפתית ואנגלית מוצדקות. לרוסית ולערבית לא מצאנו נתוני קנייה.
13. ריינבו: לפי ביזפורטל נמכרו ברבעון הראשון של 2026 רק 4 דירות, וכ-60% מהפרויקט שווק עד כה. זו נקודת הפתיחה מול ישראל קנדה.
14. גבולות: האתר לא לוקח חלק מהעמלה של מיטל ולא מציג את עצמו כמתווך. כל פרסום של מיטל באתר מציג את שמה, את המילה "מתווכת" ואת מספר הרישיון (תקנה 19).
15. לפני שליחה ליזם: עורך דין מקרקעין עובר על הנוסח, בעיקר על סעיף ההגנה של 12 חודשים, שאולי ייחשב פיצוי מוסכם ולא דמי תיווך.

## Q1. Israel: how licensed brokers work with developers on new projects

### 1.1 Commission levels actually reported (developer-paid)
- Calcalist, 10.05.2026 (Dotan Levy), review of 8 Tel Aviv projects: after 7 October developers "returned the regular commissions of 1%"; since the start of 2026 "developers are willing to pay a double commission, i.e. 2% of the apartment price", some will talk about more. Payees: marketers and brokers ("משווקים ומתווכים"). [https://www.calcalist.co.il/real-estate/article/rkrc8y6awe — ret. 2026-10-12]
- Same review in English (Ctech via Ynetnews): named projects include Israel Canada SHE (4 apartments sold since Q4 2024 launch), Africa Israel DUO (~149 apartments still for sale, ~30% of 510 for sale), Dimri Yama in Sde Dov (32 sold in ~6 months). Rainbow and Ashira are not named. [https://www.ynetnews.com/real-estate/article/sjjm00yj1ml — ret. 2026-10-12]
- Kfar Saba Magistrate's Court, ת"א 7090-09-20 מ.א חקלאי נכסים נ' מ.שטרית השקעות (Judge Ronen Peleg, 29.05.2023), 8-unit project in Pardesiya. Data points from the judgment: original licensed broker had a fixed fee of NIS 100,000 incl. VAT per unit + half of any price above NIS 2.8M, 3-year exclusivity; the developer's replacement broker was paid "1% + VAT" (NIS 146,150 + VAT for 5 units); the developer offered the original broker 2% of sale price as a compromise (NIS 181,000 for 3 units); the developer's own feasibility sheet budgeted marketing at 2% of revenue, described by the developer's manager as a "נתון מקובל בעסקאות בשוק". [https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/decision7090-09-20/he/7090-09-20.pdf — ret. 2026-10-12]
- Rabbinical court (beitdin.org.il, case 69062): a broker claimed an extra 1% "builder's commission" he allegedly lost; rejected as unproven. Weak evidence of a 1% builder-paid norm only. [https://www.beitdin.org.il/Article/51 — ret. 2026-10-12]
- Second-hand market reference (not developer-paid): about 2% + VAT per side, dropping to 1%-2% in weak markets (Bizportal). [https://www.bizportal.co.il/career/news/article/20039772 — ret. 2026-10-12, via search summary]
- Conclusion: developer-paid broker commission in Israel = ~1% normal, ~2% in the 2026 Tel Aviv slowdown. No public developer price list or written broker policy from Israel Canada or any Israeli developer was found.

### 1.2 Client registration / protection periods in Israel
- No published Israeli developer "client registration" policy, form or protection period was found (searched: רישום לקוח אצל יזם, הגנת לקוח מתווך יזם, טופס רישום לקוחות יזם, תקופת הגנה). "Not found".
- Zero-commission project marketers list "רישום וניהול קונים פוטנציאליים" as a service, with no terms. [https://zeroreal.co.il/%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%98%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%92%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9D/ — ret. 2026-10-12]
- Statutory exclusivity caps (Brokers Law s.9(ב1)): 6 months for an apartment, 1 year otherwise, but only where the property is sold "שלא במהלך עסקיו" (not in the seller's course of business). A developer sells in the course of business, so the caps do not appear to apply to developer agreements (the Kfar Saba case had 3 years and the court did not reach a late s.9 argument). Our 12-month client protection is not exclusivity, but a lawyer should confirm. [https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/72991.htm — ret. 2026-10-12]

### 1.3 Legal frame that drives the design
- s.9(א): no brokerage fee unless "חתם הלקוח על הזמנה בכתב" with the details set in regulations. s.14(א)(3): broker must be "הגורם היעיל". s.14(ב): presumption of efficient cause for a deal made during an exclusivity period in which the broker did the required marketing. s.10: personal interest requires disclosure and written consent. s.2(ג): publishing exemption only while the publisher does not present itself as a broker ("ובלבד שאינו מציג עצמו כמתווך במקרקעין"). [https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/72991.htm — ret. 2026-10-12]
- Required order details (תקנות פרטי הזמנה בכתב, 1997, per a law-firm reproduction): names, addresses and ID numbers of broker and client; transaction type; property description; approximate price; fee amount or % and whether VAT is included. [https://www.toledano.co.il/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%9D-%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%A8%D7%A7%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%9F-%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%96%D7%9E%D7%A0%D7%94-%D7%91%D7%9B/%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9A-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%9D/ — ret. 2026-10-12]
- Ethics regulations (תקנות המתווכים במקרקעין (אתיקה וחובות מקצועיות), תשפ"ד-2024, in force 9.3.2025): reg. 8 keep the order and exclusivity documents at least 7 years; reg. 9 disclose in writing any payment or benefit from a non-party ("יגלה, בכתב ... על כל תשלום או טובת הנאה"); reg. 17 written notice when serving more than one side + written record of every offer passed; reg. 18(א) consent "בכתב או בכל דרך מתועדת אחרת"; reg. 19 every marketing publication shows the broker's name, broker status and licence number. [https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%AA%D7%A7%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%9D_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%A8%D7%A7%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%9F_(%D7%90%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%94_%D7%95%D7%97%D7%95%D7%91%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%9E%D7%A7%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA) — ret. 2026-10-12]
- Design consequence: Meital's B2B agreement with the developer must itself be a compliant s.9 order signed by the developer (the paying client). Each later client-registration email is evidence of timing and causation, not a substitute for the order. If Meital also serves the buyer, the buyer must get the reg. 9 written disclosure that the developer pays her.

### 1.4 Rulings: broker (or marketer) vs developer, and what evidence decided them
1. Kinan v. B. Yair Construction (Jerusalem District Court, ע"א 36998-03-13, Judge David Mintz, 10.09.2013). Licensed broker claimed a fee from a construction company for a deal with a purchase group (Mishkenot HaUma). Preliminary question assumed all his facts true; appeal dismissed because the company never signed a written order. Writing is a substantive condition; contract and unjust-enrichment routes also fail unless "another document" shows an order. Lesson: get the developer's signed order before the first registration. [https://www.psakdin.co.il/Court/%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%9F-%D7%A0-%D7%91-%D7%99%D7%90%D7%99%D7%A8-%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%91%D7%95%D7%93%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%91%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%94-1988-%D7%91%D7%A2-%D7%9E-%D7%95%D7%90%D7%97 — ret. 2026-10-12]
2. M.A. Haklai Nechasim v. M. Shitrit Investments (Kfar Saba Magistrate's Court, ת"א 7090-09-20, 29.05.2023). Licensed broker company with a signed "הסכם להזמנת שירותי תיווך, ליווי וייעוץ"; developer cancelled after the first sales and used another broker. Broker won NIS 940,150 incl. VAT (fees for 3 units it sold + expectation damages for 5 units sold by the other broker inside the exclusivity period) + NIS 150,000 + VAT legal fees. Evidence that won: a buyer testified he came through the broker; the broker had buyers sign purchase-request forms ("בקשות רכישה") and referred them; proven marketing spend (renders, roadside sign, two ad carts, Yad2/Madlan/Facebook ads, ~NIS 50,000); s.14(ב) exclusivity presumption; the court noted the developer's complaints were first written right after the first sale. Also: the developer's claim that the broker asked buyers for 2% failed because the agreement did not forbid it and it was not collected. [https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/decision7090-09-20/he/7090-09-20.pdf — ret. 2026-10-12]
3. Broker vs real-estate developer, Tel Aviv Magistrate's Court (Judge Ayala Gazit; date and case no. not in article). 2014 agreement at 2% + VAT for a Neve Sha'anan plot; developer signed a different combination deal in 2016 with different parties. Claim (~NIS 945,000) dismissed: time gap, different parties and different deal structure broke the causal link; broker did not prove his actions. Lesson: registrations must name the project and buyer precisely; keep an activity log so the chain is not broken by time. [https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/hykpiaqfh — ret. 2026-10-12]
4. Brokers vs TAMA 38 developer (District Court on appeal; reported 16.06.2022, case no. not in article). Brokers had exclusive marketing of new units and also charged buyers 2% + VAT without telling the developer; appeal dismissed: no market custom lets brokers charge buyers of new units, and a broker acting for the developer must disclose. Lesson: Meital charges the developer only, unless the developer agrees in writing and the buyer gets written disclosure. [https://barlaw.co.il/he/%D7%AA%D7%97%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%99_%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%A1%D7%95%D7%A7/%D7%94%D7%AA%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA/%D7%A2%D7%93%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%97%D7%95%D7%AA/%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9D-%D7%9E%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9A-%D7%A9%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A2%D7%9C-%D7%91%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%91%D7%A8%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%9E%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%AA-%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A8/ — ret. 2026-10-12]
5. Amzal v. Kaspi Peretz Renewal (Tel Aviv Magistrate's Court, Judge Ronen Ilan; article dated 03.06.2026). Urban-renewal organizer, not a broker. WhatsApp "אשר לי במסודר את שכ"ט... 2% מסך המכירות" answered "מאשר" by a developer owner was held to be the core of the agreement; NIS 410,707 + linkage + NIS 41,669 costs. Lesson: a written confirmation from the developer's decision-maker is strong evidence of terms (but a licensed broker still needs the s.9 signed order). [https://www.nadlancenter.co.il/article/14644 — ret. 2026-10-12]
6. Related, buyer side: Tzarum v. Hadar (Tel Aviv Magistrate's Court, Judge Dan Sa'adon; article 18.09.2025). Signed order with a clause obliging the buyer to tell the seller he was referred by the broker, with an agreed sum if he bypassed; court enforced it (NIS 117,000) without needing to decide efficient cause. Lesson: an explicit contractual tail clause can be enforced on its own terms. [https://www.bizportal.co.il/takdin/news/article/20021737 — ret. 2026-10-12]
7. Related, same project, different unit (Jerusalem rabbinical court, case 69062): broker who showed a project, buyer later bought a different unit type there via another broker: broker got 1/3 of the fee as "starter". Lesson: the B2B agreement should say registration covers any unit in the project. [https://www.beitdin.org.il/Article/51 — ret. 2026-10-12]
- Not found: a published Israeli ruling where a broker sued a developer specifically over one buyer the developer said came "ממקור אחר" with a registration email as the key evidence.

## Q2. Electronic signature for the brokerage order and the B2B agreement

### 2.1 The statute
- Electronic Signature Law 2001, s.2(א) as amended: a statutory signature requirement is met by (1) an approved electronic signature ("מאושרת"), or (2) another electronic signature if, "ברמת ודאות מספקת בנסיבות העניין", the purposes of the signature requirement are met. First Schedule Part B (cannot be signed electronically) lists only a handwritten will; Part A lists other will types needing an approved signature. Nothing about brokers or real estate. [https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%97%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%9E%D7%94_%D7%90%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%AA — ret. 2026-10-12]
- s.3(א): admissibility not denied only because a signature is electronic. s.3(ב): a secure signature is prima facie evidence the record was not altered; for an approved signature, also that the signatory signed. s.3א: a party that had priority in designing the signing method and relies on the other side's signature bears the burden "להוכיח כי אותו צד חתם על המסר האלקטרוני"; does not apply to an approved signature. [same URL — ret. 2026-10-12]
- Amendment No. 3, 2018 (Knesset, February 2018): before it, a statutory signature needed a sophisticated (approved) e-signature; after it, "even a simple one, can potentially comply", if the purposes are achieved with sufficient certainty; burden shifts to the party with more influence over the method; excluded documents narrowed to mainly a handwritten will. [https://www.law.co.il/en/news/2018/02/28/revolution-electronic-signatures-israel/ — ret. 2026-10-12] Bill text: [https://www.law.co.il/media/computer-law/esig_amendment3_bill.pdf — ret. 2026-10-12, listed in search, not opened]
- Ministry of Justice clarification (as summarized by DocuSign): certainty is assessed case by case, factors include the signatory, the relying party, the document, the relationship and the parties' understandings; the standard had not yet been tested in court as of that page. Secondary source. [https://www.docusign.com/products/electronic-signature/legality/israel — ret. 2026-10-12, via search summary]

### 2.2 Courts on electronic or informal "writing" in brokerage
- Email accepted: a court held "ניתן לראות בהתקשרות באמצעות הדואר האלקטרוני כהתקשרות בכתב" and awarded the broker 1.25% of NIS 4.35M + VAT although no order was signed (law-firm summary; case number and date not given). [https://ds-legal.co.il/%D7%94%D7%90%D7%9D-%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%9B%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%A2%D7%9C-%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%99-%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9A-%D7%91%D7%90%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%99/ — ret. 2026-10-12]
- WhatsApp accepted: Bat Yam Magistrate's Court, case 17764-02-22 (Gohar v. Aharonovich), per a law-firm blog: a reply "אחי ברור שמאשר" satisfied the substantive writing requirement; full fee ~NIS 140,000. Not verified against the judgment. Same blog: Tel Aviv District Court 26439-11-21 (Dari v. Sagi) applied the "זעקת ההגינות" doctrine against a client who kept dodging signature. [https://shpak-law.com/%D7%93%D7%9E%D7%99-%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9A-%D7%91%D7%9C%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%A1%D7%9B%D7%9D-%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%95%D7%95%D7%9A-%D7%97%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%9D/ — ret. 2026-10-12]
- WhatsApp not enough: a broker who showed apartments but never got a signed order lost, regardless of "כמה וואטסאפים נשלחו" (Bizportal, case details in article). [https://www.bizportal.co.il/takdin/news/article/20032281 — ret. 2026-10-12, via search summary]
- Real-estate contracts by email/WhatsApp: Globes column (03.03.2022) reports two rulings enforcing property deals formed by email and WhatsApp where offer, acceptance and certainty existed. [https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001404085 — ret. 2026-10-12]
- Not found: any Israeli ruling on a brokerage order signed through an e-signature platform or SMS link, for or against.

### 2.3 What makes a click-and-sign B2B agreement provable (recommendation, built from the sources above)
- Identify the signer: the developer company's name and company number, the named authorized signatory, one-time codes to that person's email and mobile. Reason: s.3א puts the burden on us (we design the method).
- Capture: server timestamp (UTC + Israel time), IP and user agent, every step of the flow, SHA-256 hash of the final PDF, an audit page appended to the PDF, and the signed PDF emailed to both sides at once. Audit trails of IP, timestamps and events are the standard vendor practice (vendor source). [https://boldsign.com/electronic-signature-legality/israel/ — ret. 2026-10-12, via search summary]
- Content: the s.9 order details (1.3), project name, "any unit in the project", fee % + VAT, the registration mechanism, protection period, objection window, proof rule, payment trigger (signed sale contract or first payment), the reg. 9 disclosure, and a clause that the portal is not a broker and receives no share of the fee.
- Strongest option for a high-value contract: an approved electronic signature from a licensed certification authority; it carries the statutory presumption and is outside the s.3א burden rule.
- Buyer side: short order signed by SMS link (same audit trail), with the reg. 9 disclosure, consent to pass details to the developer, and the privacy notice. Privacy Protection Law Amendment 13 took effect 14.08.2025 and widened notice duties (law-firm summaries). [https://www.shibolet.com/%D7%AA%D7%99%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%9F-13-%D7%9C%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7-%D7%94%D7%92%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%A4%D7%A8%D7%98%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%91%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%95%D7%99-dpo/ — ret. 2026-10-12, via search summary]
