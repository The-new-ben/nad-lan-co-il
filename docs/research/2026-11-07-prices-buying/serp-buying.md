## 2. איך לקנות דירה (head)

**AI overview: present**, a long numbered how-to:
- Budget and equity: "בדרך כלל נדרש מינימום של 25% עד 30%"; get a mortgage pre-approval before searching; a recommended repayment ratio.
- Location and type: second-hand (fast, existing surroundings) versus new from a developer (new standard, a safe room (ממ"ד), delay risk).
- Legal and planning checks: a real-estate lawyer; land-registry and Israel Land Authority records; permits and deviations; an engineering check; "אין לחתום על 'זיכרון דברים' מחייב ללא ייעוץ משפטי".
- Negotiation and contract: register the warning note right after signing.
- Taxes and payments: purchase tax by brackets, "חובה לשלם תוך 60 יום"; milestones.
- Possession and registration: the handover protocol; final registration in the land registry.
- A pointer to the Ministry of Construction and Housing buyer guide.

### 6. מה לבדוק לפני קניית דירה יד שניה
**AI overview: present**, in three sections:
1. **Legal and title:** land-registry extract (liens, mortgages, notes); seller identity and capacity; condominium file and plan (parking, storage, roof); no memorandum of understanding (זיכרון דברים) and no money before advice.

## What the SERP says, in one paragraph
"קניית דירה" itself is owned by listing boards. The informational variants ("איך לקנות דירה", "קניית דירה ראשונה", "מה לבדוק…") are owned by **law-firm guides** led by Amit Vered, with FAQPage schema and stage-by-stage H3s. The AI overviews are built from those guides plus banks (Mizrahi) and Kol Zchut for the tax brackets.

Five sub-intents dominate the related searches:
1. **no or low equity** (and the fresh Globes warning about "0% equity" developer deals);
2. **first home** (grants, 75% financing);
3. **second-hand checks**;
4. **costs**;
5. **cheap cities and budgets under ₪1M**.

No ranking page combines a process guide with live numbers, a cash-needed tool and a budget-to-city bridge.
