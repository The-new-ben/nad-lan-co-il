### Risk found on our page
**Nine H2s on the buying hub start with "מחירי דירות / מחירי בתים / בדיקת מחירי דירות ב…".** They push the buying hub's heading set toward the "מחירי דירות" head term, which /apartment-prices/ owns (protected-keywords.md, tier B). Ten of 28 H2s also dilute the "קניית דירה" topical signal. The lower-risk shape is one H2, for example "בדיקת מחירים לפני קנייה", with a plain list of links under it. This is a heading change only; no URL changes.

Two existing child pages are close to hub sub-intents and must be linked, not duplicated:
- /buying-apartment/buying-apartment-step-by-step/ ("תהליך קניית דירה")
- /apartment-buying-checklist/ ("מה לבדוק לפני קניית דירה")

Decide which page owns "תהליך קניית דירה" before writing the new hub copy.
