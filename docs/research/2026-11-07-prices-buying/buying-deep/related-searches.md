## What the drill adds to the keyword map
1. **Downloads are a sub-intent:** "הסכם מכר דירה להורדה / pdf / doc", "חוזה מכר לדוגמא", "פרוטוקול מסירה pdf", "צ'ק ליסט", "אקסל קניית דירה". Printable checklists and generated lists win; a sample contract must stay a lawyer's job (see `tools-proposed.md`).
2. **Developer-deal mechanics are a cluster of their own:** the payment caps by construction stage, 20/80 and 10/90, the registration form, the changes annex, the guarantee wording. The AI answers cite Kol Zchut and law firms; no tool exists.
3. **The money of help:** the ₪50,000 second-hand grant, disability and IDF aid, "זכאות משכנתא לזוגות צעירים", "חוק הנגב", "משכנתא מועדפת". All official pages, scattered; nobody routes a buyer through them.
4. **"Before you see the flat" questions** ("מה לשאול כשרואים דירה", "שאלות לשאול כשרואים דירה") are separate from the legal checklist: a visit scorecard.
5. **Two brands own phrases** ("הדרך לדירה ראשונה" = a course). Do not target them.
