## Five lessons to bring home
1. **Start with "why buy" and "what can I afford", not with lawyers.** Bankrate's step 1 and Rightmove's H1 both do.
2. **Answer in the reader's own numbers:** "I make $75K", "a $300,000 house". In Hebrew: "הון עצמי 100 אלף", "דירה עד מיליון".
3. **Cover the emotional middle:** viewings, offers, chains, "why it all takes so long", moving day.
4. **Every help programme gets its own short guide with eligibility.** Israel's grants are scattered over five official sites.
5. **A personal plan that remembers** is where the leaders are heading (Bankrate is building one; Rightmove and NerdWallet have accounts). Nobody in Israel has one.
