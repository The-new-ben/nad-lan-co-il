## C. Where the rebuild beats everyone, and where it cannot (honest reading)
**Beats every column observed, once built and verified on a phone:**
1. **Tools inside the guide.** No Israeli page has one; abroad, tools sit beside the guides, not inside each step.
2. **The buyer's own case.** Status picker + personal plan + equity ladder: nobody in Israel; abroad only partly (accounts, not plans).
3. **The orphan clusters:** no or low equity with numbers, help programmes routed in one place, ownership structure, special cases (receiver, heirs, no tabu, deviations, Amidar, foreign seller), developer-payment caps, buy before sell. Each is covered today only by scattered law pages.
4. **Dated data in every stage** from our instrument files (brackets, LTV, rates, fees, grants, hazards, renewal status). The AI answers contradict themselves on deadlines (30 / 40 / 60 days) and on investment LTV (50% vs 75%): a clean, sourced table is citable.
5. **The human layer** (fears, family, faith), which autocomplete proves people type and nobody answers.

**Cannot win on these, and the plan for each:**
| Area | Leader | Why we cannot match it | What we do instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lawyer authorship (E-E-A-T) | Amit Vered, Yoni Levy, Elkayam | nad-lan is a portal, not a law office | a named reviewing professional from our pool and a dated "נבדק על ידי" line; no legal advice wording |
| Official authority | gov.il, Kol Zchut | they are the source | link the primary sources inside each tool; never paraphrase a rule without its link and date |
| Lender-backed pre-approval | Rightmove (NatWest) | needs a bank partner; the site never presents itself as a broker | documents by profile (mready.json) and the mortgage cluster |
| Cluster breadth of one law firm | Yoni Levy (11 SERPs) | one leaf = one page is their model; our rule is one owner per phrase and no rival pages | the hub owns the orphan leaves as sections; existing owner pages are strengthened, not cloned |
| Review stars | Lazy Investor, yeda-kesef | stars need genuine reviews (honesty law) | none until real reviews exist |
| Daily freshness | Bankrate | editorial staff | data blocks refresh from the instrument files; the page shows the date of each |
