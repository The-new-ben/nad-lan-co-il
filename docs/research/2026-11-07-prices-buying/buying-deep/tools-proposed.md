# Proposed tools and checks for the buying hub (7.11.2026)

These extend B1 to B14 in `../tools-teardown.md` (cash on the day, which home can I afford, stage tracker, deadline calculator, document checklist, red-flag quiz, equity-gap options, developer-deal comparator, payment-schedule builder, bracket block, professional-costs table, buy or rent, first-home check, downloadable sheet). Nothing below repeats them; where a new tool upgrades one, it says so.

**Rules applied to every tool:**
- Every number shown carries its source and date, or the tool shows a sourced range or nothing (honesty law; Google itself shows a financial disclaimer on these queries).
- No tool replaces a lawyer, appraiser or mortgage advisor. Generators produce **lists and plans**, never contracts. The site never acts as a broker.
- No new URLs. A tool lives on /buying-apartment/ or inside an existing owner page ("lives on").
- A tool is wrapped in a phrase that has **no owner page**; where the phrase has an owner, the tool lives on that owner or the hub's heading avoids the phrase (keyword guard).
- Memory: progress is kept in the visitor's browser (localStorage) by default; anything sent to us (an email reminder) needs explicit consent and the lead-privacy notice.
