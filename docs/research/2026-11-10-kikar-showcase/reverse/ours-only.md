# What our Kikar page does that none of the listed products do

Checked on 10.11.2026: our page at https://nad-lan.co.il/projects/hamedina/ (release 1.72.630), against the 221 rows and 49 products in `features.csv`.

**What "ours only" means here:** we looked for the feature in every product on the list, and the closest thing found is named. It does **not** mean the feature exists nowhere in the world, because a universal negative cannot be proven (see `limits.md`).

**Weakest evidence:** the 3D sales configurators (REALS, BMBY 3D, Maverick Frame, Volume Vision, Shadowmap). Their live demos did not render, so they were judged by their own marketing claims. A configurator sold to one developer could do some of this on a private sales link that we cannot see.

## 1. Direct-sun hours for one window of an unbuilt tower, by date and hour

**On our page:**
- **Control:** the 'שעון השמש' chip in the 3D world. It has an hour slider from 5 to 22 and the dates 21.3, 21.6, 21.9 and 21.12.
- **What it shows:** the sun's altitude and azimuth, sunrise and sunset, and 'כ-6 שעות שמש ישירה בחלון הזה'.
- **Shade line:** 'החלון בצל בשעה הזו · לפי מסלול השמש בתל אביב וגובה הבניינים מסביב'.
- **Scope:** it works for the chosen tower, floor (1-40) and corner unit (F108, F109, F110).

**Closest found elsewhere:**
- **Redfin:** a Sunscore and direct-sun hours for June and December, for a whole built home, not per window or floor (F110, F108).
- **Redfin's 3D sun simulation and Google Earth's sunlight slider:** they animate sun and shade, but give no hour count for a window (F109).
- **Shadowmap and REALS:** they claim a sun path over a house or project (F109).

## 2. The same window at three heights, with its bearing in degrees

**On our page:** the example apartment shows 'אותו חלון בקומות 20, 30 ו-38' at 278°, 266° and 256°. The text explains the 1.25° rotation per floor.

**Closest found elsewhere:** no product shows how the facing of one window changes from floor to floor. This depends on our building's rotation, so no other product would need it.

## 3. Window view with named landmarks and their distance or walking time

**On our page:**
- **Labels in the view:** 'הים התיכון 2.1 ק״מ', 'הגימנסיה העברית הרצליה 6 דק׳ הליכה' and 'בניין עיריית תל אביב-יפו 1.0 ק״מ'.
- **How the view is chosen:** by tower, floor and direction, from an eye height such as 'גובה העיניים כ-117.6 מ׳' (F014, F010).

**Closest found elsewhere:**
- **Volume Vision and REALS:** they claim the view from the exact balcony and pre-construction views (F014). Neither mentions labels or distances.
- **Waldorf Miami:** a real photo panorama with day / night (F016). No labels were recorded.
- **Matterport tags:** they pin media inside a scanned room, not landmarks outside the window (F010).

## 4. A model apartment for an off-plan tower, with time of day, design styles and an elevator to the amenities, on a free public page

**On our page:** in the example apartment's 360 viewer:
- **Times of day:** יום, שקיעה and ערב.
- **Styles:** 'העיצוב המקורי', 'עץ חם', 'בהיר' and 'אבן', labelled 'רעיון להמחשה'.
- **Elevator menu:** the door hotspot 'יציאה מהדירה למעלית' opens 'לאן?'. It goes to the apartment floor, the lobby, the pool, the gym, the spa and the car park, each a 360 (F001, F012, F017, F015).

**Closest found elsewhere:**
- **Matterport:** it needs a built, scanned home (F001, F005).
- **Zillow:** its staging works on single photos (F017).
- **Waldorf Miami:** a drag-to-look 360 opens at the plan step; no styles or times of day were seen (F001).
- **Maverick Frame and the Zaha Hadid configurator:** they claim finish packages, but no walk through the building (F029).

## 5. A basket that builds the whole purchase: unit, design, a team of professionals, and the price with tax for this buyer

**On our page:** the dialog 'לסל הדירה: המחיר המלא, הצוות והנציג' runs in five steps (הבניין, הדירה, העיצוב, הצוות, המחיר).
- **Team:** six roles can be picked from the site's directory: interior designer, real-estate lawyer, appraiser, mortgage adviser, real-estate tax CPA, and engineer or supervisor.
- **Price:** a price input recomputes purchase tax for 'דירה יחידה', 'דירה נוספת' or 'תושב/ת חוץ'.
- **Sending:** 'שליחת הסל לנציג בוואטסאפ' sends the whole basket to the rep (F132, F076).

**Closest found elsewhere:**
- **Booking.com:** its sticky basket counts rooms only (F216).
- **Maverick Frame:** it claims that the request carries the apartment, finishes and price into a CRM (F132).
- **Houzz:** it turns a design into a priced estimate, with no unit and no team (F180).
- **Property Finder and Realtor.com:** a buyer-status toggle exists only inside a mortgage calculator (F076).

## 6. A price estimate by floor for one tower, with the published deals on the same chart

**On our page:** the calculator 'מחשבון מחיר דירה בכיכר המדינה' has four parts:
- **Inputs:** location type, area, floor (1-40), and the purchase-tax status.
- **Chart:** '₪ למ״ר לפי הקומה', from about 58,000 ₪ low to about 72,000 ₪ at floors 38-39, with 'עסקאות שפורסמו' plotted on it.
- **Result:** 'סה״כ עם מס רכישה'.
- **WhatsApp:** the message is prefilled with the chosen estimate (F054, F057).

**Closest found elsewhere:**
- **Zillow, Redfin, Realtor.com, ImmoScout24 and Bayut:** they value one built home (F054).
- **Bayut and Property Finder:** they list transactions in the same building by bedroom count (F160).
- **Compass:** it lists past sales per building (F036).

None of them draws a price curve by floor inside one tower.

## 7. A walk at eye level through a park and square that are not built yet, with the same sun clock

**On our page:** in the 'סיור ברגל' tab you can walk with WASD or the arrow keys. There are four start points (טבעת ה׳ באייר, לב הפארק, בין המגדלים, ליד בית הספר) and the same date and hour sun controls. The lake and bridges are labelled 'הדמיה להמחשה בלבד' and 'מיקום להמחשה' (F011, F094).

**Closest found elsewhere:**
- **Apple Look Around and Google Street View:** street-level walks through imagery of what exists today (F094).
- **Google Immersive View:** a flyover with a time slider, but no walk (F114, F093).
- **REALS:** it claims the project inside a 3D city, with no walk (F003).

## 8. One price table where every row says what kind of number it is and when it is from

**On our page:**
- **Table:** 'מחירון · עודכן 7.10.2026', with columns 'הנתון' and 'מועד'.
- **Row types:** each row is marked as עסקה, מחיר מבוקש (labelled 'מודעה'), הערכה or חציון עסקאות.
- **Sources:** Tax Authority deals, appraisers' estimates and listings, side by side.
- **Gap sentence:** one sentence states the usual gap between asking and closing prices for expensive listings (F196, F069).

**Closest found elsewhere:**
- **Zillow:** its price history gives the source per row, but only for one listing's own events (F196).
- **Realtor.com:** RealEstimate shows three automated estimates (F069).
- **Bayut:** it validates listing fields against the registry (F202).

None of them mixes deals, estimates and asking prices in one dated table.

## 9. Nearby project prices, future renewal plans and walking-time bands on one map

**On our page:** the Mapbox section has:
- **Layers:** '₪ מחירים בסביבה (6)', with price tags such as ₪90K, and '◆ תוכניות עתידיות (12)'.
- **Walking time:** bands of 5, 10 and 15 minutes.
- **Places:** 7 categories with counts.
- **3D and satellite:** both on the same map (F091, F112).

**Closest found elsewhere:**
- **Madlan:** it lists nearby building sites with their expected end (F112).
- **Zillow and Redfin:** their price pins are for listings on the search map, not on the unit page.
- **REALS:** it claims a lifestyle layer (F091).

The combination was not found.

## Looked unique but is not, said honestly

- **Five languages including Arabic (right to left):**
  - Booking.com and Airbnb run dozens of languages (F211).
  - Property Finder has Arabic and English, and Emaar has English, Arabic and Russian.
  - What is rare is a single project page in he / en / fr / ru / ar on an Israeli site.
- **Picking tower, then floor, then apartment in a 3D city:**
  - BMBY 3D, REALS and Maverick Frame claim it (F037, F003).
  - Waldorf Miami does it by floor band (F040).
  - Ours is the only one seen working on a free public page, but the feature itself is not unique.
- **A scheduled video tour:** Redfin, Zillow, Lennar, Idealista and Domain have one (F153).
- **Saying a value is unknown:** Zillow writes 'Not available' and Redfin writes 'We cannot show automated home-value estimates' (F193).
- **Day / sunset / night in 3D:** Google Immersive View has a time slider (F114).
