Date of research: 11 November 2026. Researcher: Claude Code research agent. Public pages only: no accounts, no forms, no payments.

## How to read this file

- Every row carries an evidence label:
  - **SEEN-PUBLIC**: the public page was fetched on 11.11.2026 and its text was read directly. The fetch tool reads page text and does not run JavaScript, so 3D viewers and live selectors could not be operated. As the rules require, no flow was clicked through to payment.
  - **DOC**: the company's own documentation, terms PDF, press release, app-store listing or help centre.
  - **REVIEW**: a third party, such as trade press, a law firm, a software directory or a news site.
  - **UNVERIFIED**: appeared only in a search snippet, or on a marketing page that could not be opened.
- "n/d" means the source does not document the item. It does not mean the product lacks it.
- This is product research. The Israeli legal section is information for the owner, not legal advice.

## 2. Step counts and money: summary

- **Typical steps from landing to a paid hold among the leaders: 5 to 7.** Taylor Morrison 6 (5 named steps plus reserving), Pulte about 5, Unlatch about 6, Hill/Yourkeys 6 (counted from the emailed link), Emaar Vyom about 4. St Modwen takes the payment in 2 steps, but the real reservation happens offline.
- **Holding fees found:**
  - US: US$100, refundable, 48-hour hold (Taylor Morrison); "from $999" (Lennar, unverified).
  - UK: £150 pre-reservation (St Modwen, refunded on stated conditions); £500 to £1,000 typical (Barratt); a menu of £500, £2,000, £5,000 or £10,000 (Hill). **A 14-day cooling-off period with full refund under the New Homes Quality Code**: https://consumercode.co.uk/reservation-agreements-what-home-buyers-need-to-know/ (read through search; the page returned 403 to the fetch tool). Barratt refunds less costs, with a £50 admin deduction itemised, and gives a 42-day reservation period.
  - Australia: AUD 1,000 to 5,000 holding deposit (Mirvac), by card or transfer, part of it retainable; a "refundable holding deposit" (Stockland).
  - France: deposit at most 5%, 2% or 0% depending on the time to the notary deed, held in escrow, with a **10-day statutory withdrawal period**.
  - Dubai: EOI typically AED 20,000 to 50,000 (review); token payment in the app (Emaar, amount n/d).
  - Israel (practitioner reports, not law): ₪5,000 to ₪10,000 (ynet); ₪10,000 to ₪30,000; and ₪20,000 to ₪100,000 (law-firm blogs).

## 4. How the next step is signposted (what the sources actually show)

The evidence here is thin. No source documents a pulse, glow or coach mark on a unit selector, and the fetch tool could not run the 3D viewers. What the sources do document:

- **A running total as the signpost** (Taylor Morrison): the price and monthly payment change with each choice, then an itemised list appears, then "reserve". The buyer always knows what the next tap costs.
- **One emailed link that walks the buyer through "step by step"** (Hill/Yourkeys), followed by a status tracker with real-time updates.
- **A dated promise as micro-copy** (St Modwen): a call within 72 hours and a visit within 10 days, otherwise the £150 comes back.
- **"Instant confirmation"** as the closing signal (Emaar Vyom).
- **Published journey pages with numbered stages** (Cogedim, 8 stages; Mirvac, "8 steps"; Berkeley, "your buying journey").

## 5. Usability evidence on signposting and phone-first configurators

1. **Tutorials and coach marks often do not help simple interfaces.** NN/g (Kendrick, 8.3.2020) tested 70 participants on 4 iOS apps. Task success was 91% with the tutorial and 94% without (not significant). Perceived ease was 4.92 with the tutorial and 5.49 without, on a 7-point scale; the drop with the tutorial was significant. NN/g says the same effect was seen with coach marks and overlays, and recommends contextual help over upfront walkthroughs. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/mobile-tutorials/
2. **Weak signifiers cost attention.** In NN/g's eyetracking study, flat or ghost-style clickable elements drew more fixations and more uncertainty than strong signifiers. NN/g says the problem is weak or missing signifiers, not flat style itself. https://nngroup.com/articles/flat-ui-less-attention-cause-uncertainty ; https://www.nngroup.com/articles/clickable-elements/
3. **Motion catches the eye through peripheral vision, and it also distracts.** Use motion to show state changes and feedback, not as decoration; a permanent pulse competes with the task. https://www.nngroup.com/articles/animation-usability/ ; Apple HIG on motion: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/motion
4. **Progressive disclosure** improves learnability, efficiency and error rate: show the main options first and hold back the rest. https://nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/
5. **Checkout friction is measurable.** Baymard (page dated 28.10.2026) lists these reasons for abandonment: forced account creation 18%, a checkout that is too long or complicated 17%, not trusting the site with card details 19%, total cost not visible upfront 12%. The average US checkout shows 23.48 form elements, against an ideal of 12 to 14. https://baymard.com/lists/cart-abandonment-rate
6. **3D is liked more than it performs.** In Cockburn and McKenzie's studies, users were slower in 3D interfaces than in 2D, and slowest in virtual ones, yet they preferred 3D. Later work found no spatial-memory benefit from 3D perspective. Implication: keep a 2D list or stacking plan beside the 3D tower. https://courses.washington.edu/hcde511/a14/readings/cockburn-chi02.pdf ; https://www.cosc.canterbury.ac.nz/andrew.cockburn/2d3d.html
7. **Live price and visual feedback** are the configurator rules vendors repeat most. No peer-reviewed or Baymard study specific to configurators was found (Baymard's configurator content may be behind its paywall). Vendor source, low weight: https://magebit.com/blog/why-customers-abandon-your-product-builder-and-how-to-fix-the-ux

## 6. Israeli legal frame: information for the owner, not legal advice

**Primary text**: Sale (Apartments) (Assurance of Investments of Persons Acquiring Apartments) Law, 1974. Full text: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)_(הבטחת_השקעות_של_רוכשי_דירות)

1. **The 7% rule (section 2).** A "seller" may not take from a buyer, on account of the price, more than 7% of the price unless one of five securities is in place: a bank guarantee (or another licensed guarantee), an approved insurance policy, a first mortgage in the buyer's favour, a registered warning note (הערת אזהרה), or transfer of ownership. Since Amendment 9 the guarantee may leave out the VAT component. A breach is a criminal offence: under section 4, taking more than 7% without security carries up to one year in prison or a doubled fine. Plain-language guide: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/מתן_בטוחות_לרוכש_דירה_מקבלן
2. **Who counts as a "seller" (section 1).** Someone who sells an apartment they built or will build, either themselves or through another person, on their own land or someone else's, in order to sell it. This includes someone who bought from the builder in order to resell. It excludes a seller who did not build the apartment and received no payment for it. **"Price" means the sum the buyer undertook to pay in the sale contract.**
3. **What this changes in this project (unknown; needs a lawyer).** The apartments belong to private landowners, and resales go through brokers. The sources found do not settle whether a landowner who received units in a combination deal counts as a "seller" under section 1. If the landowner already holds a finished, registered unit, the sale is closer to a second-hand sale: the 1974 law may not apply at all, and protection would come from general contract law and the warning note. It is also open whether money paid before a contract (דמי רצינות, or money paid with a registration form) counts as paid "on account of the price", because "price" is defined by the contract. Practitioner sources disagree on whether such money is protected. One firm says a guarantee is required (https://www.m-dlaw.co.il/refund-intent-payment-apartment/); other firms say protection begins only when the contract is signed.
4. **Brokers may not take דמי רצינות.** The broker ethics regulations, in force from 9 March 2025, ban brokers from collecting any money other than brokerage fees, and name "דמי קדימה, דמי רצינות" explicitly. https://www.nadlancenter.co.il/article/11698 ; the draft: https://www.nadlancenter.co.il/article/9220 . A site that is not a licensed broker and collects a holding fee for someone else's apartment raises three open questions:
   - Does it become a broker under the Real Estate Brokers Law, 1996, which requires a licence for brokerage?
   - May it hold a third party's money at all, or only through a lawyer's trust account?
   - Does a licence for payment services apply?

   None of these was answered by the sources found.
5. **The 2022 amendment (Amendment 9 to the Sale (Apartments) Law, in force 7.7.2022).**
   - Index linkage is limited to the construction input index, and to at most half of each payment.
   - The first 20% of the price, paid at signing or before any other payment, is not linked at all (section 5ג).
   - The grace period for late delivery is shorter: one month instead of two.
   - The bank guarantee may leave out the VAT component.

   Sources: https://barlaw.co.il/client-updates/news-for-apartment-buyers-in-israel-major-amendments-to-the-sale-apartments-law ; https://www.firon.co.il/news/the-sale-law ; https://www.mondaq.com/real-estate/1216572/strengthening-protections-for-home-buyers-under-the-recent-amendment-to-the-sale-law
6. **Market practice (not law).** Developers ask buyers to sign registration forms and pay ₪5,000 to ₪10,000 (ynet consumer article: https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/BkwvIYql00), or ₪10,000 to ₪30,000 and more (law-firm blogs). Lawyers advise against signing or paying before an independent legal review, and advise asking for a written refund date. Under typical contract drafts, up to 7% is paid into a trust account held by the developer's lawyer.
7. **Cancelling a distance sale (Consumer Protection Law, section 14ג, 14 days).** The sources found did not establish whether this applies to a holding fee for an apartment.
