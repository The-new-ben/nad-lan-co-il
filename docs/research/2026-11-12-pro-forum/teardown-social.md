**Evidence marks in the Source column**
- **(O)**: page opened and read in full by this agent on 12.11.2026.
- **(I)**: official page; either its body is rendered by JavaScript or sits behind a login prompt (so the fetch returned only the title), or it was not opened in this pass (rows 30, 129, 151, 166). The behaviour stated comes from the search index's excerpt of that same official page. Treat as "official text, partly verified" and re-check before building on it.
- Anything not confirmed is either marked **unverified** in the row or listed under "Not checked / blocked" at the end.

**Columns.** Effort = build effort for a custom WordPress plugin (S = days, M = 1 to 3 weeks, L = more than a month or needs outside services). Value = value for a small, new professional community in Israel (Hebrew, right-to-left, phone first, entry without long registration).

## (a) The 25 micro-features that most create activity, dynamism and fun without complicating use

1. **Author-reply boost (X weight 75 vs like 0.5)**: rank and notify threads where the asker or the pro answers back; push "the pro answered you".
2. **"Looking for a pro" question card**: a question auto-formatted into a request card (trade, city, budget), as Facebook does with question posts.
3. **Tag a pro in a reply = recommendation on his card**: Nextdoor's "What neighbors say" tab fed by mentions.
4. **One-tap recommendation request with relationship field** (LinkedIn): "client / colleague / subcontractor".
5. **Fave link the pro sends by WhatsApp after a job** (Nextdoor): reviews that cannot be bought.
6. **Response-time badge** ("usually replies within a day", LinkedIn's 24 h / 80% rule).
7. **Request a quote** with no phone in the request; pro answers "send proposal" or "no thanks" (LinkedIn).
8. **Expert / verified-licence badge inside every thread** (Facebook group experts), with "verified first" comment sort (Instagram).
9. **Pinned accepted answer** on legal and tax questions (Forum app).
10. **Polls with 2-4 options and a deadline** (LinkedIn).
11. **Professional reactions**: useful, congratulations, thanks (LinkedIn set).
12. **Peer awards** such as "Insightful" that lift the post (Facebook Groups).

## (b) What a professional gets from each network, and what is missing for local real-estate pros

- **LinkedIn**: identity, written recommendations, a services page with a request-for-proposal flow and response badges, newsletters. Missing: the request-for-proposal marketplace is limited to US locations (index excerpt), the best parts (showcase, request button, custom button) sit behind Premium, Featured content is not searchable and needs login, and there is no professional-licence verification.
- **Facebook Groups**: where Israeli "מחפש קבלן" requests actually happen today; strong admin automation, anonymous posts, experts, Q&A. Missing: recommendations evaporate into comment threads, with no lasting record on the pro, no verified licence, and moderation rests on volunteer admins.
- **X / Threads**: fast public conversation, open ranking rules (X), reply controls and communities (Threads). Missing: no service or lead layer, no local trade structure, and a Hebrew professional audience that is thin.
- **Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts**: reach for before/after and explainer video without followers. Missing: no request-a-quote, no verified licence, and views do not turn into local, trackable leads.
- **Nextdoor**: the closest model, with verified-neighbour recommendations, Faves that cannot be bought and a local hub. Missing: not in Israel, consumer-side only, no pro-to-pro forum.
- **The gap to own**: one Hebrew, phone-first place where a question becomes a request, the answers tag real pros, every tag becomes a lasting public recommendation on an indexable pro card with a verified licence, and the pro is told and can answer by video.
