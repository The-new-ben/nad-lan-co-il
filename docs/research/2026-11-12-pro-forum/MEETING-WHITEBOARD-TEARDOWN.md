# Meeting + whiteboard micro-feature teardown (for the pro-forum video call)

Date: 12.11.2026. Research from public web pages only. No sign-ins, no product was opened or tested by hand.

**Who it serves.** Contractors, interior designers, architects and finishing companies in a 2 to 4 person video call inside the NadLan pro forum. Phones come first. They draw together, mark up a site photo or a floor plan, point, plan a list or a timeline, and keep the result after the call.

**How to read the table.**
- **Products.** Claude M = Claude Meetings. Claude WB = Claude Whiteboards. Claude Sheets. Zoom Ann = Zoom in-meeting annotation. Zoom WB = Zoom Whiteboard. Meet = Google Meet. Teams Ann = Teams screen-share annotation. MS WB = Microsoft Whiteboard. FigJam, Miro, tldraw, Excalidraw. Bluebeam = Bluebeam Studio Sessions and Revu. Procore = Procore Drawings. ACC = Autodesk Build Sheets, the successor of PlanGrid. PlanGrid.
- **Priority.**
  - **must:** first version of our call.
  - **next:** second round.
  - **later:** only if it earns its place.
- **Source column.**
  - A URL means the behavior was read on that page on 12.11.2026.
  - "third party" means a university IT page, a blog or a help mirror, not the vendor.
  - "unverified" means no source confirmed it. It is our design idea or a common pattern, and no product claim is made.
- **Claude Meetings caveat.** Claude Meetings, Whiteboards and Sheets were announced on 10.11.2026 and are in beta. Some news coverage conflicts with the help center. Where they conflict, the help center wins.

## What Claude Meetings does that others don't

This is from the beta pages read on 12.11.2026, not from use.

- **It builds during the call, not after it.** While people talk, Claude can draft the plan under discussion, sketch a timeline on a whiteboard and answer questions in the chat. Meet, Zoom and Teams mainly write a summary after the call ends. Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-claude-meetings-to-turn-workplace-discussions-into-plans-and-tasks/ and https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18325154-use-claude-meetings
- **Anyone in the call can ask by voice.** For example "Claude, make a whiteboard" or "Claude, make a doc". The board, doc or other output is shared live and the people on the invite can edit it. Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18325154-use-claude-meetings
- **One page per meeting holds everything.** The page has the transcript, the chat, every output and the notes. Re-adding Claude returns to the same page, and the page link is posted in the call chat at the start. Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18325154-use-claude-meetings
- **It acts in other tools with a speaker check.** With permission it uses each person's own connected tools, for example to post a Slack message. On a voice request it confirms who is speaking each time. Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18325154-use-claude-meetings
- **Strict guest rule by default.** It stays out of calls with outside guests and leaves if one joins, including another company's notetaker bot. When guests are allowed, it does not reply in chat. Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18325154-use-claude-meetings
- **No audio is saved.** It keeps the transcript, the summary and descriptions of the screen. Source: https://claude.com/resources/articles/claude-meetings
- **The board knows how to restructure itself.** You can ask to regroup by theme, turn it into a timeline, find missing steps (added as suggestions to review), or ask only about a dragged area. The board can then become a doc or a deck. Source: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18324319-get-started-with-claude-whiteboards
- **Limits.**
  - Google Meet only for now.
  - Team and Enterprise plans only for Meetings.
  - The board uses only what is on the board, not connected apps.
  - No import from a file.
  - Export is PNG only, plus "send to" Lucid, Miro and Zoho Vani.
  - Not available with zero data retention, HIPAA-ready or customer-managed key setups.
  - Sources: https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18324319-get-started-with-claude-whiteboards and https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18325154-use-claude-meetings
- **What it lacks for our users.** No sources show measurement, scale calibration, plan-specific marks such as clouds and issue pins, or a laser pointer.
  - Building pros need these.
  - This is where our call can be better than Claude Meetings for this trade.

## Smallest delightful details

- **Name on every fresh mark.** The author's name shows for a moment beside a new annotation, then fades (Zoom). On phones, show it at the last touch point.
- **One color per person everywhere.** The same color on the ink, the cursor, the avatar ring and the author filter (tldraw, FigJam).
- **A telestrator laser.** Strokes stay while you keep drawing, then fade together with a soft taper when you lift (tldraw SDK 4.4). Viewers can use it too (tldraw, Excalidraw).
- **One pointer per person.** Each new tap moves your named pointer, and old pointers never pile up (Zoom Arrow).
- **A "Following Dana" label with a stop button.** Following ends the moment you touch the canvas (Excalidraw, tldraw).
- **An arrow at the screen edge** pointing to where a collaborator is working when they are off screen (tldraw).
- **Idle cursors fade away** so the plan stays clean (tldraw).
- **Stamps stick to the thing they judge.** They move with the note and show who stamped them (FigJam).
- **"Clear my annotations" is separate from clear-all** (Teams).
- **Marks return if annotation is toggled off and on**, unless cleared (Teams).
- **"Back to content" on mobile** when you get lost in empty space (Excalidraw).
- **The pen stays selected after each stroke** (PlanGrid iOS).
- **Recalibration fixes every measurement** already on the plan (Procore, ACC). A "≈ approximate" tag keeps everyone honest (Procore).
- **Tapping a line in the call record jumps to that spot on the plan** (Bluebeam).
- **Late fixes are welcome.** If notes run in one language and someone speaks another for 30 seconds, Meet offers "Change language".
- **The link to the call page lands in the chat the moment the call starts** (Claude Meetings). Nobody asks "where is the board?".
- **A warning comes before any hard limit ends the session** (Claude Meetings, 8 hours).
