## Part 2. Vertical networks that grew, and how

### Doximity (verified US physicians)
- **Loop:** (1) a pre-populated profile already exists from public and commercial data; (2) the physician lands, completes identity and credential verification; (3) gets free workflow tools (fax with e-signature, secure messaging, Dialer voice/video telehealth showing the office caller ID, Doximity GPT letter writer); (4) daily use of the tools keeps him in the app, where colleague suggestions (co-residents, same hospital, classmates) and the newsfeed run; (5) pharma and health systems pay to reach this verified audience.
- **Single most important mechanic:** free daily-use tools behind verification. 620,000+ unique active providers used the workflow tools in the quarter to 31.3.2025.
- **Numbers (company filing):** over 2 million registered members; more than 80% of US physicians, over 60% of NPs/PAs, over 90% of graduating US medical students (31.3.2025). Revenue $570.4M FY2025 (from $475.4M), net revenue retention 119%, 116 customers paying at least $500K. Members pay nothing; customers are pharma (Marketing Solutions), health systems and recruiters (Hiring Solutions), and enterprise tool licences.
- **Why it matters for us:** Doximity is the proof that a "pre-populated profile from public data" network can grow, but its growth driver was the tools, not outreach. Our ~2,846 imported cards are the same starting point; the claim must be pulled by tools and Google, never pushed.
- Source: Doximity 2025 Annual Report to Shareholders, Item 1 and MD&A, https://s201.q4cdn.com/617428576/files/doc_events/2025/Doximity-2025-Annual-Report-to-Shareholders-ARS.pdf (O, text extracted locally). FY2026 release on businesswire returned 403 (not checked).

### Alignable (local small-business network, US/Canada)
- **Loop:** (1) members join a local community; (2) each winter, "Local Business Person of the Year": every member gets 10 votes, cannot vote for himself, must give a reason in a comment box; (3) a nominee needs at least 3 votes, the top vote-getter per community wins (70% votes, 30% Alignable panel); (4) nominees are encouraged to ask friends and groups to vote, which recruits new members; (5) winners get a profile badge, promotion and a marketing kit; anyone who uses all 10 votes gets a "Community Supporter" badge.
- **Single most important mechanic:** a yearly peer-voted local award, where the nominees themselves do the recruiting.
- **Numbers (Alignable claims):** 2023 contest had over 3,600 winners (rules page, O). 2024: 309,000+ votes, 64,000+ recommendations, 5,100+ local winners (newswire release, I).
- **Monetization:** not checked (no public price page opened).
- Source: https://www.alignable.com/forum/official-rules-alignables-2023-local-business-person-of-the-year (O).

### Houzz
- **Loop:** (1) pros upload project photos; (2) homeowners save and browse; (3) pros review other pros they worked with, which links homeowners back to the reviewer's profile; (4) yearly "Best of Houzz" badges (Design by community popularity, Customer Service by ratings and reviews of projects that year, Innovator for software users), about 2% of pros win; (5) badges sell Houzz Pro subscriptions and ads.
- **Single most important mechanic:** project photos as the portfolio, with earned badges on top ("Hires on Houzz", "Verified Hires" counting projects with a qualifying online payment of at least $1,000; display can be hidden or held until a minimum count).
- **Numbers (company claims):** 3 million+ pros, 70 million+ homeowners and enthusiasts (Feb 2025 release).
- **Tagging collaborators on a project:** described only by a marketing agency page that now returns 404; Houzz's own help pages opened describe internal subcontractor collaboration, not public credit. Unverified.
- Sources: https://www.constructiondive.com/press-release/20250214-houzz-announces-best-of-houzz-2025-winners-1/ (O); https://pro.houzz.com/pro-learn/blog/new-earn-badges-that-celebrate-the-number-of-projects (O, dated 4.5.2021); https://www.houzz.co.uk/magazine/how-to-connect-with-other-pros-on-houzz-stsetivw-vs~88957836 (O). Pricing: see FEATURE-TABLE-HE.md.

### Nextdoor (launch tactic only; product rows are in teardown-social.md)
- **Loop:** (1) a resident founds a neighbourhood; (2) it does not go live until 10 members commit; (3) members invite neighbours by word of mouth including postcards; (4) address proof, not identity, gates entry; (5) local recommendations and Faves accumulate.
- **Single most important mechanic:** the 10-member launch threshold, which turns the founder into a recruiter.
- **Numbers (2013, TechCrunch):** a year to reach the first 176 neighbourhoods, then 3,000 to 10,000 between "July" and the article date (March 2013); median community about 750 households; "mature" at over 70% household penetration.
- Source: https://techcrunch.com/2013/03/30/nextdoor-hits-10k-neighborhoods-gets-me-to-stop-running-at-night (O).

### Sermo (verified doctors' Q&A)
- **Loop (company pages via search):** licence/NPI verification with random re-verification; members may post anonymously; paid surveys ("over $25 million in honoraria" last year, company claim); revenue from research, insights and healthcare advertising.
- **Single most important mechanic:** anonymous posting inside a verified-only room, so doctors say what they would not say under their name.
- Source: support.sermo.com returned 403. All Sermo facts are (I), unverified.

### Thumbtack / Angi (request-for-quote loop)

### Midrag (Israel)
- **Loop:** staff collect customer feedback by phone; pros are ranked only by customer feedback; the site shows "12,602" recommended pros in "150" sectors and "3,164,019" (unlabelled counter) on the page read today.
- **Single most important mechanic:** phone-verified feedback collected by the operator, not typed by the pro's friends.
- Source: https://www.midrag.co.il/Content/Article/1100 (O). Fees and the 9.0 floor are already in FEATURE-TABLE-HE.md.

## Part 3. Real-estate lawyers as members

### What the rules say (text read today on Hebrew Wikisource, a mirror of the official text; a lawyer must check the official text)
Source: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/כללי_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_(פרסומת) (O), כללי לשכת עורכי הדין (פרסומת), התשס"א-2001 with amendments up to התשע"ח.
- **Where a lawyer may advertise (3(א)(5)):** "באתרי אינטרנט, ברשתות חברתיות ובישומונים ייעודיים לעורכי דין", excluding sites with violence, pornography, illegal content, sites for minors or sites that harm the dignity of the profession; pop-ups banned. **Open question:** whether "ייעודיים לעורכי דין" limits internet advertising to lawyer-dedicated sites. A real-estate site is not lawyer-dedicated.
- **Content (3(ב)):** name, degrees, languages, practice areas, and a free-text offer of services "אינו כולל כל התייחסות לשכר הטרחה". So a lawyer card on our site must have no fee field.
- **Forums (3(י)):** a lawyer may "לפרסם מאמרים מקצועיים וראיונות וכן לנהל פורומים, והכול בלי שהוא שילם על כך"; if paid, the place must say explicitly that it is an advertisement. So free answering in our forum fits the rule; any paid lawyer placement must carry "פרסומת".
- **Recommendations (3(יא)):** "רשאי עורך דין לפרסם המלצות, מכתבי תודה ודירוג במדריכי דירוג", if not misleading. Peer recommendations from contractors and client thanks are allowed in principle.
- **Misleading or undignified ads banned (2א)**; disparaging another lawyer banned (3(ד)); client names and photos only with written, free consent (3(ה), 3(ט)).
- **Ethics rule 11ב** (https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/כללי_לשכת_עורכי_הדין_(אתיקה_מקצועית), O): a lawyer may not serve a client referred to him by a non-lawyer body that operates for profit and for that purpose publicly advertises legal services. Rule 43: no loan or other benefit to obtain work. Rule 30: no share of fee for a referral unless expressly agreed. The ethics rules' old advertising chapter (6-8) is repealed; no explicit solicitation (שידול) rule was found in either text.
- **Ethics committee social-media guidance (Globes, 14.1.2015, O):** binding; lawyers may share legal news, must identify as lawyers when offering work, paid promotion must be marked "פרסומת", no self-praise or solicitation, and client conversations only in one-to-one channels. https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001000899

### Market practice (public pages read today)
- **din.co.il** (O): since 2006, about 5,635 registered lawyers, about 342,572 Q&A entries answered by named lawyers, about 14,547 client recommendations, a "ייעוץ אישי" call-back form and a "פרסום עורכי דין" (lawyer advertising) page; no visible "פרסומת" label or "not legal advice" disclaimer on the home page.
- **lawforums.co.il** (O): "קבלו תשובה מעו"ד", named lawyers listed as "מנהל/ת פורום", 27 forums in the menu ("מעל 30" claimed), 50+ lawyers; invites visitors to be connected to "עורכי דין מומלצים"; no disclaimer seen.
- So lawyers answering in public Hebrew forums under their name is established, open practice.

### Liability and defamation
- Operator exposure under חוק איסור לשון הרע (author, editor, outlet; distributor liable only if it knew or should have known) is already in israel-law-business.md. For lawyers specifically: an answer read as personal advice may create a professional-negligence claim against the answering lawyer, and a review that attacks a named lawyer is the same defamation exposure as for any pro. Not researched further today (unverified).

### Recommendation
**Yes, admit real-estate lawyers, as answering members with a plain card, and keep money away from them.**
1. Free card with only 3(ב) fields (no fee field, no "best/cheapest" claims), licence shown as "עו"ד" only after the member claims it himself.
2. Free answering in the forum under his own name (fits 3(י)); a standard line under every lawyer answer: "מידע כללי, לא ייעוץ משפטי לעניין מסוים"; follow-ups move to a private one-to-one message (fits the 2015 guidance).
3. Peer recommendations from contractors, appraisers and clients allowed, shown without star averages (3(יא), not misleading).
4. **Never** sell lawyer leads, never charge lawyers per request, never rank lawyers by payment, and never present nad-lan as offering legal services, because rule 11ב could then bar the lawyer from serving the client we sent. Lawyers excluded from popularity contests (dignity and misleading risk).
5. If later a paid lawyer plan exists, it must be a labelled "פרסומת" placement, not a referral.

**A lawyer must confirm:** (a) whether "ייעודיים לעורכי דין" in 3(א)(5) makes advertising on a general real-estate site off-limits, or whether a free member card and free forum answers are not "advertising" at all; (b) whether nad-lan routing a visitor's request to a lawyer member makes it a "גוף ... המפרסם ברבים מתן שירותים משפטיים" under 11ב, even when free; (c) whether peer endorsements and the "response within 24 hours" badge are allowed content for a lawyer; (d) the current consolidated text on Nevo (Wikisource is a mirror); (e) negligence exposure of public answers and the disclaimer wording.
