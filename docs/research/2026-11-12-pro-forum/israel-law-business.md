### B checklist: what the product must do (from the map above)
1. Short notice at every data field (s.11): purpose, voluntary, who sees it, our name and contact, rights.
2. Public profile shows only what the pro chose to publish.
3. Self-service edit + delete account; removal/correction requests answered and logged.
4. Written "notice and takedown" form on every post and profile; act fast and log the time.
5. Moderation queue for reviews; no operator edits to user text (keeps "did not edit or produce").
6. Reviews: one per verified account per pro; pro can reply publicly.
7. Licence badge only after a check against the official register (brokers, appraisers, contractors), with the check date.
8. Never take a fee tied to a property deal unless a lawyer approves the model.
9. Separate transactional messages (codes, replies) from promotional ones.
10. Promotional email/SMS only with explicit opt-in; "פרסומת" label; one-click refusal.
11. Invite a non-member pro at most once, asking for consent only.
12. Accessibility: ת"י 5568 AA, alt-text field on uploads, accessibility statement.
13. Security baseline per the 2017 regulations (access control, logs, backup) and a databases document.
14. Few admins (≤10) and no sensitive/financial data in the member DB unless needed.
15. Terms of use, community rules, minimum age, privacy policy, accessibility statement linked in the footer.
16. Keep register data as a check, do not republish lists until a lawyer clears it.

### Questions only a lawyer can close
- Is a pro network with activity tracking "systematic monitoring at scale" (privacy officer, s.17ב1)?
- Does any paid-lead or success-fee model make us a "broker" (s.1 + s.14)? Which fee shapes are safe?
- May we display licence data from `metavhim` / `shamaim` (not open-licensed) on profiles?
- Are digest emails with mixed content "דבר פרסומת"? Is an OTP SMS ever advertising?
- Our operator liability for reviews and posts given split case law; wording of the takedown policy and time limits.
- Removal request: delete reviews with the profile, or keep them (public interest vs s.14)?
- Minimum age and parental consent rules; Amendment 13 effective date and transition.
