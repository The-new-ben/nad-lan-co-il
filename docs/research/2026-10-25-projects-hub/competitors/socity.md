## Data points published (paraphrased)
- Price band for marketed projects in Tel Aviv: 35,000 to 100,000 NIS per sqm, rising towards the west and the sea.
- Over 70% of new construction in the city is under TAMA 38 / pinui-binui (cited to Calcalist); the FAQ repeats "about 70%".
- Rents for 4-5 room apartments rose more than 10% in 2025 (cited to Globes).
- Apartment values rose ~10% in 2025; average rental yield "above 2%" city-wide (FAQ).
- Rova plan (תוכנית הרבעים) covers quarters 3, 4, 5, 6; boutique projects of 15-25 units result.
- Renewal-led new build concentrated in quarters 3, 4, 5; non-renewal new build possible in quarters 1, 2, 9 and 6. Quarters 3-5 framed for living, 2 and 9 for investment (lower prices).
- Typical project duration 3-5 years from permit; +1-3 years if no permit yet (FAQ).
- Quarter table (share of population / avg 4-room price / 4-room rent):
  - Rova 1 (Ramat Aviv, Kochav Hatzafon, Afeka, Neve Avivim, Nofei Yam, Lamed, Hagush Hagadol): 14.8% / 4.5-6.0M / 9,500-14,000
  - Rova 2 (Tzahala, Revivim, Neot Afeka, Hadar Yosef, Tel Baruch, Ramat Hahayal, Neve Sharet, Shikun Dan, Maoz Aviv): 12.4% / 4.0-5.2M / 6,000-10,000
  - Rova 3 Old North (Dizengoff, Ben Yehuda, Ben Gurion, Nordau, Hayarkon, Frishman, Bograshov, King George, Arlozorov, Jabotinsky, Gordon, Basel, Hen): 13.7% / 6.0-8.0M / 14,000-22,000
  - Rova 4 New North (Weizmann, Pinkas, Ibn Gabirol, David Hamelech, Namir, Shaul Hamelech, Yehuda Hamaccabi): 11.1% / 5.8-7.5M / 11,000-18,000
  - Rova 5 Lev Hair (Rothschild, Herzl, Habima, Nahalat Binyamin, Hashmonaim, Ben Zion, Bialik, Allenby, Ahad Haam, Yehuda Halevi, Lilienblum, Sheinkin, Neve Tzedek): 9.1% / 5.2-7.5M / 10,000-18,000
  - Rova 9 East (Yad Eliyahu, Hatikva, Kfar Shalem, Bitzaron, Ramat Hatayasim, Nahalat Yitzhak, Neve Kfir, Neve Chen, Ezra, Haargazim): 18.0% / 3.5-5.0M / 5,500-7,500
