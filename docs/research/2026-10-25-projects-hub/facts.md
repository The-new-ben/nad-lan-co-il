## 1. Buying from a developer: the law

### 1a. Sale (Apartments) Law, 1973 [חוק המכר (דירות), התשל"ג-1973]
Consolidated text: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות) (Knesset database no. 2000386). Read 25.10.2026.

- **Technical specification [מפרט טכני], s.2 to s.3.** The seller must attach a specification and maintenance and use instructions to the contract, signed at signing (s.2(א), (א1)). Any change to the specification needs both parties' signatures (s.2(ב)).
  - The specification must use the minister's official form (s.3(א)). The form is the צו מכר דירות (טופס של מפרט), התשל"ד-1974, last amended in ק"ת תשפ"ב p.4074 (2022): https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/צו_מכר_דירות_(טופס_של_מפרט) and https://olaw.org.il/takanot/takanot-10333.pdf
  - The specification must include a **price list of "priced items" [פריטים מתומחרים]** for giving up, moving or adding items (s.3(א1)).
  - The buyer sends change notices by the contract deadline. That deadline may not be earlier than **6 months after the foundations stage ends** (s.3(ה)).
- **No specification or a defective one, s.5.** The seller is treated as having promised "high quality" for anything that should have been specified, and must prove it.
- **Non-conformity [אי-התאמה], s.4.** Liability covers four things:
  - any deviation from the specification, an official standard or the building regulations;
  - anything found during the **inspection period [תקופת בדק]**, unless the seller proves the buyer caused it;
  - anything found during the **warranty period [תקופת אחריות]**, if the buyer proves it comes from design, work or materials;
  - a **fundamental non-conformity** (load-bearing parts) found within **20 years** of delivery (s.4(א)(4)).
  - The warranty period is **3 years starting when the inspection period ends** (s.4(ג)).
- **Inspection periods, from the Schedule [תוספת].** Each period starts on delivery.

  | Item | Period |
  |---|---|
  | Any other non-conformity | 1 year |
  | Metalwork and carpentry, incl. aluminium and plastic | 2 years |
  | Interior flooring and cladding, incl. sinking and wear | 2 years |
  | Machines and boilers | 3 years |
  | Yard development (paths, parking, ground-floor tiles, yard systems) | 3 years |
  | Thermal insulation | 3 years |
  | Piping (water, heating, gutters, drainage, sewage), incl. leaks | 4 years |
  | Waterproofing (underground spaces, walls, ceilings, roofs) | 4 years |
  | Cracks wider than 1.5 mm in non-bearing parts | 5 years |
  | Exterior cladding coming loose, peeling or crumbling | 7 years |

- **Notice of defects, s.4א.** Defects that could be seen at delivery must be reported **within 1 year of delivery**. Hidden defects must be reported within a reasonable time after they are found.
- **Right to repair, s.4ב.** The seller gets a fair chance to repair. If the defect comes back within 2 years of the notice, or an urgent repair is not done in reasonable time, the buyer may repair it at the seller's cost.
- **Registration deadlines, s.6ב.**
  - The building must be registered as a condominium **within 1 year** of the parcellation registration or of delivery, whichever is later.
  - The buyer's rights must be registered **within 6 months** of the condominium registration or of delivery, whichever is later.
  - Delays the seller proves were beyond his control do not count.
- **Condominium bylaws, s.6.** If the bylaws change the model bylaws on common property, attachments, expense shares or decision-making, the details must be in the specification or contract. If they are not, the model bylaws apply.
- **Maintenance company, s.6א.** A clause that stops the owners from ending the first maintenance-company contract is limited to 3 years.
- **Legal fees cap, s.6ג, with the 2014 regulations.** The seller may charge the buyer for the seller's lawyer at most the **lower of 5,915 ₪ (the 2026 amount) or 0.5% of the price, before VAT**.
  - The cap **does not apply when the price is above 4,642,750 ₪**. That covers most new Tel Aviv apartments.
  - The 5,000 ₪ base is linked to the CPI every 1 January.
  - Sources: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/תקנות_המכר_(דירות)_(הגבלת_גובה_ההוצאות_המשפטיות) and the 2026 update notice ק"ת תשפ"ו p.987, https://olaw.org.il/takanot/takanot-12242.pdf
- **Contracting out, s.7א.** No contracting out except in the buyer's favour. For contracts from 7.7.2022, a waiver of delay or defect rights that is demanded as a condition for delivery, repairs or a resale is **void** (s.7א(ב)).

### 1b. Late-delivery compensation [פיצוי על איחור במסירה], s.5א, Amendment 9
Amendment 9 is חוק המכר (דירות) (תיקון מס' 9), התשפ"ב-2022, ס"ח 2983 p.914, published 30.6.2022: https://fs.knesset.gov.il/24/law/24_lsr_628987.pdf
- **Primary:** it took effect 7 days after publication, on **7.7.2022**, and applies to contracts signed **from 7.7.2022**.
- Note: Wikisource s.11(ד) prints "7.7.2023". That is a typo there. Sections 5א and 5ג in the same text, and the Reshumot commencement clause, give 2022.

**Contracts signed from 7.7.2022.** The buyer gets compensation without proving damage if delivery is **more than 1 month late**. He gets it for every month or part of a month **from the end of the first month**, or the contract's agreed amount if that is higher:
- months 2 to 4 after the contract date: **1.0 × the rent of a similar apartment** (same size and location);
- months 5 to 10: **1.25 × rent**;
- from month 11 on: **1.5 × rent**.
- Payment is due at the end of each month (s.5א(ב)). Amendment 9 deleted the old "60 days" payment sentence. The Wikisource consolidation still shows a leftover fragment of it.
- **The seller is exempt only if** the delay was caused by the buyer alone, or by force majeure or frustration under s.18(א) of the Contracts (Remedies) Law (s.5א(ג), new wording).
- The bill had explained that sellers used the old "circumstances beyond our control" clause widely: https://fs.knesset.gov.il/24/law/24_ls1_625705.pdf (30.5.2022).

**Contracts signed up to 6.7.2022 (old wording).** Compensation applies after a delay of **more than 60 days**, counted from day 1:
- months 1 to 8: **1.5 × rent**;
- from month 9 on: **1.25 × rent**.
- The parties could agree to exclude delays beyond the seller's control.

**Late-payment interest, s.5ב.** The rate is capped by the minister's regulations. It starts only once **7 days** have passed after the due date.

### 1c. Sale Law (Assurance of Investments of Apartment Buyers), 1974 [חוק המכר (דירות) (הבטחת השקעות של רוכשי דירות), התשל"ה-1974]
Consolidated text: https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_המכר_(דירות)_(הבטחת_השקעות_של_רוכשי_דירות) (Knesset database no. 2000385). Read 25.10.2026.

- **The 7% rule, s.2.** The seller may not take **more than 7% of the price** unless he gives one of these securities:
  1. a **bank guarantee [ערבות חוק מכר]**, or since 2026 a guarantee from an "other guarantor" (see the next item). It covers everything paid **except the VAT component**;
  2. an **insurance policy** from an approved insurer, with the buyer as beneficiary and the premium prepaid;
  3. a first-rank **mortgage** on the apartment or a share of the land, for the buyer or an approved trust company;
  4. a **warning note [הערת אזהרה]** under s.126 of the Land Law, with no prior charge, attachment or third-party right ahead of it;
  5. **transfer of ownership** free of any charge.
  - A guarantee may be swapped for options 3 to 5 only after construction is finished and possession is handed over (s.2א).
- **2026 change.** The Economic Plan Law for the 2026 budget (ס"ח תשפ"ו p.365, listed in the Wikisource amendment list, PDF https://fs.knesset.gov.il/25/law/25_lsr_12224370.pdf, which we did not open) added a **"נותן ערבות אחר"**: an extended credit or deposit-and-credit licensee, a stable-payment-service licensee, or an insurer that meets liquidity and capital rules (s.1, s.2(1)).
- **Written notice, s.2א1.** At signing, the seller must tell the buyer in writing about his right to security. A seller with **no financial accompaniment [ליווי פיננסי]** agreement with a bank or insurer must say so in writing before signing (s.2א1(ב)).
- **Payment voucher booklet [פנקס שוברים], s.3ב.** In a project with financial accompaniment, the accompanying bank or insurer issues a voucher booklet for each apartment.
  - The seller must take **all payments only through the vouchers**, and that includes the mortgage bank's transfers (s.3ב(ג)-(ד)).
  - Each voucher payment is an irrevocable order to issue a guarantee or policy for the buyer.
  - The guarantee must be issued **within 14 business days** of the payment (s.3ב(ה)).
- **Mortgage lenders, s.3ג.** The lending bank may transfer the loan money to the seller only after it has checked that a s.2 security exists or that the seller has committed in writing to give one.
- **VAT refund on a guarantee call, s.3ג2.** Guarantees exclude VAT, so a Finance Ministry fund pays the buyer back the VAT component, with linkage, when a guarantee is called. The bank applies within 7 days, and the fund pays within 14 days.
- **Linkage of guaranteed money, s.3א1.** Guaranteed money is linked by the contract's linkage method. If the contract sets none, it is linked to the Construction Inputs Index.
- **Costs, s.3א.** The seller pays for the security.
- **Oversight, s.3ד to s.3ט.** A ministry official [ממונה] keeps a register of buyer, seller, land, guarantor and type of security, and handles public complaints. Bank complaints go to the Supervisor of Banks, insurer complaints to the Capital Market Commissioner.
- **Payment limits, s.3.** Without a bank guarantee or policy, the seller may not take payments above the rates in the 1975 regulations (תקנות ... סייג לתשלומים על חשבון מחיר דירה). The rates themselves are not extracted here: **unverified**.

## 2. Price linkage, the Construction Inputs Index and the CPI

### 2a. The linkage rule, s.5ג of the Sale (Apartments) Law (added by Amendment 9, contracts from 7.7.2022)
Source: https://fs.knesset.gov.il/24/law/24_lsr_628987.pdf
- By default, **no linkage and no interest** on any payment, except late-payment interest under s.5ב.
- The parties may agree to link **up to half of each payment** to the Construction Inputs Index. The **20% of the price paid at signing**, or before any other payment, may **not** be linked.
- So the **largest linkable share is 40% of the price** (50% × the 80% left after the first 20%). This is the "40%" figure in the press.
- Linkage must stop **no later than the contractual delivery date**, even if delivery is late. This does not apply when the delay is the buyer's fault or force majeure under s.5א(ג).
- The index used is the residential (or commercial) construction inputs index **last published before the linkage date** (s.5ג(ד)), meaning the "known" index.

- **Single home** (individual Israeli resident), purchases 16.1.2025 to 15.1.2028. The brackets were frozen by the Economic Efficiency Law 2025:

  | Price band (₪) | Rate |
  |---|---|
  | up to 1,978,745 | 0% |
  | 1,978,745 to 2,347,040 | 3.5% |
  | 2,347,040 to 6,055,070 | 5% |
  | 6,055,070 to 20,183,565 | 8% |
  | above 20,183,565 | 10% |

  Sources: Tax Authority instruction 1/2026 (18.1.2026), https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-01-2026/he/realestate_inst-01-2026.pdf , and the official simulator page https://www.gov.il/he/service/real_eatate_taxsimulator
- **Additional home, foreign residents and companies:** a temporary order for purchases 16.1.2025 to **31.12.2026**. **8% up to 6,055,070 ₪, 10% above** (instruction 1/2026, section 2b).
  - **Unverified:** what applies from 1.1.2027. The press says the order may run on during the election period. Check the Tax Authority before publishing any 2027 rate.
- **Upgraders:** they are taxed as single-home buyers if they sell the old home within **24 months** (18 months for purchases 1.6.2023 to 31.5.2025), or **within 12 months of receiving a new home from a developer**, whichever is later.
  - A war extension applies: Tax Authority instruction 2/2026 (30.3.2026), https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-02-2026-1/he/realestate_inst-02-2026.pdf
- **Filing and payment:** the declaration is due within **30 days** of the deal (form 7000). The tax is due on the earliest legal date, **and never before 60 days** from the deal (s.51). Source: the Tax Authority 2026 buyer guide, https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/generalpage/guide-for-seller-right-in-land/he/Guides_knowrightinland_2026.pdf
- **Worked checks:** a 3,000,000 ₪ single home pays **45,538 ₪**. The same price as an additional home pays **240,000 ₪**.

## 4. Mortgages on a new apartment, and developer financing deals

### 4a. Bank of Israel limits: Proper Conduct of Banking Business Directive 329, version (13) 06/26
Source: https://www.boi.org.il/media/ez4npagt/329.pdf , read 25.10.2026.
- **LTV [שיעור מימון], s.2:**
  - **75%** for a single home [דירה יחידה]. This is the "first home" case.
  - **70%** for a replacement home [דירה חליפית], meaning upgraders.
  - **50%** for an investment home.
- **Repayment-to-income (PTI), s.5 to s.6:** at most **50%**. Above 40%, the bank must hold a 100% risk weight on the loan.
- **Variable-rate share, s.7:** at most **66.66%** of the loan.
- **Term, s.8:** at most **30 years**.
- **Reduced-price apartments (מחיר מטרה / מחיר למשתכן), s.4א(2):** the bank may value the apartment by the appraiser instead of the purchase price, up to **2.1 million ₪**. The amount was raised from 1.8M on **8.2.2026** in circular 2840: https://www.boi.org.il/media/ptciib1v/h2840.pdf
  - The same circular made permanent the rule that an any-purpose loan secured on a home may reach 70% LTV, as long as the part above 50% is no more than 200,000 ₪ (s.10א).
  - From **1.7.2026**, the PTI test counts all loans secured on the same property (circular 2840, section 9).

### 4b. Developer "20/80" deals and developer-subsidised loans [מבצעי 20/80, הלוואות קבלן]
Supervisor of Banks circular 2816, **6.4.2025**: https://www.boi.org.il/media/xwkag15z/h2816.pdf . The draft and press release came out 23.3.2025: https://www.boi.org.il/publications/pressreleases/23-3-25/
- **Cap on subsidised bullet and balloon loans (Directive 329 s.8א).** Bullet or balloon mortgages where the **developer pays all or part of the interest** may be at most **10% of each bank's housing-loan volume in a calendar quarter**.
  - Loans that had in-principle approval before 6.4.2025 are excluded.
- **Capital charge on deferred-payment projects (Directive 203 s.79).** Bank credit to a residential project under a financial-accompaniment agreement gets a **150% risk weight** when **more than 25% of the project's sale contracts** let the buyer defer **more than 40% of the price to delivery**.
  - For projects that already existed, the 150% applies only if that share rises by more than 5 percentage points over its level on the start date.
- **Validity:** both measures run from the circular's publication on 6.4.2025 **until 31.12.2026**. The circular says the Supervisor "will examine the need to extend".
  - **Unverified:** whether they will be extended into 2027. No official notice was found as of 25.10.2026.
- **Market effect (Ministry of Finance chief economist, August 2026 review, published October 2026):** https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/review-real-estate-082026/he/reviews-and-publishes_review-real-estate-082026.pdf
  - In 5 tax regions (Center, Tel Aviv, Hadera, Netanya, Beer Sheva), reported financing benefits covered **30% of free-market developer sales in August 2026**, counting deals with delivery a year or more away. That is up 3 points on the month and 10 points since May 2026, which was **20%**, the lowest since November 2024.
  - **Tel Aviv tax region: 21%** (+4 points year on year, +2 points on the month).
  - Center region: 38%. Hadera region: 73%.
  - Developers have had to report financing benefits in their Tax Authority deal reports since November 2024.

## 8. Buyer checklist from official sources

1. **Contractor register [פנקס הקבלנים].** Read 22.10.2026; see docs/research/2026-10-22-contractor-register/brief.md.
   - Search: https://www.gov.il/apps/moch/rasham/home . Each contractor's record is at `https://www.gov.il/apps/moch/rasham/details/{number}`.
   - Check that the status is not **מוקפא** (frozen) and not **מוחרג / מותלה** (excluded or suspended by the registrar).
   - Classification amounts valid **1.10.2026 to 1.4.2027**, per site, excluding VAT:
     - building ג1 up to 5,458 thousand ₪; ג5 unlimited;
     - residential area 2,000 / 3,000 / 5,000 / 10,000 m² / unlimited.
   - Main-branch work above **103,250 ₪** needs a registered contractor.
   - The registrar also oversees sale-law guarantees.
2. **Money security (Assurance law s.2, s.2א1, s.3ב).**
   - Ask which security covers every payment above 7%.
   - Ask whether the project has a bank or insurer **financial accompaniment**. If not, the seller must say so in writing before signing.
   - Pay **only by voucher**, and expect the guarantee **within 14 business days**.
   - Guarantees exclude VAT, which is refunded through a Finance Ministry fund if a guarantee is called.
3. **Specification.**
   - It must be on the official form, signed, with a **priced-items list**.
   - Note the deadline for change requests. It may not be earlier than 6 months after the foundations stage ends.
4. **Contract terms to check against the law:**
   - linkage of at most 50% of each payment, none on the first 20%, and none after the contractual delivery date (s.5ג);
   - delay compensation per s.5א, with no waiver as a condition for delivery (s.7א(ב));
   - the legal-fee cap (s.6ג, up to 5,915 ₪ + VAT or 0.5%, not above 4,642,750 ₪);
   - registration deadlines (s.6ב).
5. **Building permit and plans in Tel Aviv:**
   - TLV GIS layers: **772** (permits) and **499** (building sites), https://gisn.tel-aviv.gov.il/arcgis/rest/services/IView2/MapServer . These were used in docs/research/2026-10-15-sde-dov-soil and 2026-09-30-kikar-hamedina.
   - Plan documents: https://gisn.tel-aviv.gov.il/tabaot/
   - The city's planning-information page: https://www.tel-aviv.gov.il/About/Pages/planning.aspx
   - The city has an online status check for permit applications (tel-aviv.gov.il, Construction section).
   - **Unverified:** a public national "רישוי זמין" lookup by request number. Do not promise one.
6. **Land Registry [טאבו].**
   - Land registration extract, official service: https://www.gov.il/he/service/land_registration_extract . **The fee is unverified.** A secondary source says 18 ₪.
   - Check for a **warning note** (s.126 Land Law), mortgages and attachments.
   - Land managed by the Israel Land Authority or a housing company does not show in the extract; ask that body for a rights confirmation.
7. **Condominium registration [רישום בית משותף].** Read 22.10.2026; see docs/research/2026-10-22-condo-documents/brief.md.
   - Order the condo file (order, bylaws, plan) at https://fileextractor.justice.gov.il/tabu for **39 ₪** (2026 fee).
   - The developer must register the condo within 1 year and the buyer's rights within 6 months after that (Sale Law s.6ב).
   - For Israel Land Authority land, the condo order request goes through the Authority: https://www.gov.il/he/service/guide-apartment-building-warrant
8. **At delivery:** inspect, and report visible defects **within 1 year**. Keep the inspection-period table (section 1a).
9. **Tax:** file the purchase-tax declaration **within 30 days**. An upgrader buying from a developer has **12 months from receiving the keys** to sell the old home, or 24 months from the purchase, whichever is later.
