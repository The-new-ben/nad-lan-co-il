### Verified 2026 purchase-tax brackets (from snippets)

Single-apartment (דירה יחידה):
- 0% on the value up to 1,978,745 ₪
- 3.5% on 1,978,745 to 2,347,040 ₪
- 5% on 2,347,040 to 6,055,070 ₪
- 8% on 6,055,070 to 20,183,565 ₪
- 10% above 20,183,565 ₪

Investor / additional apartment:
- 8% on the value up to 6,055,070 ₪ (from the first shekel; no exemption tier)
- 10% above 6,055,070 ₪

**Bracket freeze:** the brackets are frozen by the Arrangements Law and will not adjust to CPI until 2028-01-15.

Worked example from snippets:
- Young couple, first apartment, 2,500,000 ₪ → total purchase tax ≈ 20,538 ₪.
- Investor, second apartment, 3,000,000 ₪ → 240,000 ₪.

These are usable in the purchase-tax calculator + the `/tax-legal/` pillar. Cite source on publication: rashut hamasim + the date of bracket freeze.
