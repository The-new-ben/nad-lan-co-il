## The main finding

The five developers' luxury projects that are **already selling** inside the radius are almost all covered by our pages:
GINDI VOGUE, DIMRI YAMA, ASHIRA (Avisror), RAINBOW (Israel Canada) and the two Chen and Itai Gindi Einstein pages.
Only **one** project that is actually selling is missing: Africa Israel's tower "סמל צפון" in the Somail compound.
The other two missing projects are **lots won in the March 2025 northern Sde Dov tender**. No sale launch was found for them yet.
A page for either one would be an early "coming soon" page, not a price-list page.

Evidence limits:
- nadlancenter tag pages show day.month with no year. A year is given below only where an article states one or where it is inferred; inferred years are marked "(year inferred)".
- I did not open the developer sites. "Official URL" is left as "not verified" instead of guessed.
- A number with no source is left out.

## Ranked shortlist: the 3 best MISSING projects to build first

1. **סמל צפון, Africa Israel Residences (Somail compound, Jabotinsky / Ben Saruk).**
   - Why it is first: it is the only missing project that is actually selling. It has a permit, a contractor and published prices by room count. It is a 48-50 floor tower. Africa Israel is a top-tier name with 480 + 140 searches a month and no page of ours aimed at it.
   - It sits in the same compound as our H Infinity page, so the two pages can link to each other.
   - Before building: open the April 2025 price article to confirm the figures and the date, and find the official project page.

2. **Chen and Itai Gindi, northern Sde Dov complex 4 (82 units, first line to the sea behind Tel Baruch beach).**
   - Why it is second: it is the most luxurious of the three. It is a small seafront lot, and the land alone costs about NIS 2.7M per unit. The developer's name gets about 590 searches a month.
   - The risk: there is no launch, no price list and no design yet, so it would be a pre-launch page.
   - Keyword guard: "גינדי שדה דב" probably belongs to the VOGUE page, so the new page must lead with "חן ואיתי גינדי" plus the seafront angle, not with that term.

3. **Y.H. Dimri, northern Sde Dov lot (275 units).**
   - Why it is third: Dimri sells at the highest price per m² in Sde Dov (NIS 82.6K/m², company level). It is a big lot with a clear second-project story next to Dimri Yama.
   - The risk: as with number 2, there is no launch and no price list yet. "דמרי שדה דב" probably belongs to the Dimri Yama page.
