# Competitor benchmark: 3D sales experiences for new projects (24.9.2026)

Method: WebSearch + WebFetch, 24.9.2026. Evidence: **code** = page text I opened. **claim** = vendor/press text I opened, feature not seen working. **unverified** = search snippet only, blocked, or not rendered. No competitor was checked by eye (no screenshots), so design notes come from text and claims. Research by an Opus 5.5 subagent; Fable should audit it before anyone builds on it.

## The bar to beat

1. **Level to beat: a Hauzd-class twin** (Hauzd, Plyo, 3D Estate, R2U). The whole building in live 3D on web and phone: every unit clickable, live price and sold/reserved status, orientation/view filters, per-unit views, sun slider, POI map, favorites, WhatsApp. Hauzd adds online reservation with a card deposit.
2. **Israel's real rivals are vendors, not developer sites.** Simplex 3D REALS: own 3D city model, window views, sun/shade by hour and season, approved future construction, deal values. BMBY DREAMS / bmby 3D: window views from real per-floor photos, live CRM inventory, remote presentations, hundreds of projects.
3. Both are **links handed out in sales meetings**. No REALS or DREAMS embed appeared on the Tidhar, Acro, DUO or Rotshtein pages checked.
4. **Israeli developer pages** (Israel Canada, Dimri, Africa Israel, Azorim, Aura, Electra, Rotshtein, Tidhar, Acro, Shikun & Binui): renders, films, plans, forms, WhatsApp, up to 6 languages. None showed a 3D picker, window view or live call.
5. **Gindi Sde Dov leads on price transparency:** ₪/m² by room count next to the area average, plus Tax Authority deals from the last 12 months.
6. **Madlan sets the decision-data bar:** 670 deals listed under the Rainbow project plus 30 nearby, each with date, m², ₪/m², rooms and floor; status timeline; transport; nuisances. Its models cover future construction, view blocking, shading and noise.
7. **Localize.city** (Madlan's US sibling) goes per unit: sunlight hours by season, view blocking by planned construction, noise.
8. **Live selling abroad:** Realsee/Beike run agent-guided live 3D viewings at scale; AEDAS puts a live adviser inside the 3D model; PropVR/DAMAC stream Unreal with video calls and AI avatars.
9. **Photoreal context is moving to drone capture:** Zillow SkyTour (Gaussian splats), Hauzd 360° drone panoramas per unit. London towers still show static photos labeled by direction.
10. **A sun slider is standard** (Plyo, 3D Estate, R2U, Simplex, Sobha). Measured sun, view and noise per unit are not; only Localize has them, without 3D.
11. **Mobile reach bar:** Plyo claims 56% of Norway's new-build units, on plain 4G phones with no streaming.
12. **No competitor checked combines** window view, facing beam, walk-in, design studio, amenities, live call, many languages and deal data on one public page. Against the best, nad-lan still lacks live inventory, measured sun/view/noise, future construction and shared co-viewing.

## 10 ideas to be clearly above all of them

1. **View today and after approved plans.** Ghost volumes of approved, unbuilt buildings (national planning portal, Tel Aviv GIS) in the window scene, with a toggle and a number, e.g. "sea visible across 41° today, 23° after plan X" (illustrative). Each ghost cites its plan number and date. Beats REALS (sales-only layer) and Localize (text only).
2. **View score per window.** Measure what fills the window frame in the 3D scene: sea %, park %, sky %, buildings %, distance to the nearest facing wall. One sortable line on the unit card. Everyone shows pictures of the view; nobody measures it.
3. **Sun hours per room.** Date and hour slider with real neighbour shadows on the window view and the plan, plus measured direct-sun hours for balcony and living room on 21 Dec, 21 Mar and 21 Jun, and a summer-afternoon heat note for west glass. Plyo and 3D Estate have only the slider; Localize only the numbers.
4. **Air directions and sea breeze.** The unit's open facades on the beam map, the summer westerly (Israel Meteorological Service wind rose) and a "3 air directions" badge. Israeli buyers price this; no competitor draws it.
5. **Noise at the window, by floor.** Road, rail and light-rail noise contours projected onto the unit's facade and floor band, labeled "modeled estimate" with its source (still to be confirmed). Madlan does neighbourhoods and Localize addresses; nobody does floors.
6. **Deal card next to the view.** Recent Tax Authority deals in the project and street, filtered by rooms and floor band, e.g. "floors 15–17: ₪60.7K–66.4K/m², 3 deals, Mar–Jun 2026" (format shown with three Sde Dov deals Madlan lists, mixed room counts). Hidden below 3 deals. Madlan's depth, placed beside the view it prices.
7. **Co-viewing, not just a video call.** Rep, buyer and a spouse abroad drive one shared scene: synced camera, pointer and beam, with "hand over control". Live captions in Hebrew, English, French and Russian; the session summary (units viewed, questions) lands on the lead. Beats Realsee (resale, voice), AEDAS (one-way) and DREAMS (rep-driven).
8. **Photo-truth slider.** Drone 360° panoramas at 3–5 real floor heights per project, blended with the 3D view on a "photo ⇄ 3D" slider with capture date. Then move the base to Google Photorealistic 3D Tiles (already piloted in `experience/pilot-google-tiles.html`) or a drone Gaussian splat. First 3D frame under 4 seconds on a mid-range Android over 4G, Plyo's bar.
9. **Live inventory and a three-unit compare.** Colour units available/reserved/sold from a developer feed stamped "updated today 10:12"; unknown status is not shown. Favorites plus a three-unit compare of view score, sun hours, noise, ₪/m² and safe-room size.
10. **Daily life from the front door.** 5, 10 and 15-minute walking zones from the building entrance to the beach, schools and light-rail/metro stations, each station with its opening year, and a "today / 2030" toggle. Rivals show POI pins; none measures from the door or shows the future network.

Gaps: Yad1 was blocked. Buildmedia, the Unreal Engine spotlight and Inman returned 403. The Hauzd app did not render to WebFetch. Vendor numbers are unaudited, and I saw no competitor's screens.
