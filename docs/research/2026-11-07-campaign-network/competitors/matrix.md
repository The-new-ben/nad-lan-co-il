# Campaign cockpit: competitor matrix

Retrieved 7 Nov 2026. Read-only desk research (web search and page reads). No accounts, forms or contact.
Full evidence with a source URL for every value: `services.json` in this folder (31 services, 4 platform rules, 13 cost benchmarks).

How to read: "n/p" = not published on the public pages checked. "(idx)" = read through a search-engine copy because the site blocked direct reading. "(3p)" = third-party source. Figures are as published, not converted.

## 4. The 8 strongest features we must match or beat

1. **Verified leads with a replacement promise.** Immobilier.co.il (checks plus free replacement), Juwai (3 verification grades), Rightmove Ascend (signed-in, verified buyers with a mandatory buyer profile).
2. **Cost per lead and ROI live in the dashboard.** realestate.com.au Ignite (per add-on, daily, phone app), Zillow (effective cost per contact, ROI, buyer satisfaction), Rightmove Fair Share against competing developments.
3. **Pay per lead with a budget cap.** Zillow PC Select lead budget, Bayut per-lead marketplace, Immobilier.co.il from 69 NIS per lead.
4. **One feed, many portals and languages.** Properstar (20+ languages), Immobilier.co.il (8 languages), REA Global Property Network, Zillow to Redfin.
5. **Live launches and community events.** Juwai Live (RSVP list and report within 1 week), Katz roadshow with pre-sale deposits, Yad2 Nadlanation with TV.
6. **Interactive 3D tied to lead capture.** Bayut Studios real-time 3D, Zonda Envision configurator and synced lot map, BMBY VR project map.
7. **Instant contact.** WhatsApp leads tied to the property (Property Finder, Bayut), live calendar booking (Rightmove), call and SMS attribution (REA), virtual numbers (Yad2).
8. **Packages by project stage and scarce top slots.** SeLoger NEO stages and Top Position capped at 4 programmes per sector, Rightmove Coming Soon to Ascend, Madlan presale to last units, Zonda Spotlight+.

## 5. The 5 weaknesses they share (our opening)

1. **No developer self-serve campaign builder.** Every developer product is sold by a rep or a form. Self-serve exists only for agents' listings; mobile apps are for monitoring only.
2. **Hidden prices.** Only 5 of 31 publish a price a developer can use: Juwai, ImmoIsrael, Immobilier.co.il (per-lead floor), Kyero (advertiser subscription, no developer plan) and Rightmove (average revenue per development, not a rate card).
3. **The developer cannot choose countries, languages or communities.** Global portals sell placement, not audiences. Israeli portals are Hebrew-only and domestic. Overseas reach runs one way (Rightmove Overseas, Yad2's Cyprus arena). None of the Israeli services checked offers Arabic.
4. **Leads leave the project.** They go to an inbox, a CRM or an agent; none lands inside a live project page the buyer keeps exploring. In the Gulf, project leads go to agents, not developers.
5. **Weak proof.** Only one service promises to replace bad leads (Immobilier.co.il); only three publish any lead verification (plus Juwai and Rightmove). Audience numbers are self-reported and contradict themselves (Immobilier.co.il 113 vs 67 portals, Properstar 45 to 120 portals, Icube 95 vs 200 salons, Kyero 3 million vs 1.2 million visits). Fairs publish no data-sharing terms and leave no lasting asset.

## 6. Platform rules: what the cockpit must not promise

| Platform | Where | What is removed | What stays |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta housing special ad category | Advertisers in the US; ads reaching the US (from Dec 2019), Canada (Dec 2020), Europe (Dec 2021). Others can opt in or out. | Age fixed 18 to 65+, no gender, minimum radius 15 miles / 25 km (US, Canada) and 15 km (Europe), no ZIP or neighbourhood, no location exclusion, no demographic or behaviour targeting, no lookalikes or saved audiences | Custom audiences (non-discriminatory), approved interests, city and country |
| Meta sensitive topics (all ads, worldwide) | Since 19 Jan 2022 | Targeting by religion, ethnicity, health, politics, sexual orientation (examples removed: "Jewish holidays", "Catholic Church") | Geography, language, own consented lists |
| Google Ads personalized advertising | Housing: US and Canada. Religion: sensitive category everywhere. | Housing: no gender, age, parental or marital status, no ZIP. Sensitive categories: no Customer Match, own data segments, audience expansion or lookalikes | Radius of at least 1 km, city, country, Canadian FSA, keywords |
| TikTok HEC categories | US and Canada (updated Apr 2026) | 18+ only, no gender, no ZIP, no interest keywords, hashtags or lookalikes, no targeting expansion | Limited interests, geography |

Plain meaning: an Israel-only campaign is not forced into the housing category; anything aimed at France, the UK, Belgium, other European countries, the US or Canada is. No platform anywhere lets us target "Jewish" or any religious or ethnic group, so community reach must come from country plus city plus language plus our own opted-in lists, community media and events.

## 8. Gaps and cautions

- Several official sites blocked direct reading; those values came from search-engine copies and are labelled "(idx)" here and "per search index" in the JSON.
- No Israel-specific CPM, CPC or cost-per-lead study was found; the web search budget for this run ran out before a deeper Hebrew search.
- Some exhibitor and partner names come from logo file names and are marked unconfirmed in the JSON.
- Several Israel-diaspora fairs face protests or bans in London, New York and Paris (press cited in the JSON); this is a reputational factor for event channels.
