A marketing and sales budget calculator for a new real-estate project: enter the apartments for sale and the average price, choose the stage and who sells, and get a range for the whole project, the budget lines one by one and the source and flag of every number (cited source, published price list or agency claim).

The anchors published in Israel: about 1% of sales value for marketing, of which about 0.75% online (WE DO MEDIA, agency claim); 2% to 4% for marketing and sales, the sales team and advertising included (Start Point, agency claim); ₪3,000 to ₪23,000 of advertising per apartment sold (Start Point, agency claim, source not named). In the Semerenko example, 60 apartments at an average of ₪2,400,000 (sales of ₪144,000,000), that is ₪2,880,000 to ₪5,760,000 for marketing and sales, and about ₪1,440,000 for marketing alone.

An exclusive new-apartment lead costs ₪128 to ₪173 before VAT in the Start Point price list, and per Semerenko a lead in a project campaign actually costs ₪300 to ₪800 (Semerenko, agency claim, source not named). Abroad: 0.75% to 2% for marketing alone and 3% to 5% for marketing and selling (Feasly, Australia, agency claim).

No Israeli price is published for these lines: research and pricing, branding, photography, website and landing pages, lead handling, outdoor signs and the site fence, print, radio and TV, PR, events and exhibitions, the sales office and model apartment, nor a commission rate of marketing companies for new projects.
