## 6. SEO safety

- **Google:** paid links must carry `rel="sponsored"`; `nofollow` is accepted, but `sponsored` is preferred (developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links, updated 10.12.2025). Google's spam policy lists "Advertorials… where payment is received for articles that include links that pass ranking credit" as link spam. Its site reputation policy hits third-party content published mainly to ride the host's rankings (developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies, updated 28.8.2026).
- **Practitioners:**
  - Site-reputation enforcement from May 2024 removed coupon and partner sections from big publishers, and practitioner Glenn Gabe reported USA Today, CNN and the LA Times vanishing for coupon queries. Google removed the first-party-oversight exception on 19.11.2024 (ppc.land/site-reputation-abuse/; mariehaynes.com/site-reputation-abuse-policy).
  - Barry Schwartz describes his own site suffering after selling followed links (seroundtable.com/google-beats-me-16638.html).
  - The consensus: properly tagged, clearly labelled ads inside the site's own topic are safe; selling articles is where sites get hurt.
- **Rules for us:**
  - Every paid outbound link gets `rel="sponsored noopener"`.
  - Visible labels "פרסומת" or "תוכן ממומן".
  - Paid text lives on the advertiser's profile or mini-site, never as a standalone guide in /urban-renewal/.
  - No paid wording inside our neutral "how to choose" advice.
  - Their claims are shown as "לדברי החברה".
  - A cap on paid cards per page.
  - Indexing stays Ben's decision.

## Risks

1. **Payments:** checkout is blocked until Morning is reconnected (HAD-184), so the pilot must be invoiced by hand.
2. **Paid link:** paid profiles currently pass a followed website link. Fix `rel="sponsored"` first.
3. **Neutrality:** our guides advise tenants on choosing an organizer, so a paid organizer next to that advice must be labelled and kept out of the advice text.
4. **Claims:** their figures conflict ("3+ years" against "9+ years"; Tel Aviv named but no Tel Aviv project). Publish only attributed claims.
5. **Name collision** with the developer Adams. Spell out "אדמס' ליווי דיירים" with the logo.
6. **Thin urban-renewal traffic:** sell shelf and content, never reach numbers.
7. **Legal:** organizer duties under urban-renewal law were not checked here (unverified), so a lawyer should look at the ad wording.
