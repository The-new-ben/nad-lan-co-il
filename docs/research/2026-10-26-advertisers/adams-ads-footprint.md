## Three corrections to the first pass (adams-research.md)

1. **Wrong Meta ID.** 61578640952040 is the Facebook *profile URL* id. The real **Page ID is 774803789040330**, and the page was created on 23.7.2025 (Ad Library "About", shot 04). I re-checked with the correct ID: no active ads (shot 05).
2. **The Meta Ad Library cannot prove "never advertised" in Israel.** For Israel it lists only ads **running today**, plus social-issue/political ads (kept 7 years) and ads that ran in the EU or UK (kept 1 year). Meta states this on the library's front page (shot 03). A boosted post that ended last month is invisible. So "no Meta ads" means "none running on 26.10.2026", nothing more.
3. **samuel-gleizer.com is not a scraper.** adams-group.co.il has a WordPress author `samuelg500gmail-com` (from /wp-json/wp/v2/users and the users sitemap). The copy is their web developer's duplicate, and its canonical points to itself. It still outranks them; it is a free finding to give them.

## Market context

Only **one** active Meta ad in Israel uses the exact phrase "ליווי דיירים" (Zcharia Group), and none uses "חברה מארגנת". Tenant organizers barely buy Meta ads, so our offer is not competing against a large existing ad budget.

## Method limits

- DataForSEO's live Google SERP in Israel returns **no paid results at all**. Even the ad-heavy control query "עורך דין גירושין" came back with zero, so that API is no evidence either way for search ads. ATC is the authority.
- DataForSEO also ignores `site:`, so site checks were run in the real browser. Google showed a captcha after about 12 searches, and I stopped there. Yad2 and easy.co.il bot walls were not bypassed.
