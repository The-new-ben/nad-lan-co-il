## 2. Proposed prices

### Advertising (the urban-renewal track on /advertise/)

| Option | What the advertiser gets | Price | Built on |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Profile | Premier profile under "ארגון דיירים" plus the sponsorship "ליווי דיירים" | ₪749 + ₪199 = **₪948 a month**, stops by itself | 477 + 5403 (exist) |
| B. Area package | A, plus one labelled "פרסומת" card on the city pages they choose (up to 6) and a private monthly report | **₪1,490 a month** | new shop product; the city pages are set by an admin |
| C. Advertiser story | A Hebrew and English story inside their profile, written to our standard, labelled "כתבת מפרסם", links rel="sponsored" | **₪1,490 once** | new shop product (or invoice) |
| B + C | three months of B plus the story | **₪5,500** (instead of ₪5,960) | invoice |

Recommendation for ADAM'S (with the second footprint pass, adams-ads-footprint.md): they have never bought Google ads and run nothing on Meta today, and their site has no ad tracking, so they are first-time buyers on a small budget. **Open with one month of A (₪948) or the one-off story (₪1,490), not a three-month minimum.** Sell what they can show people (profile, story, labelled badge, our private view and inquiry report), never reach. The three-month minimum stays only on B, and Ben may drop it.

### The management room for organizers

| Who | Price | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Residents and committees | **Free**, as the live landing already promises | The free resident side is the norm (Bllink free plan, Magen Dayarim, HOA Express Free) and is what brings organizers in |
| Organizer, per active complex | **₪149 a complex a month**, minimum **₪590** (covers up to 4 complexes) | far below per-community software abroad (₪180 to ₪1,600 a community a month, section 4), so it reads as good value; a complex can hold several buildings |
| Organizer pilot | first month free, then the price above; read-only when it lapses, tenants' data never deleted | low-risk start |
| Bundle for an organizer with about 10 complexes | A (₪948) + room (₪590 + 6 × ₪149 = ₪1,484) = ₪2,432 → **₪1,990 a month** | one invoice, one decision |

Note: the room is not ready for organizers yet (renewal-room/AUDIT.md lists the work), so the room can be offered only as a pilot with a start date Ben sets.
