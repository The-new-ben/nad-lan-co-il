## 3. אסבסט בבניין (he, Israel) — retrieved 3.11.2026 ~17:30 UTC (task 11031730-2646-0139-0000-adf61850bd91)
**AI Overview: YES.** Says: in old buildings (usually 1970s or earlier) asbestos appears on roofs (asbestos-cement / "גגון"), sewage or AC pipes, and more rarely as sprayed insulation on walls or in shelters (friable). Asbestos-cement is usually not dangerous while intact; friable is very dangerous. Law section: ban on import/manufacture/installation/new use; friable in public/industrial buildings had to be removed per the law's timetable; maintenance duties on asbestos-cement; licensed contractor + designated landfill; self-removal only for very small areas ("usually up to 50 m²") under strict guidance, "not recommended". Ends with follow-up questions (where is the material — roof, shelter, piping? intact or broken?).
