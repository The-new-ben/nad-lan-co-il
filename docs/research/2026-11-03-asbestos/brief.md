## Proposed outline (Hebrew, 5,000+ words; rendered order per the content-first law)

**H1:** אסבסט: איך מזהים, מתי הוא מסוכן, מה החוק, כמה עולה פינוי ומי מממן
**Answer paragraph (first):** asbestos-cement vs friable in one sentence; intact asbestos-cement is not dangerous until it breaks or burns; never drill/cut/break; removal only through a licensed contractor with an MoEP permit; report hazards to *6911 or 106. Then CTAs (find a licensed contractor near me / am I eligible for funding / what do I have), then the tools.

1. מה זה אסבסט ולמה הוא עדיין על הגגות בישראל - two types, ~10% fibres in asbestos-cement, the 1952 Nahariya factory and ~100 million m² of products, peak use 1976-78, ban since August 2011, ~80 million m² still in place.
2. איך מזהים אסבסט בבית, בבניין ובחצר - roofs, roof sheds (גגון), cladding, water and sewer pipes, gutters, chimneys, planters, water tanks, household items; bulk sample to a licensed lab; "treat as asbestos until proven otherwise".
3. מתי אסבסט מסוכן ומתי לא - intact vs broken / burnt / crumbling; works near you; drinking water position (WHO via MoEP).
4. אסבסט ובריאות - IARC Group 1, WHO >200,000 deaths/year, asbestosis / lung cancer / mesothelioma latencies, Israel 1.8 per 100,000 and Akko sub-district x3, symptoms and when to see a doctor, periodic checks for exposed workers.
5. מה החוק מחייב - public places (paint, inspect, 1,000 m² surveyor rule, one-third crack rule), private owners, hazard duty (stop, fence, report, permit request within 7 days), penalties table (criminal tiers + administrative sanctions as shown on Nevo).
6. פינוי אסבסט צעד אחר צעד - who holds which licence, permit timing (30 days by law, ~1.5 weeks in practice, same day for hazards), what you may do yourself (limited work, product up to 700 kg, the 2024 low-risk exemption up to 500 m² for contractors), packaging, 5 landfills + 1 transfer station.
7. כמה עולה פינוי אסבסט - public benchmark (MoEP home-contractor tender: NIS 50/m² broken asbestos-cement, NIS 1,600/m³ friable-state), labelled commercial ranges with their dates, what moves the price, roof replacement, a court-ordered NIS 4,000 per apartment share (Migdal HaEmek case).
8. פינוי בחינם ומימון המדינה - what exists and for whom: low-income hazard removal (4 eligibility criteria), northern roof programme 2024 (NIS 12m via local authorities), Gaza envelope (NIS 60.5m, ~315,000 m²), public-building friable support (NIS 50m draft 2020), solar-for-asbestos idea 2021.
9. קונים דירה ישנה, משפצים, תמ"א 38 או פינוי-בינוי - checklist; Tel Aviv survey rule (buildings up to 31.12.2000); Haifa survey condition (TAMA 38, urban renewal, listed areas); what to ask the developer / committee and put in the contract; the honest note that no national survey duty was found.
10. גג אסבסט אצל השכן, בבניין משותף או בדירה שכורה - owner duty under the law; who to call; (shared-property law not yet researched - see gaps).
11. אחרי שרפה, קריסה או פגיעת טיל - hazard protocol, MoEP 24/7 hotline, war-time hazards (13 known by Dec 2023).
12. הגליל המערבי - the story and the map (15 km radius, 342 sites, NIS 327m stage A, stage B to 2026, 348-point layer).
13. ישראל במספרים - dated data panel (17.8 million m² removed 2011-2023 under ~29,000 permits; ~NIS 1.5bn spent; ~NIS 5.3bn to finish; ~70 years at today's pace; ~4 years of certain landfill reserves; 453 licence holders).
14. למי פונים - authority directory (MoEP districts, 57 environmental units for 255 authorities, hotlines, registry, labs, landfills).
15. שאלות נפוצות - the PAA questions verbatim + related searches (בחינם, במימון המדינה, עצמאי, קנס, הטמנה, דירה שכורה).
