## Honest gaps (kept visible)
- The regulators (MoEP, EPA, HSE, ASEA) are the primary source; we cite them, we do not replace them. Their sites hold more pages in total.
- Israel has no national map of asbestos roofs; our map covers the one public layer (Western Galilee, 2011-2017 stage). The page says so.
- The licence holders are counted by district; the names stay on gov.il (we link out, we do not republish personal details).
- The commercial price ranges come from a contractor site and a price-comparison platform; they are labelled as such, the article
  itself relies on the public tender price only.
- The 2024 "low-risk work" amendment: its in-force date was not verified; the page tells readers to check it with the Ministry.
