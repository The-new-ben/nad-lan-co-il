## 5. What Google wants to show
- An **entity answer**: who publishes it, that it is the industry reference, monthly updates, chapters and items (the AI overview repeats Dekel's numbers in every variant of the query).
- The **family of price lists** with one line each (the AI overview structures it that way for "מחירון דקל", "2026", "בניה", "דקל מחירון").
- **Access**: paid subscription, phone; and for "pdf" an explicit "no free official PDF" answer.
- **Numbers** for sub-trades (demolition): Google takes them from whoever publishes ranges, even a non-Dekel site. An answer that says plainly what Dekel includes, and labels any market numbers by source and date, fills that gap honestly.
- Freshness: Facebook posts "מהדורת ספטמבר" rank because they signal the current edition.
