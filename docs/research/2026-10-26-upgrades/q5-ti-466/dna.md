Scope (SII wording, summarised):
- Part 1: the standard, in all its parts, covers design and execution of structures or elements of plain, reinforced, precast and prestressed concrete, cast in place or precast. **Not** for fibre shotcrete supporting rock tunnels, and **not** for slabs and pavements laid fully on the ground.
- Part 2: design of structural elements of reinforced and plain concrete to which part 1's general principles apply; in a conflict, part 2 governs.
- Part 3: design and execution of prestressed members (pre- or post-tensioned, bonded or unbonded tendons) under static or equivalent static loads.
- Part 4: calculation, design, production and erection of precast elements and systems, including non-load-bearing wall elements and composite (מיקשר) slab systems.
- Part 5: design and production of prestressed hollow-core precast slabs and their integration in the structure; not for slabs mainly under dynamic loads.

Notes on SII pages:
- Part 1: an erratum was published in September 2025, in Yalkut HaPirsumim 13873 on 15.10.2025.
- Part 2: in force 3 months after Reshumot publication; for two more years both the new edition and the January 1979 edition (with amendments) were valid; erratum of March 2017 published in Y.P. 7527 on 15.6.2017.
- Part 3: for two years from publication both the new edition and the July 1987 edition were valid.
- The "מעמד" (status) field is **empty** for all five parts. For comparison the same field reads "רשמי" on ת"י 27 and ת"י 4466 חלק 3.

**Official versus mandatory:** Israel Builders and Infrastructure Engineers Association, response to the draft Standards Order (Seventh Schedule amendment) 2026, dated 26.5.2026: Planning and Building regulations contain hundreds of references to standards that are not "official"; "among the mandatory standards there are standards at the core of the industry such as ת"י 466 parts 4 and 5, precast elements ... updated in 1991 and 1987".
