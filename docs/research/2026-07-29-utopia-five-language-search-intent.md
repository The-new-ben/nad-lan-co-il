## Hebrew

Query:

`UTOPIA שדה דב דירות מחיר`

Parameters: `hl=he`, `gl=il`, `pws=0`

Observed intent:

- UTOPIA project price.
- Sde Dov project prices and comparisons.
- Project map and exact location.
- Floor, apartment type and sea exposure.
- Current stage and expected timing.

Editorial response:

- Start with the project identity, Tel Aviv, 337 homes, four-building composition and the fact that no current official public price list was found.
- Separate dated UTOPIA press reports from broader Sde Dov market context.
- Explain how to compare floor, direction, area basis, payment terms and future obstruction.

## English

Query:

`UTOPIA Sde Dov Tel Aviv apartments for sale price`

Parameters: `hl=en`, `gl=us`, `pws=0`

Observed intent:

- Apartments for sale and current price.
- Whether a foreign or US buyer may purchase in Israel.
- Whether it is a good time to buy.
- Project timing, financing and purchase process.
- Clear location orientation for a non-local reader.

Editorial response:

- Explain the purchase decision without publishing unsourced eligibility, tax or mortgage rates.
- Link to the site's purchase-tax and mortgage tools.
- Explain which documents, professionals and currency questions a non-resident should raise.
- Distinguish a press timing expectation from a binding delivery date.

## French

Query:

`UTOPIA Sde Dov Tel Aviv appartements à vendre prix`

Parameters: Google France, `hl=fr`, `gl=fr`, `pws=0`

Observed intent:

- Appartement Tel Aviv à vendre.
- Immobilier Tel Aviv prix.
- Projet immobilier Sde Dov.
- Purchase tax and complete acquisition budget.

- Make the dated price evidence easy to scan.

## Arabic

`UTOPIA سديه دوف تل أبيب شقق للبيع السعر`

Parameters: `hl=ar`, `gl=il`, `pws=0`

Observed intent:

- Thin project-specific Arabic coverage.
- Ambiguity with an unrelated accommodation result using the word Utopia.
- Apartments for sale, price and project location.

Editorial response:

- The opening must immediately identify UTOPIA as a planned residential project in Sde Dov, Tel Aviv.

## Cross-language content contract

Google's visible results reward direct, entity-rich answers. The five pages therefore need:

1. Project-first title and one H1.
2. A factual opening paragraph before the interactive showroom.
3. Named entities, application numbers, dates and numerical facts in readable context.
4. Short direct answers, tables and checklists.
5. A clear boundary between official facts, dated press reporting, broader market context and independent concepts.
6. Complete source links and natural internal next steps.
7. No internal process language, keyword commentary or repeated legal-style caveats.
