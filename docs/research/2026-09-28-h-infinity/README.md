## Method notes (limits)

  PAA-proxy = question autocomplete + our own GSC conversational queries + competitor FAQs.

  Two floors are confirmed by Globes; the others are "not published".

  against Globes 1.9.2026 (1 sale in Q1, none in Q2, 11 more after the period).

## The biggest competitor gaps we fill
1. Nobody reconciles the numbers (47/50/51/52/53 floors; 200/237/242/273/278/287 homes). Anchor: permit 22.12.2021 +
   change permit 9.12.2024 = 53 floors, ~278 homes (Hagag 2025 annual report).
2. Nobody explains the recorded deals: 2024 median ~₪74K/m², 2025-26 median ~₪91K/m², top ₪29.37M for 312 m² (27.11.2025),
   versus "from ₪60-65K/m²".
3. Nobody decodes the Aug 2026 offer: ₪1M at signing; "24%-52%" is on the ₪1M, about 1.5%-2.4% gross a year on the price
   (our calculation); two versions of when the balance is due.
4. Nobody gives dated status: 86.3% financial completion (30.6.2026), end of construction planned Q4 2026, 60 homes left
   (Globes 1.9.2026), no sale in Q2 2026.
5. Nobody covers buyer protection: the occupancy condition (450 m² public-space plan at Bavli 3, deadline 31.10.2026), the
   purchasing-group history, 10% vs 15% vs ₪1M terms.
6. No page ties the compound together (DUO 668 homes, lot 122 226 homes, Somail 121, the city-hall tower, light rail with
   honest dates: Purple 7.2028, Green central 2030).
7. No Russian, French or Arabic editorial page exists at all; English pages are 250-420-word listings.
8. Nobody writes what is not published (height, spec, management fees, per-unit delivery date, pool size).
