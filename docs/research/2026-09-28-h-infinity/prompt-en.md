### History
- Sumail (Arabic السُّمَيل, also al-Mas'udiyya) was an Arab village founded in the second half of the 19th century on a
  kurkar ridge. Population: 104 (1870), 449 (1922), 651 (1931), about 850 (1945). Its residents left on 25.12.1947.
  Afterwards Jewish residents lived in the houses as an informal neighbourhood without municipal infrastructure or
  property rights. The compound's plans were approved in 2006 (Hebrew Wikipedia, "סומייל", read 28.9.2026).
- 12.2012-1.2013: Hagag exercised an option on the lot (project-tlv.info timeline).
- Q3 2014: marketing started as a purchasing group (2025 annual report). 1.12.2014: "90 apartments sold from ₪2.2 million"
  (TheMarker).
- Q4 2016: "start of construction works" (2025 annual report).
- 25.11.2021: the group's assembly unanimously approved turning it into a developer project led by Hagag (annual report).
  Globes 30.11.2021: members will receive "Sale Law guarantees on all the money", "a uniform specification and a final
  delivery date". The Ministry of Construction and Housing, quoted there: "In the purchasing-group world there is no law
  that protects the members. In the developer world the money is protected."
- Since November 2021 the company sells to buyers who are not group members (Q2 2026 report).
