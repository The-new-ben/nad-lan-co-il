## 3. The answer paragraph
> H-Infinity Tower (also marketed as Infinity Tower) is Hagag Group's residential tower at Ibn Gabirol 128, in the
> northern part of Tel Aviv's Somail compound between Arlozorov and Jabotinsky streets, a short walk from Kikar
> Hamedina. Under the permits cited in Hagag's 2025 annual report, it has a 53-floor tower and a low-rise building of 6
> residential floors over retail, about 278 apartments of 2 to 6 rooms (roughly 1 to 5 bedrooms), designed by Prof.
> Moshe Tzur and built by Electra Construction. Hagag advertises prices from ₪65,000 per square meter; deals recorded in
> the project in 2025 closed at about ₪72,000 to ₪95,000 per square meter (Madlan, Israel Tax Authority data). The
> company expects construction to finish in the fourth quarter of 2026.
