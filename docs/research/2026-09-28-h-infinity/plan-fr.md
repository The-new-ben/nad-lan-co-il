## 7. Audience notes (why this is not a translation)
- **Pièces = Israeli rooms** (a "4 pièces" = 4 rooms incl. the living room); "chambres" = bedrooms; the developer's
  English page counts bedrooms. Say it clearly: it is the first confusion of French buyers reading English listings.
- **Vue mer** is typed; views per apartment are not published; the developer's "vue infinie sur le littoral" is a claim.
- **French obligations** (declaring a foreign bank account, rental income, wealth tax on real estate) appear in the
  French guides. We only list them as questions for a French tax adviser, with no rates and no claims.
- **Olé hadash** timing: the benefit window starts one year before alyah; the exact brackets conflict between sources:
  send readers to the Tax Authority simulator.
- Tone: vouvoiement, sober, no "exclusivité", no "dernières opportunités".
- Numbers in French format (65 000 ₪, 86,3 %), with a non-breaking space before ":" "%" and "₪" where the site allows.
