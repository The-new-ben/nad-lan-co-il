## Decisions
- Hub URL /forms/ ("forms" is owned by 0 URLs). Head term family: טפסי מקרקעין / טפסים מקרקעין + טאבו + מיסוי מקרקעין + רשות מקרקעי ישראל.
- Every form gets its own anchored card (#7002 etc.) with the number + exact official name as its heading, plus a shareable "link to this form" snippet.
- Off-intent, left out: ייפוי כוח מתמשך, שטר חוב (not a government form), טופס 4 (a certificate, not a download; at most a short explanation).
