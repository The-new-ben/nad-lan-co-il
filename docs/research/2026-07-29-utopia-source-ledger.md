## Municipal design packet

The municipality's [lot 103 design-review packet](https://www.tel-aviv.gov.il/Residents/Development/DocLib/%D7%9E%D7%92%D7%A8%D7%A9%20103%20%D7%93%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F%20%D7%91%D7%A2%D7%99%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%91.pdf) adds defensible massing and parcel context:

- S1 main tower: 34 residential floors, plus ground and technical levels, with a stated maximum of about 137 metres.
- N1 mid-rise: 14 residential floors, plus ground and technical levels, with a stated maximum of about 70 metres. This helps explain why a developer-facing description may call it a 15-floor building.
- N2 and S2: seven residential floors, plus ground and technical levels, with a stated maximum of about 38 metres.
- 205 of the 337 homes are associated with tower construction and 132 with the lower and mid-rise construction.
- The statutory size mix is recorded as 167 homes up to 60 square metres gross, 85 homes from 61 to 85 square metres gross, and 85 homes above 86 square metres gross.
- The parcel is bordered by the planned Street 2 to the south, public open-space lot 603 to the north, public open-space lot 605 to the west and lot 401 to the east.

These facts support project-level explanation and illustrative massing. They do not support a complete apartment stack, exact facade polygons, current availability or apartment views.

## Price lock

1. A current official UTOPIA price list was not found in the public developer sources.
2. Do not publish the existing WordPress average of NIS 78,000 per square metre.
3. A [Globes report dated 2025-11-13](https://en.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001526410) described project prices from about NIS 5 million to NIS 30 million, depending on size, floor and sea exposure. Treat this only as a dated press-reported range.
4. The same report described an estimated NIS 7 million transaction for an approximately 88 square metre, three-room apartment on floor 18 with an approximately 10 square metre balcony. Any derived division is an **estimate, non-binding** and must show whether balcony area is included.
5. A [Globes report dated 2025-07-14](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001515692) described a mini-penthouse transaction at NIS 26 million and approximately NIS 100,000 per square metre. Attribute it to the report and date.
6. [Bizportal, 2026-06-06](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/20033505) and [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/article/r12n2qiowx) are Sde Dov market context, not UTOPIA price lists.
7. Every price comparison must separate asking price, reported transaction, incentive structure, floor, area basis and date.

## Planning discrepancies

The Hebrew developer page and municipal main application support a 34-floor tower with 15-floor and two 7-floor buildings. The English developer page says 35 floors and two 8-floor buildings. Some press reports also use 35 or 8. The statutory plan permits maxima that differ from the current application. Public copy must say that public sources use different counting conventions and that the municipal application is the best current reference for the submitted design. Do not silently blend the figures.
