Between Ibn Gabirol and the Mediterranean, SEVEN SEAS is planned as a coastal address in Tel Aviv's Sde Dov West, bringing together homes, shared leisure spaces, retail and offices. Electra Megurim and Dan Nadlan are developing the project, with residences ranging from compact apartments to larger homes. This page sets out the published figures with their dates, including the 3.2026 financial report and 5.2026 design approval.

Location

SEVEN SEAS is located in Sde Dov West, west of Ibn Gabirol Street. The site is at the Einstein promenade junction, about 280 m from the sea.

Buildings and apartments

The local committee approved the project's design plan in 5.2026. The approved mix includes a 45-floor tower and a 20-floor tower. A 9-floor mid-rise building and an 8-floor office building complete the planned composition.

The project comprises 437 apartments, ranging from 2-room micro units to homes with 6 rooms. These are room counts, with the bedroom arrangement depending on each apartment's layout. Penthouses and garden apartments add further choices within the residential mix. The plan also provides about 2,200 m² of retail and about 3,500 m² of offices.

Architecture is by Yasky Mor Sivan, with interiors and public spaces by Kobi Karp. The developer describes an outdoor pool facing the sea, alongside an indoor pool. A gym and wellness areas are also included in the developer's description. The planned amenities bring swimming, exercise and relaxation into the residential setting.

The developer's website describes “3 towers”, while the approved plan distinguishes the building types above.

Published prices

According to Electra's annual report, published 3.2026, offers and contracts covered 73 apartments. Their combined value was about ILS 635 million, including VAT. Within that total, 28 signed contracts represented about ILS 330 million.

The published average was about ILS 8.7 million per apartment, including VAT. The corresponding published figure was about ILS 80,000 per m², also including VAT. Over 60% of buyers were foreign residents, giving the early buyer profile a distinctly international character.

These averages combine purchase offers with signed contracts, so they are not an official price list. Neither published average specifies the asking price of an individual apartment within the development.

Project stage

Quiet marketing began in 12.2025, ahead of an official pre-sale launch. By 3.2026, the official pre-sale had not yet opened. The local committee approved the design plan in 5.2026. The approval relates to the design plan and does not itself establish a delivery date. The developer's website now carries pre-sale messaging for the project.

Not yet published

The developer's project page does not publish apartment sizes or a delivery date. An official price list and the main contractor are not published there either.
