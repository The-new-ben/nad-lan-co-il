In the centre of Sde Dov, north Tel Aviv, FIRST, by Hagag Group, brings a residential tower and boutique buildings into the same development, with retail and office space alongside the apartments. This page presents the project's published figures, with their dates, covering the buildings, prices, sales and land purchase.

Location

FIRST occupies plot 2270 in the centre of the new Sde Dov quarter. Its location places the development within this new residential district in north Tel Aviv.

Buildings and apartments

Details published in 4.2026 describe a 45-floor tower and three 9-floor boutique buildings. These contrasting building heights give FIRST distinct residential settings within the same project.

The apartment count published in 2.2025 totals 350 across the development. The tower contains 258 apartments, while the low-rise buildings contain 92.

The published scheme also includes about 1,491 m² of retail space. Alongside that provision, about 2,380 m² is allocated to offices. Residential and commercial uses therefore form part of the same development. The architecture is by ODA of New York with Bar Orian Architects.

Published prices and sales

A business news site's check in 7.2025 reported pre-sale prices starting at ILS 71,000 per m². The same check reported upper-floor prices of ILS 110,000 to 120,000 per m². These published prices distinguish the starting level from pricing on the upper floors.

The 7.2025 figures recorded 102 apartments sold for ILS 853 million. The published average was about ILS 8.36 million per apartment. This average describes the reported sales across apartments within that group.

By 4.2026, 155 of the 350 apartments had been sold. Including 35 registration requests, the published total reached 190 apartments marketed. The marketed total includes registration requests alongside the reported apartment sales.

A record deal published in 12.2025 concerned an apartment on floor 38. The apartment measured about 390 m², with a terrace of about 200 m². The reported purchase price was more than ILS 42 million. The published description gives the apartment and terrace areas separately.

These are published figures from different dates, not a price list. They describe pre-sale pricing, reported sales, registration requests and an individual record transaction.

The land

The land was bought through an Israel Land Authority tender in 2.2025. The purchase totalled ILS 803.5 million, including development costs. This amount records the land acquisition separately from the project's apartment sales.

Not yet published

The delivery date and a general schedule of apartment sizes have not been published. An official price list has not been published either. The record apartment's dimensions describe that particular home alone. They do not establish the sizes of other apartments within FIRST.
