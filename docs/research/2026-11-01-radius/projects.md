## עשרת הפערים החשובים / The 10 most important gaps

1. **SEVEN SEAS שדה דב**: Electra Megurim + Dan Nadlan. 437 units, towers of 45 and 20 floors. Quiet sales since Dec 2025: 73 deals, average about 80,000 ₪ per sqm and 8.7M ₪ per flat, over 60% foreign buyers (nadlancenter 25.3.2026).
2. **EINSTEIN 2.4.6.8**: Chen & Itai Gindi + Shapir. Einstein 2-8, New Lamed. 380 units in 5 buildings up to 23 floors, in marketing, occupancy March 2030 per yad2.
3. **איינשטיין 15**: Gindi + Shapir. 180 units, 30-floor tower + 12-floor boutique, under construction, occupancy Dec 2028 per yad2. Registered July-2026 deals: 4 rooms 118 sqm 7.9M ₪; penthouse about 34.6M ₪.
4. **הפילהרמונית**: Yossi Avrahami. Shai Agnon 28, Kochav HaTzafon, 2 luxury buildings with a private spa. 5 rooms from 7,390,000 ₪ (developer page, undated).
5. **מגרש 105**: About 200 private landowners, Barkat 1.15B ₪ financing. 372 units + 65-room hotel, 38-floor tower, excavation under way (nadlancenter 29.3.2026). No marketing name yet.
6. **ברודצקי צפון**: ICR (Israel Canada + ORC). Brodetsky 33-39, Ramat Aviv. 166 units, 2-6 rooms. From 6,400,000 ₪ (yad2 per 25.10 seed) and from 55,000 ₪ per sqm (nadlancenter 13.5.2026).
7. **יהודה עמיחי 5 / אייזק שטרן 18 + אייזק שטרן 22**: The owner's small streets in New Lamed. Amichai 5: Eshkol Megurim, 62 units, 13 floors (madlan snippet). Stern 22: 23-unit boutique, under construction in 2025 photos.
8. **RAMATA רדינג 22 + אביב ברדינג 31-33**: Reading corridor by the Port. RAMATA: Metropolis, 81 units, presale from 5,700,000 ₪ (yad2 25.10.2026). Aviv BaReading: 84 units, 2 rooms from 3,585,000 ₪ (magdilim 7.7.2025).
9. **הרצליה VILLE**: Azorim. 1,843 units in six complexes with a 10-dunam park, in marketing. No published price.
10. **פיתוח על הים + ריץ קרלטון**: Herzliya Pituach seafront (Ofer Investments, under construction) and the Ritz-Carlton residences in the Marina (Tidhar, 82 holiday residences). Luxury; no units or prices published for Pituach by the Sea.

**Next Sde Dov launches to watch** (land won in the 2025 tenders, design plans approved in 2026): Luzon Ronson (340 units, 'Coming Soon' 19.5.2026), Achim Israel (424), Aviv Melisron 'Aviv BaSde Dov' (320, 40-floor tower), Dimri north plot 3210 (275), Tshuva's Tashluz (177), Abu + Kuko (347). **Also strong in the Einstein zone:** ESSENCE Beit Tzuri 22 (Shikun & Binui, 111 units, presale from 4,350,000 ₪), Ahimeir 5-9 (Acro + Bait VeGag, 165 units), AFK Padova (Shikun & Binui + Metropolis, 292 units, from 3,399,000 ₪, Neot Afeka edge). **Herzliya also:** Gindi LOVE Glil Yam (4 rooms 100 sqm 4.19M ₪, ICE 31.3.2026), Tidhar Gefen (400 units).

## סתירות מול הדפים שלנו / Facts on our pages that sources contradict

Checked against the live pages on 1.11.2026 (raw HTML).

- **Rainbow:** the Rainbow page itself is now consistent (459 units in text and in schema). But the neighbour box on **zohi-sde-dov** still lists Rainbow as **480 units**. Sources: 459 units (Ashtrom contract, nadlancenter 13606, 1.1.2026; Israel Canada site). 480 was the 2021 Israel Land Authority tender figure.
- **Dimri Yama floors:** our page says a 40-floor tower; the zohi neighbour box says 39; Dimri's own page gives 8-38 floors (25.10 seed); July-2024 news said 39 + 16 + two 8-9 floor buildings. Units (458) agree everywhere.
- **Ashira:** our page says 'two 8-floor buildings' in one place and 'two 9-floor buildings' in another. The permit report (nadlancenter 14110, 12.3.2026) says 35, 16 and two 8-floor buildings.
- **Einstein Tower:** our page shows 215 units and a 28-floor tower with two 13-floor buildings (Hagag's page). nadlancenter 10799 (15.10.2024) describes the whole Einstein 33-35 compound as about 515 units, with towers of 30 and 28 floors and four 14-floor buildings; nadlancenter 9639 calls the 215-unit permit a 30-floor tower. Location also differs: our page and Hagag say the corner of Einstein and Levi Eshkol, nadlancenter says the western end of Einstein at the corner of Ibn Gabirol.
- **Utopia:** the neighbour boxes on einstein-19 and zohi-sde-dov label Utopia 'בהיתר' (permit). Nahmias began construction in October 2025 (nadlancenter 13109).
- **Shikun & Binui Sde Dov:** our page covers plot 109 only (324 rental units). The same company is building a second Sde Dov rental project, 'Sde Dov B', with 511 units (nadlancenter 15637, 22.10.2026). This is a gap, not an error.
- **Gindi Vogue:** our page gives 8 / 20 / 44 floors; the 25.10 seed (developer data) gives 9-44. Minor.
- **Recanati Residence:** our title places it in Neve Avivim; yad2 and the seed say Ramat Aviv Gimel (project-tlv: Recanati 5-15). Units (198) agree.
