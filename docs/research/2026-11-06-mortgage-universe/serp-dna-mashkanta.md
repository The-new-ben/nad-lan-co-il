**Reading.** Google splits the two spellings into two different jobs. "משכנתא" is read as a *money* query: it answers with an AI
summary, bank pages and an ad. "משכנתה" is read as a *legal object* (the right in land): it answers with the encyclopedia entity, the
Land Registry service (gov.il "בקשה לרישום משכנתה" is #7 for "משכנתה" and #52 for "משכנתא") and local professionals, and the
refinements go to registration and fees. A page that wants both must speak both languages: the money (payments, rates, tools) and the
legal object (lien, registration, fees, cancellation), and must name the spelling the way Wikipedia does.

## 3. The rules Google is applying here (what the evidence supports)

1. **Two intents share one word.** About 6 of 10 page-one slots go to lenders (navigational and transactional). The informational
   slots are few and go to the most authoritative, *dated* explainers: Kol Zchut, BoI, Wikipedia. To enter page one a non-bank page
   must be the best of those: rights + official data + tools, with a visible update date.
2. **Dates are shown only for pages that carry them.** Kol Zchut, BoI and news get a date in the snippet; banks do not. Our page has
   "עודכן לאחרונה" in the byline at the end and `dateModified`; Google shows the date it can read, so the date must also be near the top
   in a sentence ("נכון ל־...").
3. **Definition sentences are what the AI quotes.** The quoted sentences share one shape: "משכנתא היא הלוואה ... לצורך ...; הנכס משועבד
   ...". The first paragraph under H1 on our page must contain one clean definition sentence of that shape with both spellings, and
   then a number with a date.
4. **The refinement graph tells us the sub-topics Google expects on the hub:** calculators (by bank), purpose (any purpose, existing
   property, first or second apartment), "מי יכול לקחת משכנתא", and for the legal spelling: registration, the fee, the request form, the
   amendment ("תיקון משכנתה"), "משכנתה או משכנתא".
5. **Freshness of rules is live news this autumn.** Three of the top 50 are news about new BoI rules (October and November 2026). A
   section that states the 2026 rule changes with dates and the official source is both a ranking and an AI-citation asset.
6. **The AI summary is wrong on the payment cap.** Pages that state the correct rule in a short quotable sentence, with the directive
   number and date, are the candidates to replace the wrong source.
7. **Local pack for "משכנתה":** Google sees mortgage brokers as the "local" answer. We do not compete there (the site never presents
   itself as a broker or adviser).
