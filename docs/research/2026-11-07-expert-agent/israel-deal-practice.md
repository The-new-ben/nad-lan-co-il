# Israel deal practice: what a veteran real-estate professional knows

Research input for the nad-lan.co.il AI advisor. nad-lan.co.il is legally a **publisher**, not a broker. This file maps the deal the way a 40-year veteran works it, with Israeli documents, offices, numbers, timelines and traps, and then draws the line between what a publisher's AI may do and what only licensed professionals may do.

- Compiled: 7.11.2026, web research only. No DataForSEO, no paid APIs, no code changes.
- All retrievals: 7.11.2026, 18:54 to 19:12 Israel time. Each source's approximate time is in the source register at the end.
- Evidence tags used below:
  - **[P]** primary source: law text (Wikisource copy of the statute), regulator document (Bank of Israel, Registrar of Brokers, State Comptroller), municipal guide, or Kol Zchut (which cites the law and shows a "last updated" date).
  - **[S]** secondary source: press, portal blog or law-firm summary.
  - **[practice]** customary professional practice. It is not a legal rule, and no single source in this file proves it.
  - **[NV]** not verified. I could not confirm it from a primary source. Do not show it to users as fact.
- Numbers change. The AI must show the "valid as of" date next to every bracket and cap, and point to the official calculator or source.

## 1. Buyer of a second-hand apartment (דירה יד שנייה)

### 1.1 What the veteran does, step by step

**A. Budget and equity, before looking at any apartment**
- [ ] Set the cash the buyer really has. The mortgage covers at most a share of the price (or the appraisal, whichever is lower), and the rest is equity. [P] Bank of Israel directive 329, section 2, caps the financing share (שיעור מימון, LTV) at:
  - **75%** for a דירה יחידה (single home);
  - **70%** for a דירה חליפית (a replacement home, where the buyer will sell the old one);
  - **50%** for a דירה להשקעה (any other home).
- [ ] Check affordability. [P] Directive 329 bars a monthly repayment above **50%** of disposable income (s.5). Loans above **40%** carry a heavier capital charge for the bank (s.6), so in practice banks get stricter above 40%. The variable-rate part may not exceed **66.66%** of the loan (s.7). The maximum term is **30 years** (s.8).
- [ ] Add the costs that come on top of the price. 2026 ranges from Bizportal's guide [S]:
  - lawyer: **0.5% to 1% + VAT**, usually with a minimum fee;
  - broker: customary **2% + VAT**, negotiable, and many deals close at **1% to 1.5%**;
  - private appraiser: **1,500 to 3,500 ₪**;
  - bank appraisal: **350 to 950 ₪**;
  - home inspection (בדק בית): **1,500 to 3,500 ₪**, depending on size;
  - mortgage file fee: 0.25% capped at 360 ₪ [S, NV against BoI].
  - VAT is 18% [S].
- [ ] Purchase tax: see 1.1-H.

**B. Mortgage pre-approval (אישור עקרוני)**
- [ ] Get an אישור עקרוני for the specific property early, from more than one bank. [P] Directive 451, s.4ג (the version text on the BoI site) says the bank must hold the quoted rate for at least **24 days** from the approval (12 days when no new lien is needed), and must state that period prominently. [NV whether the current version changed this.]
- [ ] Reuse a valuation where possible. [P] Directive 451, s.9(ה): a bank may not unreasonably refuse an appraisal made for another bank, if the appraiser is on its list and the valuation is less than **90 days** old.
- [ ] Expect paperwork. [P] To classify the home as single or replacement, the bank needs a lawyer-certified affidavit and a copy of the buyer's declaration to the Tax Authority, or the purchase-tax assessment notice (329 s.3).

**C. Title: who really owns it, and what is registered against it**
- [ ] Pull a fresh נסח טאבו (registry extract) from the Ministry of Justice online service (gov.il "land_registration_extract"). [S] You need the גוש / חלקה / תת-חלקה, and the sub-parcel number usually differs from the apartment number. [P] The Tel Aviv municipal guide says the extract should be no more than **6 months** old. [practice] Veterans pull another one on signing day and again before each payment.
  - The extract fee was not verified. An old 2016 report gave 33 ₪ for a condominium file [S, NV for 2026]. Madlan warns that third-party sites charge more for the same service [S].
- [ ] Read the extract line by line [P, Tel Aviv guide]:
  - registered owners: all of them must sign;
  - mortgages (משכנתאות) and attachments (עיקולים);
  - הערות אזהרה (warning notes) in favour of anyone else;
  - expropriation or demolition notes;
  - תקנון בית משותף (the condominium by-law) and הצמדות (parking, storage, roof);
  - notes under the Planning and Building Law.
- [ ] If the home is not registered in Tabu, get an **אישור זכויות** from רמ"י (Israel Land Authority) and/or the **חברה משכנת** (the housing company that built it, such as Amidar or Shikun uFituach), and follow the company's transfer-of-rights procedure [S]. [P, gov.il new-buyer guide] Also check whether the selling company is on רמ"י's list of companies that refuse to register (סרבני רישום).

**D. Building, permits and planning (the municipality)**
- [ ] Open the **תיק בניין** (building file) at the municipal engineering archive [P, Tel Aviv guide]:
  - the building permit (היתר בנייה);
  - the completion certificate (תעודת גמר) or occupancy permit (טופס 4);
  - any legal proceedings.
  - Not every document is in the file.
- [ ] Compare the permit plans to what is actually built: closed balconies, added rooms, a split apartment, roof additions. [S] These חריגות בנייה (building deviations) can mean demolition orders, fines, municipal enforcement files and appraisal problems with the bank.
- [ ] Check zoning and building rights (תב"ע) by גוש/חלקה, not only by address [S]:
  - use the Planning Administration systems (מנהל התכנון: iplan / מבא"ת) and the municipal GIS;
  - read both the plan regulations (תקנון) and the map (תשריט).
  - [P, Tel Aviv] A building information file (תיק מידע) is valid for **one year**.
- [ ] Check היטל השבחה (betterment levy). [P, Tel Aviv] Ask the municipality for an official answer. Payment is a condition for the Tabu transfer. [P, Kol Zchut] The levy is normally **half** of the betterment value. It is charged on the owner and usually collected at sale.
- [ ] Check municipal debts, such as arnona, and get the municipal certificate for the Tabu transfer (אישור לטאבו) [P, Tel Aviv].
- [ ] Look at planning around the building: new roads, towers, urban-renewal plans. These cut both ways for price and quality of life [practice].

**E. The building and the apartment**
- [ ] Building committee (ועד בית). [P, Kol Zchut, Land Law ss.58, 62] Every owner pays in proportion to floor area, and the general assembly sets fees by majority. [practice] Ask the committee about arrears and about planned special assessments (roof, elevator, facade, waterproofing). Whether a seller's committee debt passes to the buyer was **NV**.
- [ ] Hire an engineer for a home inspection **before** signing, and price the repairs into the offer [S].
- [ ] Use a private appraiser when the price is in doubt, and expect the bank's own appraisal [S].
- [ ] Check comparables yourself (see section 3 for the data caveats).

**F. Lawyer before any signature**
- [ ] Hire the buyer's own real-estate lawyer **before** signing anything, including a זיכרון דברים (memorandum of agreement) [S].
  - A זיכרון דברים can be a binding contract. It can support registering a warning note, and breaching it can cost damages [S].
  - [P] The broker ethics regulations bar brokers from drafting offers or memoranda [S summary of the regulations; see 8.2].

**G. Contract, payments and security**
- [ ] Register a **הערת אזהרה** (warning note) for the buyer right after signing, before or against the first payment [S].
- [ ] A typical schedule [S, a norm, not a rule]:
  - **5% to 15%** at signing, against the warning note;
  - middle payments, mostly from the mortgage;
  - **15% to 20%** on key handover.
- [ ] Liquidated damages (פיצוי מוסכם) of about **10%** of the price are common, and courts may reduce them [S].
- [ ] Where the seller has a mortgage, route the payments to the seller's bank against its undertaking to release the lien [practice, NV].
- [ ] **Trap: the advance on the seller's מס שבח.** [P, Kol Zchut] In a cash deal that is not exempt, a buyer who pays more than **40%** of the price to a private seller (or more than **80%** to a contractor) must pay an advance on the seller's tax:
  - **7.5%** of the price;
  - or **15%** if the seller bought before 7.11.2001.
  - It is due within **30 days** of signing, or when the threshold is crossed, whichever is later.
  - It does not apply when an exemption is requested.
  - Lawyers handle this, but the AI must flag it.

**H. Purchase tax (מס רכישה)** [P, Kol Zchut, page updated 4.11.2026]

Single apartment (דירה יחידה), valid **16.1.2024 to 15.1.2028** (the brackets are frozen):

| Portion of the price | Rate |
|---|---|
| up to 1,978,745 ₪ | 0% |
| 1,978,745 to 2,347,040 ₪ | 3.5% |
| 2,347,040 to 6,055,070 ₪ | 5% |
| 6,055,070 to 20,183,565 ₪ | 8% |
| above 20,183,565 ₪ | 10% |

Additional apartment (דירה נוספת), and any buyer who is not an Israeli-resident individual, valid **16.1.2024 to 10.2.2027**:

| Portion of the price | Rate |
|---|---|
| up to 6,055,070 ₪ | 8% |
| above 6,055,070 ₪ | 10% |

- The calculation is layered: each rate applies only to the slice of the price inside its bracket.
- Upgraders (משפרי דיור) count as single-apartment buyers if they sell the old home in time [P, Kol Zchut]:
  - generally within **24 months**;
  - **18 months** for replacements bought 1.6.2023 to 31.5.2025;
  - for a new home from a developer, **12 months** after delivery;
  - war extensions of **27 months** for some purchase windows (for example 1.6.2025 to 30.5.2026).
  - Missing the window means paying the difference plus linkage and interest.
- Deadlines:
  - The buyer and the seller each file a declaration within **30 days** of the deal [P, Kol Zchut]. A lawyer files it through שבח.נט, and filing is online only.
  - One secondary source says 50 days [S]. Follow the Kol Zchut / Tax Authority figure.
  - The purchase-tax payment deadline in days was **NV**.

**I. Negotiation and the market, autumn 2026**
- [ ] Price from closed deals, not from asking prices.
- [ ] 2026 data:
  - [S, Madlan via ice.co.il, 17.10.2026] The average negotiated discount on second-hand homes is **4.8%** (2.8% in 2022), and only **8.4%** sold at the asking price (26% in 2022). Madlan's CEO calls 2026 "a buyer's market".
  - [S, CBS via nadlancenter, 15.10.2026] The housing price index fell **0.6%** (July–Aug vs June–July 2026) and **1.8%** year on year. Government-subsidised deals were **45.8%** of new-home sales.
  - [S] Bank of Israel policy rate: **3.25%**, held at the October 2026 decision (summary published 4.11.2026) [NV directly].

### 1.2 Traps the veteran watches for
- Signing a זיכרון דברים "just to hold the apartment" before any checks.
- An extract that shows a third party's warning note, an attachment, a mortgage with no clear release path, or co-owners who are not signing (an inheritance not yet registered, a divorcing couple).
- A home not registered in Tabu, where the transfer depends on the חברה משכנת or רמ"י procedure and on a missing link in the chain of rights.
- Building deviations that the bank's appraiser excludes from value, or that trigger enforcement after the purchase.
- An unknown היטל השבחה, or municipal debts that block the Tabu transfer.
- The buyer forgets the advance on the seller's מס שבח (7.5% or 15%).
- An upgrader who misses the 18/24/12-month sale window and pays additional-apartment purchase tax.
- An expired אישור עקרוני rate, or a bank appraisal below the price, which shrinks the mortgage, so the equity gap appears at the last minute.
- Listing figures (rooms, m²) that do not match the permit or Tabu. [P] The ethics regulations require brokers to give the size and its source.

## 2. Buyer from a developer (דירה מקבלן / על הנייר)

### 2.1 What the veteran does
- [ ] **Security for every shekel.** [P, Kol Zchut, Sale (Apartments) (Assurance of Investments) Law 1974, s.2]
  - A developer may not take more than **7%** of the price unless the buyer's money is secured by one of these:
    - a bank guarantee (ערבות חוק מכר);
    - an insurance policy;
    - a first-ranking mortgage or lien;
    - a warning note;
    - transfer of ownership.
  - The guarantee covers everything paid, and the protection is automatic.
  - [practice] Pay only through the payment vouchers (שוברי תשלום) of the project's accompanying bank (ליווי בנקאי), and collect the guarantee for each payment.
- [ ] **Payment pace.** [P, Kol Zchut] If the security is a warning note, lien or ownership transfer (not a bank guarantee or insurance), contracts from 16.5.2019 may not demand more than these cumulative stages:

| Stage | Cumulative maximum |
|---|---|
| lowest level of the building done (includes the signing advance) | 31% |
| frame up to and including the apartment's floor | 56% |
| frame of the whole building | 61% |
| exterior cladding | 67% |
| plaster and flooring in the apartment | 77% |
| interior finishes, windows, doors | 90% |
| shared systems and the yard needed for טופס 4 | 95% |
| occupancy permit and delivery | 100% |

- [ ] **The 20/80 and 10/90 offers.** The buyer pays a little now and most at delivery, often with a developer-subsidised bullet or balloon loan.
  - [P] Directive 329 s.8א caps contractor-subsidised bullet/balloon loans at **10%** of each bank's quarterly housing-loan executions, valid until **31.12.2026**.
  - [S, Ynet] Developers are cutting prices without saying so, for example through consumer clubs.
  - [practice] Price the subsidy as a discount, and stress-test whether you can close the 80% at delivery if the value or rates move.
- [ ] **The מפרט (specification).** [P, Sale (Apartments) Law 1973]
  - It must be attached and signed at contract signing (s.2), and every item must be filled in or marked not applicable (s.3).
  - Any change needs both parties' signatures.
  - A missing spec means the developer is deemed to have promised high-quality finishes (s.5).
  - The buyer may waive, add or move priced items by notice before installation, no earlier than 6 months after the foundations are finished, unless set otherwise (s.3(ה)).
- [ ] **Linkage to the construction input index (מדד תשומות הבנייה).** [P, s.5ג]
  - At most **half of each payment** may be linked.
  - The **20%** paid at signing (or first) may **not** be linked.
  - Linkage stops at the contractual delivery date.
- [ ] **Late delivery.** [P, s.5א, contracts from 7.7.2022] The buyer gets compensation without proving damage once the apartment is late by more than **1 month**:
  - **1x** the rent of a comparable apartment for months 1–4;
  - **1.25x** for months 5–10;
  - **1.5x** from month 11;
  - paid monthly.
  - Older contracts: a **60-day** grace period, then **1.5x** for the first 8 months and **1.25x** after.
  - Exempt only if the buyer alone caused the delay, or in a section 18(a) Remedies-Law situation.
- [ ] **Defects (בדק).** [P, s.4 and the Schedule] The בדק (warranty) period runs from delivery:
  - **1 year**: general;
  - **2 years**: joinery, flooring, interior cladding;
  - **3 years**: machines and boilers, the yard, thermal insulation;
  - **4 years**: piping, sealing and waterproofing;
  - **5 years**: cracks wider than 1.5 mm in non-load-bearing parts;
  - **7 years**: exterior cladding coming loose.
  - After the בדק period comes a **3-year** liability period, and fundamental structural defects are covered for **20 years**.
  - Notify visible defects within **1 year** of delivery (s.4א).
  - The developer has the right to repair (s.4ב). If a defect recurs within 2 years after repair, the buyer may fix it at the developer's cost.
  - [practice] Do a professional inspection at handover and send the report in writing.
- [ ] **Lawyer fees the developer may charge.** [P, Kol Zchut, updated 3.2.2026, Sale (Apartments) Law s.6ג]
  - The lesser of **5,915 ₪ (2026)** or **0.5%** of the price, plus VAT.
  - This applies when the price is **up to 4,642,750 ₪**.
  - Plus up to **300 ₪** for maintaining the rights list.
  - The buyer pays the lawyer directly, against a tax invoice.
- [ ] **דירה בהנחה (מחיר למשתכן / מחיר מטרה / מחיר מופחת).** [P, Kol Zchut, updated 21.10.2026]
  - Eligibility: people without a home (חסרי דירה) and some upgraders; couples, parents, singles aged 35+, some disabled singles aged 21+.
  - Process: an eligibility certificate (valid 1 year), registration open at least 8 days, the lottery, then choosing an apartment within 45 minutes. A 2,000 ₪ deposit may be required.
  - Resale is restricted for **5 years from טופס 4 or 7 years from winning**, whichever ends first.
  - Mortgage rules [P, directive 329 s.4א]:
    - the appraisal used for the loan is capped at **2.1M ₪**;
    - minimum own funds: **60,000 ₪** where the periphery grant applies, **100,000 ₪** otherwise.
- [ ] **Check the developer** [P, gov.il new-buyer guide]:
  - the permit has been issued (do not pay beyond the legal limit without it);
  - the registry extract of the land and the marketer's authority;
  - the bank accompaniment and the security mechanism;
  - the developer is not on רמ"י's registration-refusers list.
  - [practice] Also check the contractor's registration and classification, the developer's delivery track record, and buyer complaints. [P, Kol Zchut] Complaints go to the Registrar of Contractors (רשם הקבלנים).

### 2.2 Traps
- Paying "a small reservation fee" without any security. More than 7% unsecured is illegal.
- A spec with blank items or "or equivalent" everywhere. A spec signed separately from the contract.
- Linkage clauses that index the 20% signing payment, or that keep indexing past the delivery date.
- A 20/80 deal where the buyer cannot close the 80% because rates, value or income changed.
- Accepting an "up to 60 days grace plus force majeure" clause that does not fit the law for post-7.7.2022 contracts.
- Missing the 1-year notice for visible defects.
- A developer lawyer's fee above the cap.
- An upgrader on a new home who misses the 12-month sale window.

## 3. Seller

### 3.1 What the veteran does
- [ ] **Price from closed deals.**
  - Use the Tax Authority's real-estate information (nadlan.gov.il, built on the "Karmen" file) and Govmap, plus Madlan and Yad2 transaction views [S].
  - Search by address or גוש/חלקה, and filter by rooms, area and year built [S].
  - **Data caveat** [P, State Comptroller, October 2025]:
    - **903,858** of the **1,837,382** condominium apartments registered in Tabu do not appear in the Karmen file;
    - the input system accepts implausible values, and many records carry zero area, zero rooms or zero year built.
    - So comparables must be cleaned and checked by hand, never taken raw.
  - Automated estimates (for example Yad2's "yadata") describe themselves as estimates that do not replace a licensed appraiser [S]. Appraisers have complained to consumer protection about gaps between these estimates and market value [S, NV which outlet].
- [ ] **Read the market before listing.** [S, 2026] Discounts average 4.8% and few homes sell at the asking price. [practice] Price close to real comparables, and plan the negotiation room rather than over-anchoring and then making visible price cuts.
- [ ] **Prepare the file before the first buyer visit** [practice, built on the buyer checks in section 1]:
  - a fresh נסח (or the חברה משכנת / רמ"י rights confirmation);
  - the chain of ownership (inheritance orders, divorce agreements);
  - the permit and תעודת גמר, and a record of any deviations;
  - the mortgage payoff letter;
  - committee and arnona status;
  - the היטל השבחה position;
  - the municipal certificate.
  - [S] Since 2018, registering a deal whose right came from רמ"י or a חברה משכנת no longer needs tax approvals once those approvals were shown to the registering body. [NV primary]
- [ ] **מס שבח (capital gains on land).**
  - The basic rate is 25% of the real gain [S].
  - The single-apartment exemption [P, Kol Zchut, updated 26.7.2026]:
    - the qualifying home must be the seller's only home;
    - it must have been owned for at least **18 months** (counted from טופס 4 if bought on paper);
    - a spouse's or minor child's other apartment disqualifies;
    - the exemption ceiling is **5,008,000 ₪** of sale value (2024–2027), and any value above it is taxed.
  - A **non-resident** seller can get the exemption with a certificate from the home-country tax authority that they own no home there [P].
- [ ] **היטל השבחה.** The levy is about half of the betterment from plans or approvals after 1.7.1975. It is charged on the owner and usually collected at sale. Its payment is a condition for registration [P].
- [ ] **Brokers.** [P, Kol Zchut "זכותון", updated 26.8.2026; Brokers Law s.9]
  - A broker earns a fee only with a signed written order.
  - Exclusivity on the sale of a dwelling is limited to **6 months**, and it ends if there is no marketing within **2 months**.
  - Unless agreed otherwise, the broker must perform at least **2 of 6** listed marketing actions.
  - Fees are negotiable, with no legal cap.
- [ ] **Timing.** Each side files its declaration within **30 days** [P]. [practice] Coordinate the sale date with your own next purchase. Upgraders have the 18/24-month purchase-tax window, and the bank's bridging loans are an option.

### 3.2 Traps
- Pricing from asking prices or a single automated estimate.
- Unregistered building additions that kill the buyer's mortgage.
- A missing inheritance order or co-owner signature.
- Assuming the 18-month exemption without checking a spouse's property or the 5,008,000 ₪ ceiling.
- Signing an open-ended exclusivity. The law caps it, but disputes still cost time.
- Getting caught between two deals when timing the sale against a new purchase.

## 4. Rentals (חוק השכירות והשאילה, the 2017 "fair rental" chapter)

### 4.1 When the protections apply
[P, Kol Zchut] All of the following must be true:
- the lease is longer than 3 months, or has an option beyond 3 months;
- it is up to 10 years long, or the landlord can end it earlier;
- the rent is up to **20,000 ₪/month**;
- the parties are not close relatives;
- the unit is not a hotel or vacation rental, a supervised facility (מעון), sheltered housing, a dormitory, public housing, or a home under the Tenant Protection Law.

### 4.2 What the veteran checks (both sides)
- [ ] **Guarantees.** [P, s.25י, Kol Zchut updated 12.8.2026]
  - Guarantees that cost the tenant money (bank guarantee, cash deposit, insurance) are capped at the lesser of **3 months' rent** or **one-third of the rent for the whole period**.
  - Guarantees that cost nothing (a security cheque, a promissory note, guarantors) are outside the cap.
  - The landlord returns the guarantee within **60 days** of the later of handover or full payment of debts.
- [ ] **Fit for living.** [P, s.25ו] The apartment must have:
  - drainage and sewage;
  - electricity and lighting;
  - ventilation and natural light, with closable openings and a locking entrance door;
  - drinking water;
  - a separated toilet;
  - no unreasonable safety or health risk.
- [ ] **Repairs.** [P, ss.9, 25ח]
  - The landlord fixes a defect within a reasonable time, at most **30 days**.
  - An urgent defect (one that prevents reasonable living) must be fixed within **3 days**.
  - If the landlord does not fix it, the tenant may repair and recover reasonable costs after notice, or reduce the rent in proportion.
  - Damage from unreasonable use, and minor wear items, fall on the tenant.
- [ ] **Payments.** [P, s.25ט]
  - The tenant pays the rent, arnona, water, electricity, gas and heating, and the **ongoing** ועד בית costs.
  - The landlord may not shift other payments onto the tenant.
- [ ] **Broker fee.** [P, s.25ט(ב)(3), Kol Zchut updated 28.7.2026] A landlord who hired the broker may not make the tenant pay that fee. If the tenant also contacted the broker or answered the broker's ad, the tenant pays only what the tenant agreed in writing.
- [ ] **Options and ending the lease.** [P, s.25יב]
  - A landlord's option: notify at least **90 days** before the end, and only if the extension terms were set in advance.
  - A tenant's option: notify at least **60 days** before the end.
  - The same 90/60 periods apply to unilateral early termination where the contract allows it.
- [ ] **Contract.** [P, Kol Zchut] The lease is in writing, signed, and each side gets a copy. It names the parties (with ID numbers), the address, the rent and the period. The landlord must disclose known material defects, or the tenant may cancel.
- [ ] **Landlord tax.** [P, Kol Zchut updated 22.2.2026]
  - Residential rent up to **5,654 ₪/month (2026)** is fully exempt, with a partial exemption up to 11,308 ₪.
  - Alternatives: a flat **10%** track, or the regular tax brackets.
- [ ] [P] Fast-track eviction (פינוי מהיר) exists for tenants who stay after the lease ends. Kol Zchut has a procedure page, whose details were not reviewed here.

### 4.3 Traps
- **Landlord traps:**
  - demanding a 6-month cash deposit, which is illegal inside the scope;
  - billing the tenant for building insurance or capital repairs;
  - not returning the deposit within 60 days;
  - an option clause with no preset terms.
- **Tenant traps:**
  - paying the landlord's broker;
  - waiving repair rights in the contract (void inside the scope);
  - missing the 60-day option notice;
  - no move-in condition report (photos and a list) [practice].

## 5. Urban renewal (התחדשות עירונית)

### 5.1 State of play, November 2026
- [S, nadlancenter 10.6.2026] **תמ"א 38 expired nationwide on 18.5.2026.** No new permit applications can be filed under it.
  - Replacement plans are approved in **8** planning areas: Ramla, Yavne, Ra'anana, Hadera, Kfar Saba, Petah Tikva, Be'er Sheva and Givatayim.
  - Netanya's and Ashdod's plans are approved but not yet in force.
  - Tel Aviv, Jerusalem, Haifa, Ramat Gan and Rishon LeZion lack a building-level replacement plan. Tel Aviv's "Rovaim 3 and 4" plan operates separately.
  - Elsewhere the route is חלופת שקד (amendment 139: a plan prepared together with the permit), whose track record is still unproven.
  - [NV against a Planning Administration primary source]
- Pinui-binui (פינוי בינוי, demolish and rebuild) continues under the Pinui-Binui (Compensation) Law and the 2016 Government Authority for Urban Renewal Law.

### 5.2 Tenant rights and the veteran's checklist
- [ ] **Majorities.** [P, Kol Zchut] A pinui-binui deal needs:
  - owners of at least **two-thirds** of the apartments in the compound;
  - at least **60%** of owners in each building;
  - more than half of each building's common property.
  - An owner who refuses unreasonably may be sued for damages.
  - Reasonable grounds to refuse include: the deal is not economically fair, no alternative housing during construction, or no adequate security.
- [ ] **Elderly owners.** [P] Owners aged 70+ get a special replacement option (תמורה ייחודית), with stronger rights at 75+. They may also get a maintenance-fee subsidy for 5 years.
- [ ] **Tax.** [P, Kol Zchut] In pinui-binui, owners are generally exempt from מס שבח, היטל השבחה and מס רכישה within value limits, and the developer bears these.
- [ ] **Disclosure before signing.** [P, Kol Zchut Q&A, updated 29.1.2024]
  - The developer must convene an owners' assembly.
  - It must hand over a disclosure document at least **2 weeks** before signing, covering the deal, the developer, its obligations, expected future costs and the offer's expiry date.
  - A deal signed in a language the owner does not understand, with no summary in a language they do understand, may be declared void.
  - The Government Authority's tenant-inquiries officer handles complaints against organizers (מארגן): breach of fiduciary duty, unfairness, non-disclosure. The officer can confirm that an organization agreement has expired.
  - The Authority can appoint an appraiser at the owners' request.
  - Arnona discount: up to 5 years, for owners with at least 3 years' ownership and income within limits.
- [ ] **Security to demand** [S, a law-firm guide; practice]:
  - a separate lawyer for the tenants, paid by the developer;
  - a ערבות חוק מכר for the value of each new apartment;
  - a rent guarantee for the construction period;
  - a tax guarantee and a defects (בדק) guarantee;
  - clear timelines with exit points: if no majority or no permit by a set date, the deal lapses.
- [ ] **Check the developer** [practice]:
  - completed renewal projects and how long they took;
  - financing capacity and the accompanying bank;
  - contractor classification;
  - litigation history;
  - how many signatures it really holds versus how many it claims.

### 5.3 Traps
- Signing an exclusive organizer agreement with no deadlines.
- Accepting the developer's lawyer as "your" lawyer.
- No guarantee for rent during construction.
- Assuming TAMA 38 still applies in your city.
- Building additions by a refusing owner that are counted toward the majority. [S] The 2021 amendment allows a court to exclude illegal construction from the majority count.

## 6. Buyers from abroad (תושבי חוץ)

### 6.1 What the veteran does
- [ ] **Mortgage.** [P, directive 329]
  - "דירה יחידה" is defined only for a purchase by an Israeli citizen ("אזרח ישראלי" as defined in s.16א(א) of the Land Taxation Law), and a תושב חוץ is anyone who is not an Israeli citizen.
  - So a foreign resident's purchase is financed at most **50%** [P on the definitions; S, BoI FAQ via search, on the 50% cap].
  - Some secondary sites claim banks can lend up to 70% to foreigners [S]. That conflicts with the directive, so treat **50% as the cap** [NV for edge cases].
  - Housing loans in foreign currency, or linked to it, given to a foreign resident may be exempted from the two-thirds variable-rate limit, within the bank's quarterly aggregate (329 s.12.3) [P].
  - The exact reach of the s.16א(א) definition, for example people entitled under the Law of Return, is **NV**.
- [ ] **Purchase tax.** [P, Kol Zchut updated 4.11.2026]
  - A buyer who is not an Israeli-resident individual pays the **additional-apartment rates** (8% up to 6,055,070 ₪, 10% above, valid to 10.2.2027), even with no other home anywhere [S].
  - Exception: the single-apartment brackets apply if the buyer becomes an Israeli resident (or a returning resident) within **2 years** of purchase, or **27 months** for purchases between 28.2.2024 and 30.5.2026.
  - Form 7912 (residence declaration) is mentioned by a secondary source [S, NV].
- [ ] **Representation** [S]:
  - a **property-specific, deal-specific** power of attorney;
  - signed before a notary abroad and apostilled (and translated), or signed at an Israeli consulate;
  - the original is couriered to Israel;
  - banks often require the foreign signature to be verified by apostille or consul.
  - The Israeli lawyer signs the contract, pays the tax, registers the rights and opens bank accounts.
- [ ] **Currency** [practice]:
  - decide when to convert;
  - consider a foreign-currency or foreign-linked loan against the risk of the shekel weakening or strengthening;
  - the deal is priced in shekels.
  - Bank source-of-funds and anti-money-laundering checks are **NV** in this research, so expect documentation requests.
- [ ] **Selling later.** The single-apartment exemption from מס שבח needs a home-country certificate of owning no home there (see section 3).
- [ ] **Off-plan from abroad.** Buyers often commit to a new build 2–4 years before delivery, so check the security for every payment (section 2) [S].

### 6.2 Traps
- Assuming the 75% "first home" LTV applies.
- Assuming single-apartment purchase tax because "it's my only home".
- A general power of attorney that the bank or Tabu rejects.
- A PoA that is not valid until the original arrives in Israel.
- Exchange-rate moves between signing and the last payment.

## 7. Negotiation and the soft skills of a 40-year veteran

The legal duties below are sourced. The behaviours marked [practice] are customary professional practice. They are good skill content for the advisor, but they are not facts to show users as rules.

### 7.1 Duties a professional owes the client
[P/S, the ethics regulations as summarised by the Registrar's announcement and nadlancenter 6.3.2025; Kol Zchut זכותון]
- Explain the deal in plain language.
- Give the true size, with its source.
- Disclose known material defects, and say if the home is not fit to live in.
- Hand over the owner's questionnaire on defects, disputes and nuisances, and say so if the owner refused to answer.
- Pass on **every** offer.
- Disclose any personal interest, third-party payment or dual representation, and get written consent.
- Keep information confidential.
- Never tie the service to another product.
- Collect nothing but the lawful brokerage fee: no "seriousness deposits" (דמי רצינות) and no advance payments.

### 7.2 Reading the market [practice, sources for the data]
- Follow the CBS housing price index (bi-monthly, released with the consumer price index): the July–Aug 2026 figure was −0.6% for the month and −1.8% for the year [S].
- Follow the subsidised share of new-home sales (45.8%) [S], Bank of Israel monthly mortgage volumes and rate decisions [P site], and portal data on discounts and price cuts [S].
- Read the micro-market: deals in the same building and street in the last 12–24 months, days on market, and how many similar listings are competing.
- Know the planning pipeline (urban renewal, transport) street by street.

### 7.3 Behaviours [practice]
- **Qualify before showing.** Proof of equity and an אישור עקרוני come before property tours.
- **Ask the seller why they are selling, and what timeline they really need.** Motivation sets the room for negotiation.
- **Anchor on evidence.** Open with cleaned comparables, the inspection costs and the deviations found, never with "what you can afford".
- **Put agreements in writing**, and send everything to the client's lawyer before any signature.
- **No pressure to sign.** A veteran never pushes a זיכרון דברים "before someone else takes it".
- **Tell clients early what they do not want to hear**: price too high, a building problem, an unfinanceable deal. This is the reputation-preserving move.
- **Keep emotion out of the room.** Use the waiting time between offers, and be ready to walk away.
- **Know the building's story**: the committee, the neighbours, past disputes, and planned special assessments.
- **Timing.** Developers push at the end of quarters and campaigns. Second-hand sellers soften after weeks without offers. Holidays and wartime alerts slow deals. These timing points are [NV as quantified facts].

### 7.4 Honesty norms the advisor should copy
- Never invent price, inventory, view, direction or dates.
- Unknown means "not known", and the item is omitted rather than guessed.
- Always separate "what the law says" from "what is customary" from "my estimate".

## 8. Legal frame for the AI advisor

### 8.1 Brokers Law (חוק המתווכים במקרקעין, התשנ"ו-1996)
[P, Wikisource copy of the statute]
- **s.1** "Brokering in real estate" (תיווך במקרקעין) means bringing two or more parties together **for consideration** (בתמורה) so they enter a deal over a right in land.
  - "Client" (לקוח) means a party to an agreement with a broker for the broker's services, whether or not they pay a fee.
- **s.2(א)** No one may broker without a valid licence.
- **s.2(ב)** No one may advertise as a broker, present as a broker, or use a title that suggests being one, without a licence.
- **s.2(ג), the publisher exemption.** The Act does not apply to someone who publishes on TV, radio, in a newspaper or "בכל דרך אחרת" (in any other way) information and details about people interested in real-estate deals, **provided they do not present themselves as a broker**.
- **s.8** Duties of loyalty, fairness and customary conduct. The broker must pass on all material information and take reasonable steps to obtain it.
- **s.9** A written order is required for any fee. It sets the exclusivity rules (dwellings: up to 6 months, and exclusivity ends if no marketing within a third of the period) and the required marketing actions.
- **s.10** A broker with a personal interest needs disclosure and the client's written consent.
- **s.11** Confidentiality.
- **s.14** A fee is due only if the broker was licensed, complied with s.9, and was the **effective cause** (הגורם היעיל) of a binding agreement.
- **s.15** Brokering without a licence is punishable by up to **1 year** in prison and a fine.

**What this means for nad-lan's AI:**
- Publishing listings and project information, and information about interested parties, is inside s.2(ג), **only as long as the site never presents itself or its AI as a broker**. No "our agent", "we will close the deal for you", or "your real-estate broker".
- **Risk points for counsel to review, not a conclusion:**
  - any revenue tied to a closed deal (a success fee or commission);
  - the AI negotiating on behalf of a user;
  - the AI actively matching a specific buyer with a specific seller, and being paid for that match.
  - Each of these moves toward "bringing parties together for consideration".
  - Advertising fees paid by developers for exposure are the classic publisher model [practice, NV legally].

### 8.2 Ethics regulations
[P, Registrar of Brokers announcement 9.3.2025]
- Official title: **תקנות המתווכים במקרקעין (אתיקה מקצועית), התשפ״ד-2024**, in force 9.3.2025.
- What they cover [S, nadlancenter 6.3.2025]:
  - **Advertising:** no acts that appear to come from the owner (ads, invitations) without the owner's consent; no showing a property known not to be for sale; every ad carries the broker's name and licence number.
  - **Disclosure:** the owner, the size and its source, defects, fitness to live in, and whether a receiver or court is involved.
  - **Money:** only the lawful brokerage fee.
  - **Professional boundaries:** brokers may not do appraisal, may not draft offer or memorandum documents, and may not provide legal or appraisal services in a deal they broker.
  - **Privacy:** no reuse of a prospect's details to pitch other properties without consent.
- **Lesson for the AI:** the regulator itself draws the line at drafting memoranda and appraising. A publisher's AI should stay further back than a licensed broker, not closer.

### 8.3 Lawyers and appraisers
- **Lawyers.** [P, the Chamber of Advocates Law]
  - Section 20 reserves these acts to lawyers when done "as a business, or for consideration even if not as a business":
    - legal advice and legal opinions;
    - drafting legal documents for another person, including the negotiation toward them;
    - representation before courts and before registries and offices such as the Land Registry and the tax authorities.
  - Section 96: a non-lawyer who does a reserved act is liable to a fine.
  - [S, Calcalist] The Bar's national ethics committee issued a stricter opinion: AI agents may not give independent legal advice or draft pleadings without close human supervision, and lawyers must check every output for hallucinations.
- **Appraisers.** [P, the Real Estate Appraisers Law 2001, s.13(a)] Valuing land as a paid service is reserved to registered appraisers.
  - Portal estimates are framed as "estimates, not a substitute for an appraiser" [S, the yadata disclaimer].

### 8.4 Privacy: Amendment 13 to the Privacy Protection Law
- Amendment 13 has been **in force since 14.8.2025** [S, several law-firm summaries].
- **s.11 notice at collection.** When collecting personal data (for example a lead form or a chat that asks for a name and phone number), tell the person:
  - whether there is a legal duty to give the data;
  - the purpose of collection;
  - who the data will be given to, and for what purposes;
  - the identity of the database owner [S, Gornitzky; E-LEGAL].
  - This is usually done with a checkbox or banner at the point of collection [S].
- **Enforcement.** The Privacy Protection Authority (הרשות להגנת הפרטיות) now has administrative enforcement and monetary-sanction powers, and is running sector sweeps [S, Shibolet].
- **AI-specific guidance.**
  - The PPA's **draft** guidance on applying the law to AI systems (published for comment around 28–30.4.2025) says:
    - personal data the AI creates by **inference or assessment** is personal data;
    - a privacy impact assessment before deployment is the desired path;
    - prohibited scraping of personal data is a "severe security incident";
    - access, correction and deletion rights will be enforced on AI systems.
  - As of this research it is still described as a draft [S, Chambers 2026 guide; NV on the PPA site].
  - The PPA also published a guide on privacy-enhancing technologies for AI (December 2025) [S].
- **Lesson for the AI:**
  - The chat is itself a collection point. Show the s.11 notice before asking for contact details.
  - Do not store profile inferences (such as income bracket or family status) without telling the user.
  - Say that the user is talking to an AI.
  - Route leads only to the recipients named in the notice.

### 8.5 Israeli guidance on AI that gives financial or legal information
- **Israel Securities Authority** [S]:
  - It warned that an AI chat in a client's personal area may be perceived as **personal** advice, and it rejected at least one chatbot request.
  - On 4.11.2026 it published a **draft** directive for licensees on services that use technological tools and AI. The draft is technology-neutral, the licensee remains liable, and independent professional judgment is required.
  - This covers investment advice in securities, not real estate. It is the closest regulatory analogy: generic information is fine, tailored recommendations are where licensing starts.
- **Financial regulators' inter-ministerial AI report** (December 2025: ISA, the Capital Market Authority, the Banking Supervision Department): it favours a proportionate, risk-based approach [S]. The BoI also hosts an "aireport.pdf" [P site, not reviewed].
- **Government AI policy principles** (Ministry of Innovation and Ministry of Justice, 14.12.2023): sector-specific, risk-based regulation, with no general AI law [S].
- **Bar ethics opinion on AI agents:** see 8.3 [S].
- **Mortgage advice:** whether mortgage advisers need a licence in Israel in 2026 is **NV**. The bank makes the credit decision.

### 8.6 Israeli portals with AI assistants (as found 7.11.2026)
- **Madlan: "מדלן AI".** Madlan's blog post (22.10.2026, per the search index; the page returned 403 to the fetcher) describes an expert real-estate AI agent:
  - free-text questions instead of filters;
  - answers built on Madlan's own data, not general web search: real deals, schools, neighbourhood crime, future planning and urban-renewal status [S, self-reported].
- **Yad2:**
  - "yadata", a machine-learning valuation calculator for sale and rent, with a "not a substitute for an appraiser" disclaimer [S];
  - "The Genie", a personal AI analyst announced February 2026 for the **car** marketplace [S];
  - no confirmed conversational AI for real estate was found [NV absence].
- **Homeless, nadlan.com, Ynet Nadlan:** no AI assistant was found in this search [NV absence. Check by hand in the owner's Chrome before claiming "no competitor has one"].
- **Others:**
  - an open-source chatbot built on Claude over Israeli government real-estate data (N12, 20.7.2025), which noted that the data does not always match reality [S];
  - Realize launched AI tools for land and apartment marketing and investment analysis [S].

## What an AI advisor may do as a publisher vs. what only licensed professionals may do

### May do (publisher scope, with the guardrails noted)
- Explain every stage of a deal in plain Hebrew and English, with checklists. This is general information, not advice for the user's specific case.
- Explain how to read a נסח טאבו, an אישור זכויות, a תיק בניין, a מפרט or a lease, and list what to ask the lawyer, appraiser, engineer and bank.
- Run illustrative calculations, labelled as such, with the "valid as of" date and a link to the official calculator:
  - purchase tax from the published brackets;
  - equity needed under the BoI LTV caps;
  - repayment-to-income ratio;
  - late-delivery compensation formulas;
  - rental guarantee caps.
- Show public deal data (nadlan.gov.il / Govmap) and published listings and projects, attributed to their sources, with the State Comptroller's data-quality caveat. Present any value estimate as an **estimate, not an appraisal**.
- Flag red flags and deadlines:
  - the advance on the seller's מס שבח;
  - the 30-day declaration;
  - the 18/24/12-month sale windows for upgraders;
  - the 1-year defect notice;
  - the 60/90-day lease options;
  - the 2-week urban-renewal disclosure period.
- Explain users' statutory rights: Sale (Apartments) Law protections, tenant protections, urban-renewal protections, broker-client rights.
- Help the user prepare for negotiation with evidence (comparables, inspection findings, market data), but **the user** negotiates.
- Offer a list of licensed professionals the user chooses from:
  - disclose any commercial relationship;
  - collect contact details only after the Amendment 13 s.11 notice and consent;
  - say clearly that it is an AI and that the site is a publisher, not a broker.

### Only a licensed professional may do
- **Licensed broker (מתווך מורשה):**
  - bring specific parties together **for consideration**;
  - negotiate on a party's behalf for a fee;
  - take an exclusivity;
  - collect a brokerage fee;
  - present as a broker in any way.
- **Lawyer:**
  - give legal advice or a legal opinion on the user's specific case;
  - draft or negotiate the contract, a זיכרון דברים, a power of attorney or a lease;
  - represent before the Land Registry, the Tax Authority (שבח.נט declarations) or the courts;
  - register warning notes;
  - run the seller's mortgage release and the tax advances.
- **Licensed appraiser (שמאי מקרקעין):**
  - any valuation as a paid service: bank appraisals, objections to a betterment-levy assessment, pinui-binui and tax appraisals.
- **Engineer / home inspector:**
  - a professional opinion on the building's condition and its defects. [NV whether this is a reserved licence, but it is in practice a professional engagement]
- **Bank / Tax Authority:**
  - the credit decision and the actual LTV and rate;
  - the binding tax assessment.
  - Tax planning goes to a CPA or lawyer.
