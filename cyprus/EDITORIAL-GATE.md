# Mandatory pre-writing gate

Ben's standing instruction applies to every new page, article, project and every assignment to ChatGPT. This is a gate before drafting; scope-specific completed research and limits are recorded below. No final buyer copy or publication is approved by a research receipt.

1. Assign one to five keyword intents and synonyms to a single owner page. Check existing pages, semantic ownership, cannibalization and the existing URL contract before minting a route.
2. Manually search Google in the target audience language. Inspect actual names/property phrasing, SERP intent, related questions, suggestions, AI answers and links that are actually shown. Do not fabricate Google features that were absent or inaccessible.
3. Read the top four or five relevant competitors in full: opening, headings, vocabulary, tone, selling points, factual support, coverage and gaps. Record the reviewed URLs, actual queries and meaningful content decisions internally. Do not infer ranking causes as established facts or copy passages.
4. Research HE and EN separately. FR, RU and AR each require their own actual search and competitor reading before drafting in that language. An English SERP is not research for five languages.
5. Write original, deep, detailed but light conversational marketing copy. Use supported numbers/tables only where useful; omit unknown material price/availability. Validate on-page content, language, ownership, internal links, rendered content-first order and mobile buyer journey.
6. Preserve provenance, dates, versions, rights and contradictions in internal research/asset records. Do not scatter sources/dates alongside buyer-facing facts. Briefly identify illustrative visuals where necessary; do not mislead about property/location/accuracy.

Research register: language, page/intent owner, one-to-five intents, synonyms, actual query, observed SERP features, competitor URL/full-read evidence, vocabulary/headings/tone analysis, supported facts, gaps, original content decision, reviewer and completion status.
