## Positioning & Messaging (their words)

- Core differentiation block: **"It's not a 360° tour. It's not a 2D render. It's real-time 3D."** — free navigation floor-by-floor, any time of day, smart filters.
- 6-step buyer journey they sell: online ads → 3D showroom (20-min sessions) → emotional connection → qualified lead → visit/online reservation → close. "Boost sales by up to 25%."
- Metrics they market: 20 min avg session · 2.5× time vs 360°/2D · case studies: +18% closing rate, 40% productivity (Casa México), pre-sales closed without a model apartment (Landmark Tijuana).

## Product & Features

1. **Real-time 3D digital twin** built by THEM from the developer's architecture files (CAD/BIM) — exterior masterplan walk, interior walkthroughs, time-of-day.
2. **Smart filters on live inventory**: price / bedrooms / orientation / view / availability.
3. **CRM/ERP-connected inventory** — real-time price+status sync, "avoid overselling" pitch, custom integrations offered.
4. **Real-estate e-commerce**: reservations + online payments inside the showroom.
5. **Lead gen**: forms, WhatsApp, strategic CTAs; favorites for buyers; admin analytics panel.
6. **Multi-platform**: web, native iOS/Android for sales teams, **Windows touchscreen app for sales centers/expos**.
7. **360° drone views** — aerial + per-unit real view.
8. **2D unit plans with dimensions** for every floorplan type ("100% floorplan types covered").
9. **Content automation**: one digital twin → renders, videos, brochure, website ("same storytelling across every piece").
10. **SDK & API + HTML embed** — they embed INTO portals/marketplaces (they have no consumer distribution of their own).

## Weaknesses (evidence)

- **Implementation: 3+ months per app** (Capterra review complaint) + "clients needed training" (not intuitive).
- **Requires the developer's architecture files + active cooperation** — the exact thing Israeli developers rarely hand over quickly (our whole independent-model approach exists because of this).
- **No public pricing** → enterprise sales cycle; LATAM enterprise SaaS pattern suggests $thousands per project + setup — **UNVERIFIED, do not quote**.
- **No Hebrew, no RTL, no Israel presence, no local SEO/content** — zero distribution: they depend on the developer's own marketing to drive traffic INTO the showroom.
- Tiny public review base (11) for a 200-client claim.
