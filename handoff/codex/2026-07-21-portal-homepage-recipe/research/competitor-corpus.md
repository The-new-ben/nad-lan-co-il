# Competitor Side-by-Side Corpus

**Observed:** 2026-07-21.
**Purpose:** identify the repeated product grammar that makes a property portal feel established, then select the parts that fit NadLan.
**Scope:** 39 named benchmarks across Israel, global mass-market, luxury and regional off-plan products. This is a product-pattern review, not a traffic or valuation ranking.

## Executive synthesis

The strongest NadLan formula is not a visual copy of one competitor. It is a deliberate hybrid:

- **Yad2:** immediate Israeli familiarity, inventory scanning and saves.
- **Madlan:** evidence, transactions, areas and map intelligence.
- **Nadlan.com:** rich project sections and an Israeli amenity vocabulary.
- **OnMap/NewKey:** project-first map, mobile contact and dense catalog behavior.
- **Zillow/Redfin/Rightmove:** search, alerts, media grouping, exact freshness and reliable detail-page structure.
- **Compass/Sotheby's/Christie's:** photography, restraint and named-human trust.
- **Property Finder/Bayut/NewHomeSource/StreetEasy:** a specific new-development contract: developer, unit range, construction stage, handover, payment plan, documents, inventory and project team.
- **RealEstate-Israel/BuyWise/Zoopla Overseas/Idealista:** languages, currency/units, remote viewing and foreign-buyer process.

The white space is the combination: **Israeli project portal + truthful freshness + approved media + exact project completeness + foreign-buyer operations + source-aware 3D.** No benchmark in this corpus combines all six at a mature level.

## Frequency conclusions

### Nearly universal

- A dominant property/project image.
- Location, price policy and essential facts.
- A direct contact or save action.
- Visible inventory/category structure.

### Very common

- Developer or broker identity.
- Status/promotional badge.
- Image count and gallery.
- Results filters and a list/map relationship.

### Common in serious new-development products

- Handover/completion and construction state.
- Unit or plan range.
- Payment-plan signal.
- Floorplans, documents and a project team.

### Rare and therefore valuable

- Exact last-verified data.
- Buyer-visible media provenance and approval.
- Evidence-backed amenity/facility taxonomy.
- True HE/EN parity with currency/units and remote process.
- A combined portal + 3D + live unit data + regulatory/source proof experience.

## Anti-patterns to reject

1. Empty or demo inventory visible to the public.
2. Generic generated imagery presented as a real project.
3. A sketch or abstract diagram occupying the main asset-discovery role.
4. Dozens of badges with no evidence contract.
5. Sponsored placement styled as verification or organic ranking.
6. Price, handover or availability without a visible source/date policy.
7. Desktop sidebars, maps or chat bubbles causing 390px overflow.
8. Editorial/AI content used to hide thin inventory.
9. A full heavy 3D viewer on the homepage when a fast poster and badge are enough.
10. A second detached data store that bypasses WordPress revisions and ownership.

## The selected NadLan position

> **The established Israeli new-project portal for confident local and overseas decisions.** Search and compare projects in a familiar way; inspect the media, facts, plans, context, freshness and 3D evidence that ordinary listing portals omit; speak to a clearly identified human when ready.

This position looks familiar at the surface and becomes differentiated through depth and truth after the click.
