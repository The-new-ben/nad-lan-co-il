# North Tel Aviv coastal neighborhoods: verified research brief

Prepared 2026-09-16. Covers the areas in the broker's posts: Tzukei Aviv / "Gimel HaHadasha", Kochav HaTzafon, Migdalei Ne'eman, Nofei Yam and Lamed HaHadasha, plus Sde Dov and plan 3700.

## Method and limits (read first)

- Every number has a link and the source's publication date. Live data pages (Madlan, Yad2, project and listing pages) are marked "accessed 2026-09-16".
- Media sources used: Globes (Hebrew and English), Calcalist, Ynet, Mako, Bizportal, Walla, Timeout, ice.co.il and nadlancenter. Secondary sources: project-tlv.info (an independent Tel Aviv planning tracker, used for boundaries and to find primary links), tlvonline.co.il (local news), magdilim.co.il, hon.co.il, and developer and listing sites.
- Could not be used in this session:
  - nadlan.gov.il: JavaScript only, no data came back.
  - tel-aviv.gov.il: most pages returned HTTP 472, including the comptroller report on plan 3700, the coastal park design file and the Yarkon bridge page. Only the Sde Dov page opened.
  - nta.co.il line pages: HTTP 403.
  - mavat.iplan.gov.il: not reached.
  - Wikipedia and Hamichlol: blocked.
  - TheMarker and Moovit: blocked by robots rules.
- The web search quota ran out partway through. Later facts were found by following links from pages already opened.
- Madlan and Yad2 pages were read through an automated text extractor. Re-check any transaction row (date, rooms, floor) on nadlan.gov.il before relying on it.
- "Inference" marks a conclusion drawn by combining sources. "Unverified" marks something I could not confirm.

## Key findings

1. "ג' החדשה" / "גימל החדשה" / "רמת אביב ג' החדשה" is not a municipal neighborhood. Sources describe it as the newer part of Tzukei Aviv (צוקי אביב), west of Namir Road and next to the older Azorei Chen towers ([Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/20036059), 2026-08-06; [Timeout](https://timeout.co.il/%D7%92%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%99-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91/), 2018-01-08). It is not part of Ramat Aviv Gimel, which lies on the other side of the Coastal Highway / Namir artery ([hon.co.il](https://www.hon.co.il/%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%AA-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%92-%D7%AA%D7%9C-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA-%D7%96%D7%94%D7%95%D7%AA/), 2019-05-09).
2. None of the streets you suggested is a seafront street in any source reviewed:
   - Levanon: part of the Green Line route in Ramat Aviv.
   - Mivtza Kadesh: north-east Tel Aviv.
   - Shai Agnon: the line between Plan Lamed and Kochav HaTzafon.
   - "Ra'anan": not found in any source.

   The seafront addresses that do appear are Michael Ne'eman St (Migdalei Ne'eman), Herzl Rosenblum 4-10 (Sea and Sun) and the Tzuk beach plan site (section 1.8).
3. A "first line to the sea" view can be built out. Plans that could block it:
   - Plan 3700: 12,000 units on the land north of Propes St out to the sea ([Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-slow-progress-in-huge-northwest-tel-aviv-plan-1001441423), 2023-03-15).
   - Nofei Yam North: heights may rise from 15 to 45 floors ([tlvonline](https://tlvonline.co.il/תוכנית-נופי-ים-צפון/), 2025-03-12).
   - Tzuk beach: an approved 14-floor tower plus a hotel ([Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/r1Kes8AlO), 2021-02-08).
   - Sde Dov: about 16,000 units with towers of up to 45 floors ([nadlancenter](https://www.nadlancenter.co.il/article/11492), 2025-02-03).
4. Resale prices in the older north-west stock have softened, while new Sde Dov units sell far higher.
   - Tzukei Aviv median: about NIS 49,000/sqm in 2026, down from about NIS 54,600 in 2024 ([Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/20036059), 2026-08-06).
   - New Sde Dov units: about NIS 79,000-85,000/sqm on average ([Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/article/r12n2qiowx), 2026-02-24).
5. Two infrastructure items to watch:
   - Green Line: the southern section is expected to open in 2028 and the full line in 2030 ([ice.co.il](https://www.ice.co.il/realestate/news/article/1114193), 2026-05-25).
   - Reading power station: the energy minister decided on 2024-01-30 to end generation ([ice.co.il](https://www.ice.co.il/realestate/news/article/998314), 2024-01-30). Whether it still runs in 2026 is unverified.

## 1. Neighborhood names, boundaries and relationships

### 1.0 Orientation (inference, built from the boundary sources in 1.2 to 1.7)

- West of Namir Road, from south to north:
  1. Kochav HaTzafon: Rokach Blvd to Shai Agnon St.
  2. Plan Lamed: Shai Agnon St to Einstein St. The part west of Levi Eshkol Blvd is called Lamed HaHadasha.
  3. Einstein St to Propes St: Ramat Aviv HaHadasha east of Levi Eshkol Blvd, and Nofei Yam plus the vacant Nofei Yam North west of it.
  4. Tzukei Aviv (Azorei Chen plus Gimel HaHadasha): Propes St to Unitzman St or the Glilot compound.
  5. Glilot, up to the Herzliya border.
- Sde Dov lies west of Nofei Yam and Lamed and north-west of Kochav HaTzafon.
- Plan 3700 covers the undeveloped land north of Propes St out to the sea.
- East of the Namir / Coastal Highway artery are Ramat Aviv (east of Lamed per [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001516560), 2025-07-24) and Ramat Aviv Gimel. Tel Baruch and HaMashtela are further north-east.

### 1.1 Official names

- The municipal statistical neighborhood list includes these north-west names: Galilot (גלילות), Tzukei Aviv (צוקי אביב), Sde Dov area (אזור שדה דב), Nofei Yam (נופי ים), Tochnit Lamed (תוכנית ל') and Kochav HaTzafon (כוכב הצפון). Ramat Aviv Gimel (רמת אביב ג') is listed separately.
  - The list uses a 2005 census base updated to 2013, in a copy hosted by a law firm. [Municipal list copy](https://rotenberglaw.co.il/_Uploads/dbsAttachedFiles/Tel-Aviv-Neighborhoods.pdf).
  - The names "ג' החדשה", "רמת אביב החדשה" and "ל' החדשה" were not found in it.
  - Uncertain: the automated read of this PDF returned inconsistent row codes, so treat the list as indicative.

  - Unitzman St (north), Namir Rd (east), Propes St (south), the sea (west). [Timeout](https://timeout.co.il/%D7%92%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%99-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91/), 2018-01-08.
  - project-tlv gives the same east, south and west edges but uses the Glilot compound as the northern edge. It lists "Gimel HaHadasha" and "Azorei Chen" as alternative names for Tzukei Aviv. [project-tlv](https://project-tlv.info/new-projects/north/tsukei-aviv/), updated 2025-06-24.

  - Azorei Chen: construction began in the 1980s, most buildings date from the 1990s.
  - Gimel HaHadasha: built later, with buildings from recent decades.
  - Luxury complexes near the shore, naming Migdalei Ne'eman and Sea and Sun.

  [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/20036059), 2026-08-06. Timeout (2018) also calls the newer projects built next to Azorei Chen "Gimel HaHadasha". [Timeout](https://timeout.co.il/%D7%92%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%95%D7%A6%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%99-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91/), 2018-01-08.

  - Towers of about 15 floors. [tlvonline](https://tlvonline.co.il/מחירים-צפון-מערב-תל-אביב/), 2025-05-25.
- **Yad2 grouping:** Yad2 treats "אזורי חן, גימל החדשה" as one search area. Its listings were on Uri Zvi Greenberg, Kedoshei HaShoah and Amichai Paglin. [Yad2](https://www.yad2.co.il/realestate/forsale?topArea=2&area=1&city=5000&neighborhood=1490), accessed 2026-09-16.
- **Street boundaries of Gimel HaHadasha: unverified.** No source gives them separately.
  - Streets in Tzukei Aviv that appear with newer stock in 2025-2026 sources: Shmuel Schnitzer, Amichai Paglin, Yair Rosenblum, Daniel Samborski and Eliyahu Berlin. [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/20036059), 2026-08-06; [project-tlv](https://project-tlv.info/new-projects/north/tsukei-aviv/), 2025-06-24.
- **Inference on the name:** "Gimel HaHadasha" reads as "the new Gimel" across the road from Ramat Aviv Gimel.
  - Ramat Aviv Gimel's western edge is the Coastal Highway. [hon.co.il](https://www.hon.co.il/%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%AA-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%92-%D7%AA%D7%9C-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA-%D7%96%D7%94%D7%95%D7%AA/), 2019-05-09.

  - Madlan's Ramat Aviv Gimel page treats Azorei Chen, Ramat Aviv HaHadasha and HaGush HaGadol as younger, separate neighborhoods that drew prestige away from it. [Madlan](https://www.madlan.co.il/area-info/%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%94-%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%AA-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%92-%D7%AA%D7%9C-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%99%D7%A4%D7%95-%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%9C), accessed 2026-09-16.
- **Inference on broker listing (a):** "near Tel Baruch beach, next to Sde Dov" best fits the southern part of Tzukei Aviv near Propes St. Tel Baruch beach is the southern end of the cliff promenade (section 3), and plan 3700's southern compound runs down to Sde Dov (section 2.5).

### 1.3 Ramat Aviv Gimel (רמת אביב ג')

- **Boundaries:** the Coastal Highway (west), KKL Blvd and Afeka (south), the Ayalon (east) and farmland near Glilot junction (north).
- **Size:** about 1,076 dunams, of which about 573 are built.
- **Age:** developed from the 1970s.

Source for all three: [hon.co.il](https://www.hon.co.il/%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%AA-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%92-%D7%AA%D7%9C-%D7%90%D7%91%D7%99%D7%91-%D7%AA%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%93%D7%AA-%D7%96%D7%94%D7%95%D7%AA/), 2019-05-09.

  - Boundaries: Propes (north), Levi Eshkol (east), Einstein (south), Sde Dov (west). [project-tlv](https://project-tlv.info/new-projects/nofei-yam/), accessed 2026-09-16.

## 2. Sde Dov district (and plan 3700 next to it)

### 2.1 Plan structure

- **Site:** a former domestic airport that closed in 2019. [project-tlv](https://project-tlv.info/places/sde-dov/), accessed 2026-09-16.
- **Master plan TA/4444** splits the district into three detailed plans:
  - South: Eshkol, plan TML/3001, approved 2021.
  - Center.
  - North.

  The page also names the Green Line as the district's rail link. [Tel Aviv municipality](https://www.tel-aviv.gov.il/Residents/Development/Pages/SdeDov.aspx), accessed 2026-09-16. The page is undated and still shows the center and north plans as in preparation, so it is likely out of date.
- **Plan 3700 is separate.** It covers the land north of Propes St (section 2.5).

### 2.2 Approved scope over time

- **2017, local committee recommendation to deposit the plan.** Source for all points: [Walla](https://nadlan.walla.co.il/item/3053023), 2017-03-29.
  - About 16,000 apartments, about 7,000 of them affordable.
  - About 350,000 sqm of employment space and about 200,000 sqm of commerce.
  - About 2,000 hotel rooms.
  - 35 towers plus lower buildings, on about 1,500 dunams.
- **Coastal committee (VALCHOF) approval.** Source: [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001271463); the date is not shown in the fetched text, but the article is from after the July 2018 deposit.
  - About 16,000 units.
  - About 3,700 hotel rooms, 1,700 more than the roughly 2,000 proposed.
  - Buildings on the first seafront line capped at 25 floors, and some towers cut from 45 to 30 floors.
  - Park HaMaslul of about 66 dunams and Park HaHof of about 177 dunams.
  - No residential permits beyond 10,000 units until the parks are built.
- **Center plan: the district appeals committee rejected objections on 2025-02-03.** Source: [nadlancenter](https://www.nadlancenter.co.il/article/11492), 2025-02-03.
  - 7,128 units.
  - About 60,000 sqm of commerce, about 155,000 sqm of employment and about 800 hotel rooms.
  - Towers of 20 and 45 floors plus two 25-floor residential towers.
  - At least 16 m between neighboring towers on different lots.
- **Towers now being marketed:**
  - Hagag FIRST: a 45-floor main tower plus three 9-floor buildings. [Ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/sj7l5vzmwe), 2025-12-18.
  - Israel Canada: a 39-floor tower plus six low buildings, 480 units. [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-israel-canada-has-sold-120-sde-ddv-apartments-1001463815), 2023-11-29.
  - Gindi Vogue: 708 units in a 44-floor tower, three 20-floor towers and three 9-floor buildings. [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-gindi-undercuts-rivals-with-much-lower-sde-dov-prices-1001512098), 2025-06-05.
- **Uncertain:** hotel-room and floor-area totals differ from stage to stage. I could not check the current statutory totals for the whole district on mavat.

### 2.3 Land tenders and land price per housing unit

- **2021 Eshkol tender:**
  - 1,574 apartments for NIS 4.4 billion, about NIS 2.8 million of land per apartment. [buyitinisrael](https://www.buyitinisrael.com/news/sde-dov-airport-land-sold-at-a-record-price-for-the-construction-of-more-than-1500-apartments/), 2021-09-22.
  - Globes confirms NIS 2.8 million per unit, before development costs, for the August 2021 tender. [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-sde-dov-tender-bid-result-disappoints-1001406669), 2022-03-22.
- **Record level:** in 2021-2022, regular tenders averaged over NIS 2.5 million per unit. The peak was NIS 3.3 million per unit including development costs, in Hanan Mor Group's August 2021 bid. [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-sde-dov-land-prices-down-over-40-1001502651), 2025-02-20.
- **March 2022:** 241 units won by Nof Ironi (Claude Nachmias) for NIS 654 million, NIS 2.71 million per unit before development costs. [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-sde-dov-tender-bid-result-disappoints-1001406669), 2022-03-22.
- **September 2024:** the Israel Land Authority issued tenders for 7,159 units in 27 areas. Counting about 2,650 units already marketed in Eshkol, nearly 10,000 of the 16,000 units had been tendered. [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-ila-issues-tender-for-over-7000-sde-dov-homes-1001488122), 2024-09-01.
  - Uncertain: 2,650 Eshkol units here vs 1,574 in the 2021 tender. There may have been further Eshkol tenders.
- **February 2025, center:**
  - Four tenders for almost 4,000 units drew NIS 6.3 billion in bids. The 2,606-unit high-density tender averaged NIS 1.9 million per unit including development. ### 2.5 Plan 3700 (צפון מערב העיר), the land beside Tzukei Aviv

- **Scope (Globes, March 2023).** Source for all points: [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-slow-progress-in-huge-northwest-tel-aviv-plan-1001441423), 2023-03-15.

  - Prices: experts cite a rise of about 20% since the airport closed.

  - Uncertain: 12,000 vs 13,000 units.
- **Five compounds, south to north.** Source: [project-tlv](https://project-tlv.info/urban-plans/3700-2/), modified 2026-06-18.
  - Compound 1: from Sde Dov to Shoshana Persitz St.
  - Compounds 2 and 3: between Persitz St and Shimon Samet St.
  - Compound 4: from Shimon Samet St to Unitzman St.
  - Compound 5: from Unitzman St to Glilot.
  - Uncertain: the exact lines of compounds 2 and 3, and street spellings.
- **Infrastructure.** Source for all points: [Globes](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-work-begins-on-large-new-tel-aviv-neighborhood-1001448943), 2023-06-08.
  - A new road of about 5 km from Rokach to Herzliya (the Ibn Gabirol extension), which will also carry the Green Line. Globes cites NIS 550 million for this project.
  - A pneumatic waste system: NIS 220 million.
  - Drainage: NIS 50 million.
  - The first phase of the coastal park: NIS 75 million.
  - The neighborhood is planned for about 50,000 residents.
- Migdalei Ne'eman residents' compensation claims against plan 3700 were rejected (section 5).
