## Tel Aviv luxury apartments and Herzliya Pituach villas, research as of 2026-09-16

## 0. Key takeaways
- Purchase tax brackets are frozen until 15.1.2028. A resident buying a single apartment pays 0% / 3.5% / 5% / 8% / 10%. Investors and foreign residents pay 8% up to NIS 6,055,070 and 10% above. The investor rate is a temporary order that Globes (2026-09-07) says expires at the start of 2027, with an automatic extension into February 2027 because of the election period. Expect policy uncertainty around year end.
- Bank of Israel rate: 3.25% (cut on 2026-09-01, effective 2026-09-03). Prime is 4.75%. Next decision is 2026-10-21.
- Bank of Israel mortgage limits (Directive 329, version 12, 08/02/2026): 75% LTV for a single apartment, 70% for a replacement apartment, 50% for an investment property. Both the single and replacement categories require a buyer who is an individual Israeli citizen, so non-citizens fall under the 50% cap. Payment-to-income is capped at 50%.
- CBS (release of 2026-09-15): national home prices rose 0.2% in June-July and are down 1.2% year on year. Tel Aviv district rose 0.7% for the two months and is down 0.9% year on year. Rents rose 4.4% year on year for new tenants and 2.6% for renewals.
- Arnona: in 2026 the Interior Ministry approved higher zone-1 rates for new Tel Aviv residential units west of HaYarkon Street and in the Dolphinarium complex, up to NIS 139.60 per sqm for units over 140 sqm. Which zone each north Tel Aviv neighborhood falls in is UNVERIFIED.
- Luxury market: Israel had 635 deals of NIS 10m or more in 2025, and 60% of them were in Tel Aviv. Herzliya Pituach private houses sold at a median of about NIS 15m in 2025. New apartments in Herzliya Pituach have a median of about NIS 72,000 per sqm. Land there trades at NIS 18,000-33,000 per sqm.
- FX (Bank of Israel representative rates, 2026-09-15): USD/ILS 3.0490 and EUR/ILS 3.5169.

## 1. Purchase tax (mas rechisha), 2026

### 1(a) Single residential apartment (dira yechida), Israeli resident
- Brackets (NIS), valid from 16.1.2024 and frozen until 15.1.2028:
  - Up to 1,978,745: 0%
  - 1,978,745 to 2,347,040: 3.5%
  - 2,347,040 to 6,055,070: 5%
  - 6,055,070 to 20,183,565: 8%
  - Above 20,183,565: 10%
  - Sources: [Israel Tax Authority, Real Estate Taxation Directive 1/2025 (amounts and freeze)](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-01-2025-3/he/realestate_inst-01-2025-3.pdf), dated 16 Jan 2025. [Kol Zchut, Purchase tax calculation](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%91_%D7%9E%D7%A1_%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%94), page updated 3 Aug 2026. [Bizportal, Purchase tax 2026: single vs additional apartment](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/20038028), 6 Aug 2026.
- Freeze: brackets normally update every 16 January according to the housing price index. The Arrangements Law froze that update until 15 January 2028. Sources: [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/20038028), 6 Aug 2026. [Tax Authority Directive 1/2025](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-01-2025-3/he/realestate_inst-01-2025-3.pdf), 16 Jan 2025.
- "Single apartment" also covers a buyer who becomes an Israeli resident or a returning resident within two years of the purchase. Sources: [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%91_%D7%9E%D7%A1_%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%94), 3 Aug 2026. [Bizportal](https://www.bizportal.co.il/realestates/news/article/20038028), 6 Aug 2026.
- Upgraders (buyers who already own one apartment) pay single-apartment rates if they sell the old apartment within 24 months of buying the new one. The window was 18 months for replacement purchases made between 1.6.2023 and 31.5.2025, under a temporary order that lapsed. Sources: [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%91_%D7%9E%D7%A1_%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%94), 3 Aug 2026. [Meitar law firm, Real estate tax updates June 2025](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Real-Estate-Tax-Updates-June-2025-HEB.pdf), 5 Jun 2025.
- Derived examples, single apartment: NIS 10m price gives NIS 513,886 tax (5.14% effective). NIS 15m gives NIS 913,886 (6.09%). NIS 25m gives NIS 1,810,215 (7.24%).

### 1(b) Additional apartment / investor
- 8% on value up to NIS 6,055,070 and 10% above. Kol Zchut shows these rates valid from 16.01.2024 to 31.12.2026. Sources: [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%91_%D7%9E%D7%A1_%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%94), 3 Aug 2026. [Tax Authority Directive 1/2025](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-01-2025-3/he/realestate_inst-01-2025-3.pdf), 16 Jan 2025.
- The Knesset Finance Committee extended the 8%/10% temporary order by two years, to the end of 2026. Before the temporary order the rate was 5%. Source: [ynet, Finance Committee approves 2-year extension](https://www.ynet.co.il/economy/article/bkmshu441x), 9 Dec 2024.
- Temporary change to watch in 2026:
  - Globes reports that the order expires at the start of 2027. It is automatically extended into February 2027 because laws that would lapse during an election period are extended (Section 38 of Basic Law: The Knesset).
  - If the order lapses, the rate for an additional apartment drops to 5% on the first tier. The article does not state the upper tiers.
  - Finance Ministry professionals favor keeping the higher rates. The Tax Authority director said the next government must decide.
  - Investors bought 3,462 apartments in Q2 2026, per Finance Ministry Chief Economist data.
  - Source: [Globes, What will happen to purchase tax?](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001554581), 7 Sep 2026.
- Derived examples, additional apartment: NIS 10m gives NIS 878,899 (8.79%). NIS 15m gives NIS 1,378,899 (9.19%). NIS 25m gives NIS 2,378,899 (9.52%).

### 1(c) Foreign resident
- There is no separate foreign-resident schedule. The single-apartment brackets apply only to an Israeli resident, or to a buyer who becomes a resident or returning resident within two years. A foreign resident therefore pays the additional-apartment schedule of 8% and 10%. Sources: [Kol Zchut (definition)](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%97%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%91_%D7%9E%D7%A1_%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%94), 3 Aug 2026. [Global Law Experts, Israel Real Estate Taxes 2026](https://globallawexperts.com/israel-real-estate-taxes-2026/), 28 Apr 2026. [Mizrahi-Tefahot, Q&A on mortgages in Israel](https://www.mizrahi-tefahot.co.il/en/about-mizrahi-tefahot/qa-mortgages-in-israel/) ("up to 8%"), undated.
- UNVERIFIED detail: a law firm says a 2025 court ruling measured the two-year residency period for an off-plan purchase from completion of construction rather than from the contract date. The firm also says the Tax Authority requires Form 7912 declarations. Confirm with counsel before relying on either point. Source: [Asnafy law firm, purchase tax benefit for foreign residents](https://asnafy.co.il/israeli-purchase-tax-for-foreign-residents/), 4 Jun 2025.
- War-related deadline extension: Tax Authority Directive 2/2026 extended statutory real estate tax deadlines that overlapped the conflict period of 28 Feb to 31 May 2026 by three months, to 31 Aug 2026 at the latest. That covers both the two-year residency window and the window for upgraders to sell. Those extended dates have now passed. Source: [Tax Authority Directive 2/2026](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-02-2026-1/he/realestate_inst-02-2026.pdf), 30 Mar 2026.

### 1(d) New immigrant (oleh) benefits
- Regime since 15.8.2024:
  - The benefit applies only if the apartment is the oleh's single residential apartment, and only to apartments valued up to NIS 20,183,565.
  - Brackets: 0% up to 1,978,745; 0.5% from 1,978,745 to 6,055,070; 8% from 6,055,070 to 20,183,565.
  - Sources: [Kol Zchut, Purchase tax discount for olim](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%94%D7%A0%D7%97%D7%94_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A1_%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%94_%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9D), updated 31 Mar 2026. [Nefesh B'Nefesh, Planning your aliyah: purchase tax](https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/finances/taxes/planning-your-aliyah-purchase-tax/), updated 15 Apr 2026.
- Eligibility window: from one year before first entry to seven years after. Military or national service does not count toward the seven years. The benefit can be used once for a residential purchase. Sources: [gov.il service page, oleh purchase tax relief (Form 2973)](https://www.gov.il/he/service/real-estate-2973b), accessed 16 Sep 2026. [Kol Zchut](https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/%D7%94%D7%A0%D7%97%D7%94_%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%A1_%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%94_%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%99%D7%9D), 31 Mar 2026.
- There is no requirement that the oleh actually live in the apartment. Olim who arrived before 15.8.2024 may elect the old track: 0.5% up to NIS 1,988,090 and 5% above. Sources: [Calcalist, Purchase tax benefits for olim](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/r1bfvsoj0), 27 Aug 2024. [NBN](https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/finances/taxes/planning-your-aliyah-purchase-tax/), 15 Apr 2026. The old-track amounts also appear in [Tax Authority Directive 1/2025](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-01-2025-3/he/realestate_inst-01-2025-3.pdf), 16 Jan 2025.
- Derived: the largest possible saving over regular single-apartment rates is NIS 177,910, reached at a value of NIS 6,055,070 or more.
- UNCERTAINTY: one law firm page says the 0.5% rate runs up to NIS 20,183,565 ([dsegev-law](https://dsegev-law.co.il/nadlan-mekarkein/%D7%9E%D7%A1-%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A9%D7%94-%D7%9C%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%9C%D7%94-%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9/), 26 Oct 2025). That conflicts with Kol Zchut, NBN and Calcalist, which all put the 0.5% ceiling at NIS 6,055,070. The sources fetched also do not clearly say what an oleh pays on an apartment above NIS 20,183,565. Presumably regular rates apply, but that is not verified.
- Related, not purchase tax: the 2026 income tax holiday for new olim and returning residents (0% in 2026-2027 on up to NIS 1m of Israeli employment or business income) was announced with the 2026 budget. I could not verify whether it was enacted. Sources: [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/israel-unveils-0-tax-rate-for-2026s-immigrants-and-returning-residents/), 6 Nov 2025. [Nimrod Yaron & Co.](https://y-tax.co.il/en/new-tax-benefit-for-new-immigrants/), 25 Nov 2025.

### Mortgage limits (Supervisor of Banks, Proper Conduct of Banking Business Directive 329, version 12, dated 08/02/2026)
- LTV caps:
  - Single apartment (dira yechida): 75% (section 2.1)
  - Replacement apartment (dira chalifit): 70% (section 2.2)
  - Investment apartment (dira lehashkaa): 50% (section 2.3)
  - Source: [BOI Directive 329 (12)](https://www.boi.org.il/media/brep4lzt/329_12.pdf), version 02/26.
- How a foreign resident is treated:
  - The directive defines a "single apartment" as a single apartment under section 9 of the Real Estate Taxation Law that is bought by an individual Israeli citizen. A "replacement apartment" also requires an individual Israeli citizen. Everything else counts as "investment". A "foreign resident" is defined as anyone who is not an Israeli citizen.
  - My reading: a non-citizen buyer is capped at 50% LTV. Mizrahi-Tefahot says the same: foreign residents can borrow up to 50% LTV.
  - Sources: [BOI Directive 329 (12)](https://www.boi.org.il/media/brep4lzt/329_12.pdf), 02/26. [Mizrahi-Tefahot Q&A](https://www.mizrahi-tefahot.co.il/en/about-mizrahi-tefahot/qa-mortgages-in-israel/), undated.
- Payment-to-income:
  - Banks may not approve a housing loan with a payment-to-income ratio above 50% (section 5).
  - Loans above 40% carry a 100% risk weight (section 6).
  - Mizrahi-Tefahot's consumer page describes the limit as 40%, which likely reflects bank practice rather than the regulatory cap.
  - Sources: [BOI Directive 329 (12)](https://www.boi.org.il/media/brep4lzt/329_12.pdf), 02/26. [Mizrahi-Tefahot Q&A](https://www.mizrahi-tefahot.co.il/en/about-mizrahi-tefahot/qa-mortgages-in-israel/), undated.
- Other limits in the directive:
  - The variable-rate portion may not exceed 66.66% of the loan (section 7). Foreign-currency loans to foreign residents are exempt from this limit (section 12.3).
  - Maximum term is 30 years (section 8).
  - Developer-subsidized bullet and balloon loans may not exceed 10% of a bank's housing loan originations in a quarter (section 8a).
  - Source: [BOI Directive 329 (12)](https://www.boi.org.il/media/brep4lzt/329_12.pdf), 02/26.
- Changes published 16 Dec 2025:
  - The PTI calculation must include all housing loans secured by the same property, including loans from other lenders. Effective 30 Jun 2026.
  - Non-purchase (all-purpose) housing loans may go up to 70% LTV if the amount above 50% LTV does not exceed NIS 200,000. This relief was made permanent.
  - The property value cap for discounted-housing LTV rules was raised from NIS 1.8m to NIS 2.1m.
  - Sources: [BOI press release, Banking Supervision steps in the housing credit market](https://www.boi.org.il/en/communication-and-publications/press-releases/the-banking-supervision-department-takes-several-steps-in-the-housing-credit-market/), 16 Dec 2025. [BOI circular 06-2](https://www.boi.org.il/media/141cr3bf/111581.pdf), 16 Dec 2025.
- Mortgage market context:
  - August 2026 mortgage volume was NIS 10.9bn.
  - The 2026 average is about NIS 10bn a month, against NIS 8.8bn in 2025 and NIS 7.8bn in 2024.
  - The share of balloon mortgages is at its lowest since late 2023.
  - Sources: [Globes English, Record mortgage taking in 2026 despite slow market](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-record-mortgage-taking-in-2026-despite-slow-market-1001555441), 14 Sep 2026. [Calcalist, record NIS 22.5bn in July-August](https://www.calcalist.co.il/local_news/article/bj9shvbffx), 14 Sep 2026.

## 6. Capital gains tax (mas shevach) on residential sales, key 2026 rules for investors
- Individuals pay a standard 25% on the real (inflation-adjusted) gain. For long holdings, apportionment rules can apply different rates to different periods; get the calculation from counsel. Sources: [PwC Tax Summaries, Israel, income determination](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/israel/individual/income-determination), last reviewed 29 Jun 2026. [Adv. Tomer Wasserman, Real estate taxation 2026](https://tw-law.co.il/blog/misui-mekarkein-2026-ptorim-mas-shevach-mas-rekhisha/), 25 Mar 2026.
- Single-apartment exemption ceiling (section 49a(a1)): NIS 5,008,000, frozen for tax years 2025-2027. In luxury sales above the ceiling, part of the gain is taxable; confirm the exact calculation with counsel. Source: [Tax Authority Directive 1/2025](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/inst-01-2025-3/he/realestate_inst-01-2025-3.pdf), 16 Jan 2025.
- Section 49h thresholds, frozen: NIS 2,252,000 (combined value of two apartments sold) and NIS 3,746,000 (maximum value). Source: same Directive 1/2025.
- Investors who own several apartments may use the preferred linear calculation. The gain accrued up to 1.1.2014 is exempt and the gain after that date is taxed at 25%. Eligibility conditions apply and were not detailed in the source. Source: [Wasserman law office](https://tw-law.co.il/blog/misui-mekarkein-2026-ptorim-mas-shevach-mas-rekhisha/), 25 Mar 2026.
- Surtax:
  - 3% on annual taxable income above NIS 721,560. The threshold is frozen through 2027.
  - From 2025, an extra 2% applies to capital income above the same threshold, including real estate gains and rental income.
  - Sources: [PwC Tax Summaries, taxes on personal income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/israel/individual/taxes-on-personal-income), last reviewed 29 Jun 2026. [AH CPA, year-end 2025 / 2026 tax guide](https://www.ahcpa.co.il/%D7%94%D7%99%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9C%D7%A7%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%AA-%D7%AA%D7%95%D7%9D-%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%94%D7%9E%D7%A1-2025-%D7%95%D7%AA%D7%97%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%AA-%D7%A9%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%94/), 9 Dec 2025.
  - UNVERIFIED: one law office says that from 2026, gains from non-exempt apartment sales count toward surtax income, which could raise the effective rate on those gains to about 30%. Source: [Wasserman law office](https://tw-law.co.il/blog/misui-mekarkein-2026-ptorim-mas-shevach-mas-rekhisha/), 25 Mar 2026.
- Risks from holding structure and trading pattern:
  - A private house held in a company can be taxed as a corporate asset, with depreciation recaptured. Example: a NIS 27m house in Arsuf was assessed NIS 4.35m in betterment tax after a NIS 4.76m depreciation deduction, and the assessment is under appeal. Source: [Globes English, Beny Steinmetz appeals tax on sale of NIS 27m house](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-beny-steinmetz-appeals-tax-on-sale-of-nis-27m-house-1001551152), 3 Aug 2026.
  - The Tax Authority increasingly reclassifies short-term resales as business income taxed at marginal rates (up to 47% plus surtax). Source: [Meitar](https://meitar.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Real-Estate-Tax-Updates-June-2025-HEB.pdf), 5 Jun 2025.

## 7. Rental income tax tracks, 2026 (individual landlord, residential, Israel)
- Exemption track: rent is tax-exempt up to NIS 5,654 a month in 2026. Sources: [PwC Tax Summaries](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/israel/individual/income-determination), last reviewed 29 Jun 2026. [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/real-estate/article/r1yacdsfmx), 14 Sep 2026.
  - UNVERIFIED mechanics: PwC says only that rent above the ceiling is taxed at marginal rates. It is widely understood that the exempt amount shrinks as rent rises above the ceiling, so at luxury rents the exemption track is effectively irrelevant. Confirm the formula.
- 10% track: a flat 10% on gross rent, with no deductions and no depreciation. Source: [PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/israel/individual/income-determination), 29 Jun 2026.
- Regular track: marginal income tax rates, with deductible expenses and straight-line depreciation on the building but not the land. Source: [PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/israel/individual/income-determination), 29 Jun 2026.
- Surtax: rental income counts as capital-source income for the extra 2% surtax above NIS 721,560, on top of the 3% general surtax. Source: [PwC, taxes on personal income](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/israel/individual/taxes-on-personal-income), 29 Jun 2026.
- Residential rental income from abroad is taxed at a flat 15% (relevant for Israeli-resident landlords with overseas property). Source: [PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/israel/individual/income-determination), 29 Jun 2026.
- Policy watch: the Housing Ministry recommends mandatory reporting of every rental, citing about 890,000 rented units and a NIS 50bn a year market. The ministry says implementation would likely wait for the next government. Source: [Calcalist](https://www.calcalist.co.il/real-estate/article/r1yacdsfmx), 14 Sep 2026.

## 8. Rental law for luxury leases (Rental and Borrowing Law, 1971, as amended in 2017, the "Fair Rental" chapter)
- The 2017 amendment added a chapter on residential leases, in force from September 2017 per Wikipedia. Source: [Hebrew Wikipedia, Rental and Borrowing Law](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%95%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%94), accessed 16 Sep 2026.
- Security cap: all cash-equivalent securities together (cash, deposited checks, bank guarantees) may not exceed the lower of three months' rent or one-third of the rent for the whole lease period. Example: a 12-month lease at NIS 5,000 a month gives a cap of NIS 15,000. Sources: [Nefesh B'Nefesh, Renting an apartment in Israel (Adv. Russell Mayer)](https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/community-and-housing/buying-and-renting/renting-in-israel/), updated 7 May 2025. [Hebrew Wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%95%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%94).
- Securities must be returned within 60 days after the lease ends or the tenant's debts are settled. Source: [NBN](https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/community-and-housing/buying-and-renting/renting-in-israel/), 7 May 2025.
- Broker fees: a landlord may not charge the tenant for a broker who acted for the landlord. The fee is paid by whoever engaged the broker, typically one month's rent plus 18% VAT. Sources: [Hebrew Wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%95%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%94). [NBN](https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/community-and-housing/buying-and-renting/renting-in-israel/), 7 May 2025.
- Repairs: the landlord must fix defects within a reasonable time and no later than 30 days after notice. The tenant is liable only for damage caused by unreasonable use. Sources: [NBN](https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/community-and-housing/buying-and-renting/renting-in-israel/), 7 May 2025. [Hebrew Wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%95%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%94).
- Habitability: a dwelling is unfit for habitation if it lacks drainage or sewage, electricity or lighting, natural ventilation or windows, a locking entrance, drinking water, or a bathroom separate from living areas, or if it poses an unreasonable safety or health risk. Source: [Hebrew Wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%97%D7%95%D7%A7_%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%9B%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%AA_%D7%95%D7%94%D7%A9%D7%90%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%94).
- A lease clause letting the landlord terminate without cause is void unless the tenant has an equivalent right. Source: same.
- CRITICAL SCOPE POINT, UNVERIFIED in a fetched source:
  - The Fair Rental chapter is commonly described as not applying to leases with monthly rent above NIS 20,000, or to leases of three months or less. An interim note in this project, citing Kol Zchut but without a URL, records the same NIS 20,000 threshold.
  - If this is correct, typical luxury leases in Sarona towers or new north Tel Aviv buildings would fall outside the chapter's mandatory protections. Deposit caps, repair deadlines and habitability duties would then depend on the contract.
  - The statute text should be checked for the threshold and for any indexation.
- Mamad (protected space), NOT FOUND:
  - I found no fetched source setting a legal requirement that a leased apartment include a mamad, and the habitability list above does not mention one.
  - Practical step: confirm in the lease whether the unit has a mamad, or access to a floor or building shelter. For new buildings, check the building permit or occupancy documents.

## 9. New construction buyer protections
- Sale (Apartments) (Assurance of Investments of Persons Acquiring Apartments) Law, 1974:
  - A seller may not take more than 7% of the price unless it provides one of five securities: a bank guarantee, an insurance policy, a first-ranking mortgage, a registered warning note (caveat) with no prior encumbrance, or transfer of ownership free of third-party rights.
  - Payments go through a payment-voucher booklet into the project account.
  - The seller must report the sale and the security chosen within 7 days.
  - Source: [NBN, Buying an apartment on paper: protect your money](https://www.nbn.org.il/life-in-israel/community-and-housing/buying-and-renting/buying-an-apartment-on-paper-protect-your-money/), 26 Jul 2021. The rules described are statutory; the article is older.
- Sale (Apartments) Law, 1973, inspection period (shnat bedek / tkufat bedek) and warranty period (tkufat achrayut): UNVERIFIED, no source fetched.
  - General understanding: a one-year general inspection period, with longer inspection periods for specific defect categories listed in the Law's schedule, followed by a three-year warranty period in which the buyer must show the defect comes from design, construction or materials. A much longer period, commonly cited as 20 years, applies to fundamental structural nonconformity.
  - Exact category-by-category periods must be checked in the statute before advising.
- Enforcement example: a Jerusalem court held that a developer lost its right to repair after persistent failure to fix defects over about six years, and awarded 55 buyers about NIS 1.5m. Source: [Globes](https://www.globes.co.il/news/article.aspx?did=1001554906), 14 Sep 2026.
- Developer-financing risk: BOI caps developer-subsidized bullet and balloon loans at 10% of each bank's quarterly housing originations. Source: [BOI Directive 329 (12), s.8a](https://www.boi.org.il/media/brep4lzt/329_12.pdf), 02/26. Cancellations of signed home purchases are rising, per Finance Ministry Chief Economist data. About two-thirds cite financial difficulty, such as failing to get a mortgage. Standard contractual compensation is 10% of the price, and Tel Aviv luxury cancellations reached about NIS 500,000. The article does not specifically blame 80/20 or balloon schemes. Source: [Globes English, Home deal cancellations proliferate in Israel](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-home-deal-cancellations-proliferate-in-israel-1001552420), 16 Aug 2026.
