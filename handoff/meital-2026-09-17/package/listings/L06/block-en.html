The location

Tzukei Aviv, between the coastal park and the Sde Dov district

Tzukei Aviv runs from Unitzman Street in the north to Propes Street in the south, between Namir Road and the sea.14 Gimel HaHadasha is the name for its newer part, built beside the Azorei Chen towers.13,15 Data published by Mako put the median deal in Azorei Chen from early 2023 to October 2025 at NIS 5.9 million, the fifth highest neighborhood median in Israel.16

The coastal park promenade opened in September 2023 and runs about 2 km along the cliff, from Tel Baruch beach in the south to Tzuk beach in the north, with a bike path along its length.17 Nathan Alterman High School, whose building won the Rokach architecture prize in 2015, is in the neighborhood.18

To the south the Sde Dov district is taking shape, where new apartments sold at an average of NIS 79,000 to 85,000 per sqm according to Calcalist in February 2026.19 The Green Line light rail is expected to open its southern section in 2028 and the full line in 2030.20

Planning

What is planned nearby

Published plans and works, and what they may mean for a buyer or tenant. Based on published reports; not a substitute for a check with the planning authority.

  • Plan TA/3700, the north-west quarter28,29

    About 1,900 dunams between the existing neighborhoods and the sea, up to the city boundary: 11,000 to 13,000 homes, 1,500 hotel rooms and about 215,000 sqm of employment and retail. In August 2024 the reallocation tables were still unapproved, so timing is uncertain.

  • Building heights in plan 370029

    A planner quoted by Globes described 6 to 8 homes per dunam in buildings of eight floors at most, plus a 400-dunam ecological coastal park. The heights set west of the building will shape the open view from lower floors.

  • The Sde Dov district15,19

    About 16,000 homes and towers of up to 45 floors in the center plan; most apartments are expected to be occupied in 2029 to 2032. New supply at higher price levels and years of construction south of the neighborhood.

  • Eliyahu Berlin 3 project13,31

    One record shows active construction of 13 floors and 41 units with completion in early 2028, while project-tlv lists it as completed. If work is active, expect building on the same street until 2028.

  • Hotel and residential tower at Tzuk beach33,34

    A 2021 plan for a 14-floor tower with 116 apartments and a hotel of about 200 rooms; the design plan was approved in May 2025. More activity and traffic at the north end of the promenade.

  • Nofei Yam North35

    In March 2025 the permit freeze was extended for re-planning, with heights that could rise from 15 to 45 floors. Taller buildings are possible south of the neighborhood.

Before you decide

The checks worth making

The questions a careful tenant asks about this home, and the documents to obtain before signing.

  • Is NIS 27,000 available without paying 12 months upfront, and what is the monthly rent with normal monthly or quarterly payments?
  • Who is the landlord (developer or private owner), and does a land registry or developer record show a mortgage, lien or caveat on the unit?
  • How will the prepayment be protected: a bank guarantee from the landlord, a lawyer's trust account released monthly, or a lease clause for refund on early termination?
  • What security does the landlord require from the tenant on top of the prepayment?
  • What is the lease term, is there an option to extend, and how will the rent be set on renewal?
  • Which floor is the unit on, what is the arnona area and bill, and what is the ceiling height?
  • What does the NIS 1,400 va'ad bayit cover, and does the tenant pay it and the arnona?
  • Has Tofes 4 been issued, and who handles defects during the lease and how fast?
  • What stays in the unit: appliances, light fixtures, window treatments, any furniture?
  • Is a brokerage fee payable now that the August offer has ended, and how much?
  • Which plan 3700 compound lies west, and is construction expected there during the lease?
  • A draft lease with an agreed mechanism protecting the prepayment
  • Confirmation of the landlord's rights in the unit
  • The building's occupancy certificate (Tofes 4)
  • A handover record and equipment list
