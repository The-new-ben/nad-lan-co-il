The residence

A designer home, facing west to the sea

The apartment sits on a high floor in a new tower at B.S.R Sarona, with a 14 sqm balcony facing west and an open view to the sea. It was planned as five rooms and converted to 4.5, on 128 sqm built.1,2

A leading designer planned the apartment and chose the materials: an island kitchen, concealed integrated refrigerators, a premium cooktop, quality parquet and designed lighting throughout.2,3

The location

Sarona: the complex, the light rail and the city within walking range

The project sits south of Sarona Market and forms the western edge of the Sarona complex, between the business district along the Ayalon and the residential neighborhoods to the west.11,12 The Landmark towers adjoin it to the south.15

In a straight line, Habima Square is about 600 m away, the Tel Aviv Museum of Art about 1.1 km, Azrieli Center about 1.2 km and Ichilov hospital about 1.6 km.16,17 Charles Clore Park on the seafront is about 2 km in a straight line.20

The Red Line has run since August 2023, and exit A of Yehudit station serves the Sarona complex.21,22 The Green Line, with stations at Carlebach and Kaplan, is expected to open in 2028, and Tel Aviv HaShalom railway station is linked to Azrieli Center by a footbridge.23,24

Before you decide

The checks worth making

The questions a careful tenant asks about a converted layout, and the documents to obtain.

  • Which tower (A, B or C) and floor is the unit on, and which rooms face the sea?
  • What was changed from 5 to 4.5 rooms? Please share the floor plan and confirm that the mamad is intact.
  • Which items stay with the unit (hidden integrated refrigerators, cooktop, oven, dishwasher, light fixtures, curtains or blinds, furniture), and is it offered furnished?
  • Is the sea view open from the living room, and does the Landmark tower block part of the southern view from the south-west balcony?
  • What is the monthly building management fee, what does it cover (guard, cleaning, gym), who pays it, and which company manages the building?
  • Can you share the latest arnona bill showing the measured area and the zone?
  • Is the parking space attached or registered to the unit? Which level is it on, is there EV charging, and where is the storage room and how big is it?
  • What securities are required (bank guarantee, deposit, guarantors), in what amount, and when are they returned?
  • Who pays the brokerage fee and how much is it? Please confirm in a signed order form.
  • Are there open defects in the unit or the common areas since occupancy, and who arranges contractor repairs during the lease, within what time?
  • Where is the gym, what are its hours, and is there a fee or registration?
  • Is the landlord the purchase group member who holds the rights to the unit, and can they show documents proving it?
  • For families: is the kindergarten cluster at Arania 14A open, and which schools are zoned for this address?
  • What is the lease length, is there an option to extend, and is the rent linked to the consumer price index?
  • The unit plan after the conversion to 4.5 rooms
  • The draft lease, including guarantees, indexation and option
  • The unit's latest arnona bill
  • The list of fittings and appliances that stay

Will the Fair Rental Law apply?
