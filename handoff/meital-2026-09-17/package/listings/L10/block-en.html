The residence

Three rooms in a low-rise neighborhood near the coastal park

Three rooms on about 90 sqm, a balcony of about 20 sqm facing an open area, and a safe room, on floor 5 of a seven-floor building on pillars with a lift. Two separate parking spaces and a large storage room by the parking, and the sea about five minutes on foot, next to the walking path.2

The rent is NIS 9,500 a month, with entry on 1 October 2026 and flexibility on the date.2

Nofei Yam is the neighborhood between Propes Street to the north, Levi Eshkol Boulevard to the east, Einstein Street to the south and the Sde Dov area to the west, and together with Ramat Aviv HaHadasha it forms HaGush HaGadol.3,4 It holds about 2,000 apartments in relatively low buildings, because of the height limits from the days of the airport.5

The location

Nofei Yam, between HaGush HaGadol and the coastal park

The coastal park promenade opened in September 2023 and runs about 2 km along the cliff, from Tel Baruch beach in the south to Tzuk beach in the north, inside a 500-dunam nature park and alongside a bike path.6,11 The walking time from the apartment to the sea is the broker's estimate and has not been measured independently.

At least two small shopping centers operate among the neighborhood's complexes, though there is no lively shopping street; several kindergartens and a school operate within HaGush HaGadol.5,12 Since November 2023 a pedestrian and cycle bridge with lifts has crossed Einstein Street between Nofei Yam and Lamed HaHadasha, and the municipal country club of Plan Lamed lies south of the street.12,13

West of the neighborhood, the Sde Dov district is being built on the airport that closed in 2019, with about 16,000 apartments. By February 2026 developers had sold more than 1,500 of them, and most are expected to be ready within 3 to 6 years.14,15 Car access relies mainly on Levi Eshkol Boulevard and Einstein Street, and the Einstein and Namir junction is congested at peak hours.12

What is planned nearby

Published plans and works, and what they may mean for a buyer or tenant. Based on published reports; not a substitute for a check with the planning authority.

    A 30-floor tower with 180 homes on Einstein Street, the neighborhood's southern edge, with completion expected in mid-2028. Until then, expect construction work and site traffic nearby. The distance from the building depends on the exact address.3

  • Einstein 1 project5

    179 apartments. Construction was planned to start in 2025, per content published in Globes in 2024; the current status is to be confirmed.

    191 vacant dunams between the neighborhood and Propes Street. A 2021 plan allows 1,530 homes but no permits were issued; in March 2025 the permit freeze was extended by three years for re-planning, and heights could rise from 15 to 45 floors. Views to the north may change in the long term.

  • Sde Dov center plan28

    7,128 homes in towers of 20 and 45 floors. Objections were rejected in February 2025; the skyline to the west and south-west will change, and works will continue for years.

  • Plan 3700, north-west Tel Aviv29

    About 12,000 homes north of Propes Street: a large new supply and long-term construction north of the neighborhood.

Before you decide

The checks worth making

The questions a careful tenant asks about the unit and the building, and the documents to obtain.

  • What is the lease term, is there an option to extend, and is the rent fixed or linked to the index?
  • Which securities will the landlord ask for (bank guarantee, promissory note, guarantors), and for what amounts?
  • Does the tenant pay a broker fee, how much, and does the amount include VAT?
  • What are the current monthly va'ad bayit and the bi-monthly arnona bill, and which area and zone appear on the bill?
  • Are the 2 parking spaces registered to the unit, side by side or in tandem, and underground or at ground level? Where is the storage room and how big is it?
  • Is the apartment furnished, and which appliances and air conditioners stay?
  • What are the working hours at the Einstein 15 site, and is other building work planned next to the building?
  • Which way does the balcony face, and what exactly is the open area in front of it?
  • Is the 1.10.2026 entry date firm, and are pets allowed?
  • Which route is the path to the sea, and is it lit and open all year?
  • The draft lease, including guarantees, indexation and option
  • The unit's latest arnona bill
  • Confirmation of the landlord's rights in the unit and both parking spaces
  • A handover record and equipment list

What does the law say about guarantees?
At rents up to NIS 20,000 the Fair Rental Law applies, and guarantees are capped at the lower of 3 months' rent or a third of the total rent: up to NIS 28,500 on a lease of 9 months or more.32,34
