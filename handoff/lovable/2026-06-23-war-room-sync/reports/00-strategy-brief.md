# 00 — Strategy brief

One page. Read this before anything else.

## Thesis

NadLan is **not** another Israeli listings board. Yad2 and Madlan own that game on brand search (yad2 = 36% of its traffic from brand query "יד 2"; "מדלן" alone = 74,000/mo). We will not out-brand them in 24 months.

What is wide open, today, with real Semrush numbers (Report 3A):
- **`פרויקטים חדשים <city>` — KD 0 across every city, $2–5 CPC.** Direct contractor-monetization fit.
- **`דירות להשכרה <city>` — KD ≤ 22, ~21k vol/mo aggregate.** Compounding authority play.
- **`area-info/<hood>`** — the Madlan template, copy-able verbatim, KD low.
- **Foreign buyers** — nobody serves them well in Hebrew RE in Israel. Aliyah, French, US, Chinese capital all underserved.

**Our wedge:** the best new-projects experience in Israel (3D showroom, foreign-buyer-ready, contractor-funded), wrapped in money-keyword SEO that compounds.

## Who we serve (in priority order)

1. **Contractors / developers** — they pay. The product is built to make them want to pay.
2. **Foreign buyers** — Aliyah, investors, returning Israelis. Highest LTV per lead.
3. **Local serious buyers** — primary market (new projects) first, secondary later.
4. **Renters** — entry-point traffic via the lowest-KD city pages. Conversion bridge: "rent → buy in 18mo".
5. **Real-estate professionals** — kept, demoted in nav, monetized via tiers + featured-upsell.

## What we are

- A **new-projects showroom platform** with the best 3D unit picker in Israel.
- A **money-page SEO engine** — programmatic city + neighborhood pages built on real data.
- A **foreign-buyer concierge** — multilingual funnels, FX, tax explainers, verified contractors.
- An **advertiser marketplace** for contractors — tiers, placement auction, lead routing, billing already wired (`offers`, `placement-auction`, `tiers`, `greeninvoice-recurring`).

## What we are not

- Not a free Yad2 clone. We don't compete on listings volume.
- Not a brokers-only directory. Pros are a feature, not the product.
- Not a "beautiful AI landing page". Every pixel either ranks, converts, or charges.
- Not English-translated Hebrew. Foreign-buyer pages are written for that persona, not localized.

## North-star metrics

- **Showroom lead → contractor handoff rate** (Premium tier success metric)
- **City money-page MRR** (placement-auction ARPU)
- **Foreign-buyer concierge conversion** (booked calls / qualified intakes)
- **`/projects/<slug>/` LCP on 4G mid-range Android** (≤ 2.5s — the demo only sells if it loads)

## What ships before we tell contractors to pay

Three pilot showrooms, fully wired:

1. **Dimri Yama** (`/project/dimri-yama/`) — luxury beachfront tower, richest existing payload.
2. **Rainbow Tel Aviv** (`/project/rainbow-tel-aviv/`) — has the real GLB and richest environment data; "Premier verified" reference.
3. **Urban-renewal pilot** (TBD slug, תמא 38 / פינוי-בינוי) — proves the template scales down and serves a different buyer persona (existing-residents + investors, not luxury).

Each pilot must demonstrate: floor pick → unit pick → exterior view → interior tour → lead, on a phone, in under 10 seconds.

## What we sell to the first 10 contractors

A package, not a website. See `04-monetization-playbook.md`:
- Verified (free) — listing + lead capture.
- **Premium (₪ 4–8k/mo)** — full 3D showroom, English page, WhatsApp routing, city placement.
- **Lighthouse (₪ 15–25k/mo)** — Premium + homepage hero + foreign-buyer concierge + monthly analytics.

Plus a per-city **placement auction** for the KD-0 `פרויקטים חדשים <city>` hero slot.
