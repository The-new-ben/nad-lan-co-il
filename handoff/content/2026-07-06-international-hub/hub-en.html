How buying from abroad actually works

  1. Choose the apartment remotely. Use the 3D building, the room-by-room walk and the window view to shortlist without flying. Every estimate on our pages is labeled as an estimate.
  2. Appoint an Israeli real-estate lawyer. In Israel the buyer's lawyer runs the deal: due diligence on the developer, the land registry, and the contract. Budget roughly 0.5-1.5% plus VAT.
  3. Sign a power of attorney so the purchase can complete while you are abroad. Your consulate or an Israeli notary handles this.
  4. Open the money route early. Currency transfer and anti-money-laundering checks on foreign funds take time; specialist FX providers usually beat retail bank rates on ILS conversion.
  5. Mortgage pre-approval if you need one. Non-residents can borrow from Israeli banks at around 50% of value. Pre-approval before signing protects your deposit.
  6. Contract and staged payments. New-build payments follow the construction schedule, each one secured under the Sale Law.
  7. Delivery and registration. Your lawyer registers the rights; expect this to run months after handover in new districts.

Questions we answer every week

Can foreigners buy property in Israel?

Yes. There is no citizenship or residency requirement for buying most private residential property in Israel. The differences are in purchase tax brackets and mortgage ceilings, not in the right to buy.

How much is purchase tax for a foreign buyer?

In 2026, foreign residents pay 8% on the part of the price up to ILS 6,055,070 and 10% above it. New immigrants may qualify for reduced brackets on a qualifying home.

Can a non-resident get an Israeli mortgage?

Yes, typically up to about 50% of the property value, from the main Israeli banks. Expect fuller documentation on foreign income and slightly higher rates than residents.

Can I complete a purchase without coming to Israel?

Yes. With an Israeli lawyer and a notarized power of attorney the entire transaction can close while you are abroad. Our 3D apartment selection exists precisely so the choosing can happen remotely too.

How is my money protected when buying a new build?

The Israeli Sale Law requires the developer to secure every payment, most commonly with a bank guarantee. No serious purchase should happen outside this mechanism.

What rental yield should I expect?

Central Tel Aviv runs roughly 3 to 3.6% gross. Secondary coastal cities can run higher. Yield is only one part of the return; most foreign buyers in Israel also weigh currency, community and long-term appreciation.
