# Focused international benchmark patterns

This is a narrow product-pattern comparison, not a global market study. Three first-party project/residence experiences were reviewed because each solves one relevant problem well.

## Pattern comparison

| Reference | Observed pattern | Evidence | Apply to EcoCity pilot | Do not copy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| One High Line, New York | Compact navigation separates building, neighborhood, interiors, amenities, availability and team; availability is a structured decision surface | [Official home page](https://www.onehighlineresidences.com/) and [official availability page](https://www.onehighlineresidences.com/availability/) | Give project story and availability distinct responsibilities; retain a persistent inquiry route; let buyers filter verified units and open plans | Prices, claims, typography, imagery or luxury tone; EcoCity has no approved inventory feed yet |
| One Sydney Harbour | Status is immediate and concrete; available homes show residence ID, beds, baths, parking, area and state; location, club and collaboration are separate modules | [Official project site](https://www.onesydneyharbour.com/) | Put dated project status near the H1; when a feed arrives, create complete unit cards rather than decorative dots; make project team and location legible | “Limited remaining,” ready-to-move-in urgency or amenity claims not true for these projects |
| Mandarin Oriental Residences, Fifth Avenue | Positioning is tied to a defined operating proposition and enumerated services, not vague luxury language | [Official residence page](https://www.mandarinoriental.com/en/residences/current/fifth-avenue-new-york) | If EcoCity supplies a verified service/specification, describe it concretely and attach it to the relevant buyer need | Hotel-service language, amenity lists, brand prestige or service promises unrelated to EcoCity |

## Five patterns worth adopting

### 1. One strong project idea

Leading pages repeat a project-specific idea rather than a generic “luxury living” slogan. For Stricker-Brandeis the idea is a two-building choice in one project. For Bnei Dan it is daily life along Yarkon Park. Every module should reinforce the correct idea with facts.

### 2. Availability as a product, not a gallery

One High Line and One Sydney Harbour expose residence data as structured choices. The EcoCity pilot should do the same only after a live contract exists. Until then, showing an empty/disabled “current information required” state is more credible than filling cards from a mockup.

### 3. Separate the building, home and neighborhood

A buyer should not hunt through one long cinematic page. The architecture, interiors/plans, location, project team, availability and inquiry paths must be separately addressable, shareable and usable by keyboard.

### 4. Status before aspiration

One Sydney Harbour puts completion and availability state at the top. The pilot should show a dated “marketing / pre-construction” label before lifestyle storytelling. Planned 2028 occupancy is qualified, never presented as a guaranteed delivery.

### 5. Concrete services and specifications

Mandarin Oriental can enumerate services because an operating brand stands behind them. EcoCity pages should enumerate only what EcoCity has approved in the specification. If the source package does not define a service, material or amenity, omit it.

## Patterns deliberately rejected

- Full-screen video that delays the page’s factual answer.
- Scroll-jacking and forced animation.
- Autoplay audio.
- Availability dots with no accessible table.
- Luxury adjectives as substitutes for plans and specifications.
- Artificial scarcity.
- View claims without viewpoint, context date and disclaimer.
- A global-language selector that serves the same English text under different URLs.

## Pilot differentiation

The international references are sales sites. nad-lan can outperform them on buyer trust by exposing a fact register, source date, missing-data state, independent-site disclosure, buyer checklist and 3D provenance. The goal is not to imitate Manhattan or Sydney aesthetics. It is to combine a premium presentation with verifiable Israeli project information.
