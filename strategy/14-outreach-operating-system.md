# Outreach Operating System

Core rule:
Every contact with developers, contractors, professionals, or property owners must be legal, documented, sender-identified, and consent-aware. No spam, no aggressive scraping, no false claims.

Required capabilities:
- CRM contact source.
- Consent status.
- Suppression list.
- Unsubscribe handling where required.
- Sender identity.
- Outreach segment.
- Message template version.
- Follow-up schedule.
- Outcome tracking.

Legal:
- Israeli direct marketing/spam exposure requires legal review before automation. `LEGAL_REVIEW`
- Privacy Authority guidance and consent/suppression handling require legal review. `LEGAL_REVIEW`

Do not build:
- Bulk auto-sender.
- Scraper-to-message pipeline.
- Outreach that claims verification or partnership without proof.
