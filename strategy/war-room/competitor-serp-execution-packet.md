# Competitor And SERP Execution Packet

Date: 2026-06-23

Status: current web evidence packet. This is not Semrush, GSC, or final rank tracking.

## Purpose

This packet converts the weak competitor row in the war-room gap map into a build-ready research layer. It is designed to prevent two mistakes:

- Competing only on easy leftovers.
- Publishing thin money pages that copy strong competitors without a product advantage.

## Evidence Standard

The packet uses current public web sources captured on 2026-06-23. It records observed surfaces, not guaranteed ranking positions or traffic volumes.

No volume, difficulty, CPC, click-through, or revenue forecast is claimed here. Those require paid SEO exports and GSC data.

## Main Finding

NadLan has to compete across the whole real-estate market, but not with one generic page type.

Competitors own separate layers:

- Yad2 owns broad classifieds and user habit.
- Yad1 and Madlan own new-project discovery.
- OnMap owns a visible map-first search angle.
- Nadlan.com shows the expected depth for project media, plans, tours, map layers, and surrounding data.
- Nadlan Center owns guide and industry-content authority.
- Banks own high-trust mortgage calculator expectations.
- Professional directories own review and supplier matching mechanics.
- English and international portals own foreign-buyer familiarity.

NadLan's route is an integrated decision system:

- verified project pages
- real asset state
- 3D and unit journey where assets exist
- city and project SEO ownership
- buyer calculators
- professional routing
- foreign-buyer concierge
- contractor dashboard and measurable lead value

## Required Build Decisions

1. Listings and city listing pages stay gated by real supply.
2. Project pages become the main B2B monetization surface.
3. The showroom is not only a visual object. It must include unit, view, floor, area, finance, professionals, and lead path.
4. Guides support money pages. They do not replace them.
5. Professional pages must be verified, routed by transaction stage, and clearly governed.
6. English buyer pages launch only with legal/tax caution and remote-buyer flow.
7. Country-abroad pages remain future pages until source, legal, tax, and partner gates exist.
