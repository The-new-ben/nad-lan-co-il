# A1 - Keyword And SERP Master Expansion

Status: starter registry. Metrics are not final.
Required expansion target: 1500-3000 rows.
Current committed CSV: starter set for page ownership and planning.

## Method

Each keyword row must eventually include:

- keyword and variants,
- language,
- category/subcategory,
- geo,
- intent and funnel stage,
- SERP type/features,
- dominant media needed,
- volume/KD/CPC/traffic value,
- top competitors and why they rank,
- NadLan target URL,
- source-of-truth page owner,
- UX/data/schema modules,
- internal links,
- cannibalization risk,
- monetization path,
- priority and build action.

Metrics are marked `NEEDS_VERIFICATION` until checked in Semrush/GSC/live SERP tools.
