## Strategic correction

Do not interpret Lovable's cautious language as a retreat from difficult real-estate categories. NadLan is a long-range category build and should compete across the full Israeli real-estate market. Staging means readiness gating, not surrender:

- real supply before commodity listing SEO;
- official or licensed assets before project/showroom promotion;
- legal review before tax, mortgage, contract, and ranking pages;
- official data before market reports and calculators;
- one canonical owner page per keyword to prevent cannibalization.
