# P1 City And Neighborhood Pages

Objective: create useful local pages without fighting Yad2/Madlan head-on before inventory depth exists.

## Tasks

1. Pick first 5 cities and 10 neighborhoods.
2. For each, collect source data: price, transactions, transport, schools, urban plans.
3. Create page template with map/data/tools/projects.
4. Link to relevant projects and legal/tools.
5. Avoid duplicate city vs neighborhood content.

## Acceptance

- Every city/neighborhood page has unique data and sources.
- No page is just boilerplate.
- Internal links follow hub/spoke model.
- Pages with insufficient data remain draft/noindex.
