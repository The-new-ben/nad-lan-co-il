- Big-money page architecture.

- Listings UX foundation.

Do not build yet:
- Do not start broad SEO or homepage redesign before Stage 1 public trust proof is green.
- Do not continue Dimri/showroom implementation until the #212 theme change is live and final screenshots are committed.
- Do not build outreach automation before legal review and suppression/consent tracking exist.
- Do not claim Semrush/GSC-backed opportunity without actual exported data.
- Do not ship fake facades, silent fallbacks, dead buttons, or unlabelled concept assets.
- Do not reject a money keyword because it is competitive or because Yad2/Madlan currently occupy the market. Stage by readiness, not by fear.

Requires owner approval:
- Any deploy, merge, server pull, plugin update, public pricing, legal/outreach wording, paid package terms, or official-project asset decision.

Requires Semrush/GSC verification:
- Keyword volumes, difficulty, click potential, cannibalization diagnosis, AI-search opportunity, and page prioritization beyond existing repo/live evidence.

Requires legal review:
- Outreach, direct marketing, consent, suppression list policy, paid placement disclosures, professional/legal service wording, refund/SLA claims, and any legal-advice-adjacent product.

Requires visual QA:
- Every trust, layout, homepage, listings, and showroom change at 1440, 768, and 390 widths with committed screenshots.
