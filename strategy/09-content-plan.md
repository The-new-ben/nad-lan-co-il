# Content Plan

Content goal:
Support the hardest commercial terms through authoritative guides, calculators, project pages, and directory surfaces.

Priority content groups:
- Buying apartment.
- Selling apartment.
- Mortgage.
- Purchase tax.
- Betterment levy.
- Real-estate lawyer.
- Tabu/registration checks.
- Urban renewal: TAMA 38, pinui-binui, tenant rights.
- New projects and developer due diligence.
- Professional selection: appraiser, lawyer, mortgage advisor, inspector.

Quality bar:
- Buyer-facing Hebrew.
- Legal/tax accuracy. `LEGAL_REVIEW` where advice-adjacent.
- No internal monetization language.
- Clear source notes.
- Useful tables/checklists where relevant.
- Internal links up/down the hub-spoke tree.

Do not:
- Use AI filler.
- Publish pages with no unique claim.
- Split one intent into multiple competing pages.
