# Programmatic SEO Plan

Programmatic SEO should scale only after trust and architecture are stable.

Candidate page families:
- City listings.
- City rentals.
- City new projects.
- Neighborhood new projects.
- Developer project pages.
- Professional category/city combinations.
- Urban renewal city pages.

Minimum indexable-page standard:
- Unique data.
- Clear intent.
- Real inventory or reliable source data.
- Buyer-facing copy, not template filler.
- Breadcrumbs, canonical, schema, and internal links.
- No public debug/template/internal text.

Thin-page policy:
- If data is missing or not unique, use noindex or keep unpublished.
- Do not index placeholder pages to chase volume.

Required before build:
- Semrush/GSC target list. `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`
- URL namespace map. `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`
- Template QA gates. `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`
