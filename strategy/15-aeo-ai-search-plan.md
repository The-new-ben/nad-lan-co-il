# AEO And AI Search Plan

Goal:
Make NadLan answer-ready for AI search while preserving normal SEO fundamentals.

Required surfaces:
- Clear entity pages.
- FAQ blocks aligned with visible content.
- Strong schema.
- Internal links and source notes.
- Concise answer sections on money pages.
- llms.txt strategy. `NEEDS_VERIFICATION`

Candidate answer intents:
- How to check a real-estate deal.
- Purchase tax basics.
- Betterment levy basics.
- How to choose a real-estate lawyer.
- How to compare new project units.
- What to check before buying from a contractor.

Do not:
- Publish unverified legal/tax advice. `LEGAL_REVIEW`
- Create answer content that contradicts visible page content.
