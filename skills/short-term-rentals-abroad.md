## The "7 warnings" section (already in the widget)

1. Regulation changes yearly. Verify the current permit at signing.
2. Gross ≠ net. Management 15-25%, cleaning, platform fee, local tax, maintenance, insurance, low season. Net is typically 3-5pp lower.
3. **Thailand <30 nights is illegal** without hotel license. Solution: 30+ night minimum.
4. Platforms will report income to authorities. EU 2024/1028 forces Airbnb/Booking to share data starting May 2026; Israeli tax authority gets it via exchange agreements.
5. Buying a 3rd property — even abroad — may trigger Israeli higher purchase-tax under evolving rulings. Pre-advice mandatory.
6. Foreign ownership zones. Thailand: condos only (quota). Dubai: only Freehold zones. Verify the area before paying.
7. Currency. A 10% depreciation in baht/euro wipes your "yield." Foreign RE is a currency bet too.
