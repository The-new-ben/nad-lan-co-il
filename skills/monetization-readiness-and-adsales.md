# Skill: Monetization, Ad-Sales & Business Readiness (real-estate content site)

> The honest playbook for selling advertising/listings/sponsorship on nad-lan.co.il and delivering real value. Built from cited competitor research (global + Israel) + a verified audit of what the site has today. Core principle: **never sell exposure we can't measure or an outcome we can't control. Sell a verifiable artifact, a guaranteed position/duration, or a pay-per-result.**

## A. What competitors actually deliver for money (the bar)

**Global (what the buyer GETS, and the guarantee):**
- **Zillow Premier Agent** = share-of-voice impression ads by ZIP. Official terms **explicitly disclaim any impression or lead guarantee** ("as is"). Reporting = lead dashboard + answer-rate. Lesson: even the leader sells best-efforts visibility, legally hedged — only sustainable because of traffic dominance.
- **Rightmove (UK)** = listing-depth upgrades. **Featured = guaranteed top-of-results for the period** (the cleanest "guaranteed deliverable"). Premium = 3 photos + highlight; claims +35% brochure views. Every membership includes a **trackable phone number + call recording** (attribution). Agency ARPA £1,530; growth is from upselling MORE products per agent.
- **REA / realestate.com.au** = depth ladder (Feature→Highlight→Premiere→Luxe), guaranteed relative position by tier. Audience Maximiser recently moved **impressions→clicks** (pay for a measurable action). Ignite dashboard = real-time views/enquiries/saves + competitor market-share.
- **Apartments.com/CoStar** = tiers with exposure multipliers (Diamond +1000% vs Silver) — but footnoted as **historical averages, not guarantees**. Deliverables include analytics portal + call recording + photo/3D specs per tier.
- **Redfin / Realtor.com ReadyConnect** = **pay-at-close referral** (~30-35% of commission). The only truly outcome-tied model; guarantees a referral, not a sale, policed by response-time SLAs.
- **Content/advice sites (Bankrate/NerdWallet/Houzz)** = performance affiliate (CPL/CPA) + display + sponsored. Houzz sells flat-monthly exposure, not guaranteed leads.

**Israel (cited ₪, pre-VAT):**
- **Free private listing** is standard (Yad2/Madlan/Homeless/Komo). Paid promotion = highlight + top placement + auto-bump for a fixed window. **WinWin benchmark: ₪49-99**, duration 60-180 days. **Komo PPV: ~₪0.50/view floor.** Yad2/Madlan current promo prices are **sales-gated/unpublished**.
- **Broker subscriptions:** unpublished; TheMarker reports Yad2 raised broker prices "tens of percent." Brokers must buy a package (can't post private).
- **Developer (Yad1):** project **mini-site + 3D tours + cross-placement + lead feed**, "packages by project type," price on inquiry. Pitch = ~4M entries / 1.6M visitors / 650K project-views monthly.
- **Sponsored content (best-documented):** TheMarker **₪15,000** (client writes) / **₪18,000** (they write), with a **guaranteed minimum 1,500 unique visitors** + Friday print. Walla ~₪4,200, Calcalist ~₪2,000, Reshet13 ~₪1,500. Niche real-estate site realistic band: **₪350-3,000/article**.
- **Leads (cited price list, ex-VAT):** new apartment domestic **₪90-120**, foreign **₪100-140**; investment **₪80-140**; mortgage **₪50-80**; lawyers **₪40-80**; renovation **₪50-200**. Exclusive leads top of range.
- **Banner CPM (Israeli digital benchmark):** **₪4-20 CPM**.

**The cross-cutting lesson:** the products that DON'T generate "I paid, what did I get?" complaints are: (1) a **verifiable asset** (a page/profile that exists regardless of traffic), (2) **guaranteed position for a fixed duration**, (3) a **reporting dashboard/recorded calls**, or (4) **pay-per-result** (lead/close). The fragile product is undifferentiated impression/"exposure."

## C. The product ladder — what we can sell HONESTLY, by phase

**Customer types & their artifact:**
1. **Real-estate professionals** (agents, lawyers, mortgage advisors, appraisers) → directory profile (Basic/Pro/Premier) + leads.
2. **Developers/contractors** → project mini-site (`nadlan_project`) + lead form + campaign.
3. **Private sellers/buyers** → promoted listing.
4. **Advertisers/brands** → banner + sponsored article (labeled ממומן).

**Phase 0 — NOW (pre-traffic). Sell only asset-based + performance-based:**
- **Pay-per-lead** (carry the risk ourselves): ₪90-150 new-apartment, ₪80-140 investment, ₪50-80 mortgage/lawyer. Value is self-evident — they pay per lead delivered.
- **Founding-member directory**: discount Pro/Premier as "founding" (e.g., free-to-₪149/mo for 6-12 months) in exchange for a testimonial. Honest because we're transparent it's launch pricing.
- **Developer/seller asset pages** priced as a *deliverable* (page built, X photos, 3D tour, lead form, live for N months) — NOT as traffic. Reprice the ₪10,990/19,990 developer SKUs as launch/performance until traffic exists.
- ❌ Do NOT sell ₪19,990 "exposure" campaigns or guaranteed-views sponsored content yet.

**Phase 1 — some traffic (3-6 mo): add** sponsored articles with **transparent live stats** (no guarantee yet), directory subscriptions at real price, listing promotion (₪49-199 highlight, 60-180 days, like WinWin).

**Phase 2 — established traffic: add** guaranteed-minimum-views sponsored content (TheMarker model, with make-good), banner CPM (₪4-20), premium developer campaigns, full rate card.

## D. Can we commit on traffic? How to report?
- **Today: NO traffic commitments** (we have neither traffic nor measurement). Commit only on what we control: the **asset** (page live for N months, spec'd), **position** (top of category for the period), and **deliverables** (article, 3D, lead form). For audience, sell **pay-per-lead** so we carry the risk.
- **Install measurement FIRST** (the #1 blocker): GA4 + Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster (Bing also powers ChatGPT Search). Then per-advertiser **monthly report**: impressions, page views, clicks, leads, time-on-page, search queries.
- **Once traffic is proven:** offer a **guaranteed minimum** (e.g., "≥X unique visitors in 30 days or we extend free / refund") — TheMarker's 1,500-uniques model — never before we reliably exceed it. Add **call-tracking numbers** (Rightmove model) for attribution.

## E. Legal must-dos (Israel)
1. **Sponsored-content disclosure is mandatory** — Consumer Protection Law §7(c): advertising disguised as an article is misleading even if true. Label every paid/benefit-driven piece clearly at the top: **"תוכן שיווקי" / "ממומן" / "בשיתוף"**, prominent and unambiguous (AG standard). A *benefit* (not just cash) triggers the duty. Fines ~₪29,490-53,070/violation (§22g) + class-action risk. **Build a reusable "ממומן" label block now.**
2. **Broker-license carve-out** — Brokers Law §2(c): publishing listings/info about interested parties needs **NO broker license**, as long as we (a) never call the site/staff "מתווך", (b) use no broker-implying title, (c) never personally match a specific buyer+seller for a transaction fee or take a % of a deal. **Stay a publisher/advertising platform**: sell listings, sponsored content, banners, and *advertising leads* priced as media — never transaction commissions. §9: brokers need a signed written order to collect fees — another reason to stay on the publishing side.
