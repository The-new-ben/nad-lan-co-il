# Skill: Authority & E-E-A-T Program (lawyer-owned site, bar-compliant)

> How nad-lan.co.il builds maximum search/AI authority WITHOUT (a) reading as a sales funnel, (b) violating Israeli Bar advertising rules, or (c) conflicting with any future property-listing/brokerage business. Grounded in verified research (sources at bottom). **This is research, not a legal opinion — before launching property listings, get a one-time ועדת אתיקה (ethics committee) review.**

## The tension we are solving
The owner is עו"ד בן בטש (bar #29020), real-estate + מיסוי מקרקעין. His name + title + license appear as a byline on every article. The owner flagged this as "too upfront / salesy" and worried about bar-rule conflict, especially since the site may later list/sell real estate. Both concerns are legitimate.

## What Israeli Bar rules actually allow (and forbid)
**Governing rules:** כללי לשכת עורכי הדין (פרסומת), התשס"א-2001 (amended 2018). Structure = *permissive whitelist*: a lawyer may publish only what §3(ב) lists.

PERMITTED (all currently used or safe to use):
- Name; the fact of being a lawyer; **practice areas** ("עוסק בדיני מקרקעין ומיסוי מקרקעין") §3(ב)(10); contact details incl. **website + email** §3(ב)(8); admission/license date §3(ב)(13); **own published articles** §3(ב)(15); teaching roles §3(ב)(16); degrees.
- **License number** — not prohibited; neutral factual trust signal. Keep it.
- Neutral CTA ("צרו קשר / קביעת פגישת ייעוץ ראשונית") — permitted via §3(ב)(8)+(14).
- Genuine, non-misleading testimonials/ratings — allowed.

FORBIDDEN (audit the whole site for these):
- **"מומחה" / "מתמחה"** (expert/specialist) claims — write "עוסק ב..." not "מומחה ל...". (Asserted in ethics commentary; exact section unverified, but treat as hard rule.)
- **Superlatives / comparative claims** — "הטוב ביותר", "מספר 1", "מהמובילים" → fall under §2(א) (misleading) + dignity clause. Many firms violate this; not a safe model.
- **Anything misleading or harming the dignity of the profession** — §2(א) umbrella catch-all.
- **Solicitation (שידול)** of a specific reader about a specific matter; fee promises ("נחסוך לך מס"); success guarantees.
- Channel rules: no pop-up ads, no banner ads, no radio/TV.

**GOOD NEWS:** our current byline ("מאת בן בטש, עורך דין · חבר לשכת עורכי הדין · רישיון 29020") contains **no "מומחה" and no superlative** → already broadly compliant. The fix needed is *framing*, not removal.

## The lawyer + real-estate-business conflict (עיסוק אחר)
**Governing rules:** כללי לשכת עורכי הדין (עיסוק אחר), התשס"ג-2003. A lawyer MAY hold another business, but:
- **כלל 1(א)(1):** never act as broker/businessperson on a matter you handle(d) as a lawyer — i.e. cannot draft the contract AND broker the same deal.
- **כלל 1(ג):** absolute separation of documents, information, accounting between the law practice and the other business. Separate brand, letterhead, email, books.
- No blended identity (no dual-role business card / law-letterhead listings).

**Implication for the future catalog/listings:** treat it as a legally distinct entity — separate brand name, company, contact, accounting — and never represent a client legally on a deal the site brokered. **This is the real compliance cliff; the byline is not.** Ethics review required before launch.

## The program — concrete moves (priority order)
1. **Reframe the byline from "written by the lawyer who wants your business" → "reviewed-by editorial authority."** Site-wide, change to:
   > *נכתב ונערך על ידי צוות נדל"ן חכם · נבדק משפטית על ידי עו"ד בן בטש, חבר לשכת עורכי הדין (רישיון 29020), העוסק בדיני מקרקעין ומיסוי מקרקעין · עודכן [תאריך]*
   This keeps every E-E-A-T signal Google rewards (named credentialed reviewer, license, practice area, freshness) while removing the funnel feel and satisfying the SQRG "reviewed by qualified attorney" YMYL pattern. **Requires owner approval — it's a positioning decision, ~22 page edits, fully reversible.**
2. **Build two missing E-E-A-T pages** (currently 404): `/about/` (אודות — editorial mission, who reviews, credentials, teaching/publications per §3(ב)(15-16)) and `/editorial-policy/` (how content is researched, sourced, reviewed, updated). Both raise authority and are 100% bar-safe.
3. **Strengthen the author page** `/author/ben-betesh/` (exists ✓): add degrees, admission year, list of articles, teaching roles, `Person.sameAs` → bar profile + jus-tice.co.il + any LinkedIn. Earned-credential signals only, no superlatives.
4. **Make CTAs information-first, not sales-first.** Mid-article CTAs (added 2026-05-31) point to calculators + related guides, NOT a second lawyer pitch — this both reduces the "salesy" feel and lowers §2(א) solicitation risk. Keep exactly ONE neutral lawyer contact CTA per article (bottom).
5. **Per-article disclaimer on every page** (present ✓, keep): "המידע כללי, אינו ייעוץ משפטי ואינו תחליף לייעוץ פרטני; אין בקריאתו כדי ליצור יחסי עורך דין-לקוח." Serves E-E-A-T transparency + §2(א) anti-misleading + pre-empts professional/commercial mixing risk.
6. **Cite primary Israeli sources in-body** (רשות המסים, בנק ישראל, חקיקה, פסיקה). Triple win: E-E-A-T trust + GEO +30% citation lift + bar-safe (factual, non-promotional).
7. **Audit the whole site for "מומחה" and superlatives** — grep all 97 pages; replace any with "עוסק ב..." / factual phrasing.
