    Questions and answers

    Where can I find a map of the light rail in Gush Dan?

    On this page: the Red Line with its 34 running stations, the Purple Line (46 stations) and the Green Line (61 stations) under construction, the Metro and Israel Railways, all on one map with the new projects around each station.

    What is the Gush Dan Metro project?

    Three planned underground lines, M1, M2 and M3, with 109 stations in the Ministry of Transport file: M1 62 stations, M2 22, M3 25. The station positions in that file date from 12.2021 and may still change.

    How many stations does the Red Line have?

    34 stations, 10 of them underground, from Petah Tikva Central Station to HaKomemiyut in Bat Yam, per the Ministry of Transport timetable of 22.11.2026.

    How are the walking minutes worked out?

    From the straight-line distance between the project and the station, times 1.3 for the street grid, at about 80 metres a minute. It is an estimate; the real walk depends on crossings and entrances.
